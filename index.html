Moto Repair Log

Log what actually broke on your bike and what it cost you. See what other owners of the same model have had to fix. No account, takes a minute.

What should I log?
  • Something broke: anything that failed or wore out early and needed fixing, including warranty and recall work (mark who paid).
  • Scheduled service: optional. Valve checks, big services, anything from the service schedule.
  • Nothing broke yet: just as useful. Bikes with zero problems need to count too, or the numbers only reflect the unlucky owners.
Demo mode: entries are saved only in this browser. Paste your Apps Script URL into API_URL to go live.
