# Surface: motorcycleproblems.com landing page (three variants for review)

Scope: home/landing page, Persuade mode. Three variants built in full at the user's request (A assigned, B pick, C competitive challenger), code-led (no image generation on this machine). Review files only in site/landing-variants/, live site untouched.

Audience/job: used-bike buyers first (which model and year, what to check before paying), then owners (known pattern or one-off, what fails next, open recall), then riders comparing two models.
Action: model search ("check a bike before you buy"), working typeahead over real catalogue models with real record counts; model pages are not live yet, so results print an honest preview.
Proof: real figures only (21.4M UK MOT inspections on 3.24M motorcycles, 15,995 US complaints, recalls from US/UK/Canada/Japan/EU, 5,003 models). Real per-model figures for MT-07, SV650, Z900, Street Triple. Example classified listings are synthetic and labelled. No testimonials, no user counts. Founder's note is a draft for the owner to personalise.

## Direction contract

A — THE CLASSIFIEDS (assigned, seed b0762956, own list #5)
THESIS: the page is the buyer's classifieds spread, marked up in red pen with what our records say; refuses the SaaS split hero with a stats row.
OWN-WORLD: cool newsprint grey ground, black ink, one masthead red, buyer's red ballpoint and yellow highlighter; condensed display grotesque for masthead and prices, newspaper serif body, handwritten annotation; column rules, ad boxes, no cards.
STORY: buyer recognises the listing page they already read, sees each ad annotated with real failure and recall facts, types their bike into the WANTED box.
FIRST VIEWPORT: masthead across full width with proof strap; left two columns headline + subtitle + WANTED search box; right three columns of example ads with live red-pen markup.
FORM: classifieds broadsheet, list position 5, seed b0762956.
Raise (from Hatch Show Print, declined): every masthead and section band fills the full measure.

B — THE WORKSHOP MANUAL (Impeccable's pick, own list #1)
THESIS: an owner's workshop manual chapter "Before you buy", with a numbered-callout drawing where each callout is a real failure rate; refuses opinion scores.
OWN-WORLD: white coated manual stock, black line art, one workshop orange spot colour, grey thumb-index tabs; technical grotesque, tabular figures, figure captions, spec tables.
STORY: rider sees a technical drawing of a bike with failure rates on its parts, understands "data not opinion", looks up their model in the spec-plate field.
FIRST VIEWPORT: thumb-index tabs at right edge; chapter heading and numbered checks left; Fig. 1 bike elevation centre-right with callouts; model field as a spec plate under the heading.
FORM: workshop manual, list position 1.
Raise (from Lowicz papercut, declined): depth by overlap of flat layers, never by shading.

C — THE MILEAGE DESCENT (fused challenger: mesophotic deep dive, competitive on clarity)
