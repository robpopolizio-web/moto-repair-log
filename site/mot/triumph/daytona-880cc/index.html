10.6% of Triumph Daytona (880 cc) annual MOTs in Great Britain were failed first time (161 MOTs, 2025). That is less often than the average for 501-900 cc bikes (12.3%).

The parts it failed on most: brake performance (2.5%), brakes (2.5%), brake pads (1.9%).
