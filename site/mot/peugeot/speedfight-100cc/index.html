19.8% of Peugeot Speedfight (100 cc) annual MOTs in Great Britain were failed first time (242 MOTs, 2025). That is less often than the average for up to 125 cc bikes (23.0%).

The parts it failed on most: headlight (5.0%), tyre (3.7%), handlebar (2.9%).
