9.5% of Harley-Davidson Xl (1200 cc) annual MOTs in Great Britain were failed first time (3,568 MOTs, 2025). That is about as often as the average for over 900 cc bikes (9.3%).

The parts it failed on most: number plate and id (2.8%), reflectors (2.1%), headlight (2.1%).

Typical mileage at MOT: 7,950 miles.
