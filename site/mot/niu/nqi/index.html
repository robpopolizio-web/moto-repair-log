11.9% of Niu Nqi annual MOTs in Great Britain were failed first time (662 MOTs, 2025). That is about as often as the average for electric bikes (12.6%).

The parts it failed on most: brake pads (2.7%), tyre (2.4%), headlight (2.3%).

Typical mileage at MOT: 14,550 miles.
