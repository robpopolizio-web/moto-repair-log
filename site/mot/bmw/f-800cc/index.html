8.4% of BMW F (800 cc) annual MOTs in Great Britain were failed first time (5,392 MOTs, 2025). That is less often than the average for 501-900 cc bikes (12.3%).

The parts it failed on most: headlight (2.5%), rear shock (1.5%), tyre (1.0%).

Typical mileage at MOT: 20,450 miles.
