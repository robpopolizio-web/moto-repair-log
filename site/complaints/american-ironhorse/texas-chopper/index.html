Recalls (3)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

SUSPENSION/SHOCK ABSORBER (suspension): 2005 · 05V199000 · 2005-05-05 · 199 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles equipped with shock absorbers manufactured by progressive suspension, inc. The shock absorbers have a spring plate assembly that was installed upside down resulting in the shock absorber failing to hold the weight of the motorcycle and in some instances could suddenly decompress while the motorcycle is in operation.

Risk. Shock absorber failure will cause the motorcycle to lean greatly to the side of the defective shock absorber increasing the risk of a crash, serious injuries or death.

Fix. Dealers will inspect all shock absorbers and replace all defective shock absorbers free of charge. The recall began on may 18, 2005. Owners may contact american ironhorse at 1-888-618-4766.

AMERICAN IRONHORSE /BATTERY (electrical and instruments): 2004 · 04V395000 · 2004-08-10 · 2,170 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the battery (ytx24hl) could fail during operation.

Risk. The battery failure causes the motorcycle engine to stop suddenly, which could result in a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace these batteries. The recall began september 1, 2004. Owners should contact american ironhorse at 1-817-665-2074.

AMERICAN IRONHORSE/BRAKE ROTOR-CARRIER (brakes and abs): 2002-2004 · 03V521000 · 2003-12-11 · 2,395 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles equipped with optional brake rotor-carriers,the rotor-carriers can crack while in service and possibly break.

Risk. This can cause a partial loss of the brake system or the potential for rotor-carrier detachment, which could result in a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the rotor-carrier assembly. Owner notification began january 26, 2004. Owners should contact american ironhorse at 1-817-665-2045.
