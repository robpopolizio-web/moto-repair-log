Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Frame: 1996 · 95V245000 · 1995-12-20 · 1,601 bikes

Problem. Improper weld procedures were used to attach the tongue channel to the trailer frame.

Risk. Possible separation of the tongue from the trailer could occur increasing the risk of a vehicle accident.

Fix. Dealers will install four bolts to reinforce the tongue channel weld to the frame.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov
