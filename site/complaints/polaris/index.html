SLINGSHOT R, SLINGSHOT S, SLINGSHOT SL · Rearview Camera Image May Not Display/FMVSS 111 (rider aids (traction, stability)): 2020-2023 · 23V466000 · 2023-07-03 · 22,218 bikes

Problem. Polaris Industries Inc. (Polaris) is recalling certain 2020 Slingshot GT, 2021-2023 Slingshot S, Slingshot S Tech, 2020-2023 Slingshot R, Slingshot SL, and 2022-2023 Slingshot SLR vehicles updated with Ride Command Software. After shifting out of reverse, the rearview camera image may freeze and display a blank screen if shifted into reverse again. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."

Risk. The loss of the rearview camera image while in reverse can reduce the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will update the Ride Command software. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed July 28, 2023. Owners may contact Polaris' customer service at 1-855-863-2284. Polaris' number for this recall is T-23-03.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. A software error may cause the rear camera image to freeze when shifting out of reverse and display a black screen if shifted into reverse again.

What the new part or software changes. The Ride Command software will be updated to address the reported concern.

Fixed in production. On June 9, 2023, the availability of the Ride Command software update was paused. The update was not deployed in production units.

Maker's recall no. T-23-03

How the problem was found.

  • In May 2023, during testing on Model Year 2024 Slingshot pre-production units, Polaris observed that after shifting out of reverse, the rear camera image may freeze and may not be displayed if shifted to reverse again. Thereafter, Polaris initiated an investigation, which included testing performed internally on Slingshot production units with the Ride Command software SLS7AB.M32304A update.
  • On June 27, 2023, Polaris’ Executive Review Committee determined that on Model Year 2020-2023 Slingshot vehicles updated with Ride Command software SLS7AB.M32304A (released April 2023), after shifting out of reverse, the rear camera image may freeze and the rear camera image may not be displayed if shifted to reverse again. SLINGSHOT R, SLINGSHOT S, SLINGSHOT SL, SLINGSHOT SLR · Alternator May Fail and Cause Fire (electrical and instruments): 2022-2023 · 23V106000 · 2023-02-20 · 163 bikes

Problem. Polaris Inc. (Polaris) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Slingshot R, Slingshot S with Technology Package 1, Slingshot SL, Slingshot SLR, and Slingshot S motorcycles. The alternator may have been improperly heat-treated during manufacturing.

Risk. An improperly heat-treated alternator may crack and separate during operation, increasing the risk of a fire.

Fix. Dealers will replace the alternator, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed March 8, 2023. Owners may contact Polaris' customer service at 1-855-863-2284. Polaris' number for this recall is T-23-01.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The annealing process was missed by the supplier during the manufacture of the alternator.

What the new part or software changes. The remedy alternators will have had the annealing process completed.

Fixed in production. On January 9, 2023, Polaris stopped shipment of units with the suspect alternators. Shipments resumed on January 19, 2023 when units were built with alternators that had the annealing process completed.

Part made by: Posco International America Corp, United States · Maker's recall no. T-23-01

How the problem was found.

  • In October 2022, Polaris received two reports of failed alternators on its off-road vehicles. In response, Polaris began investigating, which included outreach to the alternator supplier. Polaris learned from the supplier that the annealing process was missed within a certain lot of alternators, which were assembled in certain Model Year 2022-2023 Slingshot vehicles.
  • On February 14, 2023, Polaris’ Executive Review Committee determined that the alternators may have been improperly manufactured by the supplier and may crack and separate during operation, posing a fire hazard and risk of serious injury. No incidents have been identified on Model Year 2022-2023 Slingshot vehicles related to the reported concern.
