Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Frame: 1996 · 95V245000 · 1995-12-20 · 1,601 bikes

Problem. Improper weld procedures were used to attach the tongue channel to the trailer frame.

Risk. Possible separation of the tongue from the trailer could occur increasing the risk of a vehicle accident.

Fix. Dealers will install four bolts to reinforce the tongue channel weld to the frame.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

What owners wrote

The 4 most recent complaints, as filed (lightly reformatted).

2001 Polaris 1633 · 4,000 miles · Brakes & ABS · OH · filed 2004-07 · NHTSA 10083310While attempting to stop, the brakes failed without warning. As a result the driver had to pump them rapidly. Please provide additional information.
2001 Polaris 1633 · Clutch & gearbox · FL · filed 2002-05 · NHTSA 762023Split gear in transmission came off, damaged other components and locked up the drive train. Dealer and manufacturer knew of problem. Service bulletin was issued spring of 2001. Vehicle was delivered to me new with the problem and I was never notified that there was a problem by either the manufacturer or the dealer. The failure could have easily caused serious injury or death to me or others had the failure occurred at any other time. This is a known problem but the dealer and the manufacturer were both negligent in delivering the vehicle with the problem, and failing to notify the consumer of the safety recall. If notice process is not changed, others may be injured or killed as a result of this serious failure to act on known information.
1999 Polaris 1633 · Fuel system · PA · filed 2002-03 · NHTSA 8005487At certain speeds gas cap backs its way out of the tank.this causes the gas to spray on driver. Tanks have been replaced twice, and the problem is still occurring. Consumer feels this is very unsafe.
1999 Polaris 1633 · Fuel system · CA · filed 2001-12 · NHTSA 755730The motorcycle was being driven at the speed limit, in fairly heavy big rig (truck) traffic when the fuel pump died, I was almost killed by a near miss, passing between trucks as motorcycle coasted to a stop. I consider this to be a safety issue, as dozens of my fellow victory motorcycle riding friends have also had similar fuel pump failures, also with near misses with other vehicles. The pump gave no warning, it just stopped working. Apparently the designed is known to be flawed, as the manufacturer (polaris) has changed the pump to a diferent design for 2002 model year. I feel this is a real safety issue, especially on a motorcycle.
