Mirrors and windscreen: Replacement rear reflectors missing from kit: 2017-2019 · 19V574000 · 2019-08-05 · 12,733 bikes

Problem. Harley-Davidson Motor Company (Harley-Davidson) is recalling certain Touring and CVO Touring motorcycles, including 2017-2019 FLHTCU, FLHTCUL, FLHTK ANV, FLHTKL, FLTRU, FLHTK, and 2016-2019 FLHTKSE, FLHTKSE ANV, and FLTRUSE models equipped with a Harley-Davidson Detachable Tour-Pak Luggage Conversion Kit (Part numbers 53000291, 53000291A, and 53000567). The kit did not include replacement rear red reflectors for the ones that are eliminated by installing this kit. As such, these motorcycles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."

Risk. Without the rear red reflectors, the motorcycle has reduced visibility to other drivers, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Harley-Davidson has notified owners, and dealers will install the replacement reflectors, free of charge. The recall began August 19, 2019. Owners may contact Harley-Davidson customer service at 1-800-258-2464. Harley-Davidson's number for this recall is 0629.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Engineering oversight.

What the new part or software changes. The remedy kit for this recall includes two red reflex reflectors and an instruction sheet describing where to place the reflectors on the subject motorcycles.

Fixed in production. P&A H-D Detachables Tour-Pak Luggage Conversion Kits (part numbers 53000291, 53000291A, and 53000567) were held in our distribution centers and updated to part numbers 53000291B and 53000567A by including two reflex reflectors and an instruction sheet for reflector placement into the kits. All future production of subject kits now includes the additional reflectors and instruction sheet.

Maker's recall no. 0629

How the problem was found.

  • 4/20/19 – Harley-Davidson’s Technical Subcommittee (TSC) is notified by our Parts and Accessories Engineering group that the current P&A Detachables Tour-Pak kits for 2014 - current Touring models have been sold without reflectors to replace the original equipment rear reflector that may be removed on some models to install this kit. A preliminary investigation is initiated.
  • 5/14/19 – Based on the preliminary investigation findings, a formal investigation is commenced. Research is begun to determine the markets these kits have been shipped to and an Engineering review of each model configuration is initiated to determine if these vehicles remain compliant with the kit installed based on the location of the original equipment rear reflector.
  • 6/10/19 – Additional research is initiated on the rear fender tip lamp to evaluate the reflex reflector feature incorporated into the tip lamp lens and its compliance to the applicable regulations.
  • 7/12/19 – All investigation tasks were completed, and this topic was escalated to the full Recall Investigation Committee (RIC).
  • 7/22/19 – A review is conducted with the full Recall Investigation Committee, which recommended escalation to executive management.
  • 7/29/19 – Upon review of the results of the RIC’s investigation and analysis, Harley-Davidson’s executive management determined that installation of the subject kits may cause the vehicle to fail to conform to FMVSS 108 and, therefore, that a recall should be initiated to address the issue in vehicles in which these kits were installed. To date, there have been no identified crashes or injuries …

Clutch: Clutch fluid may leak and cause loss of control: 2017-2018 · 18V734000 · 2018-10-18 · 177,636 bikes

Problem. Harley-Davidson Motor Company (Harley-Davidson) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Police Road King (FLHP), Road King (FLHR), Road King Special (FLHRXS), Electra Glide Ultra Classic (FLHTCU), Ultra Limited (FLHTK), Ultra Limited Low (FLHTKL), Ultra Limited Shrine (FLHTK SHRINE), Police Electra Glide (FLHTP), Street Glide (FLHX), Street Glide Special (FLHXS), Road Glide Ultra (FLTRU), Road Glide (FLTRX), Road Glide Special (FLTRXS), Freewheeler (FLRT), Tri Glide Ultra (FLHTCUTG), CVO Limited (FLHTKSE) and CVO Street Glide (FLHXSE) motorcycles, 2017 Softail Slim S (FLSS), Fat Boy S (FLSTFBS) and CVO Pro Street Breakout (FXSE) motorcycles, 2018 115th Anniversary Ultra Limited (FLHTK ANV), 115th Anniversary Street Glide (FLHX ANV), 115th Anniversary Street Glide Special (FLHXS ANX), 115th Anniversary Tri Glide Ultra (FLHTCUTG ANV), 115th Anniversary CVO Limited (FLHTKSE ANV) and CVO Road Glide (FLTRXSE) motorcycles. The secondary clutch actuator cylinder may leak fluid internally and the clutch master cylinder may lose the ability to generate enough lift to disengage the clutch.

Risk. If the clutch cannot be disengaged it can result in a loss of control, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Harley-Davidson will notify owners, and dealers will install a new secondary clutch actuator piston assembly, free of charge. The recall began November 5, 2018. Owners may contact Harley-Davidson customer service at 1-800-258-2464. Harley-Davidson's number for this recall is 0173.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. An internal leak of fluid past the secondary clutch actuator piston/seal.

What the new part or software changes. The secondary actuator piston assembly in the recall kit (P/N 91500093) has a lip seal with a different material. Additionally, the surface finish of the piston assembly has been changed.

Fixed in production. The updated secondary actuator piston seal assembly was introduced at the start of model year 2019 production.

Part made by: Brembo North America, Inc, United States · Maker's recall no. 0173

Clutch: Clutch may not fully disengage: 2016 · 16V471000 · 2016-06-27 · 27,232 bikes

Problem. Harley-Davidson Motor Company (Harley-Davidson) is recalling certain model year 2016 Electric Glide Ultra Classic (FLHTCU), Electra Glide Ultra Classic Low (FLHTCUL), Ultra Limited Low (FLHTKL), Ultra Limited (FLHTK), CVO Street Glide (FLHXSE), Street Glide (FLHX), Street Glide Special (FLHXS), Road Glide Special (FLTRXS), Road Glide (FLTRX), Police Electra Glide (FLHTP), Road Glide Ultra (FLTRU), Fat Boy S (FLSTFBS), Softail Slim S (FLSS), and CVO Softail Pro Street Breakout (FXSE) motorcycles. In the affected motorcycles, the clutch master cylinder may lose the ability to generate enough lift to disengage the clutch, especially if the motorcycle has been parked for an extended period of time.

Risk. When the engine is running and the motorcycle is in gear, if the clutch cannot be fully disengaged, the motorcycle may move unexpectedly, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Harley-Davidson will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the clutch master cylinder for the presence of gas in the fluid, if so, the dealer will flush the clutch system and rebuild the clutch master cylinder, free of charge. The recall began July 19, 2016. Owners may contact Harley-Davidson customer service at 1-800-258-2464. Harley-Davidson's number for this recall is 0169.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. A chemical reaction inside the sealed clutch system creates gas bubbles which may cause loss of clutch lift.

What the new part or software changes. The dealer will flush the clutch master cylinder with the recommended wash solution and rebuild the clutch master cylinder using the recall kit provided.

Fixed in production. The updated population includes bikes built up through the end of model year 2016 production.

Part made by: Hayes Brake Performance Systems, Inc., United States · Maker's recall no. 0169

How the problem was found.

  • May - June 2016: Harley-Davidson’s Recall Investigation Committee, as part of its post-recall monitoring function related to Recall 0165 (NHTSA Recall No. 15V216) involving certain MY2014 and 2015 models, observed a spike in the number of similar warranty claims/reports in MY2016 motorcycles.   • June 2016: Investigation of the reports indicated that the process changes applied to a specified production range of MY2016 motorcycles may not have effectively eliminated or mitigated the condition. (This does not affect the remedy used in the prior recall, which involved a different process. That process -- a distilled water wash to clean out debris, an isopropyl alcohol spray, system bleed, …
  • June 17, 2016: Upon review of the results of the RIC’s investigation and analysis, Harley- Davidson’s executive management made its determination that a safety defect exists in the subject population and declared a recall to remedy the issue.

Brake light switch: 2010-2012 · 11V506000 · 2011-10-19 · 250,757 bikes

Problem. Harley-davidson is recalling certain model year 2009-2012 touring, cvo touring and trike vehicles manufactured from june 6, 2008, through september 16, 2011. See the above list for the models included in the recall. The rear brake light switch may be exposed to excessive heat from the exhaust system.

Risk. The excessive heat may cause the switch to not activate the brake lamp or activate the brake lamp when no brake is applied and/or cause a brake fluid leak at the brake light switch, affecting rear brake performance. Either condition may increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. Harley-davidson will notify owners, and dealers will a install a rear brake light switch kit, free of charge. The safety recall is expected to begin during october 31, 2011. Owners may contact harley-davidson at 1-414-343-4056.

Japan: Road Glide Ultra · 2019 · no. 001285419.pdf · 5,489 bikes

The hydraulic clutch system with secondary clutch actuator part numbers 37200131 and 37200131A may, on certain motorcycles, exhibit an internal leak of fluid past the secondary clutch actuator piston/seal. If this leak continues for an extended period, the clutch master cylinder reservoir could lose enough fluid to expose the hydraulic clutch circuit to air, which may result in the inability to generate enough lift to disengage the clutch. If this condition remains undetected, it could lead to a loss of control of the vehicle when started in gear, first shifted into gear, or coming to a stop.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)
