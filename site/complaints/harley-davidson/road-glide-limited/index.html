Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Brake light: Unintended brake light illumination/FMVSS 108: 2020-2022 · 22V781000 · 2022-10-18 · 199,419 bikes

Problem. Harley-Davidson Motor Company (Harley-Davidson) is recalling certain 2019-2022 Trike and CVO Trike and 2020-2022 Touring and CVO Touring motorcycles. Please refer to Addendum A for specific vehicle model details. Due to a software error, the brake lights may illuminate without the rider applying the brakes. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."

Risk. Unintended illumination of the brake lights may confuse other drivers, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will update the software on Touring and CVO Touring motorcycles. On Trike and CVO Trike motorcycles, a new pressure switch will also be installed. All repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 2, 2022. Owners may contact Harley-Davidson customer service at 1-800-258-2464. Harley-Davidson's number for this recall is 0635.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Certain motorcycles in the identified population motorcycles may exhibit a condition that may result in the brake lamp(s) remaining illuminated.

What the new part or software changes. Updated software will address this condition on Touring and CVO Touring models. Updated software and a new pressure switch will address this condition on Trike and CVO Trike models.

Fixed in production. Production motorcycles beginning on 06/24/2022 have been updated and are not subject to the recall condition.

Part made by: Robert Bosch LLC, United States · Maker's recall no. 0635

How the problem was found.

  • 5/12/2022: The TSC reopened its investigation after receiving reports indicating a higher occurrence rate of this potential issue.
  • 5/13/2022 – 8/24/2022: The TSC reviewed updated Warranty and Customer Contact records. A software change was implemented at H-D assembly plants that eliminated this potential issue on future production. The TSC elected to monitor this issue monthly.
  • 9/2/2022 – 9/13/2022: After reviewing field data while in monitor status, the TSC moved this issue back to Active status. An updated search and analysis of warranty and customer contacts data was performed. The TSC escalated this issue to H-D’s Field Action Committee (FAC).   • 9/21/2022 – 10/10/2022: The EDA met twice to discuss and review the information available. In addition, H-D reached out to NHTSA’s Office of Defect Investigation to discuss the circumstances associated with this issue.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (4)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Road Glide Limited 2026 · 2026-04-30 · no. 2026216 · 6,561 bikes · Engine

On certain motorcycles and trikes (three-wheeled motorcycles), the airbox backplate breather port may be blocked. As a result, pressure could build inside the engine crankcase. If this happens, hot oil could be ejected from the fill spout when the dipstick is removed. Safety Risk: An oil leak could create the risk of a burn injury. Corrective Action: Harley-Davidson will notify owners by mail and advise you to bring your motorcycle to a dealership to inspect and, if necessary, correct the breather port opening.

Source: Transport Canada

Japan: Road Glide Limited · 2023 · no. 001611118.pdf · 4,072 bikes

Regulatory Non-Compliance: We have learned that certain model year 2019 – 2022 Trike/CVO Trike motorcycles (see attached Addendum A) and certain model year 2020 – 2022 Touring/CVO Touring motorcycles (see attached Addendum A) may fail to conform to Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard/Canadian Motor Vehicle Safety Standard No. 108, Lamps, reflective devices, and associated equipment, in addition to other regulations including ECE R78. The subject motorcycles may exhibit a condition that can result in the brake lamp(s) remaining illuminated regardless of whether the brakes are being applied.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)

Canada: Road Glide Limited 2020-2022 · 2022-10-27 · no. 2022614 · 11,836 bikes · Brakes

On certain motorcycles, the brake lamp(s) could stay on after you release the brakes. Safety Risk: A brake lamp that stays on when the motorcycle isn't braking could cause confusion for other drivers and increase the risk of a crash. Corrective Actions: Harley-Davidson will notify owners by mail and instruct you to take your motorcycle to a dealership to update the motorcycle software. For Trike models, the dealer will also replace the brake switch.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Road Glide Limited 2014-2022 · 2022-08-15 · no. 2022463 · 264 bikes · Lights And Instruments

This recall is for certain motorcycles equipped with an accessory detachable Tour-Pak luggage carrier conversion kit. The turn signal telltale may flash quickly when the Tour-Pak luggage isn't mounted, like when a bulb fails. As a result, you may not get warning when a turn signal bulb actually fails. Canadian regulations require a change in the turn signal flash rate when a bulb fails. Note: This recall affects Harley-Davidson accessory Tour-Pak luggage carrier conversion kit part numbers 53000916 and 53000917. These parts may have been installed on new motorcycles or sold as accessory parts through Harley-Davidson dealerships. Safety Risk: A turn signal telltale that doesn't work properly …

Source: Transport Canada
