Recalls in Canada and Japan (3)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Japan: Fat Boy Lo · 2015-03-20 · no. 001082132.pdf · 1,460 bikes

On certain model year 2012 Dyna Model and Softail Model motorcycles, the front brake master cylinders may experience corrosion in the threads for the brake line banjo bolt. A corrosive condition at the banjo bolt threaded joint may lead to a leak causing a loss of front brake performance. If this condition remains undetected it could result in a sudden loss of brake fluid, which could lead to a loss of front brake function, possibly resulting in a crash.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)

Canada: Fat Boy Lo (Flstfb) 2012 · 2014-12-15 · no. 2014569 · 1,475 bikes · Brakes

On certain motorcycles, the front brake master cylinder line connection threads may corrode, which could cause a brake fluid leak and a reduction in braking performance. If operated in this condition, this could eventually result in a loss of front brake function. These issues could increase the risk of a crash causing injury and/or damage to property. Correction: Dealers will flush and replace brake fluid, as well as in inspect the master cylinder and replace as necessary.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Fat Boy Lo (Flstfb) 2011 · 2011-02-08 · no. 2011059 · 735 bikes · Electrical

On certain motorcycles equipped (from the factory) with a security system, as well as others having had the security system installed as an after-sale accessory, the Body Control Module (BCM) case may not have been properly sealed during the manufacturing process. An improper seal could allow water intrusion into the BCM, which may cause the engine to stall. Loss of propulsion, in conjunction with traffic and road conditions, and the rider's reactions, could increase the risk of a crash causing property damage and/or personal injury. Correction: Dealers will replace the Body Control Module.

Source: Transport Canada
