Fuse: 1999-2000 · 04V134000 · 2004-03-18 · 81,496 bikes

Problem. On certain touring and police model motorcycles, the 40 amp main circuit breaker can "open" for reasons other than which it was designed, causing an unexpected interruption of all electrical power to the motorcycle.

Risk. This condition could cause the motorcycle to stall, which could result in a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the circuit breaker. Owner notification began april 23, 2004. Owners should contact harley-davidson at 1-414-343-4056.

Clutch: 2000 · 02V272000 · 2002-10-03 · 782 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the braided clutch cable could contact the rear brake line and cause the brake line to abrade over time, resulting in a loss of rear brake fluid.

Risk. The loss of rear brake fluid could cause the loss of rear brake function, possibly without warning and could cause a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the braided clutch cable. Owner notification began october 21, 2002. Owners who take their vehicles to an authorized dealer on an agreed upon service date and do not receive the free remedy within a reasonable time should contact harley-davidson at 1-414-342-4680.

2023 Harley-Davidson CVO Road Glide · Clutch & gearbox, Fuel system · MD · filed 2025-09 · NHTSA 11688826UNKNOWN. Vehicle has had four (4) instances of going into "limp mode" when taking off from a stop. It is randomly occuring and cannot be duplicated or repeated. The condition arises with no warning or indicators that it is about to happen. Two different dealerships have assessed the vehicle and have no suggestions as to the cause or remedy of the issue. It is quite concerning that this issue could arise when attempting to pull out into traffic and the vehicle is unable to come up to speed, causing a potential collision. Fortunately, this issue has only occurred in a scenario where the vehicle not being able to come up to speed was not a problem (in all 4 instances it only has happened with no traffic or in stop-and-go traffic).
