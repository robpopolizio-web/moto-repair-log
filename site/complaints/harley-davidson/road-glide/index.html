Engine computer (ECU) and gear indicator: Neutral gear displays when transmission is actually in gear: 2026 · 26V730000 · 2026-11-03 · 21,643 bikes

Problem. Harley-Davidson Motor Company (Harley-Davidson) is recalling certain 2026 FLHXLSE, FLTRXSTSE, FLHXSTSE, FLHXSE, FLHXL, FLTRXP, FLTRX, FLTRXL, and FLHX motorcycles. The engine control module (ECM) may communicate the incorrect gear position of neutral when the transmission is actually in gear.

Risk. The motorcycle can experience unintended forward motion, increasing the risk of an injury.

Fix. Dealers will replace the gear position sensor, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed November 16, 2026. Owners may contact Harley-Davidson customer service at 1-800-258-2464. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became became searchable on NHTSA.gov on November 6, 2025. Harley-Davidson's number for this recall is 0194.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Due to a supplier manufacturing issue, plastic molding flash may be present on the gear position sensor, resulting in an incorrect gear position signal being sent to the ECM.

What the new part or software changes. The new gear position sensor is made from a new tool which eliminates the flash condition.

Fixed in production. On 08/03/2026, a screening process was instituted at H-D's York Vehicle Operations to ensure no gear position sensors with the plastic material flash condition were used in the vehicle assembly process.

Fix type: Replace · Part made by: SSI Technologies, LLC, United States · Maker's recall no. 0194

How the problem was found.

  • 04/16/2026: Harley-Davidson's (H-D's) Technical Subcommittee (TSC) was advised of a gear position sensor flash condition observed on a 2026 FLTRX model during a service event.
  • 04/30/2026: Initial TSC meeting to review the matter.
  • 05/05/2026 – 10/01/2026: An investigation into the issue was conducted, including implementation of Quarantine Material Requests (QMRs) to inspect sensors for flash and damaged spring clips; parts inspections, and continued monitoring.
  • 10/01/2026: The TSC escalated the matter to H-D's Field Action Committee (FAC) for further consideration.
  • 10/20/2026: The FAC reviewed the TSC's findings and analysis and escalated the matter to H-D's Executive Decision Authority (EDA).
  • 10/27/2026: Based upon the information developed during the investigation, the EDA authorized a voluntary recall to address this condition. H-D is aware of two injuries potentially related to this condition.

Fixed in production. Vehicles built after 2/11/2026 have been inspected under a new inspection process to ensure they do not present the recall condition.

Charging system (stator, regulator) and wiring: Damaged voltage regulator harness may short: 2024 · 24V672000 · 2024-09-16 · 46,923 bikes

Problem. Harley-Davidson Motor Company (Harley-Davidson) is recalling certain 2024 FLHX, FLHXSE, FLTRX, FLTRXSE, and FLTRXSTSE motorcycles. The voltage regulator output wire may rub on the front corner of the crankcase, exposing the wire and causing an electrical short circuit. A short can result in a loss of electrical power and drive power.

Risk. The unexpected loss of drive power and the loss of electrical power while in motion increases the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will inspect and as necessary, repair the voltage regulator output wire harness, and install a secondary retention strap, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 23, 2024. Owners may contact Harley-Davidson customer service at 1-800-258-2464. Harley-Davidson's number for this recall is 0186.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Variation in the routing of the regulator wire can result in contact between the regulator output wire and the front corner of the crankcase.

What the new part or software changes. The recall repair will include inspection of the voltage regulator output wire harness and installation of a secondary retention strap. In the unlikely event that damage to the voltage regulator output harness is found, a recall kit consisting of a wire harness extension will be installed to replace the damaged wiring harness prior to the installation of the secondary retention strap.

Fixed in production. On 07/29/2024, a secondary retention strap was introduced for future production of the affected models.

Part made by: Harley-Davidson Motor Company, United States · Maker's recall no. 0186

  • 07/25/2024 - The Harley-Davidson Technical Subcommittee (TSC) is made aware of a potential issue on certain model year 2024 Touring models where the 60 amp main fuse is opening.
