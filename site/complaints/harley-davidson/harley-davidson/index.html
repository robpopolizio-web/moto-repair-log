Recalls (5)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix.

Brakes and ABS: 2001 · 01V150000 · 2001-05-01 · 211 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. These motorcycles were built with a brake system that could contain out of specification brake fluid.

Risk. This condition could cause a loss of braking effectiveness, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers replaced the brake fluid in these motorcycles. Since all the vehicles have been contained or transported to harley-davidson facilities, this campaign is 100% completed.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Brakes and ABS: 1993 · 92V167000 · 1992-11-12 · 4,272 bikes

Problem. Screws (part number 3655) used to mount the front wheel brake disc, the jiffy stand spring, and front wheel cap, do not meet harley- davidson's strength requirements and may break during vehicle operation.

Risk. If the screw used to mount the front wheel brake discsbreaks, the front brake will be inoperative, increasing stopping distances whichmay result in an accident. Breakage of the screws for mounting the jiffy standspring will cause the stand to extend, which may cause loss of vehicle controlduring a turn. Breakage of the screw that holds the front wheel hub cap canresult in the hub cap being hurled off the motorcycle injuring persons in itspath.

Fix. Replace part 3655 with stronger screws.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Lights: 1991 · 91V001000 · 1991-10-02 · 19,950 bikes

Problem. Turn signal control modules may continue to flash in the presence of a non-functioning turn signal lamp which is nonconformance to fmvss 108.

Risk. Driver would not be aware that turn signal lamp is notflashing. Other drivers would not be warned when the motorcycle was changinglanes or making a turn, and could result in an accident.

Fix. Replace turn signal control module, as necessary.

Brakes and ABS: 1992 · 91V138000 · 1991-08-21 · 8,405 bikes

Problem. Friction pad separates from the backing plate and subsequently from the front or rear brake caliper assembly.

Risk. Separation of the friction pad may cause a reduction instopping ability, which may result in an accident.

Fix. Replace friction pads.

Electrical and instruments: 1990-1991 · 91V117000 · 1991-07-01 · 61,386 bikes

Problem. Malfunctioning 30 amp circuit breakers during operation can result in disruption of the electrical system, including lights, ignition, and accessories without prior warning.

Risk. Disruption of the electrical system results in loss ofheadlights and stoplights, making vehicle less visible and increasing accidentpotential of the rider.

Fix. Replace 30 amp circuit breaker.
