Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Brake hose or line: 2005 · 05V428000 · 2005-09-22 · 288 bikes

Problem. Certain motorcycles may have front brake lines which could leak due to a manufacturing defect.

Risk. Should this component fail when in use on the motorcycle, all functionality of the front brake system would be lost without warning, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will inspect the front brake line and replace if the braze is not visible. The recall began on october 10, 2005. Owners should contact harley-davidson at 1-414-343-4056.
