Labels and stickers and brake fluid: Brake fluid warning labels may detach/FMVSS 122: 2021 · 22V160000 · 2022-03-16 · 4,177 bikes

Problem. Harley-Davidson Motor Company (Harley-Davidson) is recalling certain 2021-2022 XL1200X, XL883N, and 2021 XL1200NS XL Sportster motorcycles. The brake fluid warning labels may detach from the brake fluid reservoir caps. As such, these motorcycles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 122, "Motorcycle Brake Systems."

Risk. Missing brake fluid warning labels may lead to improper brake system maintenance, affecting brake performance and increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Harley-Davidson will mail replacement labels to owners, free of charge. Owners may also take their motorcycles to a dealer for label inspection and replacement, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 4, 2022. Harley's number for this recall is 0632.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Brake Master Cylinder DOT4 Decal (P/N 44791-07) may contain a non-uniform edge which may prevent the decal from permanently adhering to the cover surface.

What the new part or software changes. The updated decals do not contain the manufacturing defect that causes this condition in the recalled vehicles.

Fixed in production. Updated decals were introduced into production on approximately 02/08/2022.

Part made by: Hitachi Astemo, Ltd, United States · Maker's recall no. 0632

How the problem was found.

  • 02/03/2022 - Harley-Davidson’s Technical Subcommittee (TSC) was made aware of a potential issue with MY22 XL (all models) built at York Vehicle Operations. An audit performed on 01/28/2022 revealed that the brake fluid warning decal, part number 44791-07 (DECAL, FRT MSTR CYL/DOT 4), was missing from the front brake master cylinder cap on one of the vehicles inspected during the audit.
  • 02/03/2022 - 02/28/2022 - The TSC conducted a root cause and scope analysis. This included an audit of vehicles as well as discussions with the supplier. The TSC’s investigation indicated that due to a non-uniform edge on the decal, the brake fluid warning statement on some of these vehicles may not be permanently affixed.
  • 03/03/2022 – The Field Action Committee (FAC) reviewed the TSC’s findings and analysis and escalated the matter to H-D’s Executive Decision Authority (EDA).
  • 03/08/2022 – Based upon the information developed during the investigation, the EDA determined that this condition may constitute a noncompliance with FMVSS 122, S5.1.9, and authorized a recall to address this condition. There have been no reports of any accidents or injuries related to this issue.

Headlight: Headlights may fail: 2019-2021 · 21V225000 · 2021-03-31 · 31,346 bikes

Problem. Harley-Davidson Motor Company (Harley-Davidson) is recalling certain 2019-2021 Sportster (XL) motorcycles and 796 headlight assemblies that may have been sold as replacement parts for 2005-2019 Sportster, 2005-2017 Softail, 2005-2017 Dyna, and 2005-2011 V-Rod motorcycles. The glass bulb within the headlight assembly may fail, causing a loss of both high and low beams.

Risk. A loss of both headlight beams can reduce visibility and increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. Harley-Davidson will notify owners, and dealers will install a headlamp bulb shield, free of charge. The recall began April 15, 2021. Owners may contact Harley-Davidson customer service at 1-800-258-2464. Harley-Davidson's number for this recall is 0177.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. A glossy, chrome finish on the interior of the bulb shield in the Headlamp Assembly (68297-05A) may cause a hole to develop in the tip of the dual- filament bulb.

What the new part or software changes. The remedy is currently under development. This report will be amended when the remedy has been confirmed.

Fixed in production. Beginning on or about 03/23/2021, production vehicles began receiving new headlamp assemblies with a bulb shield built with a matte inner surface finish which does not cause the recall condition.

Part made by: Lincoln Industries, Inc., United States · Maker's recall no. 0177

  • 12/17/2020 – 3/8/2021 – Additional warranty analysis (including an evaluation of the possible effect of warranty policy changes) and customer contact searches were performed. Field parts were inspected and analyzed, the headlamp assembly supplier and bulb supplier were consulted, and a lab test was developed that could replicate what we were seeing on field returned parts. The investigation …
  • 3/23/2021 The FAC reviewed the TSC’s findings and analysis and escalated the matter to H-D’s Executive Decision Authority (EDA).
  • 3/24/2021 – Based upon the information developed during the investigation, the EDA authorized a recall to address this condition. There have been no reports of crashes or injuries related to this issue.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (1)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Iron 1200 2019-2021 · 2021-04-01 · no. 2021195 · 1,178 bikes · Lights And Instruments

On certain motorcycles, a problem with the headlamp bulb shield could cause the headlamp bulb to fail. This can cause the headlight to turn off suddenly. Note: If the headlamp bulb fails, the Security Lamp will turn on. Safety Risk: A headlight that fails could reduce visibility and increase the risk of a crash. Corrective Actions: Harley-Davidson will notify owners by mail and advise you to arrange service with an Authorized Harley-Davidson dealer, who will confirm the motorcycle is covered by this recall. If covered, the dealer will install a new headlamp bulb shield.

Source: Transport Canada
