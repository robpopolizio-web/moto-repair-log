Recalls (17)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Gearbox: 1986-1988 · 88V177000 · 1988-11-15 · 43,058 bikes

Problem. There may be structural integrity problems with the shifter forks used in the transmissions.

Risk. Shifter forks might fracture, causing the transmission tomalfunction, and possibly cause the rear wheel to lock up; this could result inan accident.

Fix. Replace the shifter forks in the transmission.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Brakes: 1981 · 81V011000 · 1981-01-26 · 1,884 bikes

Problem. If the rear shock absorber should "over-travel" when motorcycle is operated over rough terrain, the current position of the rear brake caliper could cause the rear brake line to contact the right saddle bag carrier. This could cause a deformation in the rear brake line.

Risk.

Fix. All involved vehicles will be inspected and the rear brake torque arm will be replaced with one of a new design. The right saddle bag carrier will be modified, if necessary.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Wheel bearing: 1980 · 80V126000 · 1980-09-24 · 4,262 bikes

Problem. The wheel bearings on the involved motorcycles may have had inadequate initial bearing lubrications and sealing. Depending on the degree of insufficient lubrication, this problem could cause failure of the front and/or rear wheel bearings.

Risk.

Fix. Dealer will inspect and replace the wheel bearings at no charge to owner.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Brake lever or pedal: 1980 · 80V084000 · 1980-07-24 · 6,214 bikes

Problem. On certain vehicles, the rear brake control may disengage from the frame due to stresses primarily caused by the length of the brake control mounting bolts.

Risk.

Fix. Dealer will replace the rear brake control mounting bolts and add a lock plate without charge.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Wheel bolts and axle nut: 1980 · 80V085000 · 1980-07-07 · 4,690 bikes

Problem. On certain vehicles there may be a gradual loosening of the rear sprocket bolt(s) and a decrease in the clamping force necessary to maintain a secure attachment of the rear wheel sprocket to the wheel.

Risk.

Fix. Dealer will replace the (5) rear wheel sprocket bolts at no charge.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Steering lock: 1980 · 80V070000 · 1980-05-29 · 3,860 bikes

Problem. The adjusting screw, which mounts through a fork lock lever assembly into the fork lock itself, may not be fully and securely engaged because the screw may not be long enough. This may allow the fork pin to drop and accidentally engage the fork lock.

Risk.

Fix. Dealer will install an adjusting screw kit free of charge.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Ignition switch: 1980 · 80V037000 · 1980-03-31 · 2,440 bikes

Problem. There is a potential malfunction within the fork lock pin return mechanism which might allow the fork pin to drop and inadvertently engage the fork lock during left hand turns at low speeds.

Risk.

Fix. Dealer will be inspect and, if necessary, replace the ignition switch/fork lock assembly without charge to owner.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Final drive gear (shaft drive): 1980 · 80V020000 · 1980-02-26 · 62 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the head of the rear wheel sprocket mounting bolt could break off and eventually wedge itself between the aluminum chain housing and the sprocket. This defect is due to a manufacturing process which may cause the bolts to become brittle.

Risk.

Fix. The dealer will replace all five rear wheel sprocket mounting bolts on all involved vehicles.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Brake caliper: 1980 · 80V009000 · 1980-02-14 · 1,343 bikes

Problem. The rear brake caliper casting on certain vehicles could be porous and could allow brake fluid to seep from the caliper assembly. If this occurs, the rear brakes will gradually lose fluid and pressure.

Risk.

Fix. Dealer will test and replace, if necessary, the rear brake caliper on affected vehicles.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Brake master cylinder: 1979 · 79V169000 · 1979-07-09 · 21,970 bikes

Problem. The rear brake master cylinder plastic piston employs a non-specified material which may allow excessive water absorption. As a result, the plastic piston might stick in the master cylinder piston bore and cause brake drag.

Risk.

Fix. Dealer will replace rear brake master cylinder pistons without charge to owner.

Brake master cylinder: 1979 · 79V100000 · 1979-04-17 · 370 bikes

Problem. Certain vehicles may have been equipped with front brake master cylinders which have fissures in the cylinder bodies. This would allow brake fluid leakage through the fissured section.

Risk.

Fix. Dealer will replace defective front brake master cylinders without charge to owners.
