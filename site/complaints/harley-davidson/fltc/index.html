Recalls (4)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix.

Electrical and instruments: 1994, 1996-1998 · 98V158000 · 1998-07-14 · 55,013 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: fl touring motorcycles. A loss of electrical power through the ignition switch can occur due to excessive current and other factors.

Risk. This condition can cause the engine to fail to start, operate erratically, or stall.

Fix. Dealers will replace the ignition switch/circuit breaker and install a relay kit.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Throttle and cruise control: 1993-1994 · 93V211000 · 1993-12-13 · 4,038 bikes

Problem. The components within the right-hand switch assembly may generate heat, which is conducted by the metal throttle guide tubes, sufficient to melt the interliner between the cable housing and the throttle cable, thus restricting the movement of the throttle cable.

Risk. Restriction of the throttle cable movement may cause thethrottle to bind, which may result in loss of vehicle control and an accident.

Fix. Repair the throttle cable.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Throttle and cruise control: 1989 · 89V115000 · 1989-07-20 · 2,509 bikes

Problem. Cruise control system is susceptible to water contamination and, if it becomes wet while in use, could create a short circuit.

Risk. Motorcycle speed would increase automatically withoutany action on the part of the driver presenting the potential for an accident.

Fix. Replace cruise control module.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Other or not stated: 1983-1984 · 84V044000 · 1984-04-04 · 114 bikes

Problem. Component of two-piece upper brace clamp which helps to attach the sidecar to the motorcycle frame contains chemical impurities in the casting and was subjected to improper heat treatment during manufacturing. Upper brace clamp may fracture.

Risk.

Fix. Upper brace clamps will be inspected and replaced without charge.
