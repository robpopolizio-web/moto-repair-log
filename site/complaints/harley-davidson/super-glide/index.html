Recalls (3)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Fuel system: 1990-1991 · 92V173000 · 1992-11-30 · 66,046 bikes

Problem. The fuel inlet needle does not seal properly against the fuel inlet seat.

Risk. The fuel overflow system allows fuel to leak onto theground, which could result in a fire if exposed to a source of ignition.

Fix. Replace the fuel inlet needle.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Fuel tank: 1986-1992 · 92V108000 · 1992-07-22 · 29,986 bikes

Problem. The fuel tank leaked as a result of impact testing performed by harley-davidson.

Risk. Spilled fuel can ignite when exposed to a source ofignition.

Fix. Repair the motorcycles to avoid the fuel spillage condition.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Brake caliper: 1987-1990 · 90V188000 · 1990-11-01 · 42,321 bikes

Problem. The upper mounting bolt for front brake caliper(s) might fracture while in use.

Risk. The front brake system will remain functional, however,the bolt's mating threaded bushing can gradually move out of position far enoughfor the bushing to contact spokes on the motorcycle's front wheel. Front wheellockup could occur and result in an accident.

Fix. Replace upper caliper mounting bolts.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Recalls in Canada and Japan (14)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Super Glide Sport (Fxr2), Super Glide Sport (Fxr3) 1999 · 1999-10-22 · no. 1999191 · 632 bikes · Fuel System

Certain motorcycles may have been manufactured with a fuel tank vent fitting that is plugged. Under certain conditions this could cause the carburetor to overflow fuel which could result in a fire. This condition could result in personal injury. Correction: The channel on the fuel tank vent fitting will be opened.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Super Glide (Fxr) 1992-1998 · 1998-11-25 · no. 1998233 · 30 bikes · Fuel System

Abrasion leading to possible fuel leakage can occur between the fuel line and the accessory custom horn cover. The custom horn cover is specified for all 1992 and later model year harley-davidson motorcycles except xl883 models. Correction: Accessory custom horn cover will be removed and replaced with the original equipment horn cover. Fuel line will be inspected and replaced if necessary.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Super Glide (Fxr) 1990-1991 · 1992-12-31 · no. 1992169 · 5,293 bikes · Fuel System

Under certain circumstances, the fuel overflow system could release an amount of fuel onto the ground while the motorcycle is parked, which exceeds the design specifications and creates a potential fire hazard. Correction: carburetors will be removed and inspected and inlet valve or complete carburetor assembly will be replaced as required.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Super Glide (Fxr) 1993 · 1992-12-09 · no. 1992158 · 178 bikes · Brakes

The screws mounting the front wheel brake disc(s) (all models) as well as the jiffy (kick) stand spring anchor (fxd models only) could break. The breakage of these screws on the front brake disc(s) could cause a loss of stopping power in the front brake without prior warning, potentially resulting in an accident. The breakage of the screw that holds the jiffy stand spring anchor on fxd models would allow the jiffy stand to extend without warning. An extended stand could contact the ground during a left hand turn, possibly startling the rider and affecting his or her control of the motorcycle. Correction: defective screws will be replaced on affected vehicles.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Super Glide (Fxr) 1987-1992 · 1992-08-14 · no. 1992096 · 1,494 bikes · Fuel System

During a severe impact, the front fork assembly could shear the steering stop causing certain components to contact the fuel tank resulting in fuel leakage and a possible fire. Correction: vehicles will be modified to prevent this condition from occurring.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Super Glide (Fxr) 1992 · 1991-08-27 · no. 1991120 · 317 bikes · Brakes

The friction pad could separate from the backing plate and dislodge from the front or rear brake caliper assembly. This condition could occur without prior warning and cause a reduction in stopping ability. Correction: front and rear brake friction pads will be replaced.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Super Glide (Fxr) 1990-1991 · 1991-06-27 · no. 1991099 · 4,587 bikes · Electrical

The 30 amp circuit breaker might malfunction during vehicle operation, resulting in a possible temporary disruption of the electrical system function including lights, ignition and accessories. Correction: circuit breaker will be replaced.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Super Glide (Fxr) 1987-1990 · 1990-11-19 · no. 1990181 · 1,657 bikes · Brakes

The upper mounting bolt on the front brake caliper(s) might develop a fracture during normal motorcycle use. Should a mounting bolt fracture, the bolt's mating threaded bushing might gradually move out of position and make contact with the spokes of the motorcycle's front wheel. If this condition were to occur, the front wheel might suddenly lock up resulting in a possible crash. Correction: upper mounting bolts will be replaced on affected vehicles.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Super Glide (Fxr) 1986-1988 · 1988-12-08 · no. 1988168 · 2,282 bikes · Powertrain

The transmission shifter fork(s) might fracture causing the transmission to malfunction and possibly causing the rear wheel to lock up. This could result in loss of vehicle control and a crash without prior warning. Correction: shifter forks will be replaced on affected vehicles.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Super Glide (Fxr) 1987 · 1987-09-09 · no. 1987097 · 654 bikes · Suspension

A cracking condition could develop in the seven spoke front and rear cast wheels. If not detected, these cracks could propogate through the spokes and cause the wheel hub and rim to separate without prior warning. This could result in loss of vehicle control and a crash without prior warning. Correction: wheels will be replaced with wheels of a different tested design.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Super Glide (Fxr) 1986 · 1986-07-31 · no. 1986081 · 150 bikes · Structure

The possibility exists that one of the pivot bolts could lose all torque and vibrate free of the vehicle causing a severe handling problem and possible vehicle crash. Some prior warning would occur noticeable engine vibration.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Super Glide (Fxr) 1982 · 1982-07-14 · no. 1982059 · 499 bikes · Brakes

The rear hydraulic brake system might be capable of generating excessive pressure which could exceed the burst strangth of the rear brake caliper. The caliper could fracture without prior warning leading to a total loss of rear brake and could result in a vehicle crash.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Super Glide (Fxe) 1978-1979 · 1979-06-08 · no. 1979102 · 14 bikes · Brakes

The front brake master cylinder plunger piston bore diameter may be 0.015 inches oversize resulting in a reduced interference fit between the piston seals and the cylinder bore which could allow hjydraulic fluid leakage and eventual loss of front braking action. Vehicles sold in the province of ontario only.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Super Glide (Fxe) 1975 · 1977-01-17 · no. 1976142 · 684 bikes · Other

Vehicles were produced with an exhaust system which does not comply with the noise level requirements of c.m.v.s.s. 1106.

Source: Transport Canada
