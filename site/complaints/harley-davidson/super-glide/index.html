Recalls (3)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix.

Fuel system: 1990-1991 · 92V173000 · 1992-11-30 · 66,046 bikes

Problem. The fuel inlet needle does not seal properly against the fuel inlet seat.

Risk. The fuel overflow system allows fuel to leak onto theground, which could result in a fire if exposed to a source of ignition.

Fix. Replace the fuel inlet needle.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Fuel system: 1986-1992 · 92V108000 · 1992-07-22 · 29,986 bikes

Problem. The fuel tank leaked as a result of impact testing performed by harley-davidson.

Risk. Spilled fuel can ignite when exposed to a source ofignition.

Fix. Repair the motorcycles to avoid the fuel spillage condition.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Brakes and ABS: 1987-1990 · 90V188000 · 1990-11-01 · 42,321 bikes

Problem. The upper mounting bolt for front brake caliper(s) might fracture while in use.

Risk. The front brake system will remain functional, however,the bolt's mating threaded bushing can gradually move out of position far enoughfor the bushing to contact spokes on the motorcycle's front wheel. Front wheellockup could occur and result in an accident.

Fix. Replace upper caliper mounting bolts.
