Recalls (2)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Brake hose or line: Loss of rear brakes: 2025-2026 · 26V234000 · 2026-04-13 · 16,994 bikes

Problem. Harley-Davidson Motor Company (Harley-Davidson) is recalling certain 2025-2026 FLHC, FXBB, FXLRS, and FXLRST Softail motorcycles. The rear brake line may contact the Body Control Module (BCM), resulting in a hole in the brake line, a brake fluid leak, and loss of the rear brakes.

Risk. Loss of the rear brakes increases the risk of a crash.

Fix. Harley-Davidson will replace the BCM caddy and associated hardware, free of charge. In addition, the rear brake line will be inspected and, if damaged, will be replaced along with associated parts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 1, 2026. Owners may contact Harley-Davidson customer service at 1-800-258-2464. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov April 16, 2025.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. There is insufficient clearance between the rear brake line and the BCM.

What the new part or software changes. The revised BCM caddy repositions the BCM and provides greater clearance from the rear brake line.

Fixed in production. Vehicles shipped after 3/18/2026 will contain the new BCM caddy.

Fix type: Inspect, Replace · Part made by: Hdmc, United States · Maker's recall no. 0192

How the problem was found.

  • On 3/5/2026, Harley-Davidson's (H-D's) Technical Subcommittee (TSC) was made aware of a Service Report alleging inoperable brakes on a 2025 FXLRST model motorcycle.
  • 3/6/2026 – 3/25/2026:
  • A review of warranty and service records identified three additional claims of brake fluid loss / inoperable rear brakes.
  • All FXBB, FLHC, FXLRS, and FXLRST motorcycles within H-D control were placed on ship hold pending further investigation.
  • Field parts were returned and inspected.
  • The CAD assembly models were reviewed for all Softail model motorcycles.
  • Pre-production Softail motorcycles which completed full-vehicle test mileage accumulation were inspected.

Labels and stickers: Incorrect tire pressure on label/FMVSS 120: 2018-2019 · 24V719000 · 2024-09-26 · 12,929 bikes

Problem. Harley-Davidson Motor Company (Harley-Davidson) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Softail FXBB Street Bob and 2018-2019 Softail FXLR Low Rider motorcycles. The front tire inflation pressure on the VIN label may be less than the stated front gross axle weight rating (GAWRs). As such, these motorcycles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 120, "Wheels and Rims - Other than Passenger Cars."

Risk. Incorrect tire pressure information may result in overloading the front tire, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Harley-Davidson will mail labels with installation instructions and owner's manual inserts to owners, free of charge. Letters informing owners of the safety risk were mailed November 13, 2024. Owner notification letters were mailed December 30, 2024. Owners may contact Harley-Davidson customer service at 414-343-4056. Harley-Davidson's number for this recall is 0628.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

What the new part or software changes. The partial overlay label and Owner’s Manual insert include revised front tire inflation values to correct the original printed values.

Fixed in production. On vehicles shipped on or after 6/17/19, VIN labels have been updated to list the correct recommended cold inflation pressure. Also, updated owner's manuals have been included with the motorcycles.

Part made by: Hdmc, United States · Maker's recall no. 0628

Recalls in Canada and Japan (3)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Japan: Street Bob · 2026 · no. 002017845.pdf · 1,285 bikes

Contact between the brake line and the BCM, over time, could lead to a hole in the brake line and a loss of brake fluid. If brake fluid loss remains undetected, rear braking may be compromised, increasing the risk of a crash.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)

Japan: Street Bob · 2015-03-20 · no. 001082132.pdf · 1,460 bikes

On certain model year 2012 Dyna Model and Softail Model motorcycles, the front brake master cylinders may experience corrosion in the threads for the brake line banjo bolt. A corrosive condition at the banjo bolt threaded joint may lead to a leak causing a loss of front brake performance. If this condition remains undetected it could result in a sudden loss of brake fluid, which could lead to a loss of front brake function, possibly resulting in a crash.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)

Canada: Street Bob 2012 · 2014-12-15 · no. 2014569 · 1,475 bikes · Brakes

On certain motorcycles, the front brake master cylinder line connection threads may corrode, which could cause a brake fluid leak and a reduction in braking performance. If operated in this condition, this could eventually result in a loss of front brake function. These issues could increase the risk of a crash causing injury and/or damage to property. Correction: Dealers will flush and replace brake fluid, as well as in inspect the master cylinder and replace as necessary.

Source: Transport Canada
