Recalls (2)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Brake caliper: Brake caliper corrosion may cause brake drag: 2017-2019 · 18V927000 · 2018-12-21 · 12,871 bikes

Problem. Harley-Davidson Motor Company (Harley-Davidson) is recalling certain 2016-2019 Street 500 (XG500), Street 750 (XG750), and 2017-2019 Street 750A (XG750A) motorcycles. The brake calipers may corrode internally after exposure to certain driving environments such as coastal regions or road salt, resulting in increased brake drag.

Risk. Excessive brake drag can increase the risk of crash.

Fix. Harley-Davidson has notified owners, and dealers will install new front and rear brake calipers, free of charge. The recall began April 9, 2019. Owners may contact Harley-Davidson customer service at 1-800-258-2464. Harley-Davidson's number for this recall is 0174.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Corrosion in the unanodized caliper piston bore area when the motorcycle is subjected to a corrosive external environment over time, especially in coastal environments or coastal environments where road salt is used.

What the new part or software changes. The brake calipers in the recall kits (P/N 91500097 or P/N 91500098) will have an anodized piston bore that provides corrosion resistance.

Fixed in production. Harley-Davidson ceased shipments of the subject motorcycle models on December 6, 2018. Production will re-commence in early-2019 using updated brake calipers, which will have an anodized piston bore that provides corrosion resistance.

Part made by: Brembo S.p.A., Italy · Maker's recall no. 0174

Exhaust: 2006 · 07V303000 · 2007-07-17 · 2,403 bikes

Problem. Certain motorcycles were built with a condition whereby the pant leg of some riders can have direct contact with the exhaust pipe.

Risk. This condition could cause the pant leg to char or burn, which could lead to the possibility of injury to the rider.

Fix. Dealers will add a new heat shield, p/n 66820-06, which provides additional coverage of the exhaust pipe. The recall began on july 12, 2007. Owners may contact harley davidson at 1-414-343-4056.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (1)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Japan: Street Rod · 2019 · no. 001299010.pdf · 1,490 bikes

Brake calipers (P/Ns 41300158, 41300159 & 41300190) on certain motorcycles may experience corrosion in the caliper bore area if subjected to a corrosive external environment over time, especially in coastal environments or coastal environments where road salt is used. This corrosion could result in the pistons dragging in the caliper bore. If this condition remains undetected it could lead to increased brake drag, possibly resulting in a crash.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)
