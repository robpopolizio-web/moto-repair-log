Side or centre stand: 2009 · 08V659000 · 2008-12-15 · 3,285 bikes

Problem. Harley-davidson is recalling 3,285 my 2009 fxd, fxdf, fxdc, fxdl, and fxdb motorcycles. The jiffy stand spring anchor may have been built with a defective weld that may allow the jiffy stand assembly to not retract as intended.

Risk. This condition could lead to a crash which could cause injury or death to the rider.

Fix. Dealers will remove the original jiffy stand spring anchor and replace it with a new spring anchor free of charge. The recall is expected to begin on or about december 22, 2008. Owners may contact harley-davidson at 1-414-342-4680.

Exhaust: 2008 · 07V535000 · 2007-11-20 · 538 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles built and shipped to california, the catalytic muffler protective heat shield was not installed. This condition could allow direct contact with this muffler and cause injury.

Risk. If contact occurs while riding, it could cause loss of control of the motorcycle, leading to injury or death.

Fix. Dealers will install the heat shields to the mufflers free of charge. The recall began on november 26, 2007. Owners may contact harley davidson at 1-414-343-4056.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (12)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Dyna Super Glide (Fxd) 2009 · 2008-12-18 · no. 2008449 · 294 bikes · Other

On certain motorcycles, the jiffy stand spring anchor may have been manufactured incorrectly. A defective weld may prevent the jiffy stand assembly from retracting as intended. This could lead to a crash which could cause personal injury or death. Correction: Dealers will replace the spring anchor assembly.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Dyna Super Glide (Fxdp) 2007 · 2007-02-05 · no. 2007026 · 23 bikes · Tires

Certain motorcycles were assembled with a 90/90-19 front tire instead of 100/90-19. The 90/90-19 front tire creates an unknown and untested configuration, which may cause performance issues. This could result in a crash, which could cause injury or death. Correction: Dealers will replace the front tire.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Dyna Super Glide (Fxdi), Dyna Super Glide 35Th Anniversary (Fxd135) 2006 · 2006-11-16 · no. 2006313 · 602 bikes · Electrical

On certain motorcycles, the battery hold-down bolt may loosen under normal riding conditions. Should this occur, the battery may be able to move out of position allowing the terminals to contact the metal battery caddy cover and abrade through the positive terminal protective boot. As a result, the battery could short, causing sparks and a fire, which could cause a crash leading to injury or death to the rider. Correction: Dealers will replace the center battery caddy bolt with an updated bolt that has a locking patch on the threads.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Dyna Super Glide (Fxd), Dyna Super Glide 35Th Anniversary (Fxd35) 2006 · 2005-11-23 · no. 2005370 · 306 bikes · Powertrain

On certain motorcycles, the transmission may go into a false neutral position even though the neutral indicator light is illuminated. If this happens, the motorcycle could drop into gear unexpectedly and create the risk of a crash or injuries. Correction: Dealers will replace the shifter cam (drum), the detent arm, and the neutral switch.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Dyna Super Glide (Fxdi), Dyna Super Glide (Fxdp) 2004 · 2005-09-26 · no. 2005280 · 550 bikes · Other

On certain motorcycles, the throttle cables could come in contact with the tunnel portion of the fuel tank. In this condition, the cables could eventually wear through the tank, causing a fuel leak. A fuel leak, in the presence of an ignition source, could result in a fire. Correction: Dealers will inspect the cables and tank, reposition the cables and, if necessary, repair or replace the tank and/or cables.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Dyna Super Glide (Fxd) 2005 · 2005-02-14 · no. 2005026 · 262 bikes · Fuel System

Certain motorcycles may have been produced with defective fuel shut-off valves. The functionality of the ON position and the RESERVE position of the valve may have been reversed. Consequently, when the control handle of the valve is set in the ON position the valve actually selects the RESERVE position fuel flow path. If a bike operated with the valve in the ON position begins to run out of fuel, the expected fuel reserve will not be available. If this occurs it could create a quit while riding situation which could lead to a crash. Correction: Dealers will replace the fuel valve.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Dyna Super Glide (Fxd) 2003 · 2004-05-21 · no. 2004174 · 758 bikes · Lights And Instruments

On certain Dyna, Xl and Touring model motorcycles, the tail light bulb can fall out of the socket, thereby rendering the tail light and the brake light inoperative, which could lead to a crash, causing the possibility of death or injury to the rider. Correction: Dealers will replace the Wagner (original equipment supplier) bulbs with Sylvania (supplier) bulbs which provide the proper fit into the sockets.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Dyna Super Glide (Fxd) 2004 · 2004-02-06 · no. 2004051 · 150 bikes · Fuel System

On certain motorcycles, the rubber fuel hose could separate from the inner liner causing a fuel leak that could result in a fire. Correction: Dealer will replace the fuel line assembly.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Dyna Super Glide (Fxd) 1999 · 1999-02-15 · no. 1999022 · 810 bikes · Engine

On certain motorcycles, the engine cam sprocket bolt on could lose its clamp load and/or break. This condition could cause the engine to quit running without prior warning. Under certain conditions loss of engine power could result in diminished vehicle control and a possible crash. Correction: The cam sprocket bolt and flat washer as well as the crank sprocket bolt and flat washer will be replaced on affected vehicles.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Dyna Super Glide (Fxd) 1996 · 1996-07-17 · no. 1996115 · 23 bikes · Brakes

The rear brake master cylinder on these vehicles contains a seal that in certain situations may move out of position on the rear master cylinder piston. This would result in loss of rear brake performance. Correction: master cylinder assembly will be replaced on affected vehicles.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Dyna Super Glide (Fxd) 1990-1991 · 1992-12-31 · no. 1992169 · 5,293 bikes · Fuel System

Under certain circumstances, the fuel overflow system could release an amount of fuel onto the ground while the motorcycle is parked, which exceeds the design specifications and creates a potential fire hazard. Correction: carburetors will be removed and inspected and inlet valve or complete carburetor assembly will be replaced as required.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Dyna Super Glide (Fxd) 1990-1992 · 1992-08-14 · no. 1992096 · 1,494 bikes · Fuel System

During a severe impact, the front fork assembly could shear the steering stop causing certain components to contact the fuel tank resulting in fuel leakage and a possible fire. Correction: vehicles will be modified to prevent this condition from occurring.

Source: Transport Canada
