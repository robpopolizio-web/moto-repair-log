Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix.

Electrical and instruments: 2021 · 22V030000 · 2022-01-25 · 3,917 bikes

Problem. Harley Davidson Motor Company (Harley-Davidson) is recalling certain 2021 Pan America 1250S (RA1250S), Pan America 1250 (RA1250), and Sportster S (RH1250S) motorcycles. The instrument cluster module may not display the speedometer and neutral indicator at start-up when the module is below freezing temperatures. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 123, "Motorcycle Controls and Displays."

Risk. Operating a motorcycle without a visible speedometer or neutral indicator may increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will update the instrument cluster module software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 8, 2022. Owners may contact Harley-Davidson customer service at 1-800-258-2464. Harley-Davidson's number for this recall is 0631.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

2021 Harley-Davidson Pan America Special · Clutch, gearbox and final drive · TX · filed 2026-09 · NHTSA 11764364The cam chain tensioner failed while driving down the road, resulting in reduced power during a pass that almost resulted in an accident. There are no maintenance requirements for this component and there are numerous reports of this failing in motorcycles equipped with the Revolution Max engine. On this engine, the cam chain tensioner is held in place with a bolt that is apparently a torque to spec item with no locking mechanism. During the course of normal operation, the bolt backs out and can have varying degrees of damage. In this case, the forward cylinder cam chain became dislodged from the cams, resulting in a dead cylinder and eventually destroying the intake cam. A cursory check was done approximately 4 weeks prior to the incident, and no excess play was noted at that time. Note that there is no spec that I am aware of for excess play or interval to check it. This is a large enough issue that there are aftermarket companies that sell kits that contain Lock Tite on bolts to remedy this.
