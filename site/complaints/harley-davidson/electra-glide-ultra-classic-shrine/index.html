Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Lights: 2002 · 02V002000 · 2002-01-04 · 28,949 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the clips that retain the wiring for the front fender tip light can become detached from the fender, causing the wires to fray and short.

Risk. This could cause the lighting system fuse to blow, leaving the vehicle without an operational front fender tip light, headlight, taillight, running lights, and passing lamps (if equipped).

Fix. Dealers will replace the fender tip light wiring harness retention clips, and inspect and replace, if necessary, any damaged wiring harnesses. Owner notification began january 9, 2002. Owners who take their vehicles to an authorized dealer on an agreed upon service date and do not receive the free remedy within a reasonable time should contact harley-davidson at 1-414-342-4680.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Canada: Electra Glide Ultra Classic Shrine (Flhtcu) 2009-2014 · 2014-05-01 · no. 2014159 · 22,485 bikes · Lights And Instruments

Certain vehicles may not comply with the requirements of Canada Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 108 - Lighting System and Retroreflective Devices. The rear reflector may have been mounted lower than what is allowed by the standard. Correction: No corrective recall action is required as this technical non-compliance is deemed to be non-safety related.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Electra Glide Ultra Classic Shrine (Flhtcu) 2009-2010 · 2009-12-09 · no. 2009346 · 8,971 bikes · Fuel System

On certain motorcycles, the fuel tank front mounts may distort in reaction to severe frame damage from a frontal collision. This may result in a fuel leak. Fuel leakage, in the presence of an ignition source, could result in a fire, causing property damage, personal injury or death. Correction: Dealers will install fuel tank front mount braces.

Source: Transport Canada
