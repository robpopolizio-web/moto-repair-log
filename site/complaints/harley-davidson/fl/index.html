Recalls (6)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Brake lever or pedal: 1998 · 97V227000 · 1997-12-15 · 2,012 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The rear brake pedal assembly may have an inadequate weld.

Risk. This condition can allow the lever hub to separate from the master cylinder lever which would affect rear brake performance.

Fix. Dealers will perform the recall service on these vehicles.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Fuel injection: 1994-1997 · 96V204000 · 1996-10-22 · 176,515 bikes

Problem. The effects of reformulated gasoline along with certain usage of the fuel supply valve affects the supply of gasoline to the carburetor. This condition requires two factors: (1) the use of reformulated fuels containing mbte and (2) starting the motorcycle with the fuel valve handle in the off position.

Risk. This condition can cause the fuel flow to be unpredictable thus causing the engine to shut down increasing the risk of a motorcycle accident.

Fix. Dealers the replace the diaphragm in the fuel valve.

Brakes: 1996 · 96V103000 · 1996-06-14 · 251 bikes

Problem. The seal on the rear master cylinder piston can move out of position.

Risk. This condition can result in a loss of rear braking performance increasing the potential for a vehicle accident.

Fix. Dealers will inspect and, if necessary, replace the rear brake master cylinder.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Fuel system: 1990-1991 · 92V173000 · 1992-11-30 · 66,046 bikes

Problem. The fuel inlet needle does not seal properly against the fuel inlet seat.

Risk. The fuel overflow system allows fuel to leak onto theground, which could result in a fire if exposed to a source of ignition.

Fix. Replace the fuel inlet needle.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Battery cable: 1989-1990 · 92V125000 · 1992-09-04 · 6,838 bikes

Problem. The positive battery cables installed as part of harley- davidson's previous recall 077 (nhtsa recall 92v011000) can fail in service.

Risk. Failure of the positive battery cable results in loss ofelectrical system functions of ignition, lights, and accessories.

Fix. Install new replacement battery cables.

Battery cable: 1988-1990 · 92V011000 · 1992-01-22 · 34,693 bikes

Problem. The positive battery cable can chafe because of its proximity to the oil tank, causing a short circuit if the cable should ground.

Risk. The heat from a short circuit could result in a vehiclefire.

Fix. Replace the postive battery cable.
