Recalls in Canada and Japan (3)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Sportster 883 Low (Xl 883L) 2007 · 2007-07-06 · no. 2007223 · 770 bikes · Other

On certain motorcycles, the rider's pant leg can contact the exhaust pipe. This could cause the pant leg to char or burn, and could injure the rider. Correction: Dealers will install an exhaust shield.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Sportster 883 Low (Xl 883L) 2007 · 2007-02-05 · no. 2007026 · 23 bikes · Tires

Certain motorcycles were assembled with a 90/90-19 front tire instead of 100/90-19. The 90/90-19 front tire creates an unknown and untested configuration, which may cause performance issues. This could result in a crash, which could cause injury or death. Correction: Dealers will replace the front tire.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Sportster 883 Low (Xl 883L) 2007 · 2006-12-19 · no. 2006364 · 643 bikes · Steering

On certain motorcycles, the voltage regulator installed during vehicle assembly has greater body thickness than designed. Under certain conditions, the regulator may contact the front fender, impeding the operator's ability to steer the vehicle. This could result in a crash, which could cause injury or death. Correction: Dealers will relocate the voltage regulator.

Source: Transport Canada
