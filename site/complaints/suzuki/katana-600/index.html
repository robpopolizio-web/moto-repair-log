Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Bodywork, fairing and brackets: 1998 · 98V087000 · 1998-04-29 · 4,103 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The bracket portion of the passenger grab rail can break.

Risk. This could cause the passenger to lose their balance and fall from the motorcycle, or could disturb the rider's ability to control the vehicle increasing the risk of a vehicle crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the passenger grab rail with a new, stronger grab rail.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov
