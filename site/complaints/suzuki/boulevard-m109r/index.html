Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Fuel pump or line: 2006-2007 · 07V148000 · 2007-04-04 · 12,331 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the fuel hose that connects the two metal fuel delivery pipes mounted on the throttle body may have been clamped at a location on the pipes that is not completely round, which could allow fuel leakage to occur.

Risk. In the presence of an ignition source, leaked fuel can cause a fire, presenting the risk of injury or death.

Fix. Dealers will replace the fuel delivery pipe assembly free of charge. The recall began on april 6, 2007. Owners may contact suzuki at 714-572-1490.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (3)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Boulevard M109R (Vzr1800) 2006-2015 · 2015-07-20 · no. 2015320 · 4 bikes · Accessories

On certain motorcycles, the accessory backrest mount hardware kits may have contained bolts that were only to be used when mounting the kit with a backrest and saddlebag supports. This could cause the mounting bolts to stick out past the inside part of the rear fender which may, under certain conditions, come in contact with the rear tire causing a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash causing injury and/or damage to property. Correction: Dealers will replace the accessory backrest mounting hardware kit bolts with the correct length bolts.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Boulevard M109R (Vzr1800) 2012 · 2012-07-26 · no. 2012236 · 15 bikes · Label

Certain motorcycles fail to conform to the requirements of Canada Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 120 – Tire Selection and Rims. Due to a typographical error, the size of the front tire, as indicated on the compliance label, is incorrectly identified as 130/70-18M/C63V instead of 130/70R18M/C63V. Correction: Dealers will replace the compliance label.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Boulevard M109R (Vzr1800) 2006-2007 · 2007-04-02 · no. 2007106 · 907 bikes · Fuel System

On certain motorcycles, the fuel delivery pipe assembly may have been manufactured incorrectly. The fuel hose that connects to the two metal fuel lines may have been clamped at a location on the fuel pipes that is not completely round, which could allow fuel leakage to occur. In the presence of an ignition source, leaked fuel can cause a fire, presenting the risk of injury or death. Correction: Dealers will replace the fuel delivery pipe assembly.

Source: Transport Canada
