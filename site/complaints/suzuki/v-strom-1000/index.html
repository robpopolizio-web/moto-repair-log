Rectifier/Regulator Failure Causing Engine Stall (electrical and instruments): 2008-2012 · 16V018000 · 2016-01-19 · 68,344 bikes

Problem. Suzuki Motor of America, Inc. (Suzuki) is recalling certain model year 2008-2012 AN400, AN400A, DL1000, GSX1300R, 2008-2009 GSF1250S and GSF1250SA, 2008 GSX1300BK and GSX1300BKA, 2008-2010 GSX650F, GSX-R600, GSX-R750, VLR1800, VLR1800T, and 2009-2010 SFV650 and VZ1500 motorcycles. Due to a problem with the circuit board for the regulator/rectifier power module, the motorcycle may not sufficiently charge the battery and the motorcycle may stall.

Risk. If the engine stalls while riding, there would be an increased risk of a crash.

Fix. Suzuki will notify owners, and dealers will replace the regulator/rectifier, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin March 4, 2016. Owners may contact Suzuki customer service at 1-800-444-5077. Suzuki's number for this recall is 2A56 to 2A64.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Inadequate process control of production parts prior to vehicle assembly.

Part made by: Kokusan Denki Co., Ltd., Japan · Maker's recall no. 2A56 to 2A64

Wiring Harness may Rub and Short (electrical and instruments): 2014-2016 · 15V851000 · 2015-12-18 · 2,410 bikes

Problem. Suzuki Motor of America, Inc. (Suzuki) is recalling certain model year 2014 Suzuki DL 1000 motorcycles manufactured January 17, 2014, to June 5, 2014, 2015 Suzuki DL1000 motorcycles manufactured November 4, 2014, to February 6, 2015 and 2016 Suzuki DL1000 motorcycles manufactured June 11, 2015, to July 15, 2015. The affected motorcycles have a wiring harness that may rub against the bottom of the fuel tank. Vibration from riding may cause the wiring insulation to wear off, allowing a bare wire to short circuit against the fuel tank.

Risk. A short circuit may cause the engine to stall, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Suzuki will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the motorcycles and reroute the wiring harnesses as necessary. Any damaged harnesses will be repaired or replaced. This work will be performed free of charge. The recall began on January 29, 2016. Owners may contact Suzuki customer service number at 1-714-572-1490. Suzuki's number for this recall is 2A53.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Maker's recall no. 2A53

How the problem was found.

  • In February 2015, Suzuki Motor Corporation (SMC) received an initial Field Technical Information Report (FTIR) from its Belgian distributor about connector friction on the bottom of the fuel tank causing rust. SMC judged that this was a cosmetic issue, not a functional issue. SMC also received a second FTIR from the Belgian distributor during February 2015. SMC receive an FTIR from its New …
  • In March 2015, SMC received six FTIRs from U.S. distributors concerning wiring harness rubbing on the fuel tank. SMC judged that this was a cosmetic issue, not a functional issue, and collected parts.
  • In May-August 2015, SMC reproduced the rubbing and confirmed that fuel leakage does not occur from rubbing between the wire harness and fuel tank.
  • In September-November 2015, SMC found that the wire harness can be worn by rubbing against the fuel tank, depending on the routing position.
  • In November 2015, SMC confirmed that engine stalling can occur if the bare electrical wire contacts the fuel tank. As of November 2015, SMC received a cumulative total of twelve FTIRs.
  • On December 11, 2015, SMC decided to conduct a safety-related recall to address the issue.

Poor Battery Ground Conductivity (electrical and instruments): 2014-2015 · 15V853000 · 2015-12-18 · 2,404 bikes

Problem. Suzuki Motor of America, Inc. (Suzuki) is recalling certain model year 2014 Suzuki DL1000 motorcycles manufactured January 17, 2014 to June 5, 2014, and 2015 Suzuki DL1000 motorcycles manufactured November 5, 2014, to February 6, 2015. Due to corrosion or being attached to the painted engine case, the battery ground connection may have poor electrical conductivity.

Risk. As a result of the poor conductivity, the engine may stall and be unable to be restarted, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Suzuki will notify owners, and dealers will relocate the battery ground connection to an unpainted surface and replace the ground wire protector with a high-heat resistance tube, free of charge. The recall began on January 29, 2016. Owners may contact Suzuki customer service number at 1-714-996-7040. Suzuki's number for this recall is 2A53.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Fixed in production. In June 2015, SMC stopped painting the crankcase location used for attachment of the battery ground wire.

Maker's recall no. 2A53

How the problem was found.

  • In September 2014, Suzuki Motor Corporation (SMC) received a Field Technical Information Report (FTIR) from its United Kingdom distributor concerning an engine starter that wasn't working due to a poor connection of the battery ground connecting bolt.
  • In October 2014, SMC examined the collected part and found evidence of a loosened bolt. SMC judged that the cause was likely improper maintenance and decided to monitor information from the field.
  • In October-November 2014, SMC received four similar FTIRs from European distributors.
  • In December 2014, SMC found that a collected bolt from the European distributors contained an acid film. SMC supposed that this acid film increased the electrical resistance, resulting in inability to start the engine. SMC doubted that this type of electrical disconnection would happen in normal use, except in the case of splashing with salt water, and decided to monitor information from the …
  • In May 2015, SMC received three similar FTIRs from European distributors.
  • In June 2015, SMC stopped painting the crankcase location used for attachment of the battery ground wire.
  • In August 2015, SMC evaluated the frequency of reported malfunctions from the field. As of August 2015 SMC had received a cumulative total of seventeen FTIRs that appeared to be related to this issue.
  • On December 11, 2015, SMC decided to conduct a safety-related recall to address the issue.

Ignition Switch Terminals may Corrode (electrical and instruments): 2014-2015 · 15V854000 · 2015-12-18 · 2,404 bikes

Problem. Suzuki Motor of America, Inc. (Suzuki) is recalling certain model year 2014 DL1000 motorcycles manufactured January 17, 2014, to June 5, 2014, and 2015 DL1000 motorcycles manufactured November 5, 2014, to February 6, 2015. The ignition switch terminals of the affected vehicles may corrode if they come into contact with liquid such as salt water. As a result, the motorcycle's electrical power may be disconnected or the switch may be bypassed, preventing the engine from being shut off with the ignition key.

Risk. If electrical power is lost, the engine would stall, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Suzuki will notify owners, and dealers will install a cover for the ignition switch connector, and any corroded ignition lead wire and wire harnesses will be replaced. These repairs will be performed free of charge. The recall began January 19, 2016. Owners may contact Suzuki customer service at 1-714-996-7040. Suzuki's number for this recall is 2A53.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Maker's recall no. 2A53

How the problem was found.

  • In January 2015, Suzuki Motor Corporation (SMC) received a Field Technical Information Report (FTIR) from its distributor in the Netherlands concerning an incident in which the engine could not be turned off using the ignition key because the ignition switch connector had melted. SMC examined the collected part but could not find the cause of the melting. SMC decided to monitor information from …
  • In February-March 2015, SMC received twenty-nine similar FTIRs from European distributors.
  • In March-April 2015, SMC personnel visited the United Kingdom to investigate a malfunction. They found that salt water had corroded the terminals of the ignition switch connector.
  • In April 2015, SMC began installing a cover on the connector of production motorcycles as a temporary countermeasure to upgrade splash protection. in May 2015, SMC received two similar FTIRs: one from its Austrian distributor and one from its Japanese distributor.
  • In August 2015, SMC received an FTIR from its Canadian distributor.
  • In April-November 2015, SMC investigated the cause of splash and evaluated the countermeasure under various conditions.
  • On December 11, 2015, SMC decided to conduct a safety-related recall to address the issue.

REGULATOR ASSEMBLIES (electrical and instruments): 2008-2009 · 11V108000 · 2011-02-23 · 73,426 bikes

Problem. Suzuki is recalling certain model year 2008 through 2010 motorcycles manufactured from july 2007 through september 2009, equipped with regulator/rectifier assemblies, suzuki part numbers 32800-41f11, 32800-15h10, 32800-05h11, 32800-41g10, 32800-15h00, 32800-18h00, 32800-05g10, 32800-10g10, 32800-05h20, or 32800-06g01. Some regulator/rectifier assemblies were produced with insufficient adhesion between the power module (circuit board) and the rectifier case that contains a heat sink to dissipate heat. Due to insufficient adhesion, heat generated on the power module circuit board can cause the circuit board to deform, and lift of the case.

Risk. This condition causes excessive heat on the circuit board and uncontrolled electric current output, which can result in insufficient charging current being provided to the motorcycle battery. This can cause discharge of the battery and can lead to engine stalling and/or a no-start condition. Engine stalling while riding can increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. Suzuki will notify owners and suzuki distributors will replace the regulator/rectifier with an improved part free of charge. The safety recall began on march 2, 2011. Owners may contact suzuki at 1-714-572-1490.
