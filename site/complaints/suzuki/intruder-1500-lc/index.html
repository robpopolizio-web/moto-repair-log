Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix.

Steering: 1992-1994 · 94V036000 · 1994-02-15 · 9,965 bikes

Problem. Cracks develop on handlebars between the handlebar stays (handlebar mounting brackets) due to the softness of the cushion shock absorbers of the handlebar stays.

Risk. The cracks may cause looseness of the handlebar which may result in loss of control and an accident.

Fix. Suzuki dealers will replace the handlebar stay cushion rubbers and the handlebar of the affected vehicles.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov
