Recalls (2)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix.

Fuel system: 1997-1998 · 98V133000 · 1998-06-24 · 17,007 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The gasket between the fuel tank and the fuel pump mounting plate can lose its ability to provide a tight seal, allowing fuel to drip from the gasket.

Risk. In the presence of an ignition source, fuel leakage can result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will install a redesigned fuel pump gasket on these motorcycles.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Steering: 1997 · 97V093000 · 1997-06-11 · 2,935 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. Front wheel movement and rapid side-to-side handlebar movement can occur when the front wheel is lightly loaded and hits a bump.

Risk. This movement can reduce the driver's ability to control the motorcycle resulting in a loss of control and a vehicle crash.

Fix. Dealers will install a steering damper kit on these vehicles which is designed to reduce front wheel oscillation and eliminate side-to-side handlebar movement.
