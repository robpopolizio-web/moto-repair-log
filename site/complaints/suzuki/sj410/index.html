Fuel hose or line: 1985 · 85V066000 · 1985-05-23 · 1,662 bikes

Problem. The fuel return hose is of insufficient length and can detach from the fuel pump. Consequence of defect: this could result in an engine compartment fire.

Risk.

Fix. Replace the fuel return hose with a longer one.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Steering lock: 1984-1985 · 84V102000 · 1984-09-04 · 4,895 bikes

Problem. Buzzer was not installed to remind driver to remove the ignition key when leaving the vehicle. This does not meet the requirements of federal motor vehicle safety standard 114, "theft protection".

Risk.

Fix. The steering lock assembly and a buzzer will be installed.
