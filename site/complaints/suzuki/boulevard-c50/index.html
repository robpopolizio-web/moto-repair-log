Recalls in Canada and Japan (2)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Boulevard C50 (Vl800), Boulevard C50Se (Vl800Se), Boulevard C50Sec (Vl800Sec) 2008-2010 · 2011-02-18 · no. 2011071 · 8,828 bikes · Electrical

On certain motorcycles, the regulator/rectifier assembly may have been produced with insufficient adhesion between the power module (circuit board) and the rectifier case. Due to this insufficient adhesion, heat generated on the power module circuit board can cause the circuit board to deform. This could cause excessive heat on the circuit board and uncontrolled electric current output, which can result in insufficient charging current being provided to the vehicle's battery. Battery discharge could lead to engine stalling and/or a no-start condition. Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Boulevard C50 (Vl800) 2005-2006 · 2005-12-21 · no. 2005400 · 2,122 bikes · Electrical

On certain motorcycles, the ignition switch wiring harness may have been improperly routed at the time of production. If the wiring harness is not routed properly, it can rub between the clutch cable/throttle cables. Continued rubbing can eventually lead to a short circuit which will cause the engine to stall. The lights may also go out, and the engine will be unable to be restarted. This can increase the risk of a crash. Correction: Dealers will inspect and, where required, reroute the ignition switch wiring harness.

Source: Transport Canada
