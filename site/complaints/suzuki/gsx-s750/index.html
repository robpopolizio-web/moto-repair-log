Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Fuel pump: Fuel pump o-ring twisted causing fuel leak: 2018 · 18V694000 · 2018-10-04 · 6,928 bikes

Problem. Suzuki Motor of America, Inc. (Suzuki) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Suzuki DL650A, DL650XA, GSX-R1000, GSX-R1000R, and 2018 GSX-S750 motorcycles. During installation, the fuel pump O-ring may have been twisted, reducing the sealing performance and possibly resulting in a fuel leak.

Risk. A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.

Fix. Suzuki will notify owners, and dealers will replace the fuel pump O-ring and the fuel tank inner vessel, free of charge. The recall began October 31, 2018. Owners may contact Suzuki customer service at 1-800-934-0934. Suzuki's numbers for this recall are 2A86-2A88.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

What the new part or software changes. The recalled fuel pump O-ring has a D-shaped cross-section and the remedy O-ring has a round cross-section. Ribs have been added to the remedy fuel tank inner vessel. The recalled fuel tank inner vessel does not have ribs. Following are the part numbers of the recalled components. DL650, GSX-S750, GSX-R1000 fuel pump O-ring part number: 15201-17K01 DL650, GSX-S750, GSX-R1000 fuel tank inner vessel part number: 44121-28K00

Fixed in production. Use of the countermeasure fuel pump O-ring in production began on June 1, 2018. Use of the countermeasure fuel tank inner vessel in production began on June 21, 2018.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (2)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Japan: Gsx-S750 · 2019 · no. 4444 · 52 bikes

Due to the jig which holds the aluminum body of Hydraulic ABS (Antilock Brake System) unit is inappropriate shape, in some case, an aluminum burr may be formed at the time of assembling the component parts to the body of it. If the said aluminum burr is stuck into the check valve, front wheel or a rear-wheel might be locked.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)

Canada: Gsx-S750 2018 · 2018-10-10 · no. 2018558 · 1,911 bikes · Fuel System

On certain motorcycles, the fuel pump O-ring may have been twisted during installation and may not seal properly. This could result in a fuel leak, which in the presence of an ignition source, could increase the risk of fire causing injury and/or damage to property. Correction: Dealers will replace the fuel pump O-ring and fuel tank inner vessel with improved parts.

Source: Transport Canada
