Other Beta recalls (1)

Recalls for models with no matching owner complaints. The model name is as the maker filed it.

350 RR-S, 390 RR-S, 430 RR-S, 500 RR-S · Brake hose or line: Incorrect front brake hose: 2022 · 22V325000 · 2022-05-11 · 97 bikes

Problem. Beta USA (Beta) is recalling certain 2022 350 RR-S, 390 RR-S, 430 RR-S, and 500 RR-S motorcycles. An incorrect front brake hose was installed during production.

Risk. An incorrect front brake hose may disconnect, resulting in a loss of brakes and increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Beta will replace the front brake hose, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 24 2022. Owners may contact Beta customer service at 1-805-226-4035. Beta's number for this recall is R-2022-1.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

What the new part or software changes. You can tell by the part number listed on the brake hose it will read 29.09108.000 appose to the recalled # 37.36.002.00.00. You can also tell by how the front brake hose connects to the Master Cylinder on the handler bars. The remedy component has a slight bend to the hose where it installs to the master cylinder as the recalled will go straight directly in.

Fixed in production. We notified the factory on 3/18/2021 and they were able to locate the productions that had the incorrect brake hose installed and replaced them all.

Part made by: Beta Motor SPA, Italy · Maker's recall no. R-2022-1

How the problem was found.

  • 3/17/2022: Dealer notified our service dept stating bike they received came with incorrect front brake hose.
  • 3/18/2022: Check units at our facility and confirmed they came with incorrect front brake hose. Notified the Factory with the VIN's and Invoice #'s to help them locate the production run.
  • 3/22/2022: Factory located the production runs that had the incorrect front brake hose installed notified us with a list for us to notify the dealers & customers.
  • 3/28/2022: Received correct front brake hoses to send to dealers.
