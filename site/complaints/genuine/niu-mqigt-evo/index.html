Excessive Handlebar Vibration (steering): 2022 · 22V629000 · 2022-08-17 · 38 bikes

Problem. Genuine Scooters, LLC (Genuine Scooters) is recalling certain 2022 NIU MQiGT EVO scooters. During operation, excessive handlebar vibration can occur which may cause rider fatigue or loss of vehicle control.

Risk. Excessive handlebar vibration increases the risk of a crash.

Fix. Owners are advised not to ride their scooters until the repair has been completed. The remedy is currently under development. Owners have the option to return their scooters for a full refund. Owner notification letters were mailed August 18, 2022. Owners may contact Genuine Scooters customer service at 1-773-433-1316. Genuine Scooter's number for this recall is MQiGT EVO. The scooters in this recall are also affected by recall number 22V-597.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Unidentified chassis issue.

What the new part or software changes. The bearings are a different design. The initial bearings were a standard roller bearing. The new bearings are a tapered design.

Fixed in production. The manufacturer has not produced any of these units since the recall occurred. When they go back into production, they will use the new roller bearing with the tapered design.

Part made by: Jiangsu NIU Electric Technology Co., LTD, China · Maker's recall no. MQiGT EVO

How the problem was found.

  • Genuine Scooters received three complaints of excessive handlebar vibration while driving. After discussions with the manufacturer NIU, it was determined that the excessive handlebar vibration is caused by unknown factors at this point time in time. Consequently, Genuine is conducting a recall.

Loss of Drive Power (electrical and instruments): 2022 · 22V597000 · 2022-08-08 · 38 bikes

Problem. Genuine Scooters, LLC (Genuine Scooters) is recalling certain 2022 NUI MQiGT EVO scooters. A loss of drive power may occur due to a firmware issue in the electric motor.

Risk. Loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash or injury.

Fix. Owners are advised not to ride their scooters until the repair has been completed. Dealers will provide new motors with new firmware, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed in August 2022. Owners may contact Genuine Scooters customer service at 1-773-433-1316. Genuine Scooter's number for this recall is MQiGT EVO. The scooters in this recall are also affected by recall number 22V-629.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Firmware issue within the electric motor.

What the new part or software changes. The correct motor firmware is different from the one currently installed.

Fixed in production. Production was halted by the manufacturer. When it restarts, the correct Firmware will be installed.

Part made by: Jiangsu NIU Electric Technology Co., LTD, China · Maker's recall no. MQiGT EVO

How the problem was found.

  • Genuine Scooters, LLC received 8 complaints from dealers regarding motor fault code 123 with subsequent loss of motor power of the scooters. After discussions with the scooter manufacturer, NIU, it was determined that the motor firmware was causing the motor to stop operation unexpectedly.
