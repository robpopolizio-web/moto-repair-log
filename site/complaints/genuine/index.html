BUDDY KICK · Fuel Pump Failure Can Cause Loss of Drive Power (fuel system): 2022 · 22V862000 · 2022-11-21 · 817 bikes

Problem. Genuine Scooters, LLC is recalling certain 2022 Genuine Buddy Kick 125 motorcycles. The impeller may strike the fuel pump body, causing the fuel pump to fail.

Risk. A fuel pump failure can cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the fuel pump, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 6, 2022. Owners may contact Genuine Scooters customer service at 1-773-433-1316.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

What the new part or software changes. On the defective fuel pumps, the manufacturing tolerance between the fuel pump impeller and the fuel pump body is too small. The pump impeller may come in contact with the fuel pump body causing the pump impeller to stop. When the pump impeller stops, no fuel pressure is maintained in the fuel system and the vehicle will cease operation. On the remedied fuel pumps, the dimension of the fuel pump impeller has been changed to ensure sufficient space exists between the pump impeller and pump body. Proper manufacturing tolerances ensure the pump impeller does not come into contact directly with the pump body during use.

Fixed in production. The fuel pumps were replaced in all production after 7/31/2022

Part made by: Motive Power Industry Co., Taiwan, Province of China

How the problem was found.

  • On July 17th, 2022, our warranty department noticed an increase in warranty claims for failed fuel pumps. The warranty department reported that several dealers had submitted warranty claims for failed fuel pumps on the Buddy Kick model over the previous 9 months. ROYAL ALLOY · Brake Pads May Dislodge and Cause Loss of Braking (brakes and abs): 2020 · 20V010000 · 2020-01-10 · 148 bikes

Problem. Genuine Scooters, LLC. (Genuine Scooters) is recalling certain 2020 Royal Alloy GT150 scooters. The front brake rotor may become uncentered within the front brake caliper allowing the inner brake pad to dislodge from the top of the brake caliper.

Risk. If the brake pad becomes dislodged, the scooter can experience a reduced or complete loss of braking, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Genuine Scooters will notify owners, and dealers will reposition the brake rotors with spacers to prevent the brake pads from dislodging, free of charge. The recall began January 2019. Note: Genuine Scooters requests owners to not drive their scooters. Please have the scooter towed to a dealer for repairs. Roadside assistance coverage has been provided along with owner's warranty so the tow will be free of charge. Owners may contact Genuine Scooters customer service at 1-773-433-1311.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

What the new part or software changes. There isn't a recalled component. Instead, the brake rotor is being repositioned with spacers to prevent the brake pads from dislodging.

Fixed in production. Genuine has not imported any Royal Alloy scooters other than the 148 affected by the recall. When more scooters are produced, the brake rotor will be repositioned with spacers to prevent the front brake pads from dislodging from the front brake caliper.

Part made by: Chanzhou Hande Vehicle Industry Co. LTD, China

How the problem was found.

  • On 12/13/2019 Genuine was verbally notified that a break pad from a Royal Alloy GT150 had dislodged from the wheel. On 1/2/2020, Genuine notified the manufacturer of the problem via email. On 1/9/2020, the manufacturer emailed Genuine stating they had identified the problem. They also provided the solution to fix the problem. On 1/10/2020, Genuine is reporting a recall with NHTSA.

STELLA · Faulty Circuit In ECU may Result in Engine Stall (electrical and instruments): 2014 · 14V408000 · 2014-07-07 · 800 bikes

Problem. Genuine Scooters, LLC (Genuine) is recalling certain model year 2014 Stella 125 Automatic scooters manufactured December 2013 to March 2014. The affected scooters may have a faulty engine control unit (ECU) which can cause the engine to stall without warning.

Risk. If the engine stalls while the scooter is being ridden there is an increased risk of a crash.

Fix. Genuine will notify owners, and dealers will replace the ECU with a new one, free of charge. The recall began on August 29, 2014. Owners may contact Genuine customer service 1-773-433-1311.
