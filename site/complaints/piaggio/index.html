LIBERTY 150, LIBERTY 50 · Dash and gauges and headlight: Headlight may fail: 2026 · 26V396000 · 2026-06-18 · 112 bikes

Problem. Piaggio Group Americas, Inc. (PGA) is recalling certain 2026 Liberty 50 and Liberty 150 motorcycles. A fault in the electrical circuit may cause the low beam and/or high beam headlight to unexpectedly turn off. In certain conditions, the instrument panel lighting may also fail to illuminate.

Risk. Loss of lighting can reduce visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will inspect the date code label affixed to the headlight assembly, and if the date is between January 1, 2026 and January 30, 2026, the headlight will be replaced, free of charge. PGA has not yet provided a schedule for owner notification. Owners may contact PGA's customer service at 212-380-4400, or customercare@us.piaggio.com.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. A capacitor manufactured by Samsung used in the production of the headlight - may not comply with the specified technical requirements. After a long period of production without any problems, some reels of capacitors bought through a distributor from Samsung Chinese Plant, had defective capacitors inside.

What the new part or software changes. The remedy headlights have been built with tested capacitors from a different factory.

Fixed in production. All headlamps were corrected for all future production. The last production date of headlamps with the defective capacitors was January 30, 2026. Start of production on February 2, 2026 VARROC stopped using Chinese plant reels and used Philippine plant reels only. Varroc traceability label: first ok 02FEB26

Fix type: Inspect · Part made by: Varroc Lighting Systems Viet Nam Co.,Ltd, Viet Nam

How the problem was found.

  • Will upload chronology from the Factory. First case reported March 7th That started the technical quality review. Information sent to USA 6/17/2026

LIBERTY 50 iGET 3V · Rear suspension: Swing arm may loosen: 2018 · 17V469000 · 2017-07-24 · 180 bikes

Problem. Piaggio Group Americas, Inc. (Piaggio) is recalling certain 2018 Liberty 50 iGet 3V scooters. The nut that holds the rear swing arm to the frame may loosen allowing the swing arm to move out of position.

Risk. The shifting swing arm may cause a loss of control, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Piaggio will notify owners, and dealers will check the swing arm nut torque, tightening or replacing the nut, bolt and washer, free of charge. The recall began on August 2, 2017. Owners may contact Piaggio customer service at 1-212-380-4433.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. the nut holding the swing arm bolt may not have had the correct torque applied during production.

What the new part or software changes. The new design order on the assembly line increases the torque of the swing arm nut from 40-45 Nm to 50-55 Nm. There is no problems related to the material or component,

Fixed in production. The new design order on the assembly line increases the torque of the swing arm nut from 40-45 Nm to 50-55 Nm. There is no problems related to the material or component,

How the problem was found.

  • There were 5 claims of the nut becoming loose and one accident due to the nut coming loose. There have not been any cases in the US market.

FLY 150 3V · Brakes: Front brake disc screws may loosen: 2014-2017 · 17V468000 · 2017-07-22 · 1,200 bikes

Problem. Piaggio Group Americas, Inc. (Piaggio) is recalling certain 2014-2017 Piaggio Fly 150 3V ie scooters. The front brake disc mounting screws may loosen and contact the brake caliper holding plate or the fork leg.

Risk. If a screw loosens and contacts the caliper holding plate or the fork leg, the front wheel may suddenly lock up, increasing the risk of crash.

Fix. Piaggio has notified owners, and dealers will tighten the front brake disc mounting screws after applying a thread-locking adhesive, free of charge. The recall began July 31, 2017. Owners may contact Piaggio customer service at 1-212-380-4433. Piaggio's number for this recall is 0000313.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. improper torque may have been applied during assembly.

Fixed in production. Production improved: from September 2016 1. Introduced the electronic screwdrivers with torque and angle control 2. Bracket constraint rotates if KO point 1 3. Bar code traceability 4. Definition new disk fixing screw, Scotch-Grip

Maker's recall no. 0000313

How the problem was found.

  • There have been eight warranty claims and one accident world wide. There have not been and reported failures on vehicles sold in the US market.

MOTO GUZZI V7 III RACER 7, V7 III SPECIAL 750, V7 III STONE 750, V9 BOBBER, V9 ROAMER · Brake hose or line: Front brake line may rub and leak: 2017 · 17V380000 · 2017-06-14 · 1,139 bikes

Problem. Piaggio Group Americas, Inc. (Piaggio) is recalling certain 2017 Moto Guzzi V7 III Racer 750, V7 III Stone 750, V7 III Special 750, and 2016-2017 V9 Bobber, V9 Roamer motorcycles. The front ABS brake hose may rub against the engine's secondary air system connection, possibly resulting in a brake fluid leak.

Risk. A loss of brake fluid can lengthen the distance needed to stop the motorcycle, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Piaggio will notify owners, and dealers will secure the front ABS brake hose to prevent it from rubbing. Any hose that has already been damaged, will be replaced. The repairs will be made free of charge. The recall began June 22, 2017. Owners may contact Piaggio customer service at 1-212-380-4433.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Fretting between the front ABS brake hose and the engine's secondary air system connection due to brake lines that can move out of position.

What the new part or software changes. The brake line is not a defective component but there is a possibility of the lines to move out of position and rub on the secondary air union located on the engine. If the brake pipe shows any sign of fretting the brake line will be replaced.

Fixed in production. The installation of ABS pipe mount (same solution applied to remedy the recall) has been put in place on the production starting 05/31/2017.

How the problem was found.

  • On 04/03/2017. We had one case in Europe where we found a customer intervention that could have changed the original position of the tube. There have not been any reported failures or crashes at this time.

BV 300 · Fuel pump: Defective fuel pump may result in stall: 2010 · 15V066000 · 2015-02-04 · 2,613 bikes

Problem. Piaggio Group Americas Inc. (Piaggio) is recalling certain model year 2008-2010 Vespa GTS 250, GTV 250, GTS Super 250, 2011 Vespa LX 150, LXV 150, S 150, 2010-2011 Vespa GTS 300, GTV 300, GTS Super 300, 2009-2010 Piaggio MP3 250, BV 250 Tourer, and 2010 BV 300 Tourer motorcycles. Due to a defective fuel pump, the affected motorcycles may stall while being operated or may fail to restart after being shut off for 15-20 minutes.

Risk. If the motorcycle stalls, there is an increased risk of a crash.

Fix. Piaggio will notify owners, and dealers will replace the defective fuel pump, free of charge. The recall began March 16, 2015. Owners may contact Piaggio customer service at 1-212-380-4433.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Interference between the impeller and the inner pump housing caused by decreasing pump clearance, due to the impeller swelling.

What the new part or software changes. The new components can be identified by a brown cap on the motor of the fuel pump vs. a black cap on the old component.

Fixed in production. All new production will have a component that has been properly manufactured and quality inspected by Piaggio & C S.p.a.

Part made by: Bitron S.p.a.

How the problem was found.

  • January 26, 2011 notification was sent to NHTSA regarding the fuel pump service campaign.
  • September 2014: notification was sent to Piaggio & C S.p.a about an investigation made by Italian authorities as a consequence of a claim regarding the stalling of a vehicle while running.

X9 500 · Fuel hose or line: 2005-2007 · 09V188000 · 2009-06-02 · 2,428 bikes

Problem. Piaggio is recalling 2,428 my 2006-2008 bv 500 and my 2005-2007 x9 500 scooters. The fuel hose connecting the fuel filter to the fuel pump may come loose. If this fuel hose connection is loose, or has become completely disconnected, then there will be a drop in, or loss of, fuel pressure to the engine.

Risk. Loss of fuel pressure and engine stalling will increase the risk of a crash. A fuel leak can also result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will replace the length of fuel hose connecting the fuel pump to the fuel filter free of charge. The recall began on july 3, 2009. Owners may contact piaggio at 1-212-380-4400.

MP3 250 RL · Engine: 2008 · 07V366000 · 2007-08-17 · 159 bikes

Problem. On certain motor scooters, the head of the engine attachment bolt (p/n 597080) may break.

Risk. If the bolt were to break, the engine could become loose and detach itself from the vehicle frame, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. The engine attachment bolts (vd 10.9) will be replaced. All vehicles are still in transit to the united states. None of these vehicles have been distributed to dealers or sold to customers.
