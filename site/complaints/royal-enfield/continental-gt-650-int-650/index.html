Lights: Insufficient side and rear reflectors/FMVSS 108: 2023 · 24V713000 · 2024-09-27 · 4,206 bikes

Problem. Royal Enfield North America Limited (Royal Enfield) is recalling certain 2023 Himalayan, Classic 350, Meteor 350, Hunter 350, Super Meteor 650, Int 650, and Continental GT 650 motorcycles. The side and rear reflectors are not sufficient. As such, these lights fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."

Risk. Without the proper reflective equipment, it may be difficult for other road users to see the motorcycle from the rear or side, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the side and rear reflectors, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed October 30, 2024. Owners may contact Royal Enfield's customer service at 1-866-600-1122. Royal Enfield's number for this recall is SC-43.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Molding tool cleanliness which reduced reflective operation.

What the new part or software changes. The updated side reflector part carries the letter 'N' molded into the side body of the part. The rear reflector part carries the number'25' into the side body of the reflector.

Fixed in production. The new parts were carrying the new identification were integrated into the production process. April 1st 2023.

Fix type: Replace · Part made by: Uno Minda Limited, India · Maker's recall no. SC-43

How the problem was found.

  • The defect was Identified while under performance testing by KATRI (Korean Transportation Safety Authority).

Brake caliper: Brake caliper malfunction due to corrosion: 2018-2020 · 21V045000 · 2021-02-03 · 4,403 bikes

Problem. Royal Enfield North America Limited (Royal Enfield) is recalling certain 2018-2020 Continental GT 650 and Interceptor 650 motorcycles. Road salt may cause corrosion on the brake calipers.

Risk. Corroded brake calipers may cause the brakes to drag or decrease braking performance, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Royal Enfield will notify owners, and dealers will replace front and rear calipers, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 15, 2021. Owners may contact Royal Enfield customer service at 1-866-600-1122.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Royal Enfield has discovered a brake caliper corrosion-related issue in a very small number of Interceptor INT 650, Continental GT 650 motorcycles in some specific countries outside of North America. Upon investigation, it was revealed that this corrosion is brought about by sustained, long-term exposure to riding on roads treated with certain salts or a combination of salts, to prevent formation of ice during winters.

Fixed in production. Production Data on implementation of anodized calipers 100 % implementation done with effect from : ME3FPN479MK700239 - Date of implementation : 1.12.2020

Part made by: Brembo Brake India PVT / LTD Chakan Comp, India

How the problem was found.

  • Royal Enfield made the determination to conduct this voluntary safety recall on May 13, 2020 within the United Kingdom. On May 13, 2020 Royal Enfield of North America (RENA) was informed a foreign recall was being announced and the USA was not impacted. RENA filed a notice of foreign recall with NHTSA on May 18, 2020.
  • On February 1, 2021 RENA decided to file a voluntary recall with NHTSA due to a few reasons: 1, Since the announcement of the foreign recall, RENA and its dealers have received a large volume of calls / inquiries by U.S. customers in regard to the recall and the impact on their motorcycle.
  • The company will not be stocking any replacement parts for the old caliper in the near future.
  • The new replacement caliper is aesthectically different then the original caliper. (If a customer is in need of a replacement caliper for any reason it would not match the original caliper)
  • RENA also wants to operate with an overabundance of caution. Please note, we have had zero incidents in the U.S. and Canada, zero warranty claims, zero caliper and or brake parts ordered by our dealers, zero field reports highlighting any concerns with the braking system and / or braking conditions.

2024 Royal Enfield Continental GT 650/INT 650 · Brakes & ABS · FL · filed 2026-08 · NHTSA 11755505While riding my 2024 Royal Enfield INT650 with only 200.7 miles on it the rear hydraulic brakes suffered sudden catastrophic failure with no warning. the brake pedal went completely soft and fell to the floor causing a total loss of rear stopping power and creating an immediate collision hazarded. the bike was towed to an authorized dealer which confirmed the brake fluid was boiled but blamed user error (riding the pedal) which is extremely false. This is a known systemic design defect on this motorcycle platform. The actual mechanical cause is a misadjusted factory pushrod blocking the master cylinder return port, binding the caliper sliding pins, or faulty ABS modulator valve. Any of these factory defects cause the brakes to drag automatically, self-pressurize, overheat, and boil the fluid without rider input. Simply flushing the fluid ignores the mechanical defect and leaves the vehicle unsafe.

Had I needed to stop suddenly or avoid a collision, this defect could have caused a serious accident or fatal injuries.
