Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Fuel pump: Fuel pump may fail: 2025 · 25V108000 · 2025-02-20 · 7 bikes

Problem. Indian Motorcycle Company (Indian) is recalling certain 2025 Challenger Dark Horse, Pursuit Limited, Roadmaster Elite and Springfield Dark Horse motorcycles. Due to an incorrectly crimped wire, the fuel pump may fail and result in an engine stall during operation.

Risk. An engine stall increases the risk of a crash or injury.

Fix. Dealers will replace the fuel pump, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 5, 2025. Owners may contact Indian customer service at 877-204-3697. Indian's number for this recall is I-25-01.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Improper power supply wire crimp performed by the fuel pump supplier.

What the new part or software changes. The remedy fuel pump will be manufactured subject to improved quality controls at the supplier and consistent with the engineering specifications.

Fixed in production. Suspect components and completed motorcycles were quarantined and inspected. Units produced after December 4, 2024 were built with inspected materials.

Part made by: Walbro LLC, United States · Maker's recall no. I-25-01

How the problem was found.

  • On January 13, 2025, Indian Motorcycle Company (IMC) identified a suspect part in production. The supplier was then contacted and on January 17, 2025, IMC received analysis from the supplier identifying parts in a suspect lot which were delivered to IMC. Thereafter, IMC began investigating. Containment measures on parts and vehicles were then initiated by IMC. Upon inspection of inventory, IMC …
  • On February 13, 2025, IMC’s Executive Review Committee decided that the fuel pump failure during operation resulting in an engine stall resulted in a safety-related defect.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (2)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Pursuit 2022 · 2024-12-10 · no. 2024752 · 759 bikes · Lights And Instruments

Under certain conditions, a software problem could cause the brake light to flicker without applying the brakes. Safety Risk: A brake light that turns on when the motorcycle isn't braking could cause confusion for others and increase the risk of a crash. Corrective Actions: Indian Motorcycle will notify owners in writing and advise you to take your motorcycle to a dealership to update the ABS module software.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Pursuit 2024 · 2024-07-23 · no. 2024420 · 257 bikes · Suspension

On certain motorcycles, the rear suspension push rod could fail. If this happens, the rear suspension could collapse and pinch the main wiring harness, damage the coolant reservoir and/or contact the rear tire. This could cause a sudden loss of stability and/or a loss of control. Safety Risk: A loss of stability and/or control could increase the risk of a crash. Corrective Actions: Indian Motorcycle will notify owners by mail and advise you to take your motorcycle to a dealership to replace the rear suspension push rod.

Source: Transport Canada
