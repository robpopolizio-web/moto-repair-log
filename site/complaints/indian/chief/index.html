ABS warning light: ABS warning light may fail: 2025-2026 · 26V742000 · 2026-11-11 · 290 bikes

Problem. Indian Motorcycle Company (Indian) is recalling certain 2025-2026 Chief and Chief Bobber motorcycles. The ABS warning light on the analog speedometer may fail to illuminate during an ABS fault or ABS event. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 122, "Motorcycle Brake Systems."

Risk. Failure of the ABS warning light to notify the rider of a disabled system can result in a loss of stability, increasing the risk of injury.

Fix. Dealers will update the gauge software, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed December 9, 2026. Owners may contact Indian customer service at 877-204-3697. Indian's number for this recall is I-26-03. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov on November 20, 2026.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. An error in the communication between the analog speedometer and the ABS system resulted in the ABS telltale in the gauge being set to a default of off.

What the new part or software changes. Updated speedometer software corrects the default setting for the ABS telltale, allowing proper function.

Fixed in production. On 11/9/2026 a new analog speedometer part number was implemented on the assembly line. Units produced after 8/8/2026 were quarantined will be updated to the new and correct software.

Fix type: Software · Part made by: Aumovio Guadalajara Mexico S De Rl, Mexico · Maker's recall no. I-26-03

How the problem was found.

  • On August 7, 2026, a Model Year 2027 development vehicle log was reviewed for issues with speedometer telltales. Subsequent reviews of previous model years and data revisions were initiated.
  • On September 3, 2026, IMC opened an internal investigation and review with the supplier.
  • November 4, 2026, the IMC Executive Review Committee reviewed and agreed upon a noncompliance with FMVSS 122.
  • November 9, 2026, the IMC assembly factory converted to a new analog speedometer in production.

Fix. IMC will mail owners information labels with instructions, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 15, 2026. Owners may contact IMC customer service at 877-204-3697. What the new part or software changes. Updated label corrects the rear rim size callout to the 3.5” width.

Fixed in production. On 5/19/2026 information feeding the VIN label data entry tool was updated to correct the VIN label callout. Vehicles built on or after 5/20/2026 have correct VIN label callouts.

Fix type: Mail Information/Labels · Part made by: Indian Motorcycle Company LLC, United States · Maker's recall no. I-26-01

How the problem was found.

  • On May 18, 2026, in an audit for information documentation, IMC found a discrepancy on the VIN Label for Indian Chief Vintage models.

  • June 15, 2026 the IMC Executive Review Committee reviewed and agreed upon a violation of FMVSS 120.

Problem. Indian Motorcycle Company (Indian) is recalling certain 2014-2019 Chief, Chieftain, Roadmaster, and Springfield motorcycles. The gear position switch contacts may oxidize, causing the gear position display to inaccurately show that the motorcycle is in neutral when it is actually in gear.

Risk. If the transmission is not in neutral when the motorcycle is started, the motorcycle may move unexpectedly, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the switch free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 16, 2020. Owners may contact Indian customer service at 1-877-204-3697. Indian's number for this recall is I-19-03.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The gear position switch output signal is based off of resistance. Oxidation can develop over time which unintentionally increases resistance values. In certain conditions when oxidation is present, the resistance value in first gear can align with the expected range for neutral, thus indicating neutral on the rider display with the bike in gear.

What the new part or software changes. The remedy gear position switch will contain updated resistance values. These resistance values will prevent unintended neutral indication, should the contacts become oxidized. OLD P/N: 4014718: SWITCH, GEAR, N-6, W/GROUND NEW P/N: 4019238: SWITCH, GEAR, N-6, W/GROUND

Fixed in production. Not applicable.

Part made by: Asahi Denso Co. LTD, Japan · Maker's recall no. I-19-03

  • In or about September 2018, Polaris investigated incidents identified in July and August 2018 in which the gear position switch on some Indian motorcycles may have provided an inaccurate signal which, if established, could lead to an incorrect transmission neutral status. Problem. On certain motorcycles, the rear axles are made of a material with a lower than desirable yield strength.

Risk. Under repeated heavy loads, the rear axle could deform causing excessive wear in the rear wheel bearings potentially leading to overheating and seizing of the bearings, increasing the risk of a crash.
