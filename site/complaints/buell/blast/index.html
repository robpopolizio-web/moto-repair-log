Fuel tank: 2007-2009 · 08V439000 · 2008-08-27 · 1,335 bikes

Problem. Buell is recalling 1,307 my 2007-2008 blast motorcycles. The fuel tank may experience some local deformation under high-heat conditions that creates contact of the tank with the cylinder head. In some of these cases, the cylinder head rubbing on the fuel tank has created a fuel leak.

Risk. This could result in a crash or fire, which could cause injury or death to the rider.

Fix. Dealers will install a bracket which will prevent the fuel tank from contacting the cylinder head. The recall began on september 11, 2008. Owners may contact buell at 1-414-343-8400.

Fuel system: 2004 · 04V365000 · 2004-07-23 · 656 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the valve which was designed to allow air to replace fuel in the tank during operation could malfunction thereby starving the engine for fuel.

Risk. This situation could cause the vehicle to shut down without warning, which could lead to a crash, causing the possibility of death or injury.

Fix. Buell will replace the vent valve. The recall began on august 27, 2004. Owners should contact buell at 414-343-8400.

Wheel: 2000 · 00V271000 · 2000-09-13 · 2,260 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. A rear wheel sprocket can break.

Risk. This could cause the rider to lose power, lose control, or be struck by debris.

Fix. Dealers will install a new rear wheel sprocket and belt.
