Recalls (8)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix.

Fuel system: 1998 · 99V104000 · 1999-05-06 · 10,556 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. These motorcycles were built with a fuel tank mount system that could, in the event of a crash, allow the fuel tank to separate from the motorcycle.

Risk. This could result in an explosion or fire.

Fix. Dealers will replace the fuel tank mount system.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Suspension: 1998 · 99V106000 · 1999-05-06 · 8,950 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. These motorcycles were built with a swingarm that could break.

Risk. If the swingarm should break, loss of vehicle control could occur.

Fix. Dealers will replace the swingarm.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Suspension: 1998 · 99V107000 · 1999-05-06 · 8,950 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. These motorcycles were built with a rear shock absorber front eye that could break.

Risk. If the shock eye should break, the underside of the vehicle will drag on the ground possibly causing loss of vehicle control.

Fix. Dealers will replace the rear shock absorber.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Fuel system: 1998 · 99V105000 · 1999-05-06 · 12,321 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. These motorcycles were built with a fuel tank vent valve assembly that could become plugged.

Risk. Under certain conditions, this could cause the carburetor to overflow fuel which could result in a fire. This condition could also prevent sufficient fuel flow, which could cause the engine to misfire or stall.

Fix. Dealers will replace the fuel tank vent valve assembly.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Other or not stated: 1998 · 99V097000 · 1999-04-29 · 10,255 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. These motorcycles were built with a sidestand switch that could become inoperative

Risk. This condition can cause the engine to stall/quit when riding.

Fix. Dealers will replace the sidestand switch.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Suspension: 1997-1998 · 99V086000 · 1999-04-26 · 5,208 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The front isolator mount system can lose clamp load and possibly break.

Risk. This could lead to an unintended distraction to the driver, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the front isolator mount system.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Lights: 1998 · 98V036000 · 1998-02-23 · 4,724 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. Failure of one or more turn signal lamps would not be indicated to the operator of the vehicle (no noticeable change in the flashing rate of the turn signal indicator light). This does not meet the requirements of fmvss no. 108, "lamps, reflective devices, and associated equipment."

Risk. An operator would not be aware that the turn signal lamps were not working.

Fix. Dealers will replace the turn signal lamps on these motorcycles.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Brakes and ABS: 1998 · 97V153000 · 1997-09-12 · 2,820 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The e-clip which retains the drive pin on the front brake rotor and carrier can separate from the drive pin allowing the pin to move out of position and come in contact with the front brake caliper.

Risk. This condition can prevent the front wheel from rotating increasing the risk of a vehicle crash.

Fix. Dealers will repair these motorcycles.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov
