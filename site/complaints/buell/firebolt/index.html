Brakes: 2009 · 09V109000 · 2009-04-02 · 3,316 bikes

Problem. Buell distribution company is recalling 3,316 my 2008 1125r, my 2009 xb12r and xb12scg motorcycles. The subject motorcycles have a front brake line which may contact the front tire. The condition could cause a hole to develop in the front brake line, allowing brake fluid to leak.

Risk. This condition might cause an ineffective front brake, leading to a possible crash and potential injury or death of the rider.

Fix. Dealers will re-route the front brake line. If damage to the brake line has occurred, the brake line will be replaced free of charge. The recall is expected to begin on or before april 20, 2009. Owners may contact buell distribution company at 1-414-343-8400.

Side or centre stand: 2005 · 05V148000 · 2005-04-14 · 149 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the sidestand may not retract as designed if contact is made with the ground when the vehicle is in motion.

Risk. This could, in turn, disrupt the stability of the motorcycle leading to a crash, causing death or injury to the rider.

Fix. Dealers will replace the sidestand pivot bolt with a similar bolt that is .100 inch longer free of charge. This change will allow the sidestand to operate as designed. The recall began may 5, 2005. Owners may contact buell at 1-414-343-8400.

Horn: 2004 · 03V494000 · 2003-12-02 · 581 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the wire that provides power to the horn, due to misrouting, can become frayed from contact with the horn bracket mounting bolt. As a result, the horn wire may become exposed, and when the horn button is pushed, either the horn circuit fuse or the key switch fuse can blow.

Risk. If the key switch fuse blows, the vehicle can stall, which could result in a crash. If the horn fuse blows, there will be a loss of power to the horn and rear brake light.

Fix. Dealers will inspect and correct the wire routing, if necessary. Owner notification began on december 23, 2003. Owners should contact buell at 1-414-342-4680.

Other: 2003 · 02V270000 · 2002-10-03 · 371 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the side stand legs have the potential to bend or break.

Risk. This could cause the motorcycle to fall increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the side stand. Owner notification began october 16, 2002. Owners who take their vehicles to an authorized dealer on an agreed upon service date and do not receive the free remedy within a reasonable time should contact buell at 1-414-342-4680.
