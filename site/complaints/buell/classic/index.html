Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

OUT OF POSITION FOOTPEG (accessories and equipment): 2010 · 09V465000 · 2009-12-02 · 23 bikes

Problem. Buell is recalling certain model year 2010 xb12x and xb12xt motorcycles manufactured from august 20 through september 30, 2009. The right hand side footpeg mount may have been manufactured to incorrect specifications.

Risk. This condition could allow the footpeg to move significantly out of position, which could lead to loss of control of the vehicle resulting in injury or death of the rider.

Fix. Dealers will replace the defective footpeg mount with a new footpeg mount that was manufactured to the correct specifications. This service will be performed free of charge. The safety recall began on december 14, 2009. Owners may contact buell distribution company at 1-414-343-8400.

BANK ANGLE SENSOR (fuel system): 2006-2007 · 07V026000 · 2007-02-01 · 2,044 bikes

Problem. Certain motorcycles may have a vibration at the mounting location of the bank angle sensor which, if combined with the misrouting of various wires that impinge on the sensor or its pigtail, can compromise the isolation of the bank angle sensor.

Risk. This may create a false 'tip' signal and cause the engine to quit running while being driven, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will move the bank angle sensor from the original location on the battery tray to a location on the seat latch. The recall began on march 5, 2007. Owners may contact buell at 1-414-343-8400.

SIDESTAND PERFORMANCE (frame and bodywork): 2006 · 06V245000 · 2006-07-11 · 1,021 bikes

Problem. Certain motorcycles may have been built with two issues that could compromise the sidestand's performance. Either the pivot bolt for the sidestand leg or the mounting bracket bolts could break, possibly without notice.

Risk. If either of these failures occurs, the motorcycle could fall, possibly causing death or injury.

Fix. Dealers will replace the sidestand assembly and fasteners. The recall began on july 31, 2006. Owners may contact buell at 1-414-342-4680.
