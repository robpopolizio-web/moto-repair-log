Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Accessories: 2010 · 09V465000 · 2009-12-02 · 23 bikes

Problem. Buell is recalling certain model year 2010 xb12x and xb12xt motorcycles manufactured from august 20 through september 30, 2009. The right hand side footpeg mount may have been manufactured to incorrect specifications.

Risk. This condition could allow the footpeg to move significantly out of position, which could lead to loss of control of the vehicle resulting in injury or death of the rider.

Fix. Dealers will replace the defective footpeg mount with a new footpeg mount that was manufactured to the correct specifications. This service will be performed free of charge. The safety recall began on december 14, 2009. Owners may contact buell distribution company at 1-414-343-8400.

Windscreen: 2008-2009 · 08V538000 · 2008-10-14 · 624 bikes

Problem. Buell is recalling 624 my 2008-2009 xb12xt and my 2009 xb12xp motorcycles. The windshields may become dislodged and allow the windshield to either strike or distract the rider while the vehicle is being ridden at a high speed.

Risk. This could result in a crash, which could cause injury or death to the rider.

Fix. Dealers will remove the windshield's two rubber mounted nuts and install two new fastener assemblies. The recall began on october 20, 2008. Owners may contact buell at 1-414-342-4080.

2008 Buell XB12XT · 1,000 miles · Wheels & bearings · MN · filed 2010-05 · NHTSA 10331643I am hereby filing a complaint with the nhtsa concerning a safety issue with the 2006 - 2009 buell ulysses xb12x and xb12xt motorcycles. There have been numerous reports of premature rear wheel bearing failure on the aforementioned motorcycles. Sudden and catastrophic failure of these wheel bearings can lead to severe loss of control of the motorcycle and may cause serious injury or death to riders and/or passengers. Many cases of this type of failure have been documented on websites that specialize in buell motorcycles. Listed below are a couple of links to websites that have had numerous threads posted concerning complete failure of these bearings: http://www.badweatherbikers.com/cgibin/discus/search.cgi or http://www.buellxb.com/buell-xb-forum/buell-ulysses-xb12x/ulysses-dangerous-rear-wheel-bearing-failure. The buell motorcycle company, which was closed down october 15, 2009 and was owned by harley-davidson motor company, realized the flaw in the design of the 2006 - 2009 rear wheel / bearing / axle assembly. Buell completely redesigned the assembly for the 2010 xb series motorcycles, of which only a small number were manufactured late in 2009. The new design includes larger, heavier duty bearings and also added a second bearing to the drive side of the hub for a total of three bearings in the rear wheel. The new design also added external seals to prevent dirt or moisture from reaching the actual bearing grease seal. The original design only used two inadequately sized bearings and no external seals. I believe that this bearing failure problem is clearly a safety issue and all 2006 - 2009 buell ulysses xb12x and xb12xt series motorcycles should be, in the interest of harley-davidson motor company, recalled to have the rear wheel / bearing / axle assembly replaced with the new 2010 design rear wheel and bearings. This recall should, of course, incur no cost to the customer. Thank you.
