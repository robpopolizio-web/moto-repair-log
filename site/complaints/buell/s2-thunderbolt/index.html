Recalls (9)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Rear suspension: 1995-1996 · 99V106000 · 1999-05-06 · 8,950 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. These motorcycles were built with a swingarm that could break.

Risk. If the swingarm should break, loss of vehicle control could occur.

Fix. Dealers will replace the swingarm.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Fuel tank mount: 1995-1996 · 99V104000 · 1999-05-06 · 10,556 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. These motorcycles were built with a fuel tank mount system that could, in the event of a crash, allow the fuel tank to separate from the motorcycle.

Risk. This could result in an explosion or fire.

Fix. Dealers will replace the fuel tank mount system.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Rear shock: 1995-1996 · 99V107000 · 1999-05-06 · 8,950 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. These motorcycles were built with a rear shock absorber front eye that could break.

Risk. If the shock eye should break, the underside of the vehicle will drag on the ground possibly causing loss of vehicle control.

Fix. Dealers will replace the rear shock absorber.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Fuel tank: 1995-1996 · 99V105000 · 1999-05-06 · 12,321 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. These motorcycles were built with a fuel tank vent valve assembly that could become plugged.

Risk. Under certain conditions, this could cause the carburetor to overflow fuel which could result in a fire. This condition could also prevent sufficient fuel flow, which could cause the engine to misfire or stall.

Fix. Dealers will replace the fuel tank vent valve assembly.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Throttle: 1995-1996 · 95V223000 · 1995-11-21 · 116 bikes

Problem. The throttle cable wheel of the carburetor can move on its shaft and come in contact with the carburetor support bracket.

Risk. This could prevent the throttle from returning to the idle position, increasing the potential for a vehicle accident.

Fix. Dealers will install a new throttle shaft on the affected motorcycles.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Other: 1995 · 95V153000 · 1995-08-14 · 970 bikes

Problem. The fairing mount assembly bracket can crack and detach from the frame.

Risk. If the bracket fails, the fairing could contact the front fender causing the operator to lose control of the motorcycle increasingthe potential for a vehicle accident.

Fix. Dealers will inspect the motorcycles and install an instrument panel stabilizer. Some motorcycles may already have the stabilizer installed.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Fuel system: 1995 · 95V116000 · 1995-06-06 · 1,226 bikes

Problem. Fuel seepage can occur at the mold parting line or at the area where the petcock attaches to the fuel tank.

Risk. Fuel leaks could result in a fire if an ignition source is present.

Fix. Dealers will remove and replace the fuel tanks on the involved motorcycles.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Wheel rim: 1995 · 95V083000 · 1995-04-24 · 651 bikes

Problem. Motorcycles do not conform to the requirements of fmvss no. 120, "tire selection and rims for vehicles other than passenger cars." the rims do not contain the required designation information.

Risk. Consequence of non-compliance: possibility of overloading and underinflating which could cause tire failures increasing the potential for a vehicle accident.

Fix. Owners will receive permanent, adhesive, weather-resistant rim labels with the correct designations. Owners can place the correct labels on themselves or take their motorcycles to an authorized dealer and have the dealer install the labels free of charge for them.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Brake hose or line: 1995 · 94V240000 · 1994-12-05 · 443 bikes

Problem. Front brake line assembly can crack at either of the fittings, allowing brake fluid leakage.

Risk. This can result in loss of brake system hydraulicpressure and increased braking distance.

Fix. Dealers will repair the brake line assembly.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov
