FRONT BRAKE LINE CONTACT WITH TIRE (brakes and abs): 2009 · 09V109000 · 2009-04-02 · 3,316 bikes

Problem. Buell distribution company is recalling 3,316 my 2008 1125r, my 2009 xb12r and xb12scg motorcycles. The subject motorcycles have a front brake line which may contact the front tire. The condition could cause a hole to develop in the front brake line, allowing brake fluid to leak.

Risk. This condition might cause an ineffective front brake, leading to a possible crash and potential injury or death of the rider.

Fix. Dealers will re-route the front brake line. If damage to the brake line has occurred, the brake line will be replaced free of charge. The recall is expected to begin on or before april 20, 2009. Owners may contact buell distribution company at 1-414-343-8400.

2010 Buell XB12SCG · 50 miles · Engine and cooling · IN · filed 2013-07 · NHTSA 10524751I'm reporting a design flaw, not a specific incident. This motorcycle has an under engine muffler that directs hot exhausts onto the riders left foot/lower leg when stopped. Even worse is the rider is breathing exhaust while stopped at lights/stop signs. Obviously one has to have on long pants and boots to ensure the exhaust cant burn them. Many other makes of motorcycles have gone to this design too and it should not be allowed. There needs to be an exhaust pipe that takes the exhaust to the back of the bike, just like on cars where the exhaust must come out behind the car, not under it. The muffler location is not the issue per se but the lack of an exhaust pipe coming out of the muffle to move the exhaust to the rear of the bike is the problem. Someone needs to crack down on this for all bike makers, not just buell. Btw, harley uses essentially the same engines in their bikes as buell(which was owned by hd until hd shut them down) and they put full length exhausts on their bikes so it would be an easy fix for them to put proper exhausts on all the buells they made that exhaust onto riders left foot.
