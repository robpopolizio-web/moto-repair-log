Recalls (3)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Tyre valve: 2000-2001 · 01V323000 · 2001-10-10 · 6,305 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The nuts securing the tire valve stems to the wheels can fail, resulting in a loss of tire air pressure.

Risk. If this occurs, air pressure will be lost from the wheel/tire, resulting in the possibility of loss of control.

Fix. Dealers will replace the valve nut and washer. Owner notification began october 25, 2001. Owners who take their vehicles to an authorized dealer on an agreed upon service date and do not receive the free remedy within a reasonable time should contact kawasaki at 1-949-770-0400.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Fuel tank: 2000 · 00V384000 · 2000-11-21 · 3,279 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The fuel gauge and low fuel warning system may not provide accurate indication of low fuel levels.

Risk. The operator could run out of fuel without warning, causing the operator to become distracted, or to slow unexpectedly in traffic, creating the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the fuel gauge sending components.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Fuel tank: 2000 · 00V365000 · 2000-11-14 · 4,506 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. A leaking vent pipe in the fuel tank can allow fuel to drain onto the ground.

Risk. Fuel leakage in the presence of an ignition source can result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will insert a supporting brace into the tank to dampen any effects of vibration on the vent pipe.
