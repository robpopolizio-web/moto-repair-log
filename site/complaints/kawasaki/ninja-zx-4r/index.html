Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Charging system (stator, regulator): Engine may stall: 2023 · 23V515000 · 2023-07-26 · 991 bikes

Problem. Kawasaki Motors Corp., U.S.A. (KMC) is recalling certain 2023 ZX400SPFNL motorcycles. The alternator may fail due to a loose connection between the rotor and crank shaft, causing an engine stall.

Risk. An engine stall can increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will inspect and tighten the rotor bolt, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 14, 2023. Owners may contact KMC customer service at 1-866-802-9381.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Error during installation of the alternator rotor and the alternator rotor bolt.

What the new part or software changes. Add a white mark on near the frame number.

Fixed in production. From July 2023, the proper alternator rotor installation procedure has been followed and increased tightening torque has been applied to the alternator rotor bolt during the manufacturing process.

Part made by: Kawasaki Motors Ltd., Japan

Recalls in Canada and Japan (1)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Japan: Ninja ZX-4R SE · 2024-03-27 · no. 5464 · 5,756 bikes

The spark plugs may have been damaged during the vehicle manufacturing process. It may result in unstable idle, hesitation and in the worst case, engine stalling.

2023 Kawasaki Ninja ZX-4R · 5,000 miles · Engine · UT · filed 2024-07 · fire · NHTSA 11602735The contact owned a 2023 Kawasaki ZX400. The contact stated that while driving at an undisclosed speed, cylinder three started shuddering and misfiring. As a result, flames were noticed coming from the front area, near the engine compartment, causing the vehicle to be burned down. No one was injured during the fire. A race track fire crew arrived and extinguished the fire. A police and fire report was not filed. The vehicle was towed to a local dealer, where it remains, and the cause of the fire has not been determined yet. The manufacturer was notified of the failure. 2023 Kawasaki Ninja ZX-4R · 64,000 miles · Clutch & gearbox · OR · filed 2024-07 · NHTSA 11602758The contact owns a 2023 Kawasaki ZX400 Motorcycle. The contact state while riding approximately 65 MPH and in 6th gear, the motorcycle lost motive power. In addition, while downshifting into the lower gears, the motorcycle had reduced power until 4th gear. The engine seized and the contact grabbed the clutch to slow down and pull over. The contact stated the front of the crankcase had a hole. No warning light was illuminated. The motorcycle was towed to a local dealer where it was diagnosed but the result was not available. The manufacturer was made aware of the failure. The failure mileage was approximately 64,000.
