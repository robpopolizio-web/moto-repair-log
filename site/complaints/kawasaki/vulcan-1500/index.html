Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Engine: 2003-2004 · 04V023000 · 2004-01-12 · 6,958 bikes

Problem. On certain 2003 vulcan 1500 classic, 1500 drifter, 2003-2004 1500 mean streak, 2003 1600 classic, and 2003-2004 nomad fl model motorcycles, high oil pressure upon a cold start-up can deform the oil filter cartridge.

Risk. This could cause oil to leak from the oil filter, which could result in a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the oil filter. Owner notification began on february 2, 2004. Owners should contact kawasaki at 1-949-770-0400.

Throttle: 2004 · 04V008000 · 2004-01-06 · 2,082 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, accelerator pump diaphragms may be damaged from excessive assembly clamping pressure, which can allow fuel to leak from the pump actuating rod area.

Risk. Fuel leakage in the presence of an ignition source could result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will replace the accelerator pump diaphragm. Owner notification began on january 20, 2004. Owners should contact kawasaki at 1-949-770-0400.

Carburettor: 2004 · 03V255000 · 2003-07-24 · 405 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, contaminated fuel used during the final engine inspection process may have caused deterioration of the carburetor accelerator pump diaphragm, which could lead to fuel leakage from the accelerator pump chamber.

Risk. Fuel leakage in the presence of an ignition source could result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will replace the accelerator pump diaphragm. Owner notification began on july 29, 2003. Owners should contact kawasaki at 1-866-802-9381.

Fuel hose or line: 1999 · 99V338000 · 1999-12-07 · 2,800 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The fuel hose from the fuel filter to the throttle assembly can rub against the bottom of the fuel pump plate and develop a leak.

Risk. Fuel leakage in the presence of an ignition source can result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will replace the fuel hose with a molded hose that curves away from the fuel pump plate. Also, a new bracket and grommet will be added to hold the new hose in position. The fuel return hose will also be replaced.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Fuel injection: 1999 · 99V067000 · 1999-04-06 · 2,779 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles (vn1500-j1/l) equipped with a digital fuel injection system and a vehicle-down sensor. This feature is designed to prevent the fuel injection system from continuing to pump fuel if the motorcycle falls down, by shutting off the ignition and fuel injection pump. During battery maintenance, the vehicle-down sensor can be dislodged from its normal mounted position. If the sensor is out of position, the motorcycle may not start or may stall during certain driving maneuvers.

Risk. If the sensor is not correctly installed, the engine can crank but not start or the engine could stop during certain riding situations, like leaning over in a turn. Sudden loss of engine power could cause the operator to lose their balance resulting in injury or death.

Fix. Dealers will inspect for proper mounting of the sensor and re-install if it is not in the correct position. Dealers will also affix to the motorcycle frame, a warning label that provides instructions on repositioning the vehicle-down sensor after servicing the battery.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Gearbox: 1997 · 97V233000 · 1997-12-16 · 544 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. An improperly manufactured transmission gear can cause weakening and eventual breakage of an adjacent gear.

Risk. Gear breakage can result in transmission lockup, causing the operator to lose control of the motorcycle.

Fix. Dealers will inspect these motorcycles and repair affected units.

Battery cable: 1996-1997 · 97V007000 · 1997-01-15 · 5,208 bikes

Problem. Pressure on the battery ground lead (the crimped and soldered termination of the battery cable) from the base of the operator's seat can cause the battery terminal to short out inside the battery.

Risk. This condition could disable the battery and the motorcycle's electrical system increasing the risk of a vehicle crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the existing battery cables with a newly designed cable and inspect the battery to verify that no damage has occurred.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (11)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Vulcan 1500 Classic 2003 · 2004-01-28 · no. 2004032 · 2,003 bikes · Engine

On certain motorcycles during cold starts, a delay in the response of the oil pressure relief valve can occur allowing the oil pressure to momentarily rise high enough to deform the bottom plate of the oil filter. This could cause oil to leak from the oil filter and create the potential for a crash, resulting in injury or death. Correction: Dealer will replace the oil filter with a countermeasure oil filter.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Vulcan 1500 Classic 2003 · 2004-01-20 · no. 2004018 · 1,585 bikes · Fuel System

On certain motorcycles, the accelerator pump diaphragm may be damaged by excessive assembly clamping pressure, thus allowing fuel to leak from the pump actuating rod area. Fuel leakage in the presence of an ignition source could result in a fire. Correction: Dealers will replace the accelerator pump diaphragm. Note: This recall supercedes recall 03-242.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Vulcan 1500 Mean Streak 2002 · 2003-12-16 · no. 2003335 · 747 bikes · Label

Certain motorcycles do not comply with CMVSS 123. The ignition switch fails to display the word "Ignition" as required. Correction: Since this does not pose any safety risk, no corrective action is required.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Vulcan 1500 Classic 2003 · 2003-10-14 · no. 2003242 · 1,517 bikes · Fuel System

On certain motorcycles, contaminated fuel used during final engine inspection process may have caused deterioration of the carburetor assembly pump diaphragm, which may lead to fuel leakage from the accelerator pump chamber. Gasoline in the presence of spark or flame creates the risk of a fire and possible injury or death. Correction: Dealer will replace the carburetor accelerator pump diaphragm. Note: This recall is superceded by recall 04-018.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Vulcan 1500 Classic 2002 · 2002-10-24 · no. 2002195 · 130 bikes · Powertrain

On certain motorcycles, a flange on the transmission output shaft can fracture resulting in engine damage with the possibility of engine lock-up, which poses the risk of an accident resulting in injury or death. Correction: Dealer will replace the transmission output shaft.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Vulcan 1500 2002 · 2002-05-02 · no. 2002081 · 597 bikes · Fuel System

On certain motorcycles the main wiring harness on the back of the fuel tank could be abraded by a clamp on the fuel pump leads. This could cause a malfunction of the turn signals, instrument lights, or indicator lights. Correction: Dealers will change the bending direction of the clamp to eliminate contact with the harness and secure the leads of the fuel pump.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Vulcan 1500, Vulcan 1500 Se 1987-1995 · 2001-01-26 · no. 2002153 · 4,160 bikes · Accessories

On certain motorcycles, the ribbed chrome accessory footpegs have a casting flaw which can cause the footpeg shaft to break off, leaving no support for the driver's foot. Footpeg breakage could cause the driver to lose control of the vehicle with the potential for an accident resulting in injury or death. Correction: The dealer will replace the footpegs with the original Kawasaki footpeg.

Source: Transport Canada

On certain motorcycles, the fuel hose from the fuel filter to the throttle assembly can rub against the bottom of the fuel pump plate and develop a leak. In the presence of a spark or flame, the leaking fuel could cause a fire and personal injury. Correction: Fuel hose will be replaced with a molded hose that curves away from the fuel pump plate. Also, a new bracket and grommet will be installed to hold the new hose in position. The return hose will be replaced at the same time.

Canada: Vulcan 1500 1996-1997 · 1998-04-20 · no. 1998077 · 650 bikes · Electrical

The bottom of the seat may contact the battery cable terminals. This can break off a battery terminal and cause electrical system failure and the engine to stop unexpectedly. This could cause loss of operator control and a possible crash. Correction: battery cables will be replaced, battery damper under the battery will be removed and the battery checked for damage. On certain motorcycles, an improperly manufactured transmission gear can cause weakening and eventual breakage of an adjacent gear, with which it engages. Gear breakage can result in transmission lock-up which could cause the operator to lose control of the motorcycle.
