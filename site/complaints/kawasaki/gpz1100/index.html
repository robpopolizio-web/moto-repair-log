1993 Kawasaki GPZ1100 · 25,000 miles · Clutch & gearbox · PA · filed 2005-09 · NHTSA 10137138I have a 1993 kawasaki zx-11, vin [xxx], with a 6 speed transmission and 27,824 miles on bike. I have a problem with 2nd gear, especially at slower speed and under acceleration. I can be going anywhere from 15 to 45 mph and when I accelerate the bike sputters and kicks. I had it to a dealer and they changed the sprockets and chains thinking that was the problem, but it did not fix it. It is dangerous when exiting a turn since you never know if the gear will catch. I've had a few instances when it finally catches it almost took me off the road. This is also a problem when trying to get on the gas to get away from a car, the bike can pop out of gear or the gear could skip, allowing you to be injured or killed. I also will not take a passenger now since they could be tossed off when the gear slips. I have read of other zx-11's having this problem in the past, but I've only had this bike for about 2 years and it's gotten progressively worse.
