Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Camshaft Chain Tensioner Improperly Heat-Treated (engine and cooling): 2021 · 20V774000 · 2020-12-11 · 1,770 bikes

Problem. Kawasaki Motors Corp., U.S.A. (KMC) is recalling certain 2021 Z400 ABS (ER400D), Ninja 400 (EX400H) and Ninja 400 ABS (EX400G) motorcycles. The strength of the camshaft chain tensioner may be insufficient due to improper heat treatment, possibly causing it to be damaged.

Risk. A damaged camshaft chain tensioner can cause the engine to stall, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. KMC will notify owners, and dealers will replace the camshaft chain tensioner, free of charge. Note: Owners are advised to not ride their motorcycles until the repair has been completed. The recall began December 21, 2020. Owners may contact KMC customer service at 1-866-802-9381. KMC's number for this recall is MC20-13.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Improper hear treatment of the camshaft chain tensioner.

What the new part or software changes. A white mark on the new Camshaft Chain Tensioner Body will serve as repair verification.

Fixed in production. The supplier reported to KHI that the recall condition was corrected from the delivery to KMT (engine assembly plant in Thailand) in the end of October 2020.

Part made by: Tsubakimoto Chain Co., Japan · Maker's recall no. MC20-13

How the problem was found.

  • October 2020 – Kawasaki Heavy Industries, Ltd. (KHI) received a report alleging “abnormal noise from Ninja ZX-25R engine due to worn camshaft chain tensioner” from a distributor in Japan. KHI collected failed parts to investigate the issue. No similar claims had been reported from other motorcycle products installing the same tensioner. (Ninja400, Z400, Ninja250, and Z250)
  • October 2020 – KHI inspected the worn camshaft chain tensioner and observed the wear on the plunger, where the plunger and the cam contact. KHI found that the surface hardness of plunger was out of specification. KHI asked the supplier of the tensioner to investigate the issue.
  • November 2020 - The supplier confirmed that some camshaft chain tensioners which had been delivered to KHI were not properly heat treated. KHI began investigating the scope of the issue by matching the affected units as reported by the supplier with the production lot at the production facility.
  • December 2020 – KHI could not identify similar claims on other motorcycles with the same camshaft chain tensioner, Nonetheless, KHI decided to initiate a recall campaign of all motorcycles which may have camshaft chain tensioners that weren’t properly heat treated.
