Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Brake hose or line: 1999 · 98V265000 · 1998-10-20 · 104 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The rear master cylinder and rear brake hose may have been incorrectly assembled, allowing brake fluid leakage.

Risk. Eventual pressure loss in the rear brake system can occur increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will inspect these vehicles and repair, if necessary.
