Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

KAWASAKI/REAR BRAKE ACTUATING CAM (brakes and abs): 2002 · 03V140000 · 2003-04-07 · 5,102 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the rear brake actuating cam can break where the actuating arm attaches to it.

Risk. This could cause a loss of rear brake function, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the brake cam. Owner notification began on april 24, 2003. Owners who take their vehicles to an authorized dealer on an agreed upon service date and do not receive the free remedy within a reasonable time should contact kawasaki at 1-866-802-9381.
