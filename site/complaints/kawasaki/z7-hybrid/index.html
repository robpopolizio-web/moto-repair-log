Steering and frame: Improperly welded head pipe may crack: 2024 · 24V833000 · 2024-11-05 · 299 bikes

Problem. Kawasaki Motors Corp., U.S.A (Kawasaki) is recalling certain Z7 Hybrid ABS and Ninja 7 Hybrid ABS motorcycles. The connection between the head pipe (steering head) to the frame may have been improperly welded and crack, resulting in a loss of vehicle handling and control.

Risk. Loss of vehicle handling and control increases the risk of a crash or injury.

Fix. Owners are advised not to ride their motorcycles. Kawasaki will replace the motorcycles, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 27, 2024. Owners may contact Kawasaki customer service at 1-866-802-9381.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Improper welding.

What the new part or software changes. The new units will have properly welded frames.

Fixed in production. From end of July 2024, the head pipe has been properly welded to the frame.

Part made by: Kawasaki Motors Ltd., Japan

How the problem was found.

  • ・End of July 2024: Kawasaki Motors Thailand (KMT) found that the head pipe was not properly welded to the frame on some units. The weld bead was not long enough to bind the two pieces. ・August 2024: KMT evaluated the vehicles in its current stock. Engine computer (ECU) software: ECU may improperly shift transmission to neutral: 2024 · 24V680000 · 2024-09-12 · 1,150 bikes

Problem. Kawasaki Motors Corp., U.S.A. (KMC) is recalling certain 2024 Z7 Hybrid ABS and Ninja 7 Hybrid ABS motorcycles. The electronic control unit (ECU) may cause the transmission to shift into neutral when the operator attempts to shift from first to second gear.

Risk. The unintended shift to neutral can cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will reprogram the ECM software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 27, 2024. Owners may contact KMC customer service at 1-866-802-9381. KMC's number for this recall is MC24-13.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Improper programing of transmission shift control system.

What the new part or software changes. Add a white mark on near the frame number.

Fixed in production. From 25MY model, correct ECU program will be applied.

Part made by: Kawasaki Motors Ltd., Japan · Maker's recall no. MC24-13
