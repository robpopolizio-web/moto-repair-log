The short answer

19 US owners have filed a safety complaint about the Kawasaki Vulcan 750 (model years 1993-1995, 2000-2001, 2003-2004, 2006). The most reported areas are clutch, gearbox and final drive (42%), engine and cooling (21%), electrical and instruments (16%). Half of the complaints that gave a mileage happened before 250 miles.

The bike has had 3 safety recalls. Recall work is free at any Kawasaki dealer, so check your VIN (link under each recall below).

WHEELS:HUB (wheels): 1983 · 83V087000 · 1983-09-06 · 1,301 bikes

Problem. Due to a manufacturing defect, the front wheel may develop cracks near the connection of spokes and the hub. Continued use could result in complete wheel failure.

Risk.

Fix. Dealer will inspect for the defect and replace the wheel if necessary.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

WHEELS:SINGLE:OTHER (wheels): 1982 · 81V140000 · 1981-11-18 · 11,978 bikes

Problem. The balance weights which are attached to the front wheel of the motorcycle may cause the front tire to leak air slowly. If the air pressure gets low enough, the tire may shift on the rim and deflate rapidly.

Risk.

Fix. Existing weights will be replaced with new weights that will not interfere with the sealing properties of the tire.
