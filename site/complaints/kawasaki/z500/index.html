Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Clutch lever: Clutch release lever can break: 2024 · 25V146000 · 2025-03-06 · 3,742 bikes

Problem. Kawasaki Motor Corp., U.S.A. (KMC) is recalling certain 2024 Ninja 500 (ABS, KRT Edition, KRT Edition SE ABS, SE ABS, SE 40th Anniversary Edition ABS), Z500 ABS, and Z500 SE ABS motorcycles. The clutch release shaft was not properly welded, which can cause the clutch lever to break apart.

Risk. A broken clutch release lever may not disengage, disabling the motorcycle and increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the clutch release shaft, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 24, 2025. Owners may contact KMC customer service at 1-866-802-9381. KMC's number for this recall is MC25-04.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Improper weld between the clutch release shaft and the clutch release lever.

What the new part or software changes. Make a white paint mark at the front of the frame number.

Fixed in production. In February 2024, the clutch release shaft supplier corrected the setting of the welding machine.

Part made by: New Somthai Motor Work Co., Ltd, Thailand · Maker's recall no. MC25-04

2025 Kawasaki Z500 · Other · CA · filed 2026-02 · crash · NHTSA 11715589The contact owns a 2025 Kawasaki Z500 equipped with an LS2 Helmet, Helmet Model: Advant 2, and Model Number: FF910. The contact stated that while driving 40 MPH, another vehicle failed to yield, causing the motorcycle to crash into the side of the vehicle. The contact was knocked unconscious. The contact was transported to the hospital by an ambulance. The contact stated that the modular helmet was equipped with a chin guard, and the chin guard compressed against the contact's face during the incident, causing the contact to sustain broken teeth and a fractured jaw. The contact received medical assistance. The motorcycle was towed and might be a total loss. A police report was filed. The contact had not noticed any visible fracture on the helmet. The helmet manufacturer was not contacted. The helmet was not replaced.
