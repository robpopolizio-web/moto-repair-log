Recalls (5)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix.

Electrical and instruments: 2008-2009 · 09V062000 · 2009-02-20 · 16,167 bikes

Problem. Kawasaki is recalling 16,167 my 2008-2009 klr 650 (kl650e8f/l, kl650e9f/l) motorcycles. Wires in the wiring harness might be damaged due to rubbing contact with a portion of the motorcycle frame.

Risk. This could result in loss of electrical power to the engine creating a risk of a crash resulting in injury or death.

Fix. Dealers will inspect and if necessary replace the main wiring harness free of charge. The recall began on february 24, 2009. Owners may contact kawasaki at 866-802-9381.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Engine and cooling: 2008-2009 · 09V059000 · 2009-02-19 · 16,167 bikes

Problem. Kawasaki is recalling 16,167 my 2008-2009 klr 650 (kl650e8f/l, kl650e9f/l) motorcycles. The muffler assembly is secured to the frame of the motorcycle by two bolts. It is possible that heat expansion cycles of the muffler, combined with vibration from vehicle operation may cause loosening of the muffler mounting bolts.

Risk. If the bolts loosen completely, it is possible for the muffler to separate from the motorcycle, creating a risk of a crash resulting in injury or death.

Fix. Dealers will remove the paint from the muffler mount tabs and replace the mount bolts, nut, and washer free of charge. The recall began on february 23, 2009. Owners may contact kawasaki at 866-802-9381.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Lights: 2008 · 08V520000 · 2008-10-06 · 5,804 bikes

Problem. Kawasaki is recalling 5,804 my 2008 kl650e8f and kl650e8fl (california) motorcycles. The turn signal stalk(s) can break, resulting in the signal assembly hanging by the wire harness.

Risk. This could create a possibility of a crash resulting in injury or death.

Fix. Dealers will replace all four turn signal assemblies. The recall began on october 13, 2008. Owners may contact kawasaki at 1-866-802-9381.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Engine and cooling: 2002-2008 · 07V486000 · 2007-10-16 · 33,693 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, insufficient tightening of the muffler mounting bolts could allow the bolts to loosen.

Risk. If the bolts loosen completely, it is possible for the muffler to separate from the motorcycle and create the potential for a crash.

Fix. Dealers will tighten two muffler mounting bolts to the proper torque free of charge. The recall began on october 22, 2007. Owners may contact kawasaki at 1-866-802-9381.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Frame and bodywork: 2008 · 07V244000 · 2007-06-07 · 5 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the spring retaining pin on the frame may not have been completely welded in place and could fall out, allowing the sidestand to drop into the "parking" position.

Risk. If this occurred while the vehicle was in motion, a crash could occur.

Fix. Kawasaki will repurchase these motorcycles. The recall began on june 1, 2007. All dealers/owners have been notified.
