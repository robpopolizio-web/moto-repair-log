Recalls (4)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Inner tube: 2011 · 11V313000 · 2011-06-08 · 436 bikes

Problem. Kawasaki is recalling certain model year 2011 vn900d, vulcan classic lt, motorcycles. The tire inner tubes might have been pinched during the mounting of the tires. Damage to the inner tubes could result in loss of air pressure.

Risk. Loss of tire air pressure could result in a tire failure, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace any damaged tubes free of charge. The safety recall began on june 13, 2011. Owners may contact kawasaki consumer services department at 1-866-802-9381.

Fuel cap: 2008 · 08V473000 · 2008-09-18 · 604 bikes

Problem. Kawasaki is recalling 604 my 2008 vulcan vn900c8f, vn900d8f, and my 2009 vn900b9f, and vn900b9fl motorcycles. Fuel can leak from between the tank filler neck and the fuel cap gasket.

Risk. Fuel leakage in the presence of an ignition source can result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will replace the fuel tank. The recall began on september 26, 2008. Owners may contact kawasaki at 1-949-770-0400. Note: some spare part replacement fuel tanks are involved in this campaign. The following motorcycle models may have had one of these replacement fuel tanks installed: my 2006 vn900b6f, vn900d6f, my 2007 vn900c7f, vn900d7f, my 2008 vn900b8f, vn900c8f, vn900d8f, and my 2009 vn900b9f motorcycles. Please contact your dealer if you have any questions or concerns about these replacement fuel tanks.

Fuel hose or line: 2006-2008 · 08V114000 · 2008-03-11 · 31,789 bikes

Problem. Kawasaki is recalling 31,789 vulcan 900 classic, custom and classic lt models, my 2006 vn900b6f, vn900b6fl, vn900d6f, vn900d6fl, my 2007 vn900b7f, vn900b7fl, vn900c7f, vn900c7fl, vn900d7f, vn900d7fl, my 2008 vn900b8f, vn900b8fl, vn900c8f, vn900d8f and vn900d8fl motorcycles. The fuel hose may not be properly connected to the fuel injector delivery pipe.

Risk. Continued use of the motorcycle can result in the fuel hose coming off, creasing the possibility of a fuel leak which could result in a fire or explosion.

Fix. Dealers will inspect the fuel hose connection at the fuel injector and fuel pump and will replace the fuel hose if the joint lock is found to be damaged. The recall began on march 17, 2008. Owners may contact kawasaki at 1-866-802-9381.

Ignition module: 2006 · 07V215000 · 2007-05-17 · 5,906 bikes

Problem. Certain motorcycles may stall under deceleration due to an improper setting of the engine control unit (ecu).

Risk. This could create the potential for a crash, resulting in injury or death.

Fix. Dealers will replace the ecu with one that contains revised settings that will address the engine stalling. The recall began on june 19, 2007. Owners may contact kawasaki at 1-866-802-9381.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (4)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Japan: Vulcan900 Classic · 2019 · no. 4489 · 20,460 bikes

On eligible units, the headlamp’s axis is incorrectly adjusted due to improper setting of the criteria for product acceptance of headlamp tester. As a result, direction of the headlamp’s axis may not conform to company standard.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)

Canada: Vulcan 900 Classic Lt 2011 · 2011-06-20 · no. 2011231 · 210 bikes · Wheels

On certain motorcycles, the tire inner tube(s) might have been pinched and damaged during tire mounting. Loss of air pressure could cause the rider to lose vehicle control, which may result in a crash causing property damage and/or personal injury. Correction: Dealers will inspect both inner tubes and replace them if damaged.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Vulcan 900 Classic, Vulcan 900 Classic Lt, Vulcan 900 Custom 2006-2008 · 2008-03-25 · no. 2008094 · 3,525 bikes · Fuel System

On certain motorcycles, the fuel hose may not have been securely fastened to the fuel injector delivery pipe during vehicle assembly. Fuel leakage, in the presence of an ignition source, could result in a fire, causing property damage, personal injury, or death. Correction: Dealers will inspect and, if necessary, replace the fuel hose.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Vulcan 900 Classic, Vulcan 900 Classic Lt 2006 · 2007-06-19 · no. 2007202 · 1,280 bikes · Engine

Certain motorcycles could stall under deceleration due to an improper setting on the Engine Control Unit (ECU). This could cause a crash with injury or death. Correction: Dealers will replace the ECU.

Source: Transport Canada
