Recalls (5)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix.

Engine and cooling: 2008 · 08V158000 · 2008-04-08 · 487 bikes

Problem. Kawasaki is recalling 487 my 2008 ex250j8f and ex250j8fl motorcycles. An engine crankcase vent hole in the air filter box may be blocked. If so, air pressure can build up inside the engine, causing the cylinder head cover gasket to fail and leak oil.

Risk. If enough oil leaks from the engine, it could drip or spray onto the rear tire. This can create the risk of a crash, possibly resulting in injury or death.

Fix. Dealers will inspect and repair the air filter box and replace the necessary components free of charge. The recall began on march 17, 2008. Owners may contact kawasaki at 1-866-802-9381.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Brakes and ABS: 2008 · 08V115000 · 2008-03-11 · 1,526 bikes

Problem. Kawasaki is recalling certain my 2008 ex250j8f and ex250j8fl motorcycles. The front brake hose may be damaged over a long period of use. The brake hose tends to deflect forward during suspension travel. This allows the brake hose to contact the edge of the front fork outer tube.

Risk. Over time, this contact could damage the brake hose causing the brake fluid to leak and resulting in a lack of front brake performance. This can create the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the front brake hose bracket. The recall began on march 17, 2008. Owners may contact kawasaki at 1-866-802-9381.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Lights: 2008 · 08V052000 · 2008-02-05 · 532 bikes

Problem. Kawasaki is recalling 532 my 2008 ninja ex250j8f and ex250j8fl motorcycles. The tail light bulb may prematurely fail.

Risk. Inappropriate lighting could reduce visibility creating the possibility of a crash resulting in injury or death.

Fix. Dealers will replace the tail light bulb free of charge. The recall began on february 4, 2008. Owners may contact kawasaki at 866-802-9381.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Suspension: 2007 · 07V235000 · 2007-06-11 · 155 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the top mount for the single rear shock is spot welded in position during frame manufacture, then is supposed to be completely welded to the frame in a subsequent operation. In a small number of frames, it appears this subsequent welding operation was not performed.

Risk. The welding may become loose while the motorcycle is in motion increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. All owners have been contacted and arrangements have been made to repair their motorcycles. Owners may contact kawasaki at 1-866-802-9381.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Fuel system: 2004-2007 · 07V003000 · 2007-01-03 · 29,559 bikes

Problem. On certain ninja motorcycles, the fuel tank may have been punctured or weakened by a fairing mounting screw or manufacturing operation.

Risk. Fuel leakage in the presence of an ignition source could result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will inspect the fuel tank and replace the tank if necessary. The recall began on january 11, 2007. Owners may contact kawasaki at 1-866-802-9381.
