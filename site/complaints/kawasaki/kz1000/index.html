Recalls (4)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Clutch: 1989-1991 · 91V057000 · 1991-04-11 · 1,879 bikes

Problem. A misalignment of an oil supply hole in a bushing prevents oil delivery to a portion of the clutch activating mechanism.

Risk. Inconsistent clutch operations caused by lack of oildelivery to clutch activating mechanism could cause unexpected loss of vehiclecontrol.

Fix. Replace defective needle bearing races as necessary.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Wheel: 1981-1982 · 81V140000 · 1981-11-18 · 11,978 bikes

Problem. The balance weights which are attached to the front wheel of the motorcycle may cause the front tire to leak air slowly. If the air pressure gets low enough, the tire may shift on the rim and deflate rapidly.

Risk.

Fix. Existing weights will be replaced with new weights that will not interfere with the sealing properties of the tire.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Driveshaft: 1981-1982 · 81V119000 · 1981-10-07 · 14,677 bikes

Problem. The bolt which secures the drive chain sprocket to the transmission output shaft may loosen during normal operation. If this bolt comes loose, the sprocket may separate from the shaft, resulting in lock-up of rear wheel.

Risk.

Fix. Dealer will ensure that the proper torque (58 ft. Lb.) is applied to the bolt and a system of interlocking washers will be installed to prevent the bolt from loosening. This will be done without charge to owner.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Inner tube: 1977 · 77V107000 · 1977-07-06 · 3,654 bikes

Problem. On the involved vehicles, the tubes may have been improperly vulcanized, causing either the valve stems to separate from the valve stem bases, the valve stem bases to separate from the tube, or the seams of the tubes to separate. Tube failure could occur, causing a sudden loss of air.

Risk.

Fix. Dealer will replace both front and rear tubes on all affected vehicles.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Recalls in Canada and Japan (4)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Kz1000 1989-1991 · 1991-04-24 · no. 1991068 · 6 bikes · Powertrain

Note: kz1000 p8/p9/p10 police motorcycles. Rstricted oil delivery to a portion of the clutch activating mecanism could cause erratic and inconsistent clutch operation. This could prevent the clutch from releasing when the clutch lever is pulled by the operator and may have the effect of surprising the driver who is coming to a stop, possibly resulting in an accident. Correction: outer bearing race will be inspected for proper alignment of the oil supply hole. Defective bearings will be replaced with a corrected component.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Kz1000 1975-1980 · 1986-06-20 · no. 1986069 · 125 bikes · Suspension

Handling instability can result during high speed operation due to damaged, out of specification equipment and/or the use of replacement tires not recommended for the motorcycle. Such handling problems can result in loss of vehicle control leading to accidents involving serious bodily injury or fatality. Correction: replace worn, damaged or non-conforming parts such as tires, cetain steering equipment and shock absorbers etc., and perform general maintenance procedures related to safe high speed operations. Note: police bikes only.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Kz1000 1981-1982 · 1981-12-29 · no. 1981121 · 804 bikes

Balance weights attached to the front wheel may cause the front tire to leak air. If the air pressure gets low enough the tire can separate from the rim.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Kz1000 1981-1982 · 1981-12-29 · no. 1981122 · 1,352 bikes

The bolt which secures the engine mounted drive train sprocket to the transmission output shaft may not have been sufficiently tightened. Should the bolt become loose it could allow the sprocket or drive chain to wedge in the frame and lock the rear wheel.

Source: Transport Canada
