Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Wheel cover: 1988-1989 · 88V089000 · 1988-06-01 · 1,497 bikes

Problem. Rear wheel hub damper may slip out of position and become damaged during hard acceleration, allowing excessive movement between the hub coupling and the rear wheel hub.

Risk. Damper could deteriorate completely and rear wheel hubor transmission gears may break, which could result in loss of control and anaccident.

Fix. Replace damper, if damaged; remove oil on rear hub and sprocket carrier; and install spacers in sprocket carrier to keep rear hub damper from deforming.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov
