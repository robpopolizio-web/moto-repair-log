The 5 most recent complaints, as filed (lightly reformatted).

2003 Kawasaki ZZR1200 · 7,000 miles · Engine · NY · filed 2008-02 · NHTSA 10216966This is regarding the zzr 1200 model cam pitting. At the first service interval, on 05/31/05, 12 of the 16 cam lobes were scuffed. By the 3rd service interval the cam lobes were pitted. This will wear the rockers unevenly, and cause premature failure. There are several documented incidents of this on the internet. Nothing is being done by kawasaki.
2002 Kawasaki ZZR1200 · 9,900 miles · Suspension & steering · NJ · filed 2006-11 · NHTSA 10173954Dt*: the contact stated while driving between 30 mph and 60 mph under normal road conditions, the front end of the motorcycle shook. The motorcycle was taken to the service dealer, who was not able to determine the problem. Updated 12/28/2006 - the dealer provided several repair suggestions but could not guarantee that the repairs would correct the problems.
2002 Kawasaki ZZR1200 · 3,293 miles · Engine · CA · filed 2003-10 · NHTSA 100435332002 kawasaki zzr1200. Speedometer does not hold steady. It fluctuates as much as 10 mph thereby not giving an accurate indication of speed. Dealer has replaced the cable, drive gear, speedometer; all with no improvement. According to kawasaki vice president of technical services, gary herzog, speedometer oscillation is a design characteristic for this model and that further repairs will result in no improvement.
