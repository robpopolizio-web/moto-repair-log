Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Brake light switch: Front brake switch can fail: 2018-2021 · 20V814000 · 2020-12-28 · 18,203 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain 2018-2020 MTT9GT (Tracer GT), 2019 MXTGTKL (NIKEN GT), 2017-2020 MT10, 2018-2020 XTZ12 (Super TÃ©nÃ©rÃ©), 2013-2020 FJR13, 2018 XV19B (Star Eluder), and 2018-2021 XV19 (Star Venture) motorcycles. The front brake switch can fail due to increased resistance from silicon oxide.

Risk. Failure of the front brake switch can cause the brake light to stay on constantly and/or the cruise control system to shutoff unexpectedly. Both situations increases the risk of a crash.

Fix. Yamaha will notify owners, and dealers will replace the front brake switch, free of charge. The recall began January 20, 2021. Owners may contact Yamaha customer service at 1-800-962-7926. Yamaha's number for this recall is 990140. Note: Owners are advised not to operate their motorcycle until the repairs have been performed.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. increased internal resistance caused by silicon oxide built up on the Cruse control / front brake light switch.

What the new part or software changes. New switch component has a 1mm circular mark molded onto the back of the switch to aid in identification

Fixed in production. Beginning with new production vehicles, the new switch was delivered to the switch sub-assembly area, the new switches were in turn used for new unit production.

Part made by: Pt. Indonesia Stanley Electric, Indonesia · Maker's recall no. 990140

How the problem was found.

  • In late November, Yamaha Motor Corporation USA was informed by the manufacturer, Yamaha Motor Co., LTD that such a condition exists. This was determined pursuant to quality control review, testing and a review of foreign models that use substantially similar part design.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (1)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Star Venture Tc 2018, 2020-2021 · 2020-11-25 · no. 2020572 · 4,524 bikes · Lights And Instruments

On certain motorcycles, the brake switch may not work correctly due to a manufacturing defect. As a result, the brake light could stay on. This can also cause the cruise control system not to work or could cause it to turn off. Safety Risk: A brake light that does not work properly could result in road users being confused with the rider's intentions, and increase the risk of a crash. Corrective Actions: Yamaha will notify owners by mail and instruct you to take your motorcycle to a dealer to replace the front brake switch.

Source: Transport Canada
