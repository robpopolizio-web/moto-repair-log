The non performance warning indicator illuminated prior to the failure. In addition, the contact stated that the fuel pump casing was cracked. The motorcycle was not taken to a dealer or independent mechanic for diagnostic testing or repairs. The manufacturer was contacted and did not assist. The failure mileage was 34,000.

Now I have been skipping the use of3rd gear when I ride because I am scared that it will lock up and cause me to have an accident. After researching this noise on the internet I found that their are many people out their that have the same issue. Consumers state that it is caused by 3rd gear being weak and one or more of the teeth on the gear break off. It also mentions how yamaha has since change the manufacturer of this same gear that has failed in the same xv1900 engines used in 2006,2007, and 2008 various models of yamaha. I called yamaha head quarters to see if they would help and they denied knowing of the issue or help in any way with the cost of the repair. Now I have a $14,000 dollar piece of scrap medal, because I can not afford to have it repaired since it would meet a complete removal and tear down of the engine. I do not feel safe riding my yamaha raider any more...
2011 Yamaha Raider · 900 miles · Engine · MA · filed 2012-06 · NHTSA 10461011Tl* the contact owns a 2011 yahama xv1900. The contact stated that while driving 40 mph, the vehicle stalled without warning. The contact engaged the clutched, shifted into neutral and was able to restart the vehicle after multiple attempts and resumed normal operation. The failure recurred several times. The vehicle was taken to the dealer for diagnosis but they were unable to diagnose the failure. The vehicle was not repaired. The manufacturer was notified of the issue and advised the contact that they would not be able to provide any assistance because the dealer was unable to locate a failure within the vehicle. The failure mileage was 900.

While the contact was riding his vehicle with his wife seated behind him, he struck a bump in the road. The quick release sissy bar and arm rest assembly that locks to the motorcycle became detached.
