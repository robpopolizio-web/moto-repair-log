Turn signal: 2025 · 26V367000 · 2026-06-05 · 4,173 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain 2025 XTZ690 motorcycles. The turn signal lights may fail to operate when activated. As such, these motorcycles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."

Risk. Turn signals that do not function properly may fail to indicate the rider's intention to change direction, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Owners are advised not to ride their motorycle until the remedy is complete. Yamaha will replace the meter assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed June 26, 2026. Owners may contact Yamaha's customer service at 1-800-962-7926. Yamaha's number for this recall is 990190. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning June 26, 2026.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The design of the turn signal circuitry within the Meter Assembly is inadequate, which may result in the controlling system that detects circuit breakage from functioning properly.

What the new part or software changes. The remedy component has been manufactured internal circuitry which allows the turn signal lamp(s) to operate appropriately.

Fixed in production. Units produced beginning September 2025 received a component that has been manufactured with internal circuitry which allows the turn signal lamp(s) to operate appropriately.

Fix type: Repair · Part made by: AUMOVIO Japan K.K., Japan · Maker's recall no. 990190

Brake caliper: Front brake caliper may detach: 2022 · 23V472000 · 2023-07-10 · 211 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain 2022 Tenere 700 (XTZ7N) motorcycles. The front brake calipers may have been assembled with bolts that have an incorrect thread length, which can damage the mounting hole threads in the brake caliper.

Risk. Damaged brake calipers can detach and cause a loss of braking ability, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Owners should not operate their motorcycle, other than take it to a dealer, until the remedy is performed. Dealers will inspect the length of the front brake caliper bolts and replace them, if necessary. If the caliper mounting-hole threads are damaged, the caliper will be replaced. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed July 20, 2023. Owners may contact Yamaha's customer service at 1-800-962-7926. Yamaha's number for this recall is 990168.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Vendor supplied the incorrect length (threaded portion) of front brake caliper bolts

What the new part or software changes. Threaded portion of the flange bolt is 20mm. Nominal length is 40mm and bolt is M10x1.25 with a 20mm od.

Fixed in production. after incorrect bolts were identified, stock was removed from production line and stock was purged. Vendor supplies checked and only correct dimension product has subsequently been provided. Check process has been added to vendor order fulfillment and to receiving QA inspection prior accepting into stock.

Maker's recall no. 990168

Recalls in Canada and Japan (2)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Japan: Tenere700 · 2026-05-12 · no. 5815 · 466 bikes

The design of the turn signal circuit in the speedometer was inappropriate. Because of this, the control system that detects circuit breakage may not function properly, potentially causing a false determination that the circuit is broken. As a result, the turn signals may fail to operate.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)

Japan: Tenere700 · 2024-11-05 · no. 241105_R5568.pdf · 2,769 bikes

Regarding the transmission system, since the control method for the clutch plates is not appropriate, rust-preventive oil that remains on the clutch plates is mixed with wear particles from the friction material of the friction plates. As the parts become hot while the vehicle is operated, adhesive material is produced, and there are cases where the adhesive material remains on the friction plates. Consequently, the clutch is not disengaged even if the clutch lever is pulled, and in the worst case, the vehicle may move forward while the vehicle is stopped.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)
