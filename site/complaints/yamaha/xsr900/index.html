Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix.

Throttle and cruise control: 2022-2024 · 25V248000 · 2025-04-16 · 216 bikes

Problem. Pure Sports Distributing Inc. (Yamaha of Hawaii) is recalling certain 2022 MT-09 Tracer GT, 2021-2025 MT-09 SP, 2021-2025 MT-09, 2022-2023 and 2025 MT-10 SP, 2022-2024 MT-10 and 2022-2024 XSR900 motorcycles. The brushes inside the Throttle Position Sensor (TPS) may slide excessively due to improper ECU programming, possibly causing unstable idling or an engine stall.

Risk. Unstable idling or an engine stall can lead to loss of control of the motorcycle, increasing the risk of an injury or crash.

Fix. Dealers will reprogram the engine control unit (ECU) software and replace the TPS, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on May 6, 2025. Owners may contact Yamaha of Hawaii customer service at 1-808-498-8248.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Steering: 2015-2017 · 17V221000 · 2017-03-31 · 22,433 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain 2015-2017 FJ-09, FZ-09, and XSR900 motorcycles. The lower handlebar holder may loosen due to a painting issue and the inadequate application of the thread-locking agent.

Risk. If the handlebars are not properly secured to the motorcycle, a loss of vehicle control may result, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Yamaha will notify owners, and dealers will replace the lower handlebar holder, free of charge. The recall began April 13, 2017. Owners may contact Yamaha customer service at 1-800-962-7926. Yamaha's number for this recall is 990114.

2023 Yamaha XSR900 · Engine and cooling, Fuel system · CA · filed 2025-07 · NHTSA 11673812After receiving a notification for a safety recall for a 2023 Yamaha XSR900, the recall was applied by a Yamaha dealer in Santa Maria, after the recall the motorcycle idle poorly at low temperature and stalls during acceleration, it is a very dangerous condition as it can inadvertently stop the engine during maneuvers at low speeds. Several posts in the internet complain about the same issue, the dealer says the recall was implemented correctly.
2022 Yamaha XSR900 · Electrical and instruments, Fuel system · UT · filed 2024-04 · NHTSA 11582814The vehicle started sputtering and the engine shut off in traffic, I was able to pull to the side of the road. Had I been on the interstate and not a 2 lane road I would have run a high risk of becoming an obstacle to traffic and being hit. Upon later inspection inside of the ignition starter / kill switch assembly on the right hand handlebar there was a small piece of plastic that had broken off causing the ignition circuit to become disconnected killing the engine. No warning lights came on to provide any indication of an issue. I did not take the vehicle to a dealer and opted to repair the fault myself.
2016 Yamaha XSR900 · 1,200 miles · Electrical and instruments · AZ · filed 2016-11 · NHTSA 10920618Check engine light came on after trying to start the motorcycle. It has remained on. The dealer investigated for a week. No electrical or mechanical problems were identified. The manufacturer diagnostic tool was unable to locate a problem code or clear the check engine light. The dealer opened support case with yamaha. According to yamaha the check engine light is not associated with an actual issue and is a false positive. However, they are unable to fix the problem or turn off the check engine light. Yahama has indicated that a software update will be made available, but has not committed to date. They have not issued a recall or warning. Yamaha is advising that the motorcycle is safe to drive. However, there is no way of knowing if there another problem being masked by the check engine lights staying illuminated. Based on available anecdotal information this issue has been know for months: http://www.fz09.org/forum/121-xsr900-common-problems-issues/21785-check-engine-light-vs-build-serial-number-poll.html http://www.fz09.org/forum/121-xsr900-common-problems-issues/11874-engine-light-came-xsr.html
