Brake light switch: Front brake switch can fail: 2018-2020 · 20V814000 · 2020-12-28 · 18,203 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain 2018-2020 MTT9GT (Tracer GT), 2019 MXTGTKL (NIKEN GT), 2017-2020 MT10, 2018-2020 XTZ12 (Super TÃ©nÃ©rÃ©), 2013-2020 FJR13, 2018 XV19B (Star Eluder), and 2018-2021 XV19 (Star Venture) motorcycles. The front brake switch can fail due to increased resistance from silicon oxide.

Risk. Failure of the front brake switch can cause the brake light to stay on constantly and/or the cruise control system to shutoff unexpectedly. Both situations increases the risk of a crash.

Fix. Yamaha will notify owners, and dealers will replace the front brake switch, free of charge. The recall began January 20, 2021. Owners may contact Yamaha customer service at 1-800-962-7926. Yamaha's number for this recall is 990140. Note: Owners are advised not to operate their motorcycle until the repairs have been performed.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. increased internal resistance caused by silicon oxide built up on the Cruse control / front brake light switch.

What the new part or software changes. New switch component has a 1mm circular mark molded onto the back of the switch to aid in identification

Fixed in production. Beginning with new production vehicles, the new switch was delivered to the switch sub-assembly area, the new switches were in turn used for new unit production.

Part made by: Pt. Indonesia Stanley Electric, Indonesia · Maker's recall no. 990140

How the problem was found.

  • In late November, Yamaha Motor Corporation USA was informed by the manufacturer, Yamaha Motor Co., LTD that such a condition exists. This was determined pursuant to quality control review, testing and a review of foreign models that use substantially similar part design.

Wiring: Corrosion of wiring may cause loss of control: 2012-2013 · 18V695000 · 2018-10-04 · 4,736 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain 2012-2013 Yamaha XTZ12 Super Tenere motorcycles. Over time, a gap may develop in the protective sealant where the lead wires of the accelerator position sensor (APS) and throttle position sensor (TPS) connect to the electronic control unit (ECU). This may cause corrosion on the wires, preventing the engine from returning to idle after the throttle is released.

Risk. If the engine does not return to idle when the throttle is released, it can lead to a loss of control, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Yamaha will notify owners, and dealers will bypass the wire lead connection with a new sub lead harness multi-connector, free of charge. The recall began October 26, 2018. Owners may contact Yamaha customer service at 1-800-962-7926. Yamaha's number for this recall is 990122.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. This gap can allow corrosion to form on the wire surfaces, changing electrical resistance, causing the ECU to receive incorrect signals

What the new part or software changes. New sub harness lead uses different style wire splice hat is not affected by heat.Terminal splice was changed fro SS type to s type.

Fixed in production. 2014 model redesign was undertaken and with addition of new features wire harness was changed and harmonized with European models.

Maker's recall no. 990122

How the problem was found.

  • On September 26th, 2018 Yamaha Motor Corporation USA was informed by the manufacturer, Yamaha Motor Co. LTD that such a situation exists. This was determined pursuant to a quality control review, testing and a review of foreign models that use a substantially similarly part design

Gearbox: Shift cam segment stopper may fracture: 2015 · 15V176000 · 2015-03-25 · 4,900 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain model year 2015 FJ-09 (FJ09F/FC), FZ-09 (FZ09F/FC), FZ6R (FZ6RF/FC), Super Tenere (XTZ12F/FC), Super Tenere ES (XTZ12EF/EFC), and R6 (YZF-R6) motorcycles manufactured September 1, 2014, to November 17, 2014. The affected motorcycles may have an incorrectly manufactured shift cam segment stopper, having a sharp edge on the inside of the bend instead of a smooth radius. As a result, the stopper can crack and possibly fracture at this sharp edge.

Risk. If the stopper fractures, the transmission may not shift properly, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Yamaha will notify owners, and dealers will replace the shift shaft assembly with a new one, free of charge. The recall began on April 6, 2015. Owners may contact Yamaha customer service at 1-800-962-7926.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The shift cam segment stopper on the transmission shift shaft was incorrectly manufactured with a sharp edge on the inside of its bend, rather than smooth radius

What the new part or software changes. A modified unit can also identified by the blue paint mark on the end of the shift shaft

Fixed in production. On March 13, 2015, Yamaha Motor Corporation, U.S.A. was informed by the manufacturer, Yamaha Motor Company, Ltd., that such a situation exists. This was determined pursuant to a quality control review, testing and a review of foreign models that use a substantially similarly part design.

How the problem was found.

  • On March 13, 2015, Yamaha Motor Corporation, U.S.A. was informed by the manufacturer, Yamaha Motor Company, Ltd., that such a situation exists. This was determined pursuant to a quality control review, testing and a review of foreign models that use a substantially similarly part design.

Headlight: Arcing may cause headlight failure: 2012-2013 · 14V044000 · 2014-02-10 · 24,000 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain model year 2009-2013 YZFR1 motorcycles manufactured October 2008 through August 2013 and 2012-2013 XTZ12 motorcycles manufactured October 2011 through August 2013. In the affected motorcycles, use of the headlight may generate enough heat to cause the bulb connections to expand, resulting in arcing in the headlight bulb socket which could cause the socket to overheat, melt and cause the headlight to malfunction.

Risk. A headlight failure reduces the rider's ability to see clearly and the motorcycle's visibility to oncoming traffic, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Yamaha will notify owners and dealers will replace the headlight socket, free of charge. The recall began on March 10, 2014. Owners may contact Yamaha at 1-800-962-7926.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Maker's recall no. 990084

This is a significant safety risk, as any ABS module failure should fail in such a way as to leave both front and rear brake operable. It is believed amongst Super Tenere owners that the failure occurs largely due to the Unified Braking System (a subsystem contained within the ABS module which applies rear brake when the front brake is applied).
