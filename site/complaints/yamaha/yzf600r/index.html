Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Rear suspension: 1995 · 96V033000 · 1996-02-12 · 4,348 bikes

Problem. The damper shaft of the rear shock can bend or break.

Risk. The shaft could fracture without warning causing a loss of control resulting in a vehicle crash.

Fix. Dealers will install a new style shock absorber assembly kit.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov
