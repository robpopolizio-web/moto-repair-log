Labels and stickers: Incorrect VIN on federal certification label: 2019-2021 · 21V048000 · 2021-02-03 · 8,281 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain 2019-2021 YZF-R3 motorcycles, 2019-2020 XMAX (CDZ300) scooters, and 2020 MT03 motorcycles. The Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) printed on the Federal Certification label may not match the VIN stamped on the vehicle. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of 49 CFR Part 567, "Certification."

Risk. The owner, referencing an incorrect VIN label, may not recognize their vehicle is involved in a safety recall, and may not have the recall remedy performed, which could increase the risk of injury, fire or a crash.

Fix. Yamaha will notify owners, and dealers will install a correct certification label, free of charge. The recall began February 19, 2021. Owners may contact Yamaha customer service at 1-800-962-7926. Yamaha's number for this recall is 990144.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Factory worker failed to compare all 17 digits of stamped VIN with all 17 digits of VIN reproduced on Vehicle Certification Label

What the new part or software changes. No difference in form function or fit. Only difference, on affected units, will be the VIN on the Vehicle Certification Label will match the official stamped VIN

Fixed in production. The Assembly plant implemented the following safeguards in September of 2020 1. Check 100% of all17 digits of the VIN plate before ASSY on motorcycle , assembler 2. Double check all17 digits of VIN plate at final inspection, Inspector 3. Triple check & record by camera (automatic camera system To be in place, tested and fully functional by end of 1st quarter 2021)

Maker's recall no. 990144

How the problem was found.

  • Yamaha Motor Corporation USA was informed of the potential for this issue by the manufacturer, Yamaha Motor Company LTD , that such a condition could exists. This was determined pursuant to quality control review, sample vehicle inspection and testing on October 5th 2020

Mirrors and windscreen: Decreased reflectiveness of rear reflector lens: 2019-2020 · 20V611000 · 2020-10-05 · 31,000 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain 2015-2020 YZF-R3, 2020 MT03 and 2018-2020 XMAX scooters. Condensation or other contaminates may adhere to the rear reflector lens reducing its effectiveness.

Risk. A less reflective rear reflector increases the risk of crash.

Fix. Yamaha will notify owners, and dealers will replace the rear reflector, free of charge. The recall began October 20, 2020. Owners may contact Yamaha customer service at 1-888-266-3085. Yamaha's number for this recall is 990139.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. This is due to condensation or other contaminants adhering to the lens mold that compromised the reflective properties of the lens.

What the new part or software changes. New lot production rear reflectors can be identified by production lot date markings of MC21 and later. M = production year. M=2020 and N=2021. -C = production month. B=February, C=March and D=April.

Fixed in production. New rear reflectors were delivered to the assembly area and older suspect reflector assemblies were disposed of. Additionally ultrasonic cleaning of the reflector mold was instituted along with written work instructions guiding the timing of periodic cleaning and lastly outflow parts undergo simple light distribution testing of each mold lot.

Part made by: Asahi Denso Co., LTD, Japan · Maker's recall no. 990139

How the problem was found.

  • In late September, Yamaha Motor Corporation USA was informed by the manufacturer, Yamaha Indonesia Motor Manufacturing that such a condition exists. This was determined pursuant to quality control review, testing and a review of foreign models that use substantially similar part design.

Brakes: Front brake lever may break: 2019 · 19V341000 · 2019-05-02 · 198 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain 2019 YZFR3 motorcycles. Porosities in the front brake lever may cause it to break if it is gripped with a strong force.

Risk. A broken front brake lever can affect front braking ability, increasing the risk of crash.

Fix. Yamaha will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and replace the front brake lever, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began May 15, 2019. Owners may contact Yamaha customer service at 1-800-962-7926. Yamaha's number for this recall is 990128.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. the front brake lever to have been manufactured with porosities in the material.

What the new part or software changes. Affected lots are stamped "K3 9A 16" and "K4 9A 16" these two date stamped, production lots must be replaced.

Fixed in production. Suspect production lots,stamped "K3 9A 16" and "K4 9A 16", have been removed from the production facility and destroyed

How the problem was found.

Gearbox: Shifter return spring may break: 2015-2016 · 18V860000 · 2018-12-04 · 11,240 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain 2015-2016 Yamaha YZFR3 motorcycles. The shift shaft torsion spring may fracture, giving the shifter a loose feel and affecting the ability to shift gears.

Risk. Difficulty shifting gears can cause a loss of control and increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. Yamaha will notify owners, and dealers will install a new shift shaft torsion spring, free of charge. The recall began December 17, 2018. Owners may contact Yamaha customer service at 1-800-962-7926. Yamaha's number for this recall is 990126.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. An improperly shot-peening process was used during manufacturing, this did not provide the required strengthen to the metal spring.

What the new part or software changes. New countermeasure shift shaft torsion springs has a black mark on the spring coil.

Fixed in production. New shift shaft torsion springs were used in prodiuction beginning with the 2016 model year production starting with VIN MH3RH06Y5*K012241.

Maker's recall no. 990126

How the problem was found.

  • In late November 2018 Yamaha Motor Corporation USA was informed by the manufacturer, Yamaha Indonesia Motor Manufacturing that such a situation exists. Coolant hose: Radiator hose may crack and leak coolant: 2015-2018 · 18V861000 · 2018-12-04 · 16,760 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain 2015-2018 Yamaha YZFR3 motorcycles. The upper radiator hose may crack resulting in a coolant leak.

Risk. If coolant leaks onto the rear tire, it can cause a loss of control, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Yamaha will notify owners, and dealers will install a new upper radiator hose, free of charge. The recall began December 17, 2018. Owners may contact Yamaha customer service at 1-800-962-7926. Yamaha's number for this recall is 990125.

Fixed in production. New radiator hose was used in production beginning with 2018 model year products, starting with VIN MH3RH06Y*JK017761 and MH3RH15Y9*K003881.

Labels and stickers: Incorrect VIN on label/FMVSS 567: 2017 · 17V423000 · 2017-06-30 · 40 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain 2017 Yamaha YZFR3 motorcycles. The affected motorcycles, intended for the US market, may have been manufactured with a Canadian Motor Vehicle Certification Label. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of 49 CFR Part 567, "Certification."

Risk. If the stamped vehicle identification number (VIN) does not match the VIN on the Vehicle Certification Label, owners may be unable to tell if their vehicle is under an important safety recall.

Fix. Yamaha will notify owners, and dealers will replace the Vehicle Certification Label, free of charge. The recall began July 21, 2017. Owners may contact Yamaha customer service at 1-800-962-7926. Yamaha's number for this recall is 990115.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Factory workers failed to install US specific Vehicle Certification Labels on US models

What the new part or software changes. Correct US specification Vehicle Certification Labels have US VIN numbers on them. Incorrect labels had Canadian VIN's printed on them.

Fixed in production. Visual inspection determined assembly line worker had inadvertently used the wrong country specification label during a one day production period. Work instructions have been changes so that confirmation will take place before certification label is applied.

Maker's recall no. 990115

How the problem was found.

  • On June 23, 2017, Yamaha Motor Corporation, U.S.A. was informed by the manufacturer, Yamaha Motor Company, Ltd., that such a situation exists. This was determined pursuant to a quality control review, testing and a sample vehicle inspection.

Clutch: Clutch pressure plate bearing may fail: 2015-2016 · 16V460000 · 2016-06-24 · 11,280 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation (Yamaha) is recalling certain model year 2015-2016 YZF-R3 F/G motorcycles manufactured January 1, 2015, to April 15, 2016. The clutch pressure plate bearing may break due to an insufficient load rating, causing the clutch to not disengage and preventing the transmission from shifting properly.

Risk. If the clutch cannot be disengaged, the rider may not be easily able to stop the motorcycle, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Yamaha has notified owners, and dealers will replace the clutch pressure plate bearing, free of charge. The recall began July 11, 2016. Owners may contact Yamaha customer service at 1-800-962-7926. Yamaha's number for this recall is 990105.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The clutch pressure plate bearing may break due to insufficient load rating of the bearing.

What the new part or software changes. Parts in the suspect lot are identified with the lot marking of "2MS" stamped on the pressure plate, new style parts are market with the lot "2MS2".

Fixed in production. A new design clutch pressure plate bearing is used in the clutch assembly.

Maker's recall no. 990105

How the problem was found.

Engine: Oil pump drive gear may fail; engine may sieze: 2015-2016 · 16V459000 · 2016-06-23 · 11,280 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain model year 2015-2016 Yamaha YZF-R3 F/G motorcycles manufactured January 1, 2015, to April 15, 2016. Due to an oil pump problem, the oil pressure in these motorcycles may not be properly regulated. As a result, the oil pump drive gear may fail and cause the engine to seize from lack of oil supply.

Risk. If the engine seizes from a lack of oil supply, the rear wheel may lock up suddenly, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Yamaha has notified owners, and dealers will replace the oil pump, free of charge. The recall began July 5, 2016. Owners may contact Yamaha customer service at 1-800-962-7926. Yamaha's number for this recall is 990105.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Oil pressure may not be properly regulated due to an improper oil relief valve design in the oil pump, which can excessively stress the oil pump drive gear.

What the new part or software changes. Parts in the suspect oil pump lots are part numbered "1WD-E3300-00" and the new style have a identifying punch mark near the pressure relief valve and are part numbered "1WD-E3300-09".

Fixed in production. The oil relief valve has been redesigned to correct oil flow and to maintain correct pressure.

Maker's recall no. 990105

How the problem was found.

  • On June 13, 2016, Yamaha Motor Corporation, U.S.A. was informed by the manufacturer, Yamaha Indonesia Motor Manufacturing that such a situation exists. This was determined pursuant to a quality control review, testing and a review of foreign models that use a substantially similarly part design.
