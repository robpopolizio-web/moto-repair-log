Recalls in Canada and Japan (1)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: V-Star 950 (Xvs95), V-Star 950 Tourer (Xvs95Ct) 2009-2012 · 2011-11-09 · no. 2011396 · 3,411 bikes · Fuel System

On certain motorcycles, the fuel supply hose (between the two fuel injectors) may have been manufactured incorrectly, which could allow a fuel leak to develop. Fuel leakage, in the presence of an ignition source, could result in a fire causing property damage and/or personal injury. Correction: Dealers will replace the fuel injector pipe assembly.

Source: Transport Canada
