Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Charging system (stator, regulator): Stator coil insulation may become damaged: 2008-2011 · 15V410000 · 2015-06-29 · 12,721 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain model year 2008-2011 WR250X motorcycles manufactured June 1, 2008, to June 1, 2011 and 2008-2015 WR250R motorcycles manufactured June 1, 2008, to June 1, 2015. In the affected motorcycles, the insulation on the stator coil, a component of the alternator, may become damaged and short circuit to due insufficient heat resistance.

Risk. If the stator coil is not insulated, the motorcyle will not charge and the battery will go dead, causing the engine to stall which increases the risk of a crash.

Fix. Yamaha will notify owners, and dealers will replace the stator coil with one that has sufficient heat resistance, free of charge. The recall began on July 30, 2015. Owners may contact Yamaha customer service at 1-800-962-7926.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. insufficient stator coil insulation/ poor insulation quality

What the new part or software changes. A new design stator can be identified by the white colored core.

Fixed in production. Countermeasure parts were incorporated into production vehicles starting with mid June, 2015 production vehicles.

How the problem was found.

  • On June 16, 2015, Yamaha Motor Corporation, U.S.A. was informed by the manufacturer, Yamaha Motor Company, Ltd., that such a situation exists. This was determined pursuant to a quality control review, testing and a review of models that use a substantially similarly part design.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (2)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Japan: Ｗｒ２５０Ｘ · 2016 · no. 001152121.pdf

Due to an inappropriate manufacturing of oil seals of the clutch push lever in the power transmission device, micro cracks were found in some of the said oil seals. If the clutch is operated continuously, the cracks may develop due to the hydraulic pressure inside the engine while running and that may cause the engine oil to leak. In the worst case scenario, the vehicle may collapse caused by the leaked oil adhering onto the rear tire.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)

Japan: Wr250X · 2015 · no. 001096967.pdf

Due to an insufficient heat resistance of the stator coil in the generator, the insulating material will be carburized when the temperature of the said coil becomes high, and a part of the coil may be short-circuited. For that reason, the amount of charging is reduced, and if it is used continuously, the battery will discharge and that will cause the engine to malfunction. In the worst case scenario, the engine may stop and not being able to restart.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)
