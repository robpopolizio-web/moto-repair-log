Recalls (2)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Engine: Engine case may leak oil onto rear tire: 2020-2021 · 21V806000 · 2021-10-15 · 233 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain 2020-2021 XVS95C motorcycles. The engine case may leak oil onto the rear tire.

Risk. Oil on the rear tire may cause a loss of control, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Owners are advised to not ride their motorcycles until they are repaired. Dealers will inspect and replace the engine, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 29, 2021. Owners may contact Yamaha customer service at 1-800-962-7926. Yamaha's number for this recall is 990150.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. the improper application of a mold-release agent during manufacturing caused irregularities in casting temperatures

What the new part or software changes. There is no outwardly visible difference in engine crankcases, In correctly manufactured parts, the mold temperatures were more closely maintained.

Fixed in production. Excess rerelease agent application was identified in June 2021, Work instructions were clarified additionally, to avoid porosities in the cast material during the casting process, the application and amount of release agent was changed to maintained the optimal temperature of casting mold beginning with June 2021 production

Maker's recall no. 990149

Electrical: Engine may stall due to melted wiring harness: 2014 · 13V298000 · 2013-07-08 · 3,900 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Company (Yamaha) is recalling certain model year 2014 XVS950CU (Bolt) motorcycles manufactured March 2013 through June 2013. Slack or sag in the wiring harness may allow it to contact the exhaust manifold causing the wires to melt, and possibly short circuit.

Risk. If the wires short circuit, the fuses will blow which may cause the engine to stall. An engine stall while riding, increases the risk of a crash.

Fix. Yamaha will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the wiring harness and secure it away from the exhaust. If the wiring harness is damaged, it will first be replaced. The recall began on August 2, 2013. Owners may contact Yamaha at 1-800-962-7926.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (2)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Bolt 2021 · 2021-10-15 · no. 2021633 · 147 bikes · Engine

On certain Bolt R-Spec motorcycles, engine oil could leak from the base of the engine onto the road. Safety Risk: Engine oil that leaks onto the road could cause a loss of control and increase the risk of a crash. Corrective Action: Yamaha will contact owners by mail and instruct you to take your motorcycle to a dealer to inspect the base of the engine crankcase for an oil leak. If an oil leak is found, the engine assembly will be replaced.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Bolt 2014 · 2013-07-16 · no. 2013246 · 626 bikes · Electrical

On certain motorcycles, a wire harness may have been routed too close to the rear exhaust and could be damaged due to heat. This could result in engine stalling and a loss of vehicle propulsion which, in conjunction with traffic and road conditions, and the rider's reactions, could increase the risk of a crash causing property damage and/or personal injury. Correction: Dealers will install retaining bands to position the wire harness away from the exhaust.

Source: Transport Canada
