Throttle and cruise control: 2022-2024 · 25V248000 · 2025-04-16 · 216 bikes

Problem. Pure Sports Distributing Inc. (Yamaha of Hawaii) is recalling certain 2022 MT-09 Tracer GT, 2021-2025 MT-09 SP, 2021-2025 MT-09, 2022-2023 and 2025 MT-10 SP, 2022-2024 MT-10 and 2022-2024 XSR900 motorcycles. The brushes inside the Throttle Position Sensor (TPS) may slide excessively due to improper ECU programming, possibly causing unstable idling or an engine stall.

Risk. Unstable idling or an engine stall can lead to loss of control of the motorcycle, increasing the risk of an injury or crash.

Fix. Dealers will reprogram the engine control unit (ECU) software and replace the TPS, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on May 6, 2025. Owners may contact Yamaha of Hawaii customer service at 1-808-498-8248.

2026 Yamaha MT-10 · Electrical and instruments · SC · filed 2026-06 · NHTSA 11746103Vehicle: 2026 Yamaha MT-10 I am reporting a safety-related defect involving my 2026 Yamaha MT-10 motorcycle. The motorcycle has experienced an intermittent no-crank/no-start condition. When the problem occurs, the instrument panel and electrical system power up normally, but pressing the starter button produces no engine cranking. The defect occurs unpredictably. At times the motorcycle starts normally, while at other times it will not start despite having battery power and the ignition system turned on. Because the condition is intermittent, it can be difficult to reproduce consistently. This defect creates a safety concern because the motorcycle may fail to start when needed, potentially leaving the rider stranded in an unsafe location such as a roadway, parking lot, fuel station, or remote area. Riders must be able to rely on the motorcycle starting when required. The motorcycle has been presented for diagnosis, and possible causes discussed have included components within the starter control circuit, such as the starter switch assembly, safety interlock system, wiring, relays, or related electrical components. However, the intermittent nature of the failure raises concerns about a potential defect affecting reliability and rider safety. I am submitting this complaint so the issue can be documented and investigated for possible defects affecting Yamaha MT-10 motorcycles.
