Engine computer (ECU) software and throttle position sensor: Unstable idling or engine stall from TPS failure: 2022-2024 · 25V248000 · 2025-04-16 · 216 bikes

Problem. Pure Sports Distributing Inc. (Yamaha of Hawaii) is recalling certain 2022 MT-09 Tracer GT, 2021-2025 MT-09 SP, 2021-2025 MT-09, 2022-2023 and 2025 MT-10 SP, 2022-2024 MT-10 and 2022-2024 XSR900 motorcycles. The brushes inside the Throttle Position Sensor (TPS) may slide excessively due to improper ECU programming, possibly causing unstable idling or an engine stall.

Risk. Unstable idling or an engine stall can lead to loss of control of the motorcycle, increasing the risk of an injury or crash.

Fix. Dealers will reprogram the engine control unit (ECU) software and replace the TPS, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on May 6, 2025. Owners may contact Yamaha of Hawaii customer service at 1-808-498-8248.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

What the new part or software changes. No difference. The worn TPS is being replaced with a new TPS.

Fixed in production. Programming has been corrected in production.

Fix type: Software · Part made by: Yamaha Motor Co., Ltd., Japan

How the problem was found.

  • March 2025, Pure Sports Distributing Inc. DBA: Yamaha of Hawaii was informed by the manufacturer, Yamaha Motor Co., LTD that such a condition could exist in units. This was determined pursuant to quality control review and testing. April 2025, Yamaha Motor corporation USA made the Throttle Sensor Assembly MT09 Series (TPS) part available and completed an order for all affected units sold to …

Brake light switch: Front brake switch can fail: 2017-2020 · 20V814000 · 2020-12-28 · 18,203 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain 2018-2020 MTT9GT (Tracer GT), 2019 MXTGTKL (NIKEN GT), 2017-2020 MT10, 2018-2020 XTZ12 (Super TÃ©nÃ©rÃ©), 2013-2020 FJR13, 2018 XV19B (Star Eluder), and 2018-2021 XV19 (Star Venture) motorcycles. The front brake switch can fail due to increased resistance from silicon oxide.

Risk. Failure of the front brake switch can cause the brake light to stay on constantly and/or the cruise control system to shutoff unexpectedly. Both situations increases the risk of a crash.

Fix. Yamaha will notify owners, and dealers will replace the front brake switch, free of charge. The recall began January 20, 2021. Owners may contact Yamaha customer service at 1-800-962-7926. Yamaha's number for this recall is 990140. Note: Owners are advised not to operate their motorcycle until the repairs have been performed.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. increased internal resistance caused by silicon oxide built up on the Cruse control / front brake light switch.

What the new part or software changes. New switch component has a 1mm circular mark molded onto the back of the switch to aid in identification

Fixed in production. Beginning with new production vehicles, the new switch was delivered to the switch sub-assembly area, the new switches were in turn used for new unit production.

Part made by: Pt. Indonesia Stanley Electric, Indonesia · Maker's recall no. 990140

How the problem was found.

  • In late November, Yamaha Motor Corporation USA was informed by the manufacturer, Yamaha Motor Co., LTD that such a condition exists. This was determined pursuant to quality control review, testing and a review of foreign models that use substantially similar part design.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (3)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Mt-10 2022-2025 · 2025-02-11 · no. 2025059 · 3,710 bikes · Engine

On certain motorcycles, a software problem could cause damage to the throttle position sensor. This could lead to an unstable idle, engine hesitation or the engine could stall. Note: This recall replaces recall M23-008 (Transport Canada recall no. 2023-339). Vehicles that received a software update under that recall also require this repair. Safety Risk: A sudden loss of engine power could increase the risk of a crash. Corrective Actions: Yamaha will notify owners by mail and advise you to take your motorcycle to a dealership to update the engine control unit (ECU) software and replace the throttle position sensor assembly.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Mt-10 2018-2020 · 2020-11-25 · no. 2020572 · 4,524 bikes · Lights And Instruments

On certain motorcycles, the brake switch may not work correctly due to a manufacturing defect. As a result, the brake light could stay on. This can also cause the cruise control system not to work or could cause it to turn off. Safety Risk: A brake light that does not work properly could result in road users being confused with the rider's intentions, and increase the risk of a crash. Corrective Actions: Yamaha will notify owners by mail and instruct you to take your motorcycle to a dealer to replace the front brake switch.

Source: Transport Canada

Japan: Mt-10 · 2020 · no. 001378415.pdf · 7,536 bikes

As for the brake light, since the mold release agent that is used for molding the rubber parts inside the brake light switch is not appropriate, the said rubber parts may contain silicon. Therefore, the inside of the switch may be filled with silicon gas, and silicon oxide may form on the contact point when the electric current flows by switch operations. If the vehicle continues to be used in that state, the brake light may not turn off.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)

2026 Yamaha MT-10 · Electrical & lights · SC · filed 2026-06 · NHTSA 11746103Vehicle: 2026 Yamaha MT-10 I am reporting a safety-related defect involving my 2026 Yamaha MT-10 motorcycle. The motorcycle has experienced an intermittent no-crank/no-start condition. When the problem occurs, the instrument panel and electrical system power up normally, but pressing the starter button produces no engine cranking. The defect occurs unpredictably. At times the motorcycle starts normally, while at other times it will not start despite having battery power and the ignition system turned on. Because the condition is intermittent, it can be difficult to reproduce consistently. This defect creates a safety concern because the motorcycle may fail to start when needed, potentially leaving the rider stranded in an unsafe location such as a roadway, parking lot, fuel station, or remote area. Riders must be able to rely on the motorcycle starting when required. The motorcycle has been presented for diagnosis, and possible causes discussed have included components within the starter control circuit, such as the starter switch assembly, safety interlock system, wiring, relays, or related electrical components. However, the intermittent nature of the failure raises concerns about a potential defect affecting reliability and rider safety. I am submitting this complaint so the issue can be documented and investigated for possible defects affecting Yamaha MT-10 motorcycles.
