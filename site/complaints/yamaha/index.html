MT-09 SP, MT-10 SP, TRACER 9 GT · Throttle and cruise control: 2021-2025 · 25V248000 · 2025-04-16 · 216 bikes

(Yamaha of Hawaii) is recalling certain 2022 MT-09 Tracer GT, 2021-2025 MT-09 SP, 2021-2025 MT-09, 2022-2023 and 2025 MT-10 SP, 2022-2024 MT-10 and 2022-2024 XSR900 motorcycles.
