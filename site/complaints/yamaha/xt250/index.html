Recalls (2)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix.

Fuel system: 2013-2015 · 14V754000 · 2014-11-25 · 4,776 bikes

Problem. Yamaha Motor Corporation, USA (Yamaha) is recalling certain model year 2013-2015 Yamaha XT250 motorcycles manufactured August 1, 2012, to October 1, 2014. The affected motorcycles contain an air induction pipe and hose which may fail after prolonged periods of high RPM operation. The air induction hose may melt causing hot exhaust gases to leak from the hose.

Risk. A melted air induction hose may cause the engine to stall, increasing the risk of a vehicle crash.

Fix. Yamaha will notify owners, and dealers will replace the air induction pipe and hose, free of charge. The recall began January 9, 2015. Owners may contact Yamaha customer service at 1-714-761-7300. Yamaha's number for this recall is 990091.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Brakes and ABS: 1980 · 80V068000 · 1980-05-28 · 7,000 bikes

Problem. On certain vehicles the brake linings could separate from the brake shoes due to insufficient brake lining bonding.

Risk.

Fix. Dealer will replace the original brake shoes with shoes not subject to this defect, free of charge.
