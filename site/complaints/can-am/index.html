Spyder CANYON, Spyder ORIGIN, Spyder PULSE · Dash and gauges: Instrument cluster may fail/FMVSS 122, 123: 2025-2026 · 26V575000 · 2026-09-07 · 14,014 bikes

Problem. Bombardier Recreational Products, Inc. (BRP) is recalling certain 2025-2026 Can-Am Origin, Can-Am Pulse, 2025-2027 Can-Am Canyon, 2024-2027 Can-Am Spyder RT, and Can-Am Spyder F3 motorcycles equipped with a touchscreen instrument cluster. The instrument cluster may reset, freeze, or go blank. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 123, "Motorcycle Control and Displays," and FMVSS number 122, "Motorcycle Brake Systems."

Risk. An inoperative instrument cluster will fail to display critical safety information, such as vehicle speed and warning lights, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. The instrument cluster software will be updated over-the-air (OTA) or by a dealer, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 6, 2026. Owners may contact BRP customer service at 1-888-272-9222. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning September 22, 2026. BRP's numbers for this recall are 2027-2 and 2026-3.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. See above.

What the new part or software changes. Modified September 15, 2026: New version of software: Addressed an issue where a memory leak could lead to a CarPlay freeze and gauge reset. Addressed an issue where queuing many UX interactions could lead to delayed visual updates, including speedometer refresh. Updated USB drivers on the coprocessor (H7) to the latest from the instrument cluster manufacturer, among other smaller H7 behavioral improvements. Addressed an issue where the loss of Bluetooth could lead to unavailability until the next vehicle boot by adding detection and recovery logic.

Fixed in production. Vehicles currently in production and those scheduled for delivery from the manufacturing plants will be updated with the revised software version prior to distribution.

Fix type: Software OTA · Part made by: Enovation Controls, LLC, United States · Maker's recall no. 2027-2 & 2026-3

  • In the summer of 2024, BRP investigated complaints regarding cluster intermittent functionality. BRP could not identified common root causes at that time

Root causes were identified: depending on driver usage of USB and Bluetooth connections, a communication failure or memory leak may occur within the instrument cluster. Spyder ORIGIN · Brake caliper: Front brake failure: 2025 · 26V220000 · 2026-04-07 · 398 bikes

Problem. Bombardier Recreational Products, Inc. (BRP) is recalling certain 2025 Can-Am Origin electric motorcycles. A fatigue failure of the front brake caliper support may occur and result in inoperative front brakes.

Risk. Front brake failure increases the risk of crash or injury.

Fix. BRP will install a caliper support reinforcement kit, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 17, 2026. Owners may contact BRP customer service at 1-888-272-9222. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov April 28, 2025.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The investigation determined that an error in the input data used for BRP’s finite element analysis (FEA) led to an unintended fatigue failure risk in the front brake caliper support. As a result, repeated hard braking could cause failure at a lower threshold than originally intended. This is a fatiguerelated failure caused by repeated hard and emergency braking. Using onroad tires accelerates the condition. From the factory, the vehicle was equipped with dual purpose tires. The owner may have installed other tires, including on-road tires. Those on-road tires may increase the potential of hard braking therefore accelerate the fatigue process.

What the new part or software changes. The Front Brake Caliper Support will now have a reinforcement kit.

Fixed in production. In the current production, the vehicles will have the same repair as per the recall. The production will resume once the required parts are available.

Fix type: Repair · Part made by: Kyb Manufacturing Taiwan Co., Ltd., Taiwan, Province Of China · Maker's recall no. Bulletin 2026-1

How the problem was found.

  • During ABS calibration testing conducted at a supplier on a MY2027 motorcycle equipped with highergrip tires, two front brake caliper support failures were reported on January 19 and 29, 2026. No accidents or injuries resulted from these events. Following receipt of the reports, BRP initiated an investigation. Spyder CANYON · Sprocket or pulley: Loss of drive power: 2025 · 25V448000 · 2025-07-03 · 843 bikes

Problem. Bombardier Recreational Products, Inc. (BRP) is recalling certain 2025 Can-Am Canyon motorcycles. On these motorcycles, the drive belt lower idler support will fail and possibly cause a belt failure.

Risk. Belt failure can result in a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will install a new drive belt lower Idler support, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed August 15, 2025. Owners may contact BRP customer service at 1-888-272-9222. The manufacturer number for this recall is Bulletin 2025-4.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The wrong material was identified on a revised drawing sent to the supplier. The actual material does not have sufficient strength to sustain peak loads seen by the support while downshifting.

What the new part or software changes. This part has a different design and material. The material used is the one that was contemplated from the start of production. With the new design, in case of a support failure, the pulley will still be held in place, avoiding getting loose and damaging the drive.

Fixed in production. When future model years will be produced, the same remedy will be used.

Fix type: Repair · Part made by: Matritech, Canada · Maker's recall no. Bulletin 2025-4

How the problem was found.

  • In May 2025, BRP received 3 cases of Drive Belt Lower Idler Support failures including one loss of propulsion. BRP started its analysis to understand the root causes of the failure. It was found that after a failure of the support in physical testing a decision was made to change the part material from the start of production. However, the drawing revision failed to reflect this change and …
  • In June 2025, BRP received 2 additional cases. With all the information collected and analyzed, BRP decided on June 27, 2025, that it had enough information to report and wants to proceed with a safety recall on the identified population of vehicles. BRP has 5 reported failure cases worldwide (4 in the US) as of June 27, 2025 but no report of accident nor injury.

Spyder ORIGIN, Spyder PULSE · Drive battery (electric bike): Water may enter high voltage battery: 2025 · 25V275000 · 2025-04-24 · 478 bikes

Problem. Bombardier Recreational Products (BRP) is recalling certain 2025 Can-Am Origin and Pulse electric motorcycles. The breather valve may not seal properly and allow moisture or water to enter the high voltage battery.

Risk. Water in the battery can result in a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash. In addition, this could lead to a battery fire when parked or riding, increasing the risk of injury.

Fix. Dealers will install a new protection plate or replace the breather valve if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 2, 2025. Owners may contact BRP customer service at 1-888-272-9222.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The battery housing in which the breather valve is inserted may be out of specifications and be too thick. If this the case, the breather valve could be not properly clipped.

What the new part or software changes. The new protective plate that will replace the actual protective plate has an integrated grommet to compress the breather valve O-ring.

Fixed in production. On March 20, 2025, BRP issued a stop shipment. All vehicles in its possession already produced and produce have the same protective plate as in the remedy.

Fix type: Replace · Part made by: Jebco Manufacturing Inc., Canada · Maker's recall no. Bulletin 2025-3

  • On March 18, 2025, an improperly clipped breather valve was detected during qualification tests by BRP Canada in Valcourt on pre-production high-voltage batteries from the Queretaro plant of BRP Mexico.
  • On March 19, 2025, a product line backtrack was requested on the assembly line in Mexico to look at the breather valve installation. On March 20, 2025, BRP issued a stop shipment to dealers from the plant or its distribution centers while it started its analysis.
