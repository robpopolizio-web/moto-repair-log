Recalls in Canada and Japan (4)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Japan: Can-Am RYKER RALLY · 2024 · no. 001740069.pdf · 73 bikes

Due to improper raw material is used, the impeller in the fuel pump may swell. This will cause the fuel supply from fuel tank to the engine to stop and cause the engine to stall.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)

Japan: Can-Am RYKER RALLY · 2023 · no. 001621084.pdf · 656 bikes

Currently, the 2 side rear indicator lights on Ryker will flash automatically when vehicle is reversing. However, use of the rear indicator lights that automatically flash conflicts with MLIT Details about “other light usage”. To follow an instruction from MLIT, BRP will release a recall on Ryker with solutions below to correct this non-compliance status of rear indicator light use. A light setting for Japan market will be released to make sure 2 side rear indicator lights will be completely turned off while the vehicle is reversing.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)

Japan: Can-Am RYKER RALLY · 2023 · no. 001595716.pdf · 656 bikes

The harness located in the front fenders may be loose due to the locking clip was not installed properly at production line. As a result, the harness may rub on the tires, damage the covering of the harness, and cause a short circuit. If this happens, the engine will stop and vehicle stability systems including the anti-lock braking system (ABS) also becomes inoperative. This condition may increase the risk of a crash.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)

Japan: Can-Am RYKER RALLY · 2022 · no. 001580377.pdf · 134 bikes

The handlebar stem bolt may break suddenly. This could lead to a loss of steering control and increase the risk of a crash.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)
