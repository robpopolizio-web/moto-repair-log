The short answer

40 US owners have filed a safety complaint about the Can-Am Spyder F3 (model years 2013, 2015-2024). The most reported areas are clutch, gearbox and final drive (45%), fuel system (18%), brakes and abs (15%). Half of the complaints that gave a mileage happened before 7,000 miles.

The bike has had 5 safety recalls. Recall work is free at any Can-Am dealer, so check your VIN (link under each recall below).

Instrument Cluster May Fail/FMVSS 122, 123 (electrical and instruments): 2024-2026 · 26V575000 · 2026-09-07 · 14,014 bikes

Problem. Bombardier Recreational Products, Inc. (BRP) is recalling certain 2025-2026 Can-Am Origin, Can-Am Pulse, 2025-2027 Can-Am Canyon, 2024-2027 Can-Am Spyder RT, and Can-Am Spyder F3 motorcycles equipped with a touchscreen instrument cluster. The instrument cluster may reset, freeze, or go blank. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 123, "Motorcycle Control and Displays," and FMVSS number 122, "Motorcycle Brake Systems."

Risk. An inoperative instrument cluster will fail to display critical safety information, such as vehicle speed and warning lights, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. The instrument cluster software will be updated over-the-air (OTA) or by a dealer, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 6, 2026. Owners may contact BRP customer service at 1-888-272-9222. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning September 22, 2026. BRP's numbers for this recall are 2027-2 and 2026-3.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. See above.

What the new part or software changes. Modified September 15, 2026: New version of software: Addressed an issue where a memory leak could lead to a CarPlay freeze and gauge reset. Addressed an issue where queuing many UX interactions could lead to delayed visual updates, including speedometer refresh. Updated USB drivers on the coprocessor (H7) to the latest from the instrument cluster manufacturer, among other smaller H7 behavioral improvements. Addressed an issue where the loss of Bluetooth could lead to unavailability until the next vehicle boot by adding detection and recovery logic.

Fixed in production. Vehicles currently in production and those scheduled for delivery from the manufacturing plants will be updated with the revised software version prior to distribution.

Fix type: Software OTA · Part made by: Enovation Controls, LLC, United States · Maker's recall no. 2027-2 & 2026-3

  • In the summer of 2024, BRP investigated complaints regarding cluster intermittent functionality. BRP could not identified common root causes at that time

Engine Output Shaft May Break (clutch, gearbox and final drive): 2017-2019 · 24V728000 · 2024-09-30 · 8,721 bikes

Problem. Bombardier Recreational Products (BRP) is recalling certain 2017-2019 Can-Am Spyder F3 and RT motorcycles. The engine output shaft may fatigue and break, resulting in a loss of drive power.

Risk. A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.

Fix. The output shaft will be replaced based on the mileage of the vehicles. Owner notification letters were mailed February 5, 2025. Owners may contact BRP customer service at 1-888-272-9222.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The potential cause is a change in the bearing fixation method which was implemented during the MY17 production combined with the use of the previous design of the output shaft which was used until MY2019 on some vehicles produced in that model year.

What the new part or software changes. Update January 22, 2025: The output shaft used for the remedy has a longer chamfer radius and is the same component used since MY2019 for our production (except the limited quantity of MY2019 vehicles included in the recalled population.)

Fixed in production. Following BRP’s analysis, the vehicles which have the latest design of the output shaft have sufficient fatigue strength. A specific machining radius was increased at that time. Those vehicles were manufactured after January 30, 2019.

Part made by: BRP-Rotax GmbH & Co KG, Austria

  • US-NHTSA contacted BRP following the reception of one VOQ related to broken engine output shaft in June 2024. In July, NHTSA provided potential other cases collected via social media by an owner and in August, another VOQ was also received. Problem. Bombardier Recreational Products Inc. (BRP) is recalling certain 2022 Cam-Am Spyder F3 and F3-S motorcycles. The rearview mirror lens may detach from the housing.

Risk. A detached rearview mirror lens can decrease rear visibility for the driver, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will install new mirror assemblies, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 9, 2023. Owners may contact BRP customer service at 1-888-272-9222.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. BRP found that there was a high probability that the issue was due to a wrong plastic injection control for the mirror housings by its supplier for a batch molded in April 2022. What the new part or software changes. The mirror housing assemblies will have a mirror housing from a certified batch from the supplier with adequate molding parameters. A change in the mold to increase the radius at the pin base was also made in abundance of caution.

Fixed in production. Vehicles manufactured after the cut-off date had already mirror housings molded from a later batch.

Defective Front Sprocket (clutch, gearbox and final drive): 2015-2019 · 22V352000 · 2022-05-18 · 30,389 bikes

Problem. Bombardier Recreational Products, Inc. (BRP) is recalling certain 2015-2019 Can-Am Spyder F3 and RT motorcycles. The front sprocket may be defective and prematurely wear, resulting in a loss of drive power.

Risk. A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will perform an interim repair until parts become available. Once parts are available, the front sprocket will be replaced, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 26, 2022. Owners may contact BRP customer service at 1-888-272-9222.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The front sprocket chamfer should start from the minor spline diameter, but the T2 supplier used the major spline diameter to build the tooling. The chamfer end was then shifted outward by spline teeth height. This has an effect on the clamping load.

What the new part or software changes. The remedy component will be adequately manufactured in the chamfer area. Once the replacement procedure is published, we may add more details to this section, as necessary.

Fixed in production. Adequately manufactured parts were received for the vehicles produced starting during the day of April 15, 2019.

Part made by: Nutech, Canada · Maker's recall no. TBD

  • Transport Canada opened an Issue Assessment in August 2021 and informed BRP. Transport Canada decided to escalate this assessment into a Preliminary Evaluation (3280-09-28) in January 2022.
