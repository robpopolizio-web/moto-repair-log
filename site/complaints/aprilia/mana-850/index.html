Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix.

Brakes and ABS: 2013-2014 · 19V561000 · 2019-07-26 · 1,701 bikes

Problem. Piaggio Group Americas. Inc. (Piaggio) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Moto Guzzi Audace 1400 and Eldorado 1400, 2017-2018 Moto Guzzi California 1400, 2017 Moto Guzzi MGX 21 Bagger, 2013-2014 Aprilia Mana 850, 2014-2016 Aprilia Dorsoduro 750, 2018-2019 Aprilia Dorsoduro 900, and 2018 Aprilia Shiver 900 motorcycles. Due to a problem with the front brake master cylinder, the front brakes may drag or apply, causing the motorcycle to unexpectedly slow down or stop completely without activating the rear brake light.

Risk. Unexpectedly slowing down or stopping while driving with no warning to following traffic can increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. Piaggio will notify owners, and dealers will inspect, and if necessary, replace the front brake master cylinder free of charge. The recall began August 16, 2019. Owners may contact Piaggio customer service at 1-212-380-4433. Piaggio's number for this recall is PA2ZZQ1904.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

2012 Aprilia MANA 850 · 15,000 miles · Wheels · CA · filed 2019-12 · NHTSA 11291688I was traveling down the freeway on my motorcycle at approximately 60 mph when all of a sudden the bike became very difficult to control and keep in the lane. I was in a high wind area and thought it must be that because the tires were brand new and had less than 100 miles on them. About 2 miles later it felt like I hit something metal though there was nothing in the lane and a few seconds later it felt like I hit something again, which I realize now was probably the tire coming off the rim. A few seconds after that I was on the final curve leading up to the benicia martinez bridge and I heard an audible bang from my left side and felt pressure against my left leg and the bike was forced into the left lane and into traffic who narrowly avoided me. I was able to regain control of the bike and pull it off to the right side of the road on the lead up to the benicia martinez bridge. The tire was completely flat and off the rim. Prior to the blowout I had not hit anything and the bike was operating normally. I was about 30 minutes into my trip. The tires were both brand new and had been mounted and balanced by a motorcycle shop that is well regarded in the area.
