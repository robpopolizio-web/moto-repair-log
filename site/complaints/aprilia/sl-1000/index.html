Engine: 2000 · 00V272000 · 2000-09-14 · 382 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles with half-fairing. The oil hose could fail on these motorcycles, causing the oil to spill under the rear wheel.

Risk. This could result in a loss of control of the motorcycle.

Fix. Dealers will replace the oil hose.

What owners wrote

The 5 most recent complaints, as filed (lightly reformatted).

2001 Aprilia SL 1000 · 9,000 miles · Clutch & gearbox, Engine, Fuel system · VT · filed 2015-05 · NHTSA 10721279The gas tank of my 2001 aprilia falco began to leak very quickly onto the engine and some electrical components. I did not drive. I brought into a dealership and after investigating the dealership advised that the gas tank had warped and the seal between gas tank and fuel line was no longer viable. According to the dealership this is a relatively common problem with aprilia & ducati due to the composite gas tanks and ethanol fuel used in the us, causing a weakening of the composites and fuel leakage.
2002 Aprilia SL 1000 · 11,000 miles · Fuel system · WA · filed 2014-06 · NHTSA 10596652Gas tank leaking fuel from fuel pump mounting plate due to plastic fuel tank deformation. Deformation reportedly caused by ethanol in gasoline. Volume of gasoline leaked was sufficient to cause a considerable fire. Fuel tank was drained to prevent fire. New fuel tank installed but subject to the same failure. No solution offered by the manufacturer.
2000 Aprilia SL 1000 · 11,900 miles · Fuel system · NY · filed 2008-08 · NHTSA 10237900Properly maintained vehicle yet it developed a fuel leak for no apparent known reason except perhaps a flaw in engineering. It is leaking from the o-ring gasket between the fuel tank and the fuel pump flange due to the plastic tank distorting over time from normal use. Piaggio/aprilia offer no fix nor do they acknowledge a problem with the design.
2003 Aprilia SL 1000 · 8,300 miles · Brakes & ABS · NV · filed 2006-05 · crash · NHTSA 10156840Dt*: the contact stated while driving 30 mph the brakes failed causing a crash. The motorcycle was driven to the dealer where it was determined the brake piston was cracked. The rider was injured. The motorcycle is a 2003 aprilia sl falco 1000.
2001 Aprilia SL 1000 · Fuel system · NY · filed 2004-12 · NHTSA 101047822001 sl 1000 was parked in garage - unmoving. Large fuel spill occured without warning. Fuel leak at fuel pump mounting plate and and plastic fuel tank junction. Replaced existing o-ring seal; new o-ring unable to seal large gap created by warped plastic gas tank. Fuel leak due to tank warping along lower edge between fuel pump mounting plate screws.
