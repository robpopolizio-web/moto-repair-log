Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix.

Engine and cooling: 2021 · 21V502000 · 2021-07-01 · 308 bikes

Problem. Aprilia is recalling certain 2021 RS 660 and Tuono 660 motorcycles. The engine crankshaft connecting rod may break due to improper heat-treatment.

Risk. A broken connecting rod can cause a sudden engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the engine, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on July 9, 2021. Owners may contact Aprilia customer service at 1-212-380-4400. Aprilia's number for this recall is PA2ZZQ2107.
