Brake master cylinder: Front brake master cylinder may let in air: 2015 · 16V691000 · 2016-09-26 · 1,628 bikes

Problem. Piaggio Group Americas, Inc. (Piaggio) is recalling certain model year 2014-2015 Aprilia Tuono V4 1000, 2016 Tuono V4 1100, 2015 Caponord 1200 ABS/ADD, and 2016 Caponord Rally motorcycles manufactured June 18, 2013, to June 13, 2016. The affected motorcycles have a front brake master cylinder that may allow air into the hydraulic circuit, causing a longer than expected braking distance.

Risk. If an abnormal distance is required to stop the motorcycle, there is an increased risk of a crash.

Fix. Aprilia will notify owners, and dealers will replace the front brake master cylinder pushrod and boot assembly, free of charge. The recall began October 04, 2016. Owners may contact Aprilia customer service at 1-866-705-1727.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Internal parts of the brake pump (actuating rod and cap) could under certain conditions allow air into the circuit

What the new part or software changes. Old Part number B044605 Push pin and rubber cap in two pieces. New Part number 2B003807 Push pin and rubber cap in one piece with modified profile of the actuating rod.

Fixed in production. All new production for this model will have the updated components PN 2B003807. The production remedy is identical to the recall.

Part made by: Brembo S.p.A., Italy

Gearbox: Sprocket may loosen and the rear wheel may lock up: 2015 · 15V007000 · 2015-01-09 · 337 bikes

Problem. Piaggio Group Americas, Inc. (Piaggio) is recalling certain model year 2014-2015 Aprilia Shiver 750 motorcycles manufactured February 10, 2014, to October 28, 2014 and 2015 Aprilia Caponord 1200 motorcycles manufactured November 14, 2013, to October 16, 2014. Due to improper machining of the output gear shaft face, the fastening screw on the sprocket may loosen and the rear wheel may lock up.

Risk. A rear wheel lock up increases the risk of a crash.

Fix. Piaggio will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the output shaft, replacing any that are found to be defective, free of charge. The recall began on January 28, 2015. Owners may contact Piaggio customer service at 1-212-380-4433.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Non-conformity of the supplier

What the new part or software changes. All defective output shafts found will be replaced with a properly manufactured component that has been quality inspected by Aprilia.

Fixed in production. All new production of this model will have this component that has been properly manufactured and quality inspected by Aprilia.

Part made by: Chongqing Qiutian Gear Co.,Ltd, China

How the problem was found.

  • There have not been any reported cases worldwide that have resulted in accidents injuries or fatalities. There have been no reports of failures in the US and no warranty claims have been entered for the affected part as of the date of this report.
