FUEL PUMP/FUEL FILTER/BITRON SPA (fuel system): 2006-2008 · 08V522000 · 2008-10-07 · 850 bikes

Problem. Piaggio is recalling 850 my 2006-2008 aprilia scarabeo 500 motorcycles equipped with bitron spa fuel pump/fuel filters. The fuel hose connecting the fuel filter to the fuel pump/fuel filter mounting flange may come loose. If this connection is loose, or has become completely disconnected, there will be a drop, or loss of, fuel pressure to the engine.

Risk. A drop or loss of fuel pressure to the engine could cause the engine to stall increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will install hose clamps to secure the existing fuel hose at both ends to the fuel pump and fuel filter. The recall is expected to begin during march 2009. Owners may contact piaggio/aprilia at 1-212-380-4431.

2006 Aprilia Scarabeo 500 · 100 miles · Fuel system · VA · filed 2008-08 · NHTSA 10236721This report pertains to a 2006 aprilia scarabeo 500 motorcycle. I bought the motorcycle new in june 2008. It is common knowledge among scarabeo owners that the manufacturer sold these bikes with clamps missing from the in-tank fuel line, between the in-tank filter and the fuel pump. My dealer assured me that this issue had been resolved on my bike before I took delivery of it. Within days of taking delivery of the bike, the engine was idling roughly, stalling at stop lights and running roughly when operated at slow speeds, below 35 mph. On several occasions, I applied power to start off from an intersection and the bike stalled. These were all symptoms of the fuel line clamp issue, as reported by many scarabeo 500 owners. I fixed the problem myself because I do not live near an aprilia dealer. Upon removing the in-tank fuel pump, I immediately noticed that there were no clamps on either end of the fuel line. I installed the clamps, as well as replaced the fuel line with line approved for submerging in fuel (sae30r10), as it was unclear as to the suitability of the stock fuel line for submerged use. Since the fix I have not had any recurrence of the problems. This is a safety issue and the manufacturer should recall all scarabeos and have them checked.
