What owners wrote

The 4 most recent complaints, as filed (lightly reformatted).

2004 Aprilia Scarabeo 150 · 3,000 miles · Fuel system · OH · filed 2008-07 · NHTSA 10235536An in-tank fuel line between the fuel pump and filter is missing hose clamps causing fuel pressure lose and erratic engine performance. Stalling and sputtering during maneuvers in traffic can be being very dangerous on a motorcycle or any two wheel vehicle. This problem effects 2004 through 2008 aprilia scarabeo 500ie and earlier year models. Thus far dealers have corrected this under warranty on a by complaint bases. Most of these failures have occurred in the first 1000 miles. An obvious design flaw. I feel the manufacture should have a recall on this problem.
2005 Aprilia Scarabeo 150 · Fuel system · GA · filed 2008-07 · NHTSA 10235509There is a well-known issue with aprilia scooters, specifically, their scarabeo model, wherein clamps on the fuel pump (located in the gas tank) are missing. This has caused on my scooter two effects, mainly. First and foremost is occasional stalling while idling. The concern here (although it hasn't happened yet) is that my scooter will stall at a very inopportune moment, such as while trying to complete a left turn, and stall in the middle of oncoming lanes. The second issue is rough running from idle through acceleration up to about 30 mph.
2003 Aprilia Scarabeo 150 · Fuel system · CA · filed 2005-09 · crash · NHTSA 10137519Possible carburetor slide breaking and piece(s) going into engine, causing engine lock up, causing rear wheel lock up, causing crash.
2001 Aprilia Scarabeo 150 · 8,900 miles · Fuel system · MA · filed 2005-09 · NHTSA 101372482001 aprilia scarabeo 150 cc motorscooter, the throttle slide will suddenly break leaving the rider w/o any power to extracate himself from traffic. The rider is suddenly exposed to automobiles traviling at medium speed with no way to get out of automobilist way. This certainly places the scooter rider in a most dangerous situation. I think there should be a recall to replace these defective throttle slides.
