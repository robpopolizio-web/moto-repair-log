F3 800, F3 800 RC · Front suspension (forks): Fork axle carrier defect may cause loss of control: 2018 · 18V458000 · 2018-07-10 · 22 bikes

Problem. MV Agusta USA, LLC (MV Agusta) is recalling certain 2018 MV Agusta Brutale 800 Dragster RC, F3 800RC, and F3 800 motorcycles. These vehicles are equipped with a fork axle carrier that may have damaged threads which can cause excessive play of the wheel clamping screw.

Risk. Wheel clamping screws that experience excessive play can cause a loss of control, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. MV Agusta will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the threaded holes in the fork axle carriers, and if necessary replace the fork leg, free of charge. Owners are advised to not ride their motorcycles until the repair has been performed. The recall began July 20, 2018. Owners may contact MV Agusta customer service at 1-215-781-1770. MV Agusta's number for this recall is 18RC02.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. A defective batch of fork axle carriers have damaged threads in the holes where clamping screws are fixed. This defect has the same effect as if threaded axle carrier holes are of larger diameter compared to design specifications, hence causing the clamping screw excessive play.

What the new part or software changes. The replacement component is marked with a green painted dot in proximity of the hole where the wheel clamping screw is fitted. Depending on the model and market worldwide, the following part numbers apply: Right fork leg , Left fork leg 8000C3202 , 8000C3203 8000C6386 , 8000C6385 8000C0604 , 8000C0605 8000C1190 , 8000C1189 8000B7130 , 8A00B7131 8000B8740 , 8A00B8739 80A0C1190 , 80A0C1189

Fixed in production. The defective condition was remedied in production upon noticing the problem. Defective parts were rectified using the helicoil technique, while all new batches arrived with parts conforming to design specifications. Compliant and rectified parts are marked with a green painted dot in proximity of the hole where the wheel clamping screw is fitted.

Part made by: VRM SpA, Italy · Maker's recall no. 18RC02

BRUTALE 800 DRAGSTER RR · Wheel rim: Rear wheel spokes may loosen: 2017 · 17V772000 · 2017-12-01 · 45 bikes

Problem. MV Agusta USA, LLC (MV Agusta) is recalling certain 2017 Brutale 800 Dragster RR motorcycles. The spokes on the rear wheel may loosen, potentially affecting the motorcycle's handling.

Risk. If the handling is compromised from the loose spokes on the rear wheel, there would be an increased risk of a crash.

Fix. MV Agusta will notify owners, and dealers will replace the rear wheels, free of charge. The recall began on December 13, 2017. Owners may contact MV Agusta customer service at 1-215-781-1770. MV Agusta's number for this recall is 2813.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The superficial metal treatment of the nipples to where the rear wheel rim spokes are attached is not to specification. The designated tightening torque required to fix the spokes is therefore insufficient and may cause the spokes to become loose or free.

What the new part or software changes. The replacement component is marked with a green painted dot on one of the nipples located in proximity of the inflating valve. Name/description of defective component: rear wheel rim. Part numbers depending on colour: 80C0C0046 (black rim with yellow hub/nipples), 80A0C0046 (black rim with red hub/nipples), 8000C0046 (white rim with red hub/nipples).

Fixed in production. The defective condition was remedied in production upon noticing the problem. The spoke tightening torque was corrected and the remedied components marked with a painted green dot on one of the nipples located in proximity of the inflating valve and/or the marking “R17” (Recall 2017) on the hub to show that it has been remedied.

Part made by: In-Motion Group srl, Italy · Maker's recall no. 2813

BRUTALE 675, BRUTALE 800, BRUTALE RR, F3 675, F3 800, RIVALE 800 · Frame: Swingarm pin securing bolt failure: 2014 · 14V189000 · 2014-04-10 · 223 bikes

Problem. MV Agusta USA, LLC (MV Agusta) is recalling certain model year 2014 Brutale RR (Corsa), Rivale 800, Brutale 800 Dragster, Brutale 800, Brutale 675, F3 800, and F3 675 motorcycles manufactured December 18, 2013, through March 10, 2014. In the affected motorcycles, the threaded bolt used to secure the swingarm pin may fail.

Risk. A failure of the bolt that secures the swingarm pin may allow the swingarm to loosen while riding, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. MV Agusta has notified owners and dealers will install a new threaded bolt, free of charge. The recall began on April 25, 2014. Owners may contact MV Agusta at 1-215-781-1770. MV Agusta's number for this recall is 2798.
