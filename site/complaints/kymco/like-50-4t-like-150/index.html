ABS wheel sensor: ABS magnetic sensor ring may wear prematurely: 2018-2019 · 20V350000 · 2020-06-12 · 1,786 bikes

Problem. KYMCO USA (KYMCO) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Like 150i scooters. Excessive grease applied to the ABS sensor magnetic ring may cause the deterioration of the magnetic ring, resulting in ABS failure.

Risk. Without ABS, controlling the scooter in an emergency stopping scenario may be affected, increasing risk of a crash.

Fix. KYMCO will notify owners, and dealers will clean the speed sensor sleeve and replace the magnetic ring in the speed sensor, free of charge. The recall began July 22, 2020. Owners may contact KYMCO customer service at 1-888-235-3417. KYMCO's recall number for this recall is Like150-2020-Recall.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Defect is caused by excessive grease on magnetic ring

What the new part or software changes. 1.no visible excessive grease in the sensor 2.intact magnetic ring without visible deterioration Process: Clean the grease in the sleeve of the speed sensor, and replace the magnet ring in the speed sensor with new one.

Fixed in production. Refrain excessive grease in sensor; the correction started from 2019/3/3

Part made by: M.H.C. (Ming Horng Industrial Co., Ltd.), Taiwan, Province of China · Maker's recall no. Like150-2020-Recall

How the problem was found.

  • Starting 2019, KYMCO Taiwan received feedback from markets globally on ABS dysfunction. With cases increase in the US, a voluntary recall is decided.

Fuel pump: Fuel pump may leak at electrical connection: 2018-2019 · 19V037000 · 2019-01-16 · 910 bikes

Problem. KYMCO USA (KYMCO) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Like 150i motorcycles. The fuel pump may leak fuel from the electrical connection area when the tank is full.

Risk. A fuel pump leak from around the electrical connection area has an increased risk of a fire.

Fix. KYMCO will notify owners, and dealers will replace the fuel pump, free of charge. The recall began March 19, 2019. Owners may contact KYMCO customer service at 1-888-235-3417.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The Defect is due to potential fuel leaking through the rubber plug around the electrical wiring inside the fuel pump when the fuel tank is overfilled, causing a potential fire hazard.

What the new part or software changes. The replacement fuel pump contains a new rubber plug that can prevent the fuel leakage.

Fixed in production. The fuel pumps on the vehicles produced after 2018/9/29 have no risk of leakage due to the new rubber plug.

Part made by: Changzhou Toyo Jiann Tsang Electric Co., Taiwan, Province of China
