Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Ignition: 2006-2007 · 06V269000 · 2006-07-26 · 918 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the mandatory engine stop switch on the right hand handlebar was not installed. This does not comply with the requirements of federal motor vehicle safety standard no. 123, 'motorcycle controls and displays.'

Risk. The operator will not be able to stop the engine from running by the fastest means possible in case of an emergency, increasing the risk of personal injury or a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the engine stop switch free of charge. The recall began on august 1, 2006. Owners may contact kymco usa customer service toll free at 1-888-235-3417.

2007 Kymco AGILITY · 11,807 miles · Final drive · IN · filed 2011-06 · NHTSA 10406913During normal operation, a component called a "chain slider" failed due to abrasive wear after 11807 kilometers (approximately 7400 miles). Subsequently, the part came free from it's mounting and lodged in the drive sprocket of my 2007 kymco venox 250 cc motorcycle, causing the rear wheel to lock up during operation. Fortunately I was operating the motorcycle at low speed and avoided a crash. I purchased a replacement part, and upon examination I realized that this part will also fail catastrophically during operation. The fragments of the failed part are available for examination.
2007 Kymco AGILITY · 200 miles · Engine · CO · filed 2007-09 · crash · NHTSA 10204181My brand new 2007 kymco people 150 scooter malfunctioned while I was at a dead stop. As I was holding it upright and reaching to turn off the ignition via the key with my right hand, the engine sporadically revved up in rpms. This engaged the drive to the rear wheel and the scooter accelerated out from underneath me. This caused the scooter to lurch forward into a wall at the place where I was in the process of parking it. As I tried to maintain control of the scooter it caused me injury to my hand which was on the handlebars. The handlebar and my hand came into contact with the wall. The scooter dealer where I purchased the scooter inspected it for possible reasons or explanations for the malfunction and were not able to determine its cause. I am in the process of having the scooter repaired. I will also file this malfunction with kymco for their advice and their recommendations. I am seeking reimbursement for the damage done to the scooter as a result of this malfunction.

It can cause crash by larger vehicles coming from behind at larger speed. For example, rider actually riding at 35 mph on a federal highway minimum speed of 45 mph eventhough rider thinks he/she is at legal speed. At this time (08/19/07) when I'm riding in a 25 mph street, I ride my scooter reading at 35 mph. It is not safe, specially to those owners that are not aware of the problem. In addition to safety issues, my motorscooter now reads ridden kilometers in excess to actual usage. Depreciation of vehicle is greater then actual due to misleading speedometer.
