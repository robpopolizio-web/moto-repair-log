Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Oil may Leak from Breather onto Rear Tire (clutch, gearbox and final drive): 2013-2014 · 15V053000 · 2015-02-02 · 149 bikes

Problem. Brammo, Inc. (Brammo) is recalling certain model year 2013-2014 Empulse and Empulse R motorcycles manufactured November 19, 2012, to July 8, 2014. Oil leaking from the transmission breather tube may blow onto the rear fender and tire.

Risk. If oil leaks and contacts the rear tire, a loss of control may occur, increasing the risk of crash.

Fix. Brammo will notify owners, and dealers will install a new transmission breather baffle kit, free of charge. The recall began on February 11, 2015. Owners may contact Brammo customer service at 1-541-482-9555.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Below 10 degrees C the oil's viscosity increases. If the gearbox is turned at high enough RPM (5000 RPM or greater) while the oil is cold, can cause the gearbox to force the oil into the breather tube. If enough oil gets into the breather tube, it could reach the top of the tube.

What the new part or software changes. From an outward appearance the remedy component will appear identical to the recalled component.

Fixed in production. Brammo's production started using the new SMRE part on 7/9/2014. Bikes produced on or after that date are not included in this recall. The recall remedy in the field will be different in that the fix is designed to be easily installed by the dealer or service technician with a supplied Gearbox Breather Baffle part.

Part made by: SMRE S.p.A., Italy

How the problem was found.

  • 12/6/2013 - Problem first reported by customer. Subsequent service found excessive oil in gearbox and problem was attributed to this. The level was corrected, and no further problems were reported.
  • 1/17/2014 - Brammo found that under sub-freezing conditions, oil could enter the breather tube. Brammo provided findings to supplier SMRE.
  • 3/21/2014 - SMRE notified Brammo of a possible production solution to the issue.
  • 5/21/2014 - Brammo provides Production Approval to SMRE for implementation of the oil breather solution. No new reports of customer issues have occurred since the first report on 12/6/2013.
  • 7/9/2014 - Production switches to new part.
  • 10/8/2014 - New report received of oil leaking from breather tube from a customer. The bike had just been serviced (no issues before servicing), so it was uncertain if improper oil or filling level was cause of issue.
  • 11/13/2014 - Brammo designs and orders prototype parts in support of a potential field revision if it is found that oil leak can occur under certain conditions.

2014 Brammo Brammo Street Bikes · 1,460 miles · Clutch, gearbox and final drive, Engine and cooling, Fuel system · CA · filed 2015-10 · NHTSA 10781155Twice during the same ride, the vehicle had a total loss of power while under quick acceleration and displayed a "system fault s86" which the manual lists as "motor controller -- motor over current". When this occurs, the motorcycle loses all power and had to be pulled to the side of the road and powered on/off. First instance I was trying to pass a vehicle on an uphill grade, the second while trying to accelerate onto the freeway. An motor power loss like this is extremely dangerous any time a vehicle is at speed, but it's especially threatening when it occurs during a passing or freeway merging situation. In both cases, I was able to get to the side of the road, but given heavier traffic or an oncoming car during a passing situation and this could be life threatening.
