The short answer

13 US owners have filed a safety complaint about the Big Dog Pitbull (model years 2002, 2004-2005, 2007-2008). The most reported areas are electrical and instruments (62%), fuel system (15%), rider aids (traction, stability) (8%). Half of the complaints that gave a mileage happened before 5,000 miles.

The bike has had 11 safety recalls. Recall work is free at any Big Dog dealer, so check your VIN (link under each recall below).

LOOSE CONNECTION/ ELECTRONIC HARNESS CONTROLLER (electrical and instruments): 2004 · 10V180000 · 2010-05-03 · 1,895 bikes

Problem. Big dog is recalling certain 2004 chopper, bulldog, ridgeback, mastiff, boxer, and pitbull motorcycles. There is a possible loose connection between the harness connector and the electronic harness controller.

Risk. This condition may cause the motorcycle to experience intermittent loss of electrical power to various components, which can lead to, among other things, a loss of headlamp function or a vehicle stall, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Big dog will notify owners and dealers will repair the motorcycles free of charge. The safety recall began on may 24, 2010. Owners may contact big dog at 1-316-219-9121.

VOLTAGE REGULATOR (electrical and instruments): 2005-2006 · 07V356000 · 2007-08-08 · 7,611 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, a warped bus bar within the voltage regulator can cause a short circuit.

Risk. This could possibly result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will replace the existing circuit breaker located between the voltage regulator positive battery lead (b+ lead) and the positive battery terminal with an alternative circuit protection device that will prevent a thermal event if there is a short to ground. The recall began on october 1, 2007. Owners may contact big dog at 1-316-219-9129 or email to customer.service@bigdogmotorcycles.com.

ELECTRONIC HARNESS CONTROL (EHC) MODULE (electrical and instruments): 2005 · 06V305000 · 2006-08-11 · 2,101 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the electronic harness control (ehc) module can fail resulting in a total shut down of the vehicle's electronic power.

Risk. This condition could occur without any prior warning and could result in a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the ehc modules free of charge. The recall began on september 19, 2006. Owners may contact big dog at 316-267-9121.

KICKSTAND SPRING (frame and bodywork): 2004-2006 · 06V019000 · 2006-01-25 · 9,726 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the kickstand spring may stretch and detach from the mounting bracket or the kickstand.

Risk. This will allow the kickstand to deploy into the extended position and cause a crash.

Fix. Dealers will install a new kickstand spring free of charge. The recall began on february 17, 2006. Owners may contact big dog at 316-267-9121.

REAR WHEEL HUB BOLTS (wheels): 2005 · 05V432000 · 2005-09-28 · 3,294 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the rear wheel hub bolts could loosen and back out allowing the hub and brake rotor to detach from the wheel with an associated loss of stopping power.

Risk. This condition could occur without any prior warning and could result in a crash.

Fix. Dealers will remove, clean and reattach the rear hub bolts free of charge. The recall began on october 14, 2005. Owners may contact big dog at 1-312-267-9121.

Problem. On certain motorcycles, a screw may become loose in the handlebar control due to the lack of a lock washer and loctite.

BIG DOG/REAR MASTER CYLINDER PISTON (brakes and abs): 2004 · 04V137000 · 2004-03-23 · 1,906 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the piston in the rear master cylinder may have been improperly sized, which could cause the rear brakes to drag or lockup.

Risk. This could result in a crash.

Fix. Dealers will remove the piston from the rear master cylinder and replace it with a new piston. Owner notification began on april 12, 2004. Owners should contact big dog at 1-316-267-9121.

BIG DOG/FUEL TANK (fuel system): 2002 · 02V089000 · 2002-03-19 · 1,035 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the fuel tanks may have been improperly mounted to the motorcycle. This can put unnecessary stress on the tank mounting tabs, causing these tabs to fail, with the possibility of fuel spillage.

Risk. Fuel spillage, in the presence of an ignition source, could result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will remove the tanks, replace the mounting grommets and related hardware, and remount the tanks. Owner notification began april 1, 2002. Owners who take their vehicles to an authorized dealer on an agreed upon service date and do not receive the free remedy within a reasonable time should contact big dog at 1-316-267-2597.

BIG DOG/REAR FENDER (frame and bodywork): 1998-1999 · 02V016000 · 2002-01-15 · 286 bikes

Problem. On some motorcycles, the rear fender front mounting tabs securing the rear fender can fracture, causing the rear fender to break away from the frame and drop onto the rear tire.

Risk. This could result in tire damage and unintended braking action.

Fix. Dealers will replace the welded tabs that partially support the rear fender, located under the seat, with two bolted-on fender clamps. The fender will be removed from the bike so that the mounting holes for the clamp can be drilled and then reinstalled with the addition of a backing plate for added rigidity. Owner notification began april 15, 2002. Owners who take their vehicles to an authorized dealer on an agreed upon service date and do not receive the free remedy within a reasonable time should contact big dog at 1-316-267-2597.
