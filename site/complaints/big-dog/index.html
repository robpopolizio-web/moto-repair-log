Other Big Dog recalls (5)

Recalls for models with no matching owner complaints. The model name is as the maker filed it.

DT CHOPPER, N-CHOPPER · Charging system (stator, regulator): 2005 · 07V356000 · 2007-08-08 · 7,611 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, a warped bus bar within the voltage regulator can cause a short circuit.

Risk. This could possibly result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will replace the existing circuit breaker located between the voltage regulator positive battery lead (b+ lead) and the positive battery terminal with an alternative circuit protection device that will prevent a thermal event if there is a short to ground. The recall began on october 1, 2007. Owners may contact big dog at 1-316-219-9129 or email to customer.service@bigdogmotorcycles.com.

BIG DOG · Electrical: 2005 · 05V052000 · 2005-02-14 · 1,586 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, an electronic component failure could occur in the electric harness control (ehc) module.

Risk. This could result in a total shut down of the motorcycle's electrical power increasing the risk of crash.

Fix. Dealers will add a resistor harness to eliminate the susceptibility of the component in the electric harness control module to fail free of charge. The recall began on march 15, 2005. Owners may contact big dog motorcycles at 316-267-9121.

MOTORCYCLE · Handlebar: 2002 · 02V285000 · 2002-10-23 · 108 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the weld on the stainless steel handlebars could break, limiting the ability to use the hand controls.

Risk. This can increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the handlebars. Owner notification began november 15, 2002. Owners who take their vehicles to an authorized dealer on an agreed upon service date and do not receive the free remedy within a reasonable time should contact big dog at 1-316-267-2597.

MOTORCYCLE · Fuel hose or line: 2002 · 02V090000 · 2002-03-19 · 462 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, a fuel valve's outlet nipple, to which the fuel line attaches, may pull out of the fuel valve, allowing fuel to exit onto the motor and primary drive.

Risk. Fuel spillage, in the presence of an ignition source, could result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will replace the fuel (shut off) valve. Owner notification began april 1, 2002. Owners who take their vehicles to an authorized dealer on an agreed upon service date and do not receive the free remedy within a reasonable time should contact big dog at 1-316-267-2597.

HUSKY · Fuel tank: 2002 · 02V089000 · 2002-03-19 · 1,035 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the fuel tanks may have been improperly mounted to the motorcycle. This can put unnecessary stress on the tank mounting tabs, causing these tabs to fail, with the possibility of fuel spillage.

Risk. Fuel spillage, in the presence of an ignition source, could result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will remove the tanks, replace the mounting grommets and related hardware, and remount the tanks. Owner notification began april 1, 2002. Owners who take their vehicles to an authorized dealer on an agreed upon service date and do not receive the free remedy within a reasonable time should contact big dog at 1-316-267-2597.
