Bodywork, fairing and brackets: 2008 · 07V580000 · 2007-12-18 · 8,061 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the rear fender strut attaching bolts can fail allowing the rear fender to detach from the motorcycle.

Risk. This could occur without prior warning and could result in an injury or a crash.

Fix. Dealers will remove existing strut bolts and replace with higher tensile strength attaching bolts and updated washers. Higher torque values will be observed when installing the replacement fasteners. The recall began on january 21, 2008. Owners may contact big dog at 316-267-9121.
