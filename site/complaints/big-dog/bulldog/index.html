LOOSE CONNECTION/ ELECTRONIC HARNESS CONTROLLER (electrical and instruments): 2004 · 10V180000 · 2010-05-03 · 1,895 bikes

Problem. Big dog is recalling certain 2004 chopper, bulldog, ridgeback, mastiff, boxer, and pitbull motorcycles. There is a possible loose connection between the harness connector and the electronic harness controller.

Risk. This condition may cause the motorcycle to experience intermittent loss of electrical power to various components, which can lead to, among other things, a loss of headlamp function or a vehicle stall, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Big dog will notify owners and dealers will repair the motorcycles free of charge. The safety recall began on may 24, 2010. Owners may contact big dog at 1-316-219-9121.

REAR FENDER STRUT BOLT (frame and bodywork): 2005, 2007 · 07V580000 · 2007-12-18 · 8,061 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the rear fender strut attaching bolts can fail allowing the rear fender to detach from the motorcycle.

Risk. This could occur without prior warning and could result in an injury or a crash.

Fix. Dealers will remove existing strut bolts and replace with higher tensile strength attaching bolts and updated washers. Higher torque values will be observed when installing the replacement fasteners. The recall began on january 21, 2008. Owners may contact big dog at 316-267-9121.

TACHOMETER BOARD (electrical and instruments): 2007 · 07V355000 · 2007-08-08 · 181 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the tachometer board may have been improperly installed.

Risk. Improperly installed tachometer boards may develop a short circuit that could cause the motorcycle to shut down. This could occur without prior warning and result in a crash.

Fix. Dealers will isolate the tachometer board from the housing. The recallbegan on august 27, 2007. Owners may contact big dog at 1-316-219-9129 or e-mail at customer.service@bigdogmotorcycles.com.

VOLTAGE REGULATOR (electrical and instruments): 2005 · 07V356000 · 2007-08-08 · 7,611 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, a warped bus bar within the voltage regulator can cause a short circuit.

Risk. This could possibly result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will replace the existing circuit breaker located between the voltage regulator positive battery lead (b+ lead) and the positive battery terminal with an alternative circuit protection device that will prevent a thermal event if there is a short to ground. The recall began on october 1, 2007. Owners may contact big dog at 1-316-219-9129 or email to customer.service@bigdogmotorcycles.com.

PIVOT BOLT REPLACEMENT (frame and bodywork): 2005 · 06V389000 · 2006-10-12 · 2,494 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the long pivot bolt may bend or break during normal operation of the motorcycle.

Risk. This could potentially cause the rear fender to abrade against the rear tire while the motorcycle is being driven. This condition could occur without any prior warning and could result in a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the long pivot bolt and, if necessary, straighten the rear frame side panel. The short left side pivot bolt and pivot shaft may also be replaced free of charge. The recall began on november 1, 2006. Owners may contact big dog at 316-267-9121.

IGNITION MODULE BATTERY TRAY (electrical and instruments): 2005 · 06V301000 · 2006-08-09 · 2,711 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the ignition modules are oriented within the battery tray in a way that makes the ignition module susceptible to vibration.

Risk. This susceptibility to vibration may contribute to a stalling condition that could occur without any prior warning and result in a crash.

Fix. Dealers will install a new ignition module mounting system free of charge. The recall began on august 25, 2006. Owners may contact big dog at 316-267-9121.
