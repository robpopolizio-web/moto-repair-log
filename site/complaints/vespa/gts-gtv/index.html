Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Fuel pump: Defective fuel pump may result in stall: 2008-2010 · 15V066000 · 2015-02-04 · 2,613 bikes

Problem. Piaggio Group Americas Inc. (Piaggio) is recalling certain model year 2008-2010 Vespa GTS 250, GTV 250, GTS Super 250, 2011 Vespa LX 150, LXV 150, S 150, 2010-2011 Vespa GTS 300, GTV 300, GTS Super 300, 2009-2010 Piaggio MP3 250, BV 250 Tourer, and 2010 BV 300 Tourer motorcycles. Due to a defective fuel pump, the affected motorcycles may stall while being operated or may fail to restart after being shut off for 15-20 minutes.

Risk. If the motorcycle stalls, there is an increased risk of a crash.

Fix. Piaggio will notify owners, and dealers will replace the defective fuel pump, free of charge. The recall began March 16, 2015. Owners may contact Piaggio customer service at 1-212-380-4433.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Interference between the impeller and the inner pump housing caused by decreasing pump clearance, due to the impeller swelling.

What the new part or software changes. The new components can be identified by a brown cap on the motor of the fuel pump vs. a black cap on the old component.

Fixed in production. All new production will have a component that has been properly manufactured and quality inspected by Piaggio & C S.p.a.

Part made by: Bitron S.p.a.

How the problem was found.

  • January 26, 2011 notification was sent to NHTSA regarding the fuel pump service campaign.
  • September 2014: notification was sent to Piaggio & C S.p.a about an investigation made by Italian authorities as a consequence of a claim regarding the stalling of a vehicle while running.
