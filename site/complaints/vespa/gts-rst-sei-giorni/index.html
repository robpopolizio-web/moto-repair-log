Brakes: Improperly galvanized brake lines reduce braking: 2020-2021 · 20V617000 · 2020-10-06 · 483 bikes

Problem. Piaggio Group Americas. Inc. (Piaggio) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Vespa GTS 300 and GTV 300 scooters. Improperly galvanized brake pipe terminals can result in excessive front and/or rear brake levers travel during braking.

Risk. Reduced braking efficiency can lengthen the distance needed to stop the scooter, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Piaggio will notify owners, and dealers will test the O-Ring, replace the brake fluid and bleed the brake system, or replace the brake pipes based on the vehicle's age, free of charge. The recall began October 21, 2020. Owners may contact Piaggio customer service at 1-212-380-4433. Piaggio's number for this recall is PP2ZZQ2003_GTS/GTV.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Based on warranty claims for production starting in May of 2020, the factory conducted testing and found that there was an incorrect galvanization process of the brake pipe terminals. The protective zinc/nickel layer used for surface treatment of the brake pipe terminals were found to have an incorrect plating process, this allows hydrogen to remain inside the surface during the treatment process that releases in the brake fluid causing hydrogen to release inside of the braking system as a bubble. The vehicles that are not affected have brake lines that had a proper galvanization process.

What the new part or software changes. Brake lines have been plated with the correct process and checked internally and marked.

Fixed in production. Internal temporary solution: 100% Dehydrogenation in production line of brake line components (VIN ZAPMD310000002720) date 10/02/2020 Internal definite solution: Brake system pipes with with new Zn-Ni plating process building from new sub-supplier.

Part made by: J.Juan S.A, Spain · Maker's recall no. PP2ZZQ2003_GTS/GTV

How the problem was found.

  • We received warranty claims from our worldwide dealer network that the front and in some cases the rear brake levers have extended play especially after a prolonged period of inactivity. Excessive play on brake levers can cause reduced braking efficiency as a consequence. Brake lever or pedal: Reduced braking ability: 2019-2020 · 19V826000 · 2019-11-18 · 1,328 bikes

Problem. Piaggio Group Americas. Inc. (Piaggio) is recalling certain 2019 Vespa GTV 300, GTS 300, and 2020 Vespa GTS 300 HPE scooters. The brake lines may have been improperly galvanized, allowing hydrogen to be released into the brake fluid.

Risk. Hydrogen in the braking system can reduce the brake lever's ability to activate the brakes, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Piaggio will notify owners, and dealers will install front and rear master cylinder brake flush fittings, and perform a complete brake system flush, free of charge. The recall began November 27, 2019. Owners may contact Piaggio customer service at 1-212-380-4433. Piaggio's number for this recall is PP2ZZQ1904_GTS.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The factory conducted testing and found that there was an incorrect galvanization process of the brake pipe terminals. The protective zinc layer used for surface treatment of the brake pipe terminals were found to have an irregular surface, this allows hydrogen to remain inside the surface during the treatment process that releases in the brake fluid causing hydrogen to release inside of the braking system. The vehicles that are not affected have brake lines that had a proper galvanization process.

Fuel pump: Defective fuel pump may result in stall: 2010-2011 · 15V066000 · 2015-02-04 · 2,613 bikes

Problem. Piaggio Group Americas Inc. (Piaggio) is recalling certain model year 2008-2010 Vespa GTS 250, GTV 250, GTS Super 250, 2011 Vespa LX 150, LXV 150, S 150, 2010-2011 Vespa GTS 300, GTV 300, GTS Super 300, 2009-2010 Piaggio MP3 250, BV 250 Tourer, and 2010 BV 300 Tourer motorcycles. Due to a defective fuel pump, the affected motorcycles may stall while being operated or may fail to restart after being shut off for 15-20 minutes.

Risk. If the motorcycle stalls, there is an increased risk of a crash.

Fix. Piaggio will notify owners, and dealers will replace the defective fuel pump, free of charge. The recall began March 16, 2015. Owners may contact Piaggio customer service at 1-212-380-4433.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Interference between the impeller and the inner pump housing caused by decreasing pump clearance, due to the impeller swelling.

What the new part or software changes. The new components can be identified by a brown cap on the motor of the fuel pump vs. a black cap on the old component.

Fixed in production. All new production will have a component that has been properly manufactured and quality inspected by Piaggio & C S.p.a.

Part made by: Bitron S.p.a.

How the problem was found.

  • January 26, 2011 notification was sent to NHTSA regarding the fuel pump service campaign.
  • September 2014: notification was sent to Piaggio & C S.p.a about an investigation made by Italian authorities as a consequence of a claim regarding the stalling of a vehicle while running.
