Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Coolant hose: 1997-1998 · 98V272000 · 1998-10-29 · 595 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The hoses supplying oil from the engine to the oil radiator and back to the engine can separate at the fittings. A leak can develop and oil can get on the tires of the motorcycle.

Risk. Oil on the tires can cause loss of control, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will install replacement oil radiator hoses of improved design and materials.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Coolant hose: 1997-1998 · 98V273000 · 1998-10-29 · 390 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The hose assembly supplying oil to the cylinder heads can separate at the fittings. A leak can develop causing oil to get on the tires of the motorcycle.

Risk. Oil on the tires can cause loss of control, increasing the risk of a crash.
