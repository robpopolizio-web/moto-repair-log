2024 Moto Guzzi V7 Stone · 2,100 miles · Fuel system · CA · filed 2025-11 · NHTSA 11698106The contact owns a 2024 Moto Guzzi V7 Stone Motorcycle. The contact stated while riding at approximately 35 MPH, the high-pressure fuel line started to leak gasoline directly onto the engine. The contact turned off the motorcycle and pushed it back to the residence. The motorcycle was towed to the dealer, where it was diagnosed and determined that the high-pressure fuel line had disconnected from the outlet of the fuel pump to the fuel line. The motorcycle was repaired. The contact was not able to notify the manufacturer of the failure because the manufacturer's voice mailbox was full. The failure mileage was approximately 2,100.
2024 Moto Guzzi V7 Stone · Other · NH · filed 2025-04 · NHTSA 11655692The transmission fails to go into neutral unless completely stopped. Also, the dash will show the bike is in neutral occasionally when coming to a red light/intersection, and when you release the clutch lever, the engine will start lunging the bike forward in a jerking motion (false neutral indication). My safety is put at risk as having the motorcycle refuse to go into neutral poses a risk for injury or an accident to myself and the other vehicles around me. The dealer informed me that the bike's clutch cable was out of adjustment, they adjusted it, it improved, but it has gotten worse once again. I contacted Moto Guzzi and they have not responded in any way. This was 2-3 days ago. The motorcycle is brand new with less than 100miles as of this writing, and no other lights or messages on the dash related to this problem. The problem has existed since I purchased the bike. I was informed by the dealer that the concern will improve with time. Safety needs to be consistent, not something I have to wait to finally occur. As it stands, the transmission is unacceptable, though the rest of the motorcycle is fantastic. I've been riding almost 30 years with 25+ motorcycles. This isn't normal and hasn't occurred with any other motorcycle I have ridden. No other motorcycle shifts this poorly.
2017 Moto Guzzi V7 Stone · 600 miles · Engine · PA · filed 2019-04 · NHTSA 11204865Engine stalls randomly, dealer claims a new engine is needed do to a thrust washer not being installed properly in manufacturing.
2017 Moto Guzzi V7 Stone · 1,100 miles · Fuel system · NC · filed 2018-12 · NHTSA 11156852Moto guzzi has decided to use a fuel filter that is an in line fuel filter not suitable to be used inside a gas tank submerged in gasoline. I started looking into this issue after several owners of this same motorcycle 2017/2018 moto guzzi v7 iii stone have had issues with their motorcycle not working .. Leaving them stranded. I changed my own fuel filter after only 1100 miles and the top portion had ballooned, changed color from clear to orange, and seemed it could be pulled apart easy. I contacted the manufacturer mahle, and at first they said that the kl-150 could be used as an inline filter for motorcycles. However, I asked a more direct of if the inline filter was designed for use within and submerged in gasoline. Their answer was "no, the kl-150 is not suitable for an in-tank fuel filter." there is another model the kl-145 that is all metal, which most people use instead of the kl-150, where it is half plastic and half metal. I'm afraid fuel pressure loss could cause an accident if someone needed to give throttle to the bike, and no fuel pressure. Mahle (fuel filter manufacturer) lisa.levra@us.mahle.com 1-248-347-9717 I have contacted piaggio (owner of muto guzzi) both in the us and italy. Sent pics, no responses.
