V100 MANDELLO, V100 MANDELLO AVIAZIONE · Rear shock: Rear shock absorber may break: 2023 · 23V572000 · 2023-08-10 · 260 bikes

Problem. Piaggo Group Americas, Inc. (Piaggo) is recalling certain 2023 Moto Guzzi V100 Mandello and Mandello Aviazione Navale motorcycles. The rear shock absorber may be missing one or both bushings, which can cause it to break.

Risk. A rear shock absorber that breaks can cause a loss of vehicle control and increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will inspect and, if necessary, replace the rear shock absorber, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 22, 2023. Owners may contact Piaggio's customer service at 1-212-380-4433.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Incorrect assembly of the lower fastening of the shock absorber to the suspension arm.

What the new part or software changes. The rear shock absorber installed with two bushings at the swingarm

Fixed in production. We will send the customer letters and dealer communication as soon as they have been reviewed by NHTSA.

How the problem was found.

  • Two cases have ben reported in the Italian market First Case July 7,2023 Second Case July 27,2023 The factory presented update campaign on 8/4/2023

AUDACE, MGX 21 BAGGER · Brake master cylinder: Motorcycle may unexpectedly slow down or stop: 2016-2018 · 19V561000 · 2019-07-26 · 1,701 bikes

Problem. Piaggio Group Americas. Inc. (Piaggio) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Moto Guzzi Audace 1400 and Eldorado 1400, 2017-2018 Moto Guzzi California 1400, 2017 Moto Guzzi MGX 21 Bagger, 2013-2014 Aprilia Mana 850, 2014-2016 Aprilia Dorsoduro 750, 2018-2019 Aprilia Dorsoduro 900, and 2018 Aprilia Shiver 900 motorcycles. Due to a problem with the front brake master cylinder, the front brakes may drag or apply, causing the motorcycle to unexpectedly slow down or stop completely without activating the rear brake light.

Risk. Unexpectedly slowing down or stopping while driving with no warning to following traffic can increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. Piaggio will notify owners, and dealers will inspect, and if necessary, replace the front brake master cylinder free of charge. The recall began August 16, 2019. Owners may contact Piaggio customer service at 1-212-380-4433. Piaggio's number for this recall is PA2ZZQ1904.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The front brake master cylinder may not have sufficient ineffective lever stroke (ineffective stroke = initial part of the lever stroke which does not cause braking effect).

What the new part or software changes. The recalled component part number 887764 has been replaced with new part number 2B006111 that has been designed with a stable and reliable production process and a new range of tolerance for the ineffective stroke for affected Moto Guzzi models. The recalled component part number 859781 has been replaced with new part number 2B006874 that has been designed with a stable and reliable production process and a new range of tolerance for the ineffective stroke for affected Aprilia Mana models. The recalled component part number 859781 or 896385 has been replaced with new part number 2B006115 that has been designed with a stable and reliable production process and a new range of tolerance for the ineffective stroke for affected Aprilia Dorsoduro 750 models. Fixed in production. In April 2019 the manufacturer switched to the new front brake master cylinder part number 2B006111, 2B006115 and 2B006874 with a new range of tolerance for the ineffective stroke.

Part made by: Heng Tong Auto Parts Inc., Taiwan, Province of China · Maker's recall no. PA2ZZQ1904

V9 BOBBER · Brake hose or line: Front brake line may rub and leak: 2016 · 17V380000 · 2017-06-14 · 1,139 bikes

Problem. Piaggio Group Americas, Inc. (Piaggio) is recalling certain 2017 Moto Guzzi V7 III Racer 750, V7 III Stone 750, V7 III Special 750, and 2016-2017 V9 Bobber, V9 Roamer motorcycles. The front ABS brake hose may rub against the engine's secondary air system connection, possibly resulting in a brake fluid leak.

Risk. A loss of brake fluid can lengthen the distance needed to stop the motorcycle, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Piaggio will notify owners, and dealers will secure the front ABS brake hose to prevent it from rubbing. Any hose that has already been damaged, will be replaced. The repairs will be made free of charge. The recall began June 22, 2017. Owners may contact Piaggio customer service at 1-212-380-4433.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Fretting between the front ABS brake hose and the engine's secondary air system connection due to brake lines that can move out of position.

What the new part or software changes. The brake line is not a defective component but there is a possibility of the lines to move out of position and rub on the secondary air union located on the engine. If the brake pipe shows any sign of fretting the brake line will be replaced.

Fixed in production. The installation of ABS pipe mount (same solution applied to remedy the recall) has been put in place on the production starting 05/31/2017.

How the problem was found.

  • On 04/03/2017. We had one case in Europe where we found a customer intervention that could have changed the original position of the tube. There have not been any reported failures or crashes at this time.

DAYTONA RS · Coolant hose: 1997 · 98V272000 · 1998-10-29 · 595 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The hoses supplying oil from the engine to the oil radiator and back to the engine can separate at the fittings. A leak can develop and oil can get on the tires of the motorcycle.

Risk. Oil on the tires can cause loss of control, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will install replacement oil radiator hoses of improved design and materials.

SPORT 1100 · Seat: 1995-1996 · 98V078000 · 1998-04-14 · 289 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The latch securing the passenger seat on these motorcycles can fail allowing the seat and passenger to fall off the rear of the motorcycle.

Risk. The passenger could sustain serious injuries or death.

Fix. Dealers will replace these seats with a new seat that incorporates an improved latch mechanism and a more rigid base. In addition, some body work around the passenger seat will be removed so that the replacement seat will fit properly.
