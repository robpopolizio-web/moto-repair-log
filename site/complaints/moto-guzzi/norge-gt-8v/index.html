2012 Moto Guzzi Norge GT 8V · 2,900 miles · Engine and cooling · OH · filed 2016-06 · NHTSA 10872790All 8v engines made by moto guzzi from 2009 to 2012 had flat lifters that are prone to failing. The only way there has not been an accident is they warn by being very noisy and owners repair them. Piaggio, who owns moto guzzi, takes each case separately and my or may not warranty them plus takes months at times to decide many times leaving the owner with a $2,000 repair. Particles from the failing lifters and cams can destroy the bearings and cause a complete loss of power and destroy the engine. They have kits to fix it but will not recall it. Suzuki has a similar problem on their 650 v-strom that they sold fewer of and they recalled them. On mine at 2,900 miles I replaced them to prevent the engine damage and they already were showing wear. Some have gone longer but then further engine damage is done. Since I did my own work it is not even under consideration for warranty even with the offer of just cover dealer cost of the parts.
2013 Moto Guzzi Norge GT 8V · 6,000 miles · Fuel system · CA · filed 2016-02 · NHTSA 10823555The moto guzzi norge is manufactured with a plastic fuel tank. The fuel tank has deformed to the point that it no longer fits properly onto the motorcycle. The deformation has resulting in the body panels, fairing panels and electrical wiring cover panels being pushed pushed out of position and must now be held in place using zip ties instead of the bolts designed to hold them in place. The tank has swelled in all directions and also greater in certain areas so that large bumps are visible and can also be felt by touch. The deformation underneath the fuel tank has resulted in interference with pressure being applied to control systems as well as interfering with the turning of the handlebars to their maximum points. One portion of the fuel tank has either deformed or become otherwise affected so that the color of the fuel tank has given way to a dark spot of unknown origin. This is a known issue as many owners of this model as well as other models of this brand that use plastic fuel tanks have experienced the problem. Moto guzzi sourced these fuel tanks from the same source that provided plastic fuel tanks to ducati for which a recall was issued to replace plastic fuel tanks. Body panels, electrical wiring covers and fairing parts held in place via zip ties can be dangerous since they can break or otherwise become damaged allowing these parts to separate from the motorcycle and cause serious injury or worse to the rider and others on the roadways. The interference with the motorcycle's controls and steering present a danger to the rider and others on the roadways since that affects the operation of the motorcycle. The deformation of the fuel tank causes a loss of value to the owner because it is obviously a defect. The tank deformation appears to have damaged other body parts because they have been bent or otherwise deformed by the pressure from the tank.
