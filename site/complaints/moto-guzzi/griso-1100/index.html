Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix.

Fuel system: 2006-2007 · 07V252000 · 2007-06-07 · 232 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, a fuel pump hose may swell and change dimensions, thereby loosening its fit around the fitting at the fuel pump. The fuel pressure could drop.

Risk. This could cause erratic motor operation, difficulty in starting the vehicle or stalling, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace all fuel pump assemblies. The recall began on september 10, 2007. Owners may contact piaggio/moto guzzi at 1-212-380-4400.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

2006 Moto Guzzi Griso 1100 · 6,000 miles · Electrical and instruments, Fuel system · PA · filed 2013-05 · NHTSA 10511827The moto-guzzi griso 1100 & 1200's are known world wide for dash failures & stepper motor failures. My dash failed at 70 mph shutting down on the highway, this failure shut engine down & gave no readings. I am thankful that I have 49 years of riding experience. These dashes are known to fail from new with up to 20,000+ miles on bikes. Mine failed at 12000 & change. But issues started sooner. Dealer was as helpful but piaggio/moto-guzzi na were too ignorant to help. The stepper motor on my bike started revving up to 3000rpm within 6000 miles. Dealers had no knowledge of what the problem was their fix was to readjust the idle which does not help. Stepper motors fail by allowing the engine to rev as if you are still on the throttle when the rider is off the throttle. If a rider is not aware of a problem it may & probably caused accidents. Again this is a world wide failure problem. The problem is no one has the fortitude to file against moto-guzzi/piaggio. It is known the italians are difficult to deal with when problems exist. One day or it possibly already happened someone may have been killed or in a severe accident due to these failures.
2007 Moto Guzzi Griso 1100 · 650 miles · Fuel system · NY · filed 2007-09 · NHTSA 10204114Driving on main street to dealer for 600 mile check the speed was approx 25-30 mph. Suddenly the engine stopped. I pulled in the clutch lever and coasted to the shoulder of the street. Bike would not start. I called the dealer. He said open the gas cap and look in at the fuel and turn on the key. If you see fuel swirling around in the tank that means the fuel hose has disconnected from the fuel pump and the engine is not getting any fuel. I made arrangements to have the bike transported to the dealer. He said there was no recall and will fix the bike by re-attaching the fuel line. The bike has about 600 miles on it and repair is covered under warranty.
