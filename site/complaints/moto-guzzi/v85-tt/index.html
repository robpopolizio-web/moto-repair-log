Accessories: Footrest may fall off: 2020 · 19V858000 · 2019-12-03 · 399 bikes

Problem. Piaggio Group Americas. Inc. (Piaggio) is recalling certain 2020 Moto Guzzi V 85 TT motorcycles. The footrest mounting pin retaining clip may break, allowing the pin to disconnect and the footrest to fall off the motorcycle.

Risk. A footrest that falls off while the motorcycle is in motion may become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Piaggio will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the retaining rings on all four footrests. If any of the clips are found to be defective, the dealer will replace the retaining clips on all four footrest pins, free of charge. The recall began December 26, 2019. Owners may contact Piaggio customer service at 1-212-380-4433. Piaggio's number for this recall is PGJZZQ1905.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The retaining ring keeping the footrest in position may break due to the deformation of the retaining clip during incorrect assembly operation.

What the new part or software changes. The remedy component P/N AP8152409 (DIN 471) has a different shape compared to the previous retaining ring P/N AP8120573 (DIN 6799) that is easier to install.

Fixed in production. the production has completed in November 2019 and there will not be December production. Starting from January 1, 2020 the new vehicles will be assembled with retaining rings P/N AP8152409.

Final drive gear (shaft drive): Final drive may leak oil onto the rear tire: 2020 · 19V857000 · 2019-12-02 · 371 bikes

Problem. Piaggio Group Americas. Inc. (Piaggio) is recalling certain 2020 Moto Guzzi V 85 TT motorcycles. The final drive output seal may leak oil onto the rear tire.

Risk. An oil leak onto the rear tire may cause a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Piaggio will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the final drive transmission box for an oil leak, and install an extra seal. If an oil leak is found, both seals and the gasket will be replaced. These repairs will be performed free of charge. The recall began December 26, 2019. Owners may contact Piaggio customer service at 1-212-380-4433. Piaggio's number for this recall is PGJZZQ1904.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The presence of sharp edgeson the final transmission box shaft end (P/N 2B004268). The lips of the internal oil seal (P/N GU90413850) at right side into the final transmission box may be damaged during assembly by the sharp edges. This damage if present will cause the oil leak to occur. The condition of the oil seal lips cannot be checked during or after assembling (which is a "blind" operation.

What the new part or software changes. The remedy component is an additional component (PN 2B0072291) added to the complete final drive transmission box.

Fixed in production. There was no production in December 2019. Starting from January 2020 the Moto Guzzi assembly plant will begin to assemble all final drive transmission boxes with component P/N 2B0072291 (oil seal housing assembly).

Maker's recall no. PGJZZQ1904

How the problem was found.

  • In June 2019 the Piaggio After-Sales Dept in Italy began to report about warranties related to oil leakage at the final transmission box. The dealers and service workshops were repairing the vehicles according to the service manual instructions by disassembling the final transmission box and reassembling it after replacing all the oil seals and gaskets.
