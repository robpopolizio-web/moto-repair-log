Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Horn: Horn may detach from motorcycle: 2019 · 19V703000 · 2019-10-03 · 4,348 bikes

Problem. Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2019 CRF450L motorcycles. The horn mount may break, allowing the horn to detach from the motorcycle.

Risk. An unsecured horn may interfere with the motorcycle while driving, affecting handling and control, or it may fall off and become a road hazard. Either of these scenarios can increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. Honda will notify owners, and dealers will replace the horn and horn mount, free of charge. The recall began November 2019. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KK4.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

What the new part or software changes. Stay horn, stay, horn, part number: 50155-MKE-A50.

Part made by: Aioi Seisakusho Co., Ltd., Japan · Maker's recall no. KK4

How the problem was found.

  • March to April 2019 Honda received the first report of a broken horn stay from the Japanese market and launched an investigation.
  • May to June 2019 Preliminary analysis suspected the horn stay was fatiguing from motor resonance. The design specifications for the horn stay were re-evaluated.
  • July 2019 Honda received the first report of a broken horn stay from the U.S. market. Additional parts analysis was conducted to determine the failure mode and if it was similar to previous reports in other markets.
  • September 2019 Honda confirmed the design for the horn stay was insufficient in tolerating motor resonance.
  • September 26, 2019 Honda determined that a defect related to motor vehicle safety existed and decided to conduct a safety recall. As of September 26, 2019, Honda has received one warranty claim, and no reports of injuries or crashes related to this issue.
