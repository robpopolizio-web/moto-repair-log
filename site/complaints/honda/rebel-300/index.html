Handlebar and handlebar or steering bolts: Handlebar lock screw may detach and interfere with steering: 2017-2023, 2025-2026 · 26V500000 · 2026-07-30 · 43,913 bikes

Problem. Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2017-2023, 2025 CMX500, 2017-2023, 2025-2026 CMX300, and 2025 Honda SCL500 motorcycles. The handlebar lock screw may loosen and detach.

Risk. A detached screw may lodge between the frame and stem, interfering with steering and increasing the risk of a crash or injury.

Fix. Dealers will replace the lock screw and apply thread lock, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed September 29, 2026. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KU8. This recall expands previous NHTSA recall number 26V146. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning August 4, 2026.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The work instructions for the screw tightening process were unclear and did not specify the proper method for tightening a one-way Phillips screw, including the need to apply sufficient axial force. In addition, following a change in the process trainer, the instruction to use both hands to apply axial force was omitted, and the tightening operation was performed with the tool tilted rather than perpendicular to the screw. As a result, the screw may not have been tightened to the specified torque. Improper management of the tightening tool during the assembly process was also identified as a contributing cause to the defect. Due to inadequate tool maintenance, the tightening tool bit became worn and was not replaced, resulting in inconsistent tightening torque of the handlebar lock screw.

What the new part or software changes. The remedy component is the same as the recalled component. The component’s installation procedure has been improved.

Fixed in production. The recall condition was corrected in production on September 22, 2025.

Fix type: Repair · Part made by: Thai Honda Manufacturing Co., Ltd., Thailand · Maker's recall no. KU8

How the problem was found.

  • March 5, 2026 Honda determined that a defect related to motor vehicle safety existed and decided to conduct a safety recall. (NHTSA ID 26V-146)
  • May 12, 2026 Honda received reports of the issue that occurred on vehicles outside of the initial recall population and began to investigate.
  • June 17, 2026 Honda received an additional report of the issue outside of the initial recall population and continued to investigate.
  • June 23, 2026 Honda investigated and analyzed the affected vehicle population.
  • July 23, 2026 Honda determined that a defect related to motor vehicle safety existed and decided to conduct a safety recall. As of July 23, 2026, Honda has had two warranty claims with no report of injuries and no reports of a death related to this issue in the US, from March 6, 2026, through July 23, 2026.

Crankcase breather: Engine crankcase cover may leak oil: 2023 · 23V261000 · 2023-04-12 · 1,461 bikes

Problem. Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023 Rebel 300 (CMX300) motorcycles. The right engine crank house cover was incorrectly painted, allowing the press-fit plug to fall out.

Risk. Oil can leak onto the exhaust pipe or rear tire without the plug, increasing the risk of a fire, crash, or injury.

Fix. Dealers will replace the right crank case cover, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 25, 2023. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-800-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KP2.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The supplier did not follow the correct production sequence.

What the new part or software changes. Recalled components were improperly produced and may have paint adhered to the inner surface of the press-fit plug hole of the crankcase cover.

Fixed in production. The manufacturing and production inspection process has been corrected.

Part made by: Uno Minda Limited, Casting Division, India · Maker's recall no. KP2

How the problem was found.

  • January 15, 2023 Honda received a report from the Japanese market of engine oil spraying from an affected vehicle while in operation and began to investigate the issue.
  • January 30, 2023 Honda received a report from the Indian market of oil leaking out from the right crankcase cover of an affected vehicle.
  • February 2023 Honda began an investigation into the potential presence of paint in the crankcase plug press-fit hole.
  • March 13, 2023 Honda received a report from the Japanese market of an affected vehicle leaking oil from the engine while in operation.
  • April 6, 2023 Honda determined that a defect related to motor vehicle safety existed and decided to conduct a safety recall. As of April 6, 2023, Honda has received no warranty claims or reports for this issue in the U.S. market. No crashes or injuries related to this issue were reported.

Lights: Dim rear reflector lens/FMVSS 108: 2020-2021 · 21V382000 · 2021-05-24 · 28,528 bikes

Problem. Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Super Cub C125, CB500X, CB650R, CBR300R, CBR500R, CBR650R, Rebel 300, Rebel 500, Monkey, 2020 CRF250L, GROM, 2021 CRF300L, and CB500F motorcycles. The rear reflector lens may not reflect brightly enough. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."

Risk. A dim rear reflector may reduce the visibility of the motorcycle to other drivers, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.

Fix. Dealers will replace the rear reflector, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 7, 2021. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KM3.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Part made by: FIEM Industries Limited, India · Maker's recall no. KM3

How the problem was found.

  • December 2020 After a compliance test by Korean motor vehicle authorities on a sampling of rear reflectors, Honda Korea was informed that the rear reflectors of a Korean market vehicle were noncompliant.
  • December 2020 – January 2021 Honda and the rear reflector supplier performed internal tests to further investigate the noncompliance and confirmed that the rear reflectors were noncompliant because of a shortened injection molding cycle time of the reflector lens.
  • January – March 2021 Honda investigated the affected population and whether the affected models were out of compliance under other countries’ regulations.
  • May 17, 2021 Honda determined that an FMVSS noncompliance existed on the referenced models and decided to conduct a noncompliance recall.

Gearbox: Circlip may detach and cause gear misalignment: 2018-2019 · 19V412000 · 2019-06-04 · 3,898 bikes

Problem. Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2019 CB300R, 2018 CBR300R, 2018 CRF250L, 2018 CRF250L Rally, and 2018-2019 CMX300 motorcycles. The circlip, on the transmission's main shaft, may detach allowing for gear misalignment.

Risk. A misaligned gear can shift the transmission from neutral into gear during engine start, potentially resulting in unexpected motorcycle movement or seize the transmission and rear wheel while the motorcycle is in motion. Both conditions increase the risk of crash or injury.

Fix. Honda will notify owners, and dealers will replace the transmission main shaft, free of charge. The recall began July 8, 2019. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KK3.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

What the new part or software changes. Transmission main shaft, transmission main shaft, part number: 23211- K33-D000, 23212-K33-D000, 23211-KZZ-9000.

Part made by: Musashi Auto Parts Co., Ltd., Thailand · Maker's recall no. KK3
