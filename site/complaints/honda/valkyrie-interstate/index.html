The 6 most recent complaints, as filed (lightly reformatted).

1999 Honda Valkyrie Interstate · 82,300 miles · Wheels · NH · filed 2010-07 · NHTSA 103421341. 1999 honda valkyrie interstate model glcf - during normal use, the left rear wheel bearing failed. 2. This same part has failed at approx. 50k miles, 70k miles and now again at 82k miles. The first two repairs were done by a local authorized honda service, using replacement parts from honda. In each instance, the remainder of the bearing assembly was sent to honda for examination, no feedback provided. Since the 2nd failure was a known repeat, the service manager made an in-depth examination of the rear wheel assembly during that repair. This did not prevent the third failure 12k miles later. Both the service manager & I contend there is a design flaw in this assembly and he has experienced what he termed an abnormal # of similar failures on other honda valkyries he has serviced. Honda refuses to acknowledge or investigate the problems despite repeated attempts to relay all related information to them. Failure of this part has the potential to cause rear wheel drag or locking, and can possibly result in loss of control of the motorcycle. Loss of motorcycle control can result in serious injury or death. 3. In the absence of any support from honda on this issue, I have made the latest repair myself.
1999 Honda Valkyrie Interstate · 70,012 miles · Suspension · NH · filed 2007-10 · NHTSA 10205117Failure of left rear wheel bearing. Bearing initially failed and replaced 2 yrs ago at 50k miles. Failed again this year at 70k miles while 600 mi from home. No area dealer had required part(s). Had to transport motorcycle home via truck rental. Bike eventually fixed at my own local dealership under warranty. Honda refuses to discuss my out-of-pocket expenses or acknowledge there may be an issue with this part failing prematurely. An informal poll of 52 other owners showed 22 experienced the same failure, 5 of us twice, and some failures occurred in as little as 12k miles. Total failure of this part has the potential to cause the operator to loose control of the motorcycle, possibly resulting in an accident and/or serious injury.
2000 Honda Valkyrie Interstate · Clutch, gearbox and final drive · MI · filed 2004-05 · NHTSA 10073628The splines on the final drive gear and final drive flange were completely dry and rusted. Honda failed to lubricate the final drive splines, due to their lack of mentioning of the critical maintenance in the manual. The consumer found the problem as the tires were being changed. Upon removal, the rear wheel splines on the final drive gear/final flange were completely rusted and showed severe wear. The service department determined that the excessive wear was due to a lack of maintenance. The consumer requested a replacement of the worn parts. (model/gl 1500cf valkyrie interstate)
