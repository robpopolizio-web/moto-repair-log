Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Speedometer: Speedometer cable incorrectly routed: 2022 · 23V195000 · 2023-03-23 · 15,848 bikes

Problem. Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2022 Honda NVA110B motorcycles. The speedometer cable was incorrectly routed and may break or detach.

Risk. A speedometer with a broken or detached speedometer cable will fail to show the motorcycle speed, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace and reroute the speedometer cable, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 1, 2023. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KP1.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The speedometer cable was routed incorrectly on the vehicle, causing the cable to either break or detach.

What the new part or software changes. While the replacement speedometer cable remains the same, the cable will be correctly routed.

Fixed in production. The recall condition was corrected by properly installing the cable.

Maker's recall no. KP1

How the problem was found.

  • February 2022 Honda received its first report of a speedometer cable detaching and launched an investigation.
  • March 2022 Honda received additional reports of speedometer cables detaching and received its first report of a speedometer cable breaking.
  • July 2022 Honda received additional reports of speedometer cables breaking and launched another investigation.
  • August 2022 Honda reproduced the cable detachment during testing.
  • December 2022 Honda reproduced the cable breakage during testing.
  • February 28, 2023 Honda identified the mechanism for the cable breakage and detachment issues.
  • March 16, 2023 Honda determined that a defect related to motor vehicle safety existed and decided to conduct a safety recall. As of March 16, 2023, Honda has received 38 warranty claims related to the issue between February 16, 2022 and March 16, 2023 with no reports of injuries or deaths related to this issue.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (1)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Navi 2022 · 2023-03-23 · no. 2023166 · 1,856 bikes · Lights And Instruments

On certain motorcycles, the speedometer cable may not be routed correctly. As a result, the cable could break or detach from the pinion gear on the front wheel. If this happens, the speedometer wouldn't display the vehicle speed. Safety Risk: Riding a motorcycle without a speedometer could increase the risk of a crash. Corrective Actions: Honda will notify owners by mail and instruct you to take your motorcycle to a Honda Powersports dealership to replace and re-route the speedometer cable.

Source: Transport Canada
