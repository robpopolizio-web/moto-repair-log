Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

FUEL: VEHICLE CRASH, CUT OFF SYSTEM (5-94) (fuel system): 1991-1993 · 95V128000 · 1995-06-27 · 54,388 bikes

Problem. The sensor's plastic case material can leak allowing the sensor to shut off the engine unexpectedly during abrupt turns or when riding over bumpy surfaces.

Risk. Sudden loss of engine power, especially while turning, can cause a vehicle crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the bank angle sensor.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov
