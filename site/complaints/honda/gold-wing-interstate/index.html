Recalls (2)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Ignition switch: 1984 · 84V067000 · 1984-05-29 · 28,750 bikes

Problem. Switch cover which provides for a good wire connection could become loose and possibly cause loss of all electrical functions.

Risk.

Fix. The ignition switch wiring harness will be replaced along with the switch cover. The cover is installed to ensure proper wire connection.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Wheel: 1978 · 77V139000 · 1977-09-13 · 796 bikes

Problem. Upon front brake application, a combination of speed and special road conditions may cause the aluminum spoke blades to crack, eventually leading to collapse of the wheel.

Risk.

Fix. The dealer will replace the front wheel, free of charge, on all affected vehicles.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

I could not get the switch to start the vehicle unless I pressed on the bottom of the switch with my finger. Is there any recourse for this with honda or honda dealerships? The bike was stationary when this happened, but while researching this issue, I saw that there were several issues when the bike was in motion. 1985 Honda Gold Wing Interstate · 68,755 miles · Electrical & lights · PA · filed 2005-10 · NHTSA 10134023Vehicle went completely dead, indicating a failure in the electrical circuit affecting ignition and instrumentation. Accessory circuit was unaffected. This occurred at a stop light, with two brief failures prior to the complete failure while the vehicle was in motion. Turning the ignition key to the off position and back to the run position cleared the fault. According to the service manual for this vehicle, the ignition switch is the only part that could cause this failure.
