Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

CRANKCASE BREATHER SEPARATOR (engine and cooling): 2007 · 07V128000 · 2007-03-21 · 8,020 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the crankcase breather separator may be defected. During the molding process, the breather passage may have been blocked by a thin layer of the injected plastic material. This blockage will create a build-up of pressure inside the crankcase and may cause the left side engine seal to be pushed out of position and an oil leak will occur.

Risk. Oil leaking from the failed seal while riding can spray onto the rear tire or the road, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the breather separator, free of charge. The recall began on march 30, 2007. Owners may contact honda at 1-800-999-1009.
