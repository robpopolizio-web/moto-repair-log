Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Fuel pump or line: 1999-2001 · 02V263000 · 2002-09-25 · 9,120 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the fuel pump can fail.

Risk. This could cause poor engine performance and possible engine stalling, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will inspect and replace the fuel pumps. Owner notification is expected to begin during november 2002. Owners who take their vehicles to an authorized dealer on an agreed upon service date and do not receive the free remedy within a reasonable time should contact victory at 1-763-417-8650.
