390 ADVENTURE X, 390 SMC R · Side Stand Spring May Break (frame and bodywork): 2025 · 25V907000 · 2025-12-23 · 2,512 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain 2025 390 Adventure R, 390 SMC R, 390 Adventure X, and 390 Enduro R motorcycles. Engine vibrations may cause the side stand spring to break and the side stand to fall to the ground.

Risk. A motorcycle side stand that falls to the ground while riding can shut off the ignition, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.

Fix. Owners are advised to secure the side stand before riding the motorcycle until the remedy has been completed. Dealers will replace the side stand spring and install a new side stand sensor retaining plate, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed on February 17, 2026. Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-888-985-6090. The manufacturer's number for this recall is KTB2514. The information in your report suggests that KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) may have been aware of this issue more than five business days before filing a report with NHTSA. Please be reminded that under Federal law, this agency is to be notified of all safety defect and/or noncompliance decisions within five business days. 49 CFR 573.6. Significant civil penalties can be assessed for this violation.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Engine harmonics

What the new part or software changes. The side stand spring on all affected motorcycles must be replaced with a new side stand spring that has a rubber protector mounted. (#A60103224033 – Side stand spring with rubber protector). The Rubber Sleeve added to the side stand spring dampens down the natural frequency of the engine vibrations. In addition to replacing the side stand spring, affected motorcycles must also have a new side stand sensor retaining plate (#A60103029100) installed at the same time. The new side stand sensor retaining plate shifts the trigger range of the side stand switch.

Fixed in production. The updated side stand spring has a rubber sleeve added to dampen down the natural frequency of the engine vibrations.

Fix type: Replace · Part made by: Technomat Springs, India · Maker's recall no. KTB2514

How the problem was found.

  • 09.09.2025- KTM NA Customer Service received a dealer report that a side stand spring broke while a customer was riding. 09.12.2025- KTM NA Customer Service received an internal report from its media team that a side stand spring broke while riding. 9.17.2025 KTM NA gathered all the information from the two reports and sent this to KTM AG, Customer Service, for further investigation. 9.20.2025 …

390 ADVENTURE X, 390 SMC R · Loss of Drive Power (engine and cooling): 2025 · 25V825000 · 2025-12-01 · 6,546 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain 2024 Husqvarna Vitpilen 401, Husqvarna Svartpilen 401, 2024-2025 390 Duke, 2025 390 SMC R, 390 Enduro R, 390 Adventure X, and 390 Adventure R motorcycles. The engine control software may cause the engine to stall at low rpms, resulting in a loss of power.

Risk. A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash or injury.

Fix. KTM and Husqvarna dealers will inspect the motorcycles and update the electronic control unit engine management system (ECU EMS) software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 23, 2026. Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-888-985-6090. The manufacturer's numbers for this recall are KTB2513 & HTB2508.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Software programing

What the new part or software changes. The ECU EMS software will be updated with the improved version KM963EU2596003.

Fixed in production. The updated software optimizes injection timing, fuel amount, ignition timing, throttle opening and ride mode.

Fix type: Software · Part made by: KTM F&E GmbH, Austria · Maker's recall no. KTB2513 & HTB2508

How the problem was found.

  • 07/29/2025 – Customer Service received a report of loss of power while riding.
  • 08/01/2025 – 11/8/2025 Customer service reports, Vehicle Owner Questionnaire reports from NHTSA, and Technical Service reports were reviewed and analyzed by KTM AG, R&D in Austria during its investigation of the condition.
  • 11/06/2025 KTM AG determined a defect existed and a recall for ECU EMS software was required. One owner reported incident has been received through NHTSA VOQ report. KTM began investigating the claim.

250 DUKE, 990 DUKE · Fuel Filler Cap Seal May Leak Fuel (fuel system): 2024 · 25V740000 · 2025-10-30 · 5,488 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. is recalling certain 2024 390 Duke, 250 Duke, 990 Duke, Husqvarna Vitpilen 401, and Husqvarna Svartpilen 401 motorcycles. The seal on the fuel filler cap may develop cracks and leak fuel.

Risk. A fuel leak increases the risk of fire or injury.

Fix. Dealers will inspect and replace the fuel cap gasket, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 15, 2025. Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-888-985-6090. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov March 31, 2026.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Material deviations may cause small cracks to occur on the fuel filler cap seal of the above models. This can lead to the possibility of fuel leaking from the fuel cap.

What the new part or software changes. New design and specification of the gasket. The new fuel cap seal part ( ) has been made with improved production processes.

Fixed in production. New design and specification of the gasket.

Fix type: Replace · Part made by: Minda Corporation Limited, India

How the problem was found.

  • 03/19/2023 – The part supplier, MINDA, observed cracked sealing during lab testing. The condition was not observed on a production motorcycle. At that time, the supplier MINDA could not reproduce the issue.
  • 03/11/2024 - Subsequent test by MINDA showed a cracked sealing. KTM requested quality improvements from the supplier MINDA. No cracked sealing was reported on production vehicles,
  • 09/04/2024 - A cracked sealing was found on a KTM AG R&D test vehicle.
  • 09/11/2024 - A second KTM AG R&D vehicle was observed to have a cracked seal. KTM AG requested MINDA to conduct further testing.
  • 03/18/2025 - KTM determined a defect existed and a recall for the fuel cap seal was required. No accidents or injuries have been reported.

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain 2019-2023 Husqvarna and KTM motorcycles with equipped with TKC80 tires. Please refer to KTM's recall report for specific model details. Cracks longer than 3/16 inch may develop in the tread grooves. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 119, "New Pneumatic Tires-Other Than Passenger Cars."

Risk. Cracks in the tread can lead to tread separation, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the affected tires, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 16, 2022. Owners may contact KTM's customer service at 1-888-985-6090. KTM's numbers for this recall are KTB2208 and HTB2207.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Under Investigation

What the new part or software changes. Replacement tires of similar products will be installed.

Fixed in production. CTA is in the process of implementing product improvements. April 29, 2022: Additionally, affected tires have been blocked from distribution in the USA since May 1, 2022.

Part made by: Continental Tire the Americas, LLC, United States · Maker's recall no. KTB2208; HTB2207

1190 ADVENTURE R · Wiring Harness could Fray and Brake Line may Melt (brakes and abs): 2013-2016 · 16V854000 · 2016-11-28 · 7,801 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain model year 2015-2016 1290 Super Adventure motorcycles manufactured December 1, 2014, to March 10, 2016, and 2013-2016 1190 Adventure and Adventure R motorcycles manufactured November 20, 2012, to April 25, 2016. In the affected vehicles, the wiring harness can fray and contact the ABS modulator. If the brake line is electrically conductive, the brake line may overheat and melt.

Risk. If the brake line melts, brake failure may result, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. KTM will notify owners, and dealers will re-position the wiring harness for the electronic control unit, free of charge. The manufacturer has not yet provided a notification schedule. Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-888-985-6090.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The wiring harness was not originally secured to the frame at the time of production.

Fixed in production. 2017 MY the wiring harness of street vehicles with ABS have been properly routed.

Maker's recall no. TB1706

How the problem was found.

  • In June 2016 a vehicle in Germany was identified through a customer service claim. Subsequently, an investigation of the vehicle was was done by KTM R&D, Austria.

450 EXC · HANDLEBAR CLAMPS (steering): 2011 · 11V300000 · 2011-05-25 · 1,228 bikes

Problem. Ktm is recalling certain model year 2010 and 2011 motorcycles manufactured from november 2009 through november 23, 2010. Due to improper heat treatment, the handlebar clamps can develop cracks, allowing the handlebar to move from its set position.

Risk. Unexpected movement of the handlebar may cause the driver to crash, possibly resulting in injury or death to the rider or others.

Fix. Authorized ktm dealers will replace the defective clamps free of charge. The safety recall began on may 25, 2011. Owners may contact ktm customer service at 1-888-985-6090 for assistance.
