Recalls (2)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Drivetrain: 2007 · 07V243000 · 2007-06-05 · 1,092 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, during use the chain slider guard mounting bolts may become loose and possibly fall out. This could result in the possibility of the chain slider guard to become detached.

Risk. Subsequently, the chain slider guard could become caught in the drive chain which may lead to a crash.

Fix. Dealers will inspect the chain slider guard mounting bolts and properly secure them. The recall began on june 8, 2007. Owners may contact ktm at 1-440-985-3553.

Brake master cylinder: 2004-2006 · 07V065000 · 2007-02-23 · 2,684 bikes

Problem. On certain 950 motorcycles, the rear brake may feel spongy or soft when applying the brake. This could lead to improper rear braking.

Risk. Improper rear braking could result in a crash.

Fix. Ktm dealers will replace the rear brake system components. The recall began on march 1, 2007. Owners may contact ktm at 1-440-985-3553.
