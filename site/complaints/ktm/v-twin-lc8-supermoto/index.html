Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

REAR BRAKE (brakes and abs): 2005-2006 · 07V065000 · 2007-02-23 · 2,684 bikes

Problem. On certain 950 motorcycles, the rear brake may feel spongy or soft when applying the brake. This could lead to improper rear braking.

Risk. Improper rear braking could result in a crash.

Fix. Ktm dealers will replace the rear brake system components. The recall began on march 1, 2007. Owners may contact ktm at 1-440-985-3553.

2012 KTM V-twin LC8 Supermoto · 9,543 miles · Engine and cooling, Fuel system · CA · filed 2017-05 · NHTSA 10984717Fuel filters clog suddenly and with very short mileage time frame. When clogged they cause fuel pump cavitation that puts air in fuel line. Air in fuel line causes engine to perform erratically and loss of power/sudden power surge. When this happens on freeway/traffic it can cause a loss of control, or a sudden decrease in speed that can lead to motorcycle being hit/run over by other traffic. Ktm has no recommendation on when fuel filters should be replaced. They have also increased the price of the fuel filters from $60 to over $150 in the last 3 years.

I know multiple people with the same bike who's tanks start to leak after they expand like this, that worries me. The tank also does not hold gasoline completely inside, as is evident by the fact that any decals on my bike bubble off from fuel absorbing into the tank then the decal.
