Throttle Failure (throttle and cruise control): 2022-2023 · 26V196000 · 2026-03-27 · 7,974 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. is recalling certain 2022-2023 390 Duke and 2022-2024 RC 390 motorcycles. Water may enter the throttle (e-throttle) control unit and cause it to fail, resulting in loss of drive power while riding.

Risk. Loss of drive power while riding increases the risk of a crash or injury.

Fix. KTM will inspect and replace the e-throttle control, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 30, 2026. Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-888-985-6090. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov March 31, 2026. KTM's number for this recall is KTB2606 & HTB2603. This recall expands previous NHTSA recall number 25V598.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Moisture can migrate into the electronic component of the throttle e-throttle and erode the electrical connection. The ECU detects a deviation between the two voltage values delivered from the e-throttle. The vehicle fault reaction is to set the engine to idle speed. After the ignition change, the e-throttle resumes operation until the next fault detection.

What the new part or software changes. Replacement parts can be recognized with the production date after 2405 (May 2024)

Fixed in production. Starting January 2024, the supplier implemented a 100% camera-based inspection to check printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) after conformal coating and automatic cleaning of PCBA after soldering.

Fix type: Replace · Part made by: Uno Minda Ltd., India · Maker's recall no. KTB2606 & HTB2603

How the problem was found.

  • This recall expands NHTSA 25V598 to include certain KTM models: 2022-2023 KTM 390 Duke and
  • 2022-2024 KTM RC 390. Through ongoing data analysis KTM determined that additional models should be included in the recall population. Q3/Q4 2022: First Single Cases.
  • Jan 2023: Start of KTM internal investigation. Coordination, root cause analysis & testing with manufacturer and supplier
  • Jan 2024: 100% camera base inspection to check PCBA after conformal coating
  • Feb 2024: Automatic Cleaning of circuit board after PCBA & wire terminal soldering
  • Sept 2024: First contact of Transport Canada regarding consumer report to TC
  • Sept 2024: Vehicle inspection of reported vehicle
  • … 10 more steps in the defect report.

Loss of Drive Power (engine and cooling): 2024-2025 · 25V825000 · 2025-12-01 · 6,546 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain 2024 Husqvarna Vitpilen 401, Husqvarna Svartpilen 401, 2024-2025 390 Duke, 2025 390 SMC R, 390 Enduro R, 390 Adventure X, and 390 Adventure R motorcycles. The engine control software may cause the engine to stall at low rpms, resulting in a loss of power.

Risk. A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash or injury.

Fix. KTM and Husqvarna dealers will inspect the motorcycles and update the electronic control unit engine management system (ECU EMS) software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 23, 2026. Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-888-985-6090. The manufacturer's numbers for this recall are KTB2513 & HTB2508.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Software programing

What the new part or software changes. The ECU EMS software will be updated with the improved version KM963EU2596003.

Fixed in production. The updated software optimizes injection timing, fuel amount, ignition timing, throttle opening and ride mode.

Fix type: Software · Part made by: KTM F&E GmbH, Austria · Maker's recall no. KTB2513 & HTB2508

How the problem was found.

  • 07/29/2025 – Customer Service received a report of loss of power while riding.
  • 08/01/2025 – 11/8/2025 Customer service reports, Vehicle Owner Questionnaire reports from NHTSA, and Technical Service reports were reviewed and analyzed by KTM AG, R&D in Austria during its investigation of the condition.
  • 11/06/2025 KTM AG determined a defect existed and a recall for ECU EMS software was required. One owner reported incident has been received through NHTSA VOQ report. KTM began investigating the claim.

Fuel Filler Cap Seal May Leak Fuel (fuel system): 2024 · 25V740000 · 2025-10-30 · 5,488 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. is recalling certain 2024 390 Duke, 250 Duke, 990 Duke, Husqvarna Vitpilen 401, and Husqvarna Svartpilen 401 motorcycles. The seal on the fuel filler cap may develop cracks and leak fuel.

Risk. A fuel leak increases the risk of fire or injury.

Fix. Dealers will inspect and replace the fuel cap gasket, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 15, 2025. Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-888-985-6090. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov March 31, 2026.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Material deviations may cause small cracks to occur on the fuel filler cap seal of the above models. This can lead to the possibility of fuel leaking from the fuel cap.

What the new part or software changes. New design and specification of the gasket. The new fuel cap seal part ( ) has been made with improved production processes.

Fixed in production. New design and specification of the gasket.

Fix type: Replace · Part made by: Minda Corporation Limited, India

How the problem was found.

  • 03/19/2023 – The part supplier, MINDA, observed cracked sealing during lab testing. The condition was not observed on a production motorcycle. At that time, the supplier MINDA could not reproduce the issue.
  • 03/11/2024 - Subsequent test by MINDA showed a cracked sealing. KTM requested quality improvements from the supplier MINDA. No cracked sealing was reported on production vehicles,
  • 09/04/2024 - A cracked sealing was found on a KTM AG R&D test vehicle.
  • 09/11/2024 - A second KTM AG R&D vehicle was observed to have a cracked seal. KTM AG requested MINDA to conduct further testing.
  • 03/18/2025 - KTM determined a defect existed and a recall for the fuel cap seal was required. No accidents or injuries have been reported.
