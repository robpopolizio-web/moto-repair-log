Fuel tank mount: Fuel leak from tank cover mounting insert area: 2015-2016 · 19V087000 · 2019-02-07 · 1,588 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain 2015-2016 1290 Super Adventure motorcycles. Fuel may leak from the fuel tank cover mounting insert area.

Risk. A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.

Fix. KTM has notified owners, and dealers will inspect and replace the fuel tank cover mounting insert, free of charge. The recall began March 1, 2019. Owners may contact KTM customer service 1-888-985-6090. KTM's number for this recall is TB1904.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. During the manufacturing process, molded material did not reach optimal temperature, resulting in unbalanced distribution of materials around the threaded insert.

What the new part or software changes. Parts are imprinted with the date of manufacturer. Remedied parts are those manufactured from February 2016 forward.

Fixed in production. Improved moldering process. The component manufacturer has added a second heater coil to the manufacturing process. The second coil increases the temperature and improves the flowing behavior of the material which results in even distribution around the threaded insert.

Part made by: Elkamet Kunststofftechnik GmbH, Germany · Maker's recall no. TB1904

How the problem was found.

  • On November 16, 2017 KTM Sportmotorcycle GmbH (the Austrian vehicle manufacturer) contacted the Supplier of the 1290 Super Adventure fuel tanks regarding customer service claims of fuel tank leakage reports on Model Year 2015 and 2016 vehicles. Elkamet Kunststofftechnik GmbH (the Supplier of the fuel tank) confirmed an investigation was conducted and found that non-conformities occurred in the …

Brake hose or line: Wiring harness could fray and brake line may melt: 2015-2016 · 16V854000 · 2016-11-28 · 7,801 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain model year 2015-2016 1290 Super Adventure motorcycles manufactured December 1, 2014, to March 10, 2016, and 2013-2016 1190 Adventure and Adventure R motorcycles manufactured November 20, 2012, to April 25, 2016. In the affected vehicles, the wiring harness can fray and contact the ABS modulator. If the brake line is electrically conductive, the brake line may overheat and melt.

Risk. If the brake line melts, brake failure may result, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. KTM will notify owners, and dealers will re-position the wiring harness for the electronic control unit, free of charge. The manufacturer has not yet provided a notification schedule. Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-888-985-6090.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The wiring harness was not originally secured to the frame at the time of production.

Fixed in production. 2017 MY the wiring harness of street vehicles with ABS have been properly routed.

Maker's recall no. TB1706

How the problem was found.

  • In June 2016 a vehicle in Germany was identified through a customer service claim. Subsequently, an investigation of the vehicle was was done by KTM R&D, Austria.

Rear shock: Rear shock may leak oil and lose function: 2015-2016 · 16V279000 · 2016-05-10 · 1,238 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain model year 2015-2016 1290 Super Adventure motorcycles manufactured December 1, 2014, to December 11, 2015. These motorcycles have a rear shock absorber that may leak oil from the rebound damping plug, resulting in a loss of shock function.

Risk. Loss of function of the rear shock may affect the handling of the motorcycle, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. KTM will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the rebound damping plug for leaking and replace the shock absorber, as necessary, and install a new version of the suspension control unit software, free of charge. The recall began June 28, 2016. Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-888-985-6090. KTM's number for this recall is TB1611.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The original suspension control unit (SCU) software was not properly calibrated.

What the new part or software changes. The Suspension Control Unit (SCU) will be flashed with a new version software.

Fixed in production. Model year 2016 production from December 11th, 2015 onward has the latest version SCU software.

Part made by: WP PERFORMANCE SYSTEMS GmbH, Austria · Maker's recall no. TB1611

How the problem was found.

  • Initial warranty claims began in June 2015. After a series of warranty claims for the affected model showed a defect pattern, the service bulletin was revised and identified to be a vehicle recall May 2016.
