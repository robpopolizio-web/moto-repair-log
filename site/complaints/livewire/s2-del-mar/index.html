Electrical and instruments: 2024 · 24V601000 · 2024-08-12 · 788 bikes

Problem. LiveWire EV, LLC (LiveWire) is recalling certain 2024 S2 Del Mar and S2 Mulholland motorcycles. The high voltage fuse may degrade and unexpectedly open, resulting in a loss of drive power.

Risk. Unexpected loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will install a high voltage fuse with an increased amperage rating, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed August 2024. Owners may contact LiveWire customer service at 1-855-387-4337. LiveWire's number for this recall is 1003.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Suspension: 2024 · 24V216000 · 2024-03-20 · 165 bikes

Problem. LiveWire EV, LLC (LiveWire) is recalling certain 2024 S2DM (S2 Del Mar) motorcycles. The rear axle and lower shock joint nuts may not have been properly tightened.

Risk. A loose axle or shock nut can result in a loss of stability, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will inspect, tighten any loose nuts and replace damaged components as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 26, 2024. Owners may contact LiveWire customer service at 1-855-387-4337. LiveWire's number for this recall is 1002.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Electrical and instruments: 2024 · 24V149000 · 2024-02-26 · 523 bikes

Problem. LIVEWIRE EV, LLC (LiveWire) is recalling certain 2024 S2DM (S2 Del Mar) motorcycles. The Vehicle Supervisory Control (VSC) software may initiate and cause a powertrain shutdown as a result of internal software voltage thresholds being exceeded.

Risk. An unexpected powertrain shutdown can cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. LiveWire will release an over-the-air (OTA) software update. Owner notification letters were mailed March 5, 2024. Owners may contact LiveWire customer service at 1-855-387-4337. LiveWire's number for this recall is 1001.
