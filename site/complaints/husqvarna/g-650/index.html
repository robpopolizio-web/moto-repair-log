Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Unexpected Engine Stall (engine and cooling): 2013-2014 · 16V124000 · 2016-03-02 · 1,015 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain model year 2013-2014 Husqvarna TR650 Terra and TR650 Strada motorcycles manufactured January 1, 2012, to December 31, 2013. The affected motorcycles may unexpectedly stall.

Risk. A stall increases the risk of a crash.

Fix. The remedy for this recall is still under development. KTM issued an interim notification to owners on August 16, 2016. The recall began April 2017. Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-440-985-3553.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Calibration in the original ECU software.

Part made by: Magneti Marelli, Italy

How the problem was found.

  • March 6, 2013 BMW sold the Husqvarna brand to KTM AG. The MY 2013 & 2014 vehicles subject to this recall were manufactured by BMW, and sold by its US distributor Husqvarna Motorcycles North America, LLC., up to the time of the sale of the Husqvarna brand to KTM AG, Austria.
  • 2013-2014 NHTSA had 14 complaints filed regardng stalling issues. KTMAG began investigating the claims and sought cooperation from BMW and Magneti Marelli (the ECU manufacture).
  • 2015 KTM Canada received two customer service contacts regarding stalling issues. October 9, 2015 KTM submitted a draft 573 report identifying the stalling issue.
  • Feb 23, 2016, KTMNA submitted an update of its efforts with Magneti Marelli and BMW to investigate the issue and determine a repair.
  • June 2, 2016, BMW advised KTMAG that it will not assist with the repair investigation with Magneti Marelli. Following the notice from BMW, KTMAG and Magnetti Marelli reached an agreement to cooperate with the investigation of the BMW product without support from BMW.
  • March 2017, KTM and Magneti Marrelli finalized the repair plan and logistics for shipping the defective ECU units from dealers to the Michigan facility of Magneti Marelli for remapping, then return to the Husqvarna Motorcycle dealer.
