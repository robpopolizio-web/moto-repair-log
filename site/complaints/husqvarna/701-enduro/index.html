Tyre: Cracks in tire tread grooves/FMVSS 119: 2020 · 22V588000 · 2022-08-05 · 27,399 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain 2019-2023 Husqvarna and KTM motorcycles with equipped with TKC80 tires. Please refer to KTM's recall report for specific model details. Cracks longer than 3/16 inch may develop in the tread grooves. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 119, "New Pneumatic Tires-Other Than Passenger Cars."

Risk. Cracks in the tread can lead to tread separation, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the affected tires, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 16, 2022. Owners may contact KTM's customer service at 1-888-985-6090. KTM's numbers for this recall are KTB2208 and HTB2207.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Under Investigation

What the new part or software changes. Replacement tires of similar products will be installed.

Fixed in production. CTA is in the process of implementing product improvements. April 29, 2022: Additionally, affected tires have been blocked from distribution in the USA since May 1, 2022.

Part made by: Continental Tire the Americas, LLC, United States · Maker's recall no. KTB2208; HTB2207

  • Continental Tire 573 Saftey Recall Report 22T-006 provides the following chronology: February 2022: CTA filed a Noncompliance Information Report (NHTSA ID 22T003) to initiate a recall of the complete ContiScoot tire line because of potential tread groove cracking. Clutch slave cylinder: Inoperable clutch: 2020 · 21V792000 · 2021-10-12 · 8,398 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain 2018-2020 690 Enduro R, Vitpilen 701, 701 Enduro, 701 Supermoto, 2019-2020 690 SMC R, Svartpilen, 2018-2019 690 Duke, and 2020 701 Enduro LR motorcycles. The bellow-style gasket on the clutch slave cylinder may have been damaged during manufacturing, which could cause the clutch to become inoperable and not disengage when the clutch lever is pulled in.

Risk. An inoperable clutch increases the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will inspect the clutch slave cylinder and replace it if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed December 3, 2021. Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-888-985-6090. KTM's numbers for this recall are KTB2112 and HTB2110.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Damages of the gasket caused by deviations during assembly may affect the function of the clutch slave cylinder

What the new part or software changes. If the bellow style gasket clutch slave cylinder is mounted it must be replaced by the new version that has a new part number.

Fixed in production. The part manufacturer changed the design of the clutch slave cylinder. The new design does not have a bellow-style gasket.

Part made by: Gustav Magenwirth GmbH & Co. KG, Germany · Maker's recall no. KTB2112/HTB2110

  • In November 2018, increased warranty claims were recognized by KTM AG Customer Service (the Austrian manufacturer).

Fuel hose or line: Fuel lines may chafe and cause fuel leak: 2020 · 21V363000 · 2021-05-17 · 171 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling all 2020 Husqvarna 701 Enduro LR motorcycles. The fuel lines on the front fuel tank may chafe against the tank, possibly resulting in a fuel leak.

Risk. A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.

Fix. Dealers will install two new fuel lines and an additional clamp, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 11, 2021. Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-888-985-6090. KTM's number for this recall is HTB2105.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The cause of the defect is from the shape and mounting position of the original fuel lines on the front fuel tank.

What the new part or software changes. Replacement parts are identified with a unique newly assigned part number.

Fixed in production. In the assembly process, the newly designed parts will be used and installed.

Part made by: Conti Tech, Austria · Maker's recall no. HTB2105

How the problem was found.

  • In December 2020, while performing long-term durability testing by KTM AG R&D, it was determined that a condition exists with the front tank fuel line assembly that may cause abrasions to the lines. As a result, KTM R&D directed KTM AG Quality Control to investigate the cause. 2020 Husqvarna 701 Enduro · Engine · CO · filed 2021-07 · NHTSA 11426010The clutch slave cylinder on the KTM 690 / Husqvarna 701 (same engine) is a known weak point. Mine failed after 130 miles on the odometer. When the cylinder seal fails the clutch lever will not allow the driver of the vehicle to disengage the clutch. The driver has two options - avoid stopping because it is unlikely the driver will be able to restart the vehicle or kill the engine at the risk of not being able to get to a safe place to get out of traffic. I took the motorcycle to the dealer (Fay Myers Denver CO) yesterday and was told they have seen the same problem with four other motorcycles just at that dealership. There are many Youtube videos warning owners that the clutch slave cylinder needs to be replaced immediately. I should have followed that advice but I'm wondering why there isn't a recall when this is a known and pervasive problem. One of the Youtube videos mentions that this problem happened to someone who bought the motorcycle and was riding home for the first time from the dealership. There are no warning lamps messages or other symptoms to let the driver know there is a problem. I called customer support Husqvarana at 1 855 282 6227 on Tuesday 7/20 and again today 7/24 and have not received a return phone call. I will likely get the motorcycle back from repairs sometime next week.
2020 Husqvarna 701 Enduro · Engine · NV · filed 2021-07 · NHTSA 11423826As I approached an intersection with a stop sign for my direction of travel I pulled the clutch lever in to slow my speed and downshift the motorcycle. The clutch lever pulled all the way to the handlebar without disengaging the clutch. I had to lock/skid the rear brake to avoid being hit by an oncoming automobile. Fortunately the driver of the vehicle saw me skidding past the stop sign and swerved to avoid hitting me. I had to put the motorcycle into first gear and press the starter button as I pushed to get moving again. At the next intersection I again had to use the brakes to kill the engine to avoid being hit by oncoming vehicles. Using the starter motor was once again required to get the motorcycle moving. I had the ABS disabled as I was returning to the highway after traveling on a dirt road. Had the ABS been activated I would not have been able to avoid being hit by the automobile. Husqvarna refused to repair the failed "O Ring" in the Clutch Slave Cylinder claiming it's not a safety issue! Had I not been a rider with 50 plus years of experience this could have been a fatal accident. This is a know problem for Husqvarna/KTM and they refuse to address it. The internet is rife with other riders having the exact same problem. My local Husqvarna dealer offered to sell me an aftermarket replacement Clutch Slave Cylinder even though my motorcycle is still under the original factory warranty. They told me that Husqvarna has refused to cover countless requests to repair the faulty seal under the warranty. Had my wife or daughter been operating the motorcycle there is a good chance they would not have reacted in time to avoid injury or death.
