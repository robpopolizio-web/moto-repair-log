Rear Brake Caliper May Crack and Break (brakes and abs): 2022-2024 · 26V443000 · 2026-07-08 · 6,257 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Husqvarna FE 501S, Husqvarna FE 350S, 2024 GasGas ES 350, and GasGas ES 500 motorcycles. During backwards rolling, the brake caliper may crack near the primary pin casting, which may cause the caliper to break when riding.

Risk. Cracks or breaks in the rear brake caliper can reduce braking ability, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealer will replace the rear brake caliper, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 31, 2026. Owners may contact KTM and Husqvarna customer service at 1-888-985-6090. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov July 15, 2026.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Weakness in the primary pin area.

What the new part or software changes. The component manufacture developed a new design of brake caliper in the affected area (primary pin area) that increased the pin area from 6.5 to 10.5 millimeters.

Fixed in production. The new designed brake caliper has been used in production starting in Model Year 2025.

Fix type: Replace · Part made by: J.Juan S.A.U., Spain

How the problem was found.

  • As part of ongoing market monitoring activities, KTM Austria Customer Service received the first report of a rear brake primary pin fracture in March 2024. The reported case was subsequently shared with the component supplier, J.Juan, for review.
  • KTM Austria and J.Juan initiated a detailed investigation of the component and the associated operating conditions in early 2024. Following a comprehensive analysis, the companies identified and confirmed the root cause of the issue in November 2024.
  • Based on the investigation findings, an improved version of the component was developed and implemented in production beginning with Model Year (MY) 2025 vehicles. Front Brake Caliper May Crack And Break (brakes and abs): 2022-2024 · 25V074000 · 2025-02-07 · 5,116 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Husqvarna FE 350s, FE 501s, and 2024-2025 GASGAS ES 350 and ES 500 motorcycles. The front brake caliper may crack in the area around the upper guide pin.

Risk. A broken front brake caliper can cause a loss of front brake function, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will inspect and replace the front brake caliper, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 11, 2025. Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-888-985-6090.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Irregularities were found in the casting structure of one of the brake supplier's sub-suppliers. In the worst case, this can lead to the front brake caliper cracking in the area around the upper guide pin.

What the new part or software changes. Casting irregularities are seen in the top portion of the caliper and noted in the manufactures bulletin. Vehicles produced later may also have "centering holes” that are not affected by the recall or replacement.

Fixed in production. The part manufacturer changed the sub-suppliers to the sub-supplier which had no casting issue.

Fix type: Inspect, Repair · Part made by: J. Juan, Spain

How the problem was found.

  • KTM Sportmotorcycle GmbH was made aware of the problem on February 8, 2024, through ServiceNow Ticket CS0518390 that came from the US. KTM GmbH initiated an investigation on February 8, 2024, and forwarded a part for analysis to an external laboratory on February 13, 2024. Cracks in Tire Tread Grooves/FMVSS 119 (tyres): 2020-2023 · 22V588000 · 2022-08-05 · 27,399 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain 2019-2023 Husqvarna and KTM motorcycles with equipped with TKC80 tires. Please refer to KTM's recall report for specific model details. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 119, "New Pneumatic Tires-Other Than Passenger Cars."

Brake Pad Retaining Clip Mounted Incorrectly (brakes and abs): 2022 · 22V014000 · 2022-01-14 · 394 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain 2022 Husqvarna 350s and Husqvarna 501s motorcycles. The brake pad retaining clips on the front brake caliper may have been mounted incorrectly, which could result in loose or detached front brake pads.

Risk. Loose or detached front brake pads can reduce braking performance, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.

Fix. Owners are advised to not ride their motorcycles until the repair has been performed. Dealers will replace the front and rear brake pad retaining clips, free of charge. Owners notification letters were mailed February 2, 2022. Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-888-985-6090. KTM's number for this recall is HTB2111. This recall supersedes NHTSA recall 21V-678. Motorcycles that were previously repaired under recall 21V-678 will need to have the new remedy performed under this recall.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Brake pad retaining "R" clips on the front brake caliper may be mounted incorrectly by the manufacturer.

What the new part or software changes. This new recall is to replace the originally mounted retaining “R” clips with new version “R” clips that has an additional retaining mechanism. We are also replacing the retaining “R” clips on the rear brake caliper for consistency on the motorcycle. This is to prevent the mixing up of retaining “R” clips by equipping the motorcycle with like clips on both the front brake and rear brake caliper.

Fixed in production. The new clip is being used in the assembly process.

Part made by: J Juan SA, Spain · Maker's recall no. HTB2111

Fork Piston Rod may Detach from Fork Cap (suspension): 2015 · 15V055000 · 2015-02-02 · 249 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain model year 2015 Husqvarna FE 350 S and FE 501 S motorcycles manufactured from July 23, 2014, to October 17, 2014. In the forks of the affected motorcycles, the threaded connection between the piston rod and fork cap may not have been sufficiently tightened and therefore the piston rod could become stuck within the fork leg.

Risk. If the piston rod detaches within the fork leg, the front suspension could malfunction and destabilize the motorcycle, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. KTM will notify owners, and Husqvarna motorcycle dealers will inspect the torque of the fork piston rod to the fork cap, correcting it as necessary, free of charge. The recall began January 21, 2015. Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-888-985-6090. KTM's number for this recall is TB 1503. Note: This recall supersedes recall 14V-772 in its entirety.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The cause of the defect is due to a change in the production process at the fork supplier, resulting in improper tightening torque of the piston rod to the fork cap. To remedy this defect, the supplier has changed its torqueing procedure to achieve the proper tightening torque of the piston rod to the fork cap.

What the new part or software changes. The piston rod is not tightened properly to the fork cap in defective fork leg assemblies. The corrective action is to tighten the piston rod to the fork cap at the proper torque. Upon completion of the repair a “dot” is punched onto the piston rod lock nut to identify the remedied component.

Part made by: WP Performance Systems GmbH, Austria · Maker's recall no. TB 1503

How the problem was found.

  • The fork supplier WP Performance Systems GmbH has started an investigation after initial reports of piston rods detaching from the fork cap around October 15, 2014. By October 16, 2014 the software used at WP’s assembly of the forks has been revised to reflect the proper tightening torque and the entire stock of already produced fork legs has been inspected.
