Inoperable Clutch (clutch, gearbox and final drive): 2018-2020 · 21V792000 · 2021-10-12 · 8,398 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain 2018-2020 690 Enduro R, Vitpilen 701, 701 Enduro, 701 Supermoto, 2019-2020 690 SMC R, Svartpilen, 2018-2019 690 Duke, and 2020 701 Enduro LR motorcycles. The bellow-style gasket on the clutch slave cylinder may have been damaged during manufacturing, which could cause the clutch to become inoperable and not disengage when the clutch lever is pulled in.

Risk. An inoperable clutch increases the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will inspect the clutch slave cylinder and replace it if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed December 3, 2021. Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-888-985-6090. KTM's numbers for this recall are KTB2112 and HTB2110.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Damages of the gasket caused by deviations during assembly may affect the function of the clutch slave cylinder

What the new part or software changes. If the bellow style gasket clutch slave cylinder is mounted it must be replaced by the new version that has a new part number.

Fixed in production. The part manufacturer changed the design of the clutch slave cylinder. The new design does not have a bellow-style gasket.

Part made by: Gustav Magenwirth GmbH & Co. KG, Germany · Maker's recall no. KTB2112/HTB2110

  • In November 2018, increased warranty claims were recognized by KTM AG Customer Service (the Austrian manufacturer).

License Plate Bracket May Contact Rear Wheel (frame and bodywork): 2018-2020 · 21V627000 · 2021-08-10 · 1,506 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain 2018-2020 Husqvarna Motorcycle 701 Vitpilen and 2019-2020 701 Svartpilen motorcycles. The gear teeth on the license plate holder and swingarm may be positioned incorrectly, possibly resulting in a loose rear axle nut, causing the license plate holder to contact the rear wheel.

Risk. License plate bracket interference with the rear wheel can increase the risk of a crash or injury.

Fix. Dealers will inspect and realign the gear teeth on the license plate holder and swingarm, and inspect the tightness of the rear wheel axle collar, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on October 1, 2021. Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-888-985-6090. KTM's number for this recall is HTB2106.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. During the assembly, the gear teeth on the license plate holder and swingarm may have incorrect alignment, which may result in the rear wheel axle nut becoming loose and cause the license plate bracket to fall down and contact the rear wheel.

What the new part or software changes. The unit must be inspected by an authorized Husqvarna Motorcycle dealer to determine if the condition exists and is corrected.

Fixed in production. No correction was needed in the line due to the dicontinued production of the Svartpilen and Vitpilen models in Feb 2020.

Part made by: Leiber Group Gmbh & Co. Kg, Poland · Maker's recall no. HTB2106

How the problem was found.

  • In January 2021, KTM Sportmotorcycle GmbH (the Austrian manufacturer) customer service received a report of the loose axle nut. KTM AG Quality Control investigated and determined the cause was related to a misalignment of the gear teeth on the license plate carrier and swingarm. Possible Fuel Leak (fuel system): 2018-2020 · 20V170000 · 2020-03-19 · 1,500 bikes

Problem. KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Husqvarna 701 Svartpilen and 2018-2020 Husqvarna 701 Vitpilen motorcycles. Fuel may leak from the fuel level sensor and fuel pump mounting area.

Risk. A fuel leak can increase the risk of a fire in the presence of an ignition source, and increase the risk of damage or injury.

Fix. KTM will notify owners, and Husqvarna Motorcycle Dealers will replace the affected fuel tank, install new gaskets, and update software for proper fuel calibration, free of charge. Owners will be notified to take their motorcycles to a Husqvarna Motorcycle North America, Inc. (HMNA) dealership for repairs. The recall began July 7, 2020. Owners may contact HMNA customer service at 1-888-985-6090. HMNA's number for this recall is HTB2012.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The cause of the defect is due to the unevenness that may occur on the sealing surfaces in the area of the fuel pump and fuel level sensor during the vehicle's life cycle. If unevenness occurs it does not allow for proper sealing between the fuel level sensor mounting area and fuel pump mounting area.

What the new part or software changes. Parts are imprinted with the date of manufacturer. Remedied parts are those manufactured from March 2020 forward

Fixed in production. To remedy this defect, a new fuel tank has been developed to install on the motorcycle to prevent fuel leakage. The new fuel tank has an increased wall thickness to prevent unevenness and improve sealing behavior. The increased wall thickness reduces the tank volume by approximately 0.8 liters. The total fuel tank capacity of the new fuel tank is approximately 11.2 liters

Maker's recall no. HTB2012

How the problem was found.

  • KTM Sportmotorcycle GmbH (the Austrian vehicle manufacturer) became aware of an issue through a warranty claim. As a result of the information received and investigation by KTM Sportmotorcycle, it was determined a recall should be issued for affected vehicles.
