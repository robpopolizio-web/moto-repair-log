Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Fuel tank: 1999 · 99V299000 · 1999-10-28 · 857 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The fuel hose disconnects between fuel pump regulator and fuel tank outlet nozzle. The fuel pump and hose are located inside the fuel tank assembly.

Risk. This condition could cause the engine to stop without warning affecting the handling of the motorcycle.

Fix. Excelsior-henderson has gone out of business. The correction was to install a plastic safety tie to secure the fuel hose.
