Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

FUEL CAP ASSEMBLY (fuel system): 2005-2007 · 08V071000 · 2008-02-14 · 3,292 bikes

Problem. Hyosung is recalling 3,292 my 2005-2007 gt650 and gv650 motorcycles. These motorcycles were built with fuel tank cap gaskets that prevent proper tank ventilation.

Risk. This could result in vehicle stalling which could result in a crash and/or fuel leakage which could result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will modify the existing gas cap gasket. The recall began on april 1, 2008. Owners may contact hyosung at 1-770-447-5571.
