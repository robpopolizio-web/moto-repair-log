Recalls (2)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Fuel system: 2005-2007 · 08V071000 · 2008-02-14 · 3,292 bikes

Problem. Hyosung is recalling 3,292 my 2005-2007 gt650 and gv650 motorcycles. These motorcycles were built with fuel tank cap gaskets that prevent proper tank ventilation.

Risk. This could result in vehicle stalling which could result in a crash and/or fuel leakage which could result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will modify the existing gas cap gasket. The recall began on april 1, 2008. Owners may contact hyosung at 1-770-447-5571.

Handlebar: 2006 · 07V015000 · 2007-01-23 · 862 bikes

Problem. On certain cruisers equipped with allen head handle bar bolts, the handle bar bolts are too short.

Risk. These bolts could loosen causing a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the bolts. The recall began on february 26, 2007. Owners may contact hyosung at 770-447-5571.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (1)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Gv650 Aquila 2006 · 2007-02-28 · no. 2007050 · 107 bikes · Steering

On certain motorcycles, the handlebar bolts are too short, and, may not have received adequate tightening torque during vehicle assembly. These bolts could loosen, causing a loss of steering control and increasing the risk of a crash. Correction: Dealers will replace the 25mm handlebar bolts with 30mm bolts, and torque new bolts to specs.

Source: Transport Canada

Fuel leak / stalling: 2005-2007 · Preliminary evaluation PE07056 · 2007–2008

Odi received a vehicle owner questionnaire (voq) alleging a hyosung gv650 leaked fuel from a fuel tank seam.after a hyosung dealer replaced the tank, fuel again leaked from the motorcycle and it began to stall.subsequently, hyosung advised the customer that the problem could have been avoided if the dealer had followed the instructions in its april 2007 tsb.that tsb acknowledged the subject vehicles fuel tank was not venting properly due to an improperly fabricated gas cap gasket.without proper venting, the bikes would stall or the fuel tank would collapse and leak or both.on february 12, 2008, after discussing the issue with odi staff, hyosung agreed to conduct a safety recall to modify the gas cap gasket.with that recall, this pe is closed. It led to recall 08V071000.
