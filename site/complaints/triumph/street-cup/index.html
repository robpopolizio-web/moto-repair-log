Clutch Cable may Cause Loss of Electrical Power (electrical and instruments): 2017-2018 · 19V133000 · 2019-02-27 · 12,654 bikes

Problem. Triumph Motorcycles America, LTD (Triumph) is recalling certain 2016-2019 Bonneville T120 and Bonneville T120 Black, 2017-2019 Bonneville T100 and T100 Black, 2017-2018 Street Cup and Street Scrambler, and 2016-2018 Street Twin motorcycles. The clutch cable may contact the main harness cover and cause damage to wiring within the main harness. This may lead to a loss of electrical power to various electrical circuits.

Risk. Loss of electrical power may cause the headlight or indicator lighting to malfunction or the engine to stall, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Triumph will notify owners, and dealers will replace the original securing guide for the clutch cable and main harness with an updated one, free of charge. The recall began March 27, 2019. Owners may contact Triumph customer service at 1-678-854-2010. Triumph's number for this recall is SRAN560.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The headstock tidy (guide) situated behind the head stock on the left-hand side of the main frame is used to position the clutch cable and main harness, which in its original condition may allow the clutch cable to contact the main harness sheath, potentially causing damage to the wiring within the main harness.

What the new part or software changes. Affected models are equipped with headstock tidy Triumph Part No: T2501779. This component will be replaced with Triumph Part No: T2502438 for models Street Twin, Street Scrambler and Street Cup and T2502688 for models Bonneville T100, Bonneville T100 Black, Bonneville T120 and Bonneville T120 Black. The remedy part will be included within in-service kits T2502437 and T2502687.

Fixed in production. Triumph introduced a modified headstock tidy (Triumph Part No: T2502438 for models Street Twin, Street Scrambler and Street Cup on 7th August 2018 and T2502688 for models Bonneville T100, Bonneville T100 Black, Bonneville T120 and Bonneville T120 Black on 14th January 2019.

Maker's recall no. SRAN560

How the problem was found.

  • November 2018: Central Warranty Dept. observed claims of in-service non-functioning headlamps, turn signals and engine stalls on certain Bonneville family models. Arrangements were made for return of reported malfunctioning parts.
  • December 2018 – January 2019: Triumph’s Quality Dept. conducted an investigation on certain Bonneville family models into the issue, including inspection of returned parts. Scope of investigation enlarged to include certain Street Twin, Street Scrambler and Street Cup models. 21st February 2019: Quality Dept. briefed Recall Committee on the investigation findings. Wiring for Hazard Warning Lights may be Damaged (lights): 2017-2018 · 17V426000 · 2017-07-03 · 636 bikes

Problem. Triumph Motorcycles America, LTD (Triumph) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Triumph Street Cup motorcycles. The wiring harness for the hazard warning lights may be pulled and strained during normal steering action.

Risk. The pulled wires may cause the wires to pull loose from the connector resulting in a loss of the hazard warning lights, reducing the motorcycles visibility when hazard warning lights are wanted, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Triumph will notify owners, and dealers will extend the wiring length by installing a supplemental harness, free of charge. The recall began July 18, 2017. Owners may contact Triumph customer service at 1-678-854-2010. Triumph's number for this recall is Safety Recall 549.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The attachment of the electrical block connector to the headlamp bowl bracket restricts the free movement of the connector, which in turn places a strain on the electrical sub harness from the right-hand switch cube.

What the new part or software changes. The remedy component will not be attached to the headlamp bowl bracket, but will instead be free to move with the action of the steering.

Fixed in production. The condition was corrected in production by a change to the assembly process, whereby the subject electrical connector was no longer attached to the headlamp bracket. This change avoided straining the right hand switch cube sub-harness. This change was introduced on April 1st, 2017.

Maker's recall no. Safety Recall 549

How the problem was found.

  • 28th Jan 2017: First in-service report of inoperative hazard warning lights.
  • Mar 2017: Triumph received additional warranty claims involving inoperable hazard warning lamps. Preliminary investigation commences. 4th Apr 2017: Triumph Motorcycles Ltd Quality Dept. commences investigation. Parts associated with claims returned to UK for examination during April.
  • May 2017: Additional warranty claims investigated. Information and parts returned from the field to Triumph Motorcycles Ltd Quality Dept. to assist with investigation. 25th Jun 2017: Triumph Motorcycles Ltd Quality Dept. completes its investigation; root cause identified. 27th Jun 2017: Triumph Quality Dept. updates Triumph Recall Committee of investigation findings. 2018 Triumph Street Cup · Electrical and instruments, Steering · CA · filed 2026-11 · NHTSA 11776581The electrical wiring harness near the steering head and headlight bracket area of my 2018 Triumph Street Cup has worn through its protective tape, and bare wire is visible. I took the motorcycle to an authorized Triumph dealer because I felt increased resistance when turning the handlebars. The technician confirmed the cables are rubbing and worn, and told me this is causing the steering resistance and could lead to electrical failure and a crash. This puts my safety and others' at risk, because steering resistance or electrical failure while riding could cause a loss of control. The motorcycle is available for inspection. I saw no warning lamps or messages; the first symptom was steering resistance. When the wear began is unkwown, probably since day 1. The dealer told me a safety recall exists for this issue on 2018 Street Cup models. Triumph told me my motorcycle has no open recalls and to see my dealer. The dealer quoted about $1,000 in parts and 9 hours of labor. Other owners online report similar cable rubbing on this model. I am concerned the recall is not showing for my VIN and other owners may be unaware of this safety issue. The dealer's written repair order is available on request.
2017 Triumph Street Cup · Frame and bodywork, Other or not stated · TX · filed 2024-05 · NHTSA 11586516Kick stand spring came off of the bike. Kick stand was dragging and engine ignition was cut off by the safety switch. I lost control of the bike mid-turn d/t wheel rear drag. I did not crash d/t decades of riding experience., but did leave my traffic lane. Examination of the vehicle showed inadequate spring retention tabs that were badly worn. A Triumph forum stated others had similar experience. Crashes have resulted. The Triumph manufacturer was aware of the problem and blamed spring fatigue, but the factory had not issued a recall. Numerous Triumph motorcycles have the same kick stand design..
