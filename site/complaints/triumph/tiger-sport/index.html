Pannier May Detach During Operation (accessories and equipment): 2022-2024 · 26V716000 · 2026-10-28 · 4,008 bikes

Problem. Triumph Motorcycles America, Ltd. (Triumph) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Tiger Sport, 2025-2027 Tiger Sport 660, 2025-2027 Tiger Sport 800, and 2026-2027 Tiger Sport 800 Touring motorcycles equipped with original equipment or accessory panniers. The motorcycles may experience insufficient pannier retention, which could result in a pannier detaching from the motorcycle during operation.

Risk. A detached pannier can cause an uneven change in vehicle weight distribution, affecting motorcycle stability, and increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the pannier locking mechanism on all motorcycles, inspect the upper pannier mounting assembly and replace components, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed November 17, 2026. Owners may contact Triumph customer service at 1-678-854-2010. Triumph's number for this recall is SRAN 649. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov on November 15, 2026.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The pannier attachment system design, including the slider mechanism, retention pin and third mounting point, may not consistently provide adequate engagement with the mounting bracket. Testing identified the potential for a missing third bush on the mounting assembly as fitted to the motorcycle.

What the new part or software changes. The remedy incorporates design improvements to the slider mechanism, retention pin and third mounting arm adjustment, along with updated customer fitment instructions. In addition, manufacturing process controls have been improved to eliminate the risk of a missing bush within the upper mounting assembly. Validation testing confirmed significantly improved retention performance with no adverse effects on vehicle stability.

Fixed in production. Redesigned remedy components will arrive at Triumph production facility on the 04th November in readiness for resumption of production of all affected models.

Fix type: Replace · Part made by: Givi S.P.A., Italy · Maker's recall no. SRAN 649

How the problem was found.

  • 30/06/26: Development testing on a new model highlighted a potential issue with pannier disconnection. Internal investigation commences. In service records checked and defective parts requested from markets and examined.
  • 28/07/26 - Formal Quality investigation commences. Parts requested from service. Fit up trials and disassembly of panniers and mounting brackets commence to determine root cause issues.
  • 19/10/26: Quality Dept conclude investigations. Meeting considers conclusion of Quality Dept investigation. Further clarification required

Transmission May Pop Out of Gear (clutch, gearbox and final drive): 2012-2013 · 13V212000 · 2013-05-21 · 349 bikes

Problem. Triumph Motorcycles (Triumph) is recalling certain model year 2012-2013 Speed Triple ABS motorcycles manufactured June 2012, through January 2013. The transmission on some motorcycles may have been built with an incorrect detent spring.

Risk. As a result of the incorrect spring, the transmission may inadvertently shift out of gear while the motorcycle is moving, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Triumph will notify owners, and dealers will install a revised detent spring, free of charge. The recall began on June , 17 2013. Owners may contact Triumph at 1-678-854-2010 for more information.
