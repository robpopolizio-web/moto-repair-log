Recalls (4)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Fuel hose or line: 2000-2004 · 04V156000 · 2004-04-07 · 18,998 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the fuel connector linking each of the feed and return fuel hoses to the fuel pump mounting plate may fracture following in-service handling.

Risk. Fuel could escape from the fractured body of the connector onto the engine or side of the bike. Fuel leakage, in the presence of an ignition source, can result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will replace the connector. The recall began on july 30, 2004. Owners should contact triumph at 1-678-854-2010.

Clutch lever: 2000-2001 · 01V339000 · 2001-11-02 · 3,427 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The clutch control cable can fray and fail. The barrel at the end of the inner cable seizes in the control lever bore, thereby stressing the inner cable.

Risk. This causes the rider to be unable to engage the clutch, increasing stopping distances and inhibiting driving control.

Fix. Dealers will replace the clutch cable. Owner notificationbegan january 25, 2002. Owners who take their vehicles to an authorized dealer on an agreed upon service date and do not receive the free remedy within a reasonable time should contact triumph at 678-854-2010.

Fuel tank: 2000-2001 · 00V312000 · 2000-10-11 · 1,929 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. A fuel leak could occur from the fuel lever sender located inside the fuel tank.

Risk. If this condition occurs in the presence of an ignition source, possibly could result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will replace the fuel sender.

Suspension: 2000 · 00V043000 · 2000-02-16 · 137 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The final drive chain side plate was not hardened correctly.

Risk. This chain could fracture causing a loss of drive to the rear wheel.

Fix. Dealers will replace the drive chains.

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Sprint Rs 2000-2004 · 2004-04-27 · no. 2004147 · 1,200 bikes · Fuel System

On certain motorcycles, the male fuel connector linking each of the feed and return fuel hoses to the fuel pump mounting plate may be found to contain a fracture following in-service handling. Fuel could escape from the fractured body of the connector onto the engine or side of the bike. Fuel leakage, in the presence of an ignition source, can result in a fire. Correction: Dealer will replace the connector.

When removed for routine maintenance, the tank requires significant pressure to get it reinstalled. Also, when installed, the tank does not fit into the recess properly and interferes with the movement of the handlebars in sharp right and left hand turning.
2004 Triumph Sprint RS · Fuel system · PA · filed 2015-09 · NHTSA 10778902Exact same concern as the report listed in complaint 10554076. The tank, once removed from bike, loses shape and cannot be re-installed without *extreme* and excessive force. Prior to removing the tank, I noted that the tank was straining the three mounting bushings on the frame. Based on dialogue with other rs and sprint owners here and abroad, there seems to be a consistent dialogue as this issue occurring with the introduction of ethanol into the common fuel supply.
2004 Triumph Sprint RS · 26,000 miles · Fuel system · TX · filed 2015-04 · NHTSA 10706291There is an existing recall on this model and several others for the plastic fuel connectors, defect investigation pe03053 / nhtsa campaign id number: 04v156000. My 2004 sprint rs, which had the recall work done, was parked for extensive, unrelated, work, when a friend with a triumph daytona came over. He had not had the recall done on his bike and his fuel connector broke while he was here. I removed the connector from my 2004 sprint rs and installed it on his daytona and got him back on the road. He ordered me a replacement connector from a triumph dealer, triumph part number t1241165. The part that arrived, dated october 1st, 2014, was the plastic fitting that was subject to recall. The dealer informed me that my part number is correct, and that he is no longer able to order the aluminum part used to replace the plastic one in the recall, as that part number has been superseded by the number for the plastic part. In brief: triumph is supplying a part subject to recall instead of the replacement part that should be supplied.
