Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Wheel: 2001-2002 · 02V242000 · 2002-09-10 · 1,557 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the rear wheel needle roller bearing as fitted only to certain single sided swinging arm motorcycles can deteriorate and seize causing the rear wheel to lock-up.

Risk. This can cause a loss of control of the motorcycle.

Fix. Dealers will replace all rear wheel bearings. Owner notification began september 19, 2002. Owners who take their vehicles to an authorized dealer on an agreed upon service date and do not receive the free remedy within a reasonable time should contact triumph at 1-678-854-2010.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Recalls in Canada and Japan (2)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Daytona 955 1997-2004 · 2004-04-27 · no. 2004147 · 1,200 bikes · Fuel System

On certain motorcycles, the male fuel connector linking each of the feed and return fuel hoses to the fuel pump mounting plate may be found to contain a fracture following in-service handling. Fuel could escape from the fractured body of the connector onto the engine or side of the bike. Fuel leakage, in the presence of an ignition source, can result in a fire. Correction: Dealer will replace the connector.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Daytona 900 1994 · 1994-03-22 · no. 1994061 · 56 bikes · Other

These vehicles do not comply with c.m.v.s.s 115 - vehicle identification number. V.I.n. Code depicts vehicles as 1993 models and not as 1994 models. Correction: new v.I.n. Labels reflecting 1994 model year will be installed on affected vehicles.
