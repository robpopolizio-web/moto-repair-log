Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Accessories: Reflex reflector may detach/FMVSS 108: 2020 · 20V451000 · 2020-08-03 · 603 bikes

Problem. Triumph Motorcycles America, LTD (Triumph) is recalling certain Tiger 900, Rally, Rally Pro, GT, GT Low and GT Pro motorcycles. The rear reflex reflectors may detach. As such, these motorcycles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, reflective devices, and associated Equipment."

Risk. Detachment of the rear reflector may reduce the visibility of the motorcycle to other drivers, increasing risk of crash.

Fix. Triumph will notify owners, and dealers will replace the rear reflex reflectors, free of charge. The recall began August 19, 2020. Owners may contact Triumph customer service at 1-800-232-3780. Triumph's number for this recall is SRAN 582.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Problems in the reflex reflector manufacturing process and tooling resulted in parts containing incorrectly dimensioned spigots. The spigots are used to locate and fasten the reflex reflector to the motorcycle. The supplier corrected the process and tooling to ensure parts conform to the drawing specification.

What the new part or software changes. Triumph dealers will replace the defective rear reflex reflectors (Triumph part nos: T2700650 and T2707111) with a replacement remedy reflector contained in Triumph remedy spares kit T2706969.

Fixed in production. Assured parts were received from the supplier. The condition was corrected in production on 28/11/19 at VIN AA9677 by use of remedy reflex reflectors.

Part made by: ECIE Srl, Italy · Maker's recall no. SRAN 582

How the problem was found.

  • April 2020: Triumph Central Warranty Team identifies a number of warranty claims for missing rear reflex reflectors on Tiger 900 family models. The issue is raised with the Manufacturing Dept. for review and investigation.
  • April to mid-May: Triumph Manufacturing Dept. undertakes review of manufacturing processes and liaises with Triumph Quality Dept. and supplier. An affected provisional range of motorcycles is identified. Mid-May to mid-July 2020: Triumph Central Warranty Team continues to monitor in-service warranty claims.
  • July 28th 2020: Meeting called to review the data and investigation outcome. Decision to conduct a non- compliance recall agreed.
