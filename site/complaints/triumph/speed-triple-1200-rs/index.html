Cooling system: Engine may overheat and leak coolant: 2022-2024 · 24V177000 · 2024-03-06 · 1,545 bikes

Problem. Triumph Motorcycles America, Ltd. (Triumph) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Speed Triple RS, and Speed Triple RR motorcycles. The engine may overheat and cause the fan motor to fail, resulting in a coolant leak.

Risk. A coolant leak can increase the risk of injury.

Fix. Dealers will replace the original engine software calibration with a new version, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 26, 2024. Owners may contact Triumph customer service at 1-678-854-2010. Triumph's number for this recall is SRAN 620.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. In conditions of high ambient temperature and slow-moving traffic, the radiator fan may surpass its maximum operating temperature, resulting in fan motor failure. Ignoring the warning light on the instrument panel and continuing to ride at low speeds may potentially cause coolant displacement.

What the new part or software changes. Adjusting the software calibration reconfigures the vehicle's ignition system, leading to a decrease in engine temperature.

Fixed in production. This revised tune went into production on 13th Feb 2024.

Part made by: Alexon Co Ltd., Thailand · Maker's recall no. SRAN 620

Cooling fan: Radiator fan motor may fail and cause coolant leak: 2022-2023 · 23V006000 · 2023-01-16 · 1,351 bikes

Problem. Triumph Motorcycles America, Ltd. (Triumph) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Speed Triple RS and Speed Triple RR motorcycles. The radiator fan may overheat, causing the fan motor to fail.

Risk. An inoperative fan motor can result in leaking or spraying hot coolant, increasing the risk of injury or crash.

Fix. Dealers will install an additional heatshield, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed February 1, 2023. Owners may contact Triumph's customer service at 1-678-854-2010. Triumph's number for this recall is SRAN 605.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Under high ambient temperature and slow moving traffic situations, the maximum operating temperature of the radiator fan can be exceeded, leading to a malfunction of the fan motor. If the rider ignores the instrument warning light and continues to operate the motorcycle at low speed, coolant may be displaced.

What the new part or software changes. Defective motorcycles will not have the heat shield fitted. Remedied motorcycles will have the heatshield fitted.

Fixed in production. Affected models will be fitted with the additional heatshield once production of these models recommences at the end of January 2023.

Part made by: Alexon Co Ltd, Thailand · Maker's recall no. SRAN 605

How the problem was found.

  • May22: First Warranty claim for replacement fan received. End of June22: The Triumph Warranty department observes an increase in Warranty claims received and commences an investigation. There are no seized fan Field or Service Reports. There are no deaths or injuries to report.
  • Aug22: Triumph Quality department requests parts back from the field to verify in-service claims.
  • Jan23: Triumph Recall Committee agree to launch a voluntary in-service campaign.

Brake bolts: Rear brake disc bolts may loosen: 2022 · 22V207000 · 2022-03-31 · 910 bikes

Problem. Triumph Motorcycles America, Ltd. (Triumph) is recalling certain 2022 Speed Triple RS and Speed Triple RR motorcycles. The rear brake disc bolts may loosen.

Risk. A loose rear brake disc bolt may contact other components, preventing the wheel from rotating freely and increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the rear brake disc bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 27, 2022. Owners may contact Triumph customer service at 1-678-854-2010. Triumph's number for this recall is SRAN 596.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. An incorrect thread form in the rear axle fails to activate the thread locking compound delivered by the mating bolt.

What the new part or software changes. The O/E bolt includes encapsulated thread locking compound whereas the remedy bolt will be supplied as a plain bolt.

Fixed in production. The condition was corrected in production commencing on 14th Feb 2022.

Part made by: Hota Industrial MFG. Co. Ltd, Taiwan, Province of China · Maker's recall no. SRAN 596

How the problem was found.

  • During on going testing, Triumph observed at 20,000 miles on one motorcycle one of the four retaining bolts used to fix the rear brake disc to the rear axle to be loosening.
