Gearbox: Loss of ability to shift gears: 2019-2020 · 20V014000 · 2020-01-15 · 1,304 bikes

Problem. Triumph Motorcycles America, LTD (Triumph) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Speed Twin motorcycles. The gear shift lever linkage may separate from shifter ball joint, resulting in an inability to change gears.

Risk. A loss of ability to change gears may increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. Triumph will notify owners, and dealers will secure the attachment with a different thread locking compound, free of charge. The recall began January 27, 2020. Owners may contact Triumph customer service at 1-678-854-2010. Triumph's number for this recall is SRAN 569.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The original contaminated thread locking compound provides reduced adhesion and permitted a loosening of the ball joint.

What the new part or software changes. No components are being replaced. Application of new liquid thread locking compound will be introduced to the ball joint threads.

Fixed in production. Triumph corrected the condition in production by applying the correct thread locking compound from VIN 994414 on 19/12/19.

Maker's recall no. SRAN 569

How the problem was found.

  • June 2019: Quality Dept. receives feedback from Warranty Dept. regarding reported cases of loose ball joint on Speed Twin models. Quality Dept. commence investigation.
  • Dec 2019: Quality Dept. concludes investigations. 08 Jan 2020: Triumph's Warranty Dept. briefed Triumph Recall Committee on reports of riders unable to correctly select gears due to loose ball joint on gear change linkage. Based on the information presented, the Recall Committee made a safety defect determination decision. There are no reports of accidents, injuries or fatalities related to this …

Coolant hose: Coolant leak near rear tire: 2019 · 19V167000 · 2019-03-05 · 848 bikes

Problem. Triumph Motorcycles America, LTD (Triumph) is recalling certain 2019 Speed Twin motorcycles. Improper routing of the coolant hose may cause it to contact the exhaust header pipe, damaging the hose and resulting in a coolant leak near the rear tire.

Risk. Loss of coolant near the rear tire may cause a loss of traction, increasing the risk of crash.

Fix. Triumph will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the coolant hose routing, rerouting the hose and replacing it if necessary, free of charge. The recall began April 15, 2019. Owners may contact Triumph customer service at 1-678-854-2010. Triumph's number for this recall is SRAN563.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The coolant hose may have been incorrectly routed during assembly.

What the new part or software changes. There are no distinguishing features on the remedy component from the recalled component. The remedy involves re-routing the hose. If the hose has been damaged, it will be replaced with a new hose (same part number).

Fixed in production. Triumph corrected the condition in production at VIN SMTD54HF6KT939756, on 31st January 2019

Maker's recall no. SRAN563

How the problem was found.

  • January 2019: An internal factory audit identified the misrouting on a motorcycle randomly selected for a full audit. A review of other motorcycles then took place to determine the extent of the issue. 27th February: Triumph’s Quality Dept. briefed Triumph’s Recall Committee on the investigation findings. Based on the information presented, the Recall Committee made a safety-defect determination. …

Recalls in Canada and Japan (7)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Speed Twin 2023-2024 · 2026-02-13 · no. 2026058 · 1,366 bikes · Electrical

On certain motorcycles, the recall repairs completed by a Triumph dealer during recall SRAN 627 (Transport Canada recall no. 2024-618) may not be effective. As a result, a second repair may be required. On certain motorcycles, the wiring for the alternator may not be routed correctly. As a result, the wiring could become damaged by movement and heat and cause a short circuit. Safety Risk: A short circuit can create the risk of a fire. Corrective Actions: Triumph will notify owners by mail and advise you to take your motorcycle to a dealership to replace the wiring for the alternator.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Speed Twin 2025 · 2025-03-04 · no. 2025115 · 212 bikes · Lights And Instruments

On certain motorcycles, the red rear turn signals may have been installed too close to the brake lamp. Canadian regulations require a minimum distance between red turn signals and the brake lamp. Safety Risk: Red turn signals that are too close to the brake lamp could create confusion and cause others not to recognize when the motorcycle is braking and/or turning. This could increase the risk of a crash. Corrective Actions: Triumph will notify owners by mail and advise you to take your motorcycle to a dealership to replace the red rear turn signals with amber ones.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Speed Twin 2023-2024 · 2024-10-21 · no. 2024618 · 1,361 bikes · Electrical

On certain motorcycles, the wiring for the alternator may not be routed correctly. As a result, the wiring could become damaged by movement and heat and cause a short circuit. Safety Risk: A short circuit can create the risk of a fire. Corrective Actions: Triumph will notify owners by mail and advise you to take your motorcycle to a dealership to install a wire alignment clip. If the wiring is damaged, it will be repaired or replaced.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Speed Twin 2019-2020 · 2020-01-15 · no. 2020007 · 167 bikes · Powertrain

On certain motorcycles, the gear shift lever may become loose. This could make it difficult or impossible to change gear. Safety Risk: An inability to change gear could increase the risk of a crash. Corrective Actions: Triumph will notify owners by mail and instruct you to take your motorcycle to a dealer to apply a new thread-locking compound on the shift linkage ball joints.

Source: Transport Canada
