Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix.

Engine and cooling: 1994-1996 · 97V173000 · 1997-10-08 · 1,275 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The owner's manual warns the rider not to leave the engine running unnecessarily when the motorcycle is stationary and to ride off immediately after starting.

Risk. If this warning is ignored and the engine is left running at an increased speed above idle while the motorcycle is at a standstill with the air stream required for cooling missing, the exhaust system temperature will rise considerable increasing the risk of a fire.

Fix. Owners will be sent a warning label for their motorcycle and an owner's manual insert.

What owners wrote

The 2 most recent complaints, as filed (lightly reformatted).

1996 BMW R1100RSL · Electrical and instruments · CO · filed 2001-04 · NHTSA 744283Wiring harness shorted out causing a failure to continue operating. Other owners of the same model bmw motorcycle (r1100gs and r1150gs, 1995-2001) have experienced similar failures, including a recent near fatality when the electrical system and all lights failed at night. The owner was almost killed by a truck. The problem results from cracked insulation in the wiring harness, causing short circuiting and varying degrees of electrical failure. Property damage up to $2000 has been reported; it would not be surprising to learn that a major injury accident has occurred following one of these failures. Since motorcycle accidents are often fatal, the operator would probably not have reported the electrical failure.
