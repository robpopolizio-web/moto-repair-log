Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Brake hose or line: 1988 · 91V107000 · 1991-06-18 · 1,122 bikes

Problem. Front brake hydraulic line may contact and rub against fuel tank or speedometer cable resulting in scuffing and subsequent wearing on brake lines outer surface which could cause a hydraulic fluid leak. Consequence of defect: hydraulic fluid leak due to the damaged lines impairs brake performance.

Risk.

Fix. A spiral hose to protect the brake line from contact will be installed, as well as a hose clamp and support, so brake line will not change.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Recalls in Canada and Japan (2)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: R100Rt 1987-1988 · 1991-08-02 · no. 1991113 · 72 bikes · Brakes

On certain motorcycles, the front brake hydraulic line may rub against the fuel tank or speedometer cable. This may result in wearing of the brake line with the possibility of a brake fluid leak. This would impair the performance of the front brake. Correction: The brake line will be inspected and replaced if necessary. In addition, a protective sleeve and an additional clamp will be installed.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: R100Rt 1985-1989 · 1989-03-02 · no. 1989026 · 266 bikes · Brakes

On certain motorcycles, the contacting surfaces which activate the front and rear brake light switches may become misaligned. Misalignment of either of these surfaces could cause that brake light switch to fail prematurely. If this should occur, the brake light would not function for that particular brake. This could, depending on operating conditions, result in a crash with another vehicle or vehicles. Correction: Front and brake lever will be modified, a cap will be placed on the rear brake stop bolt and the front and rear brake light switches will be replaced with a newer switch if the vehicle is not already so equipped.

Source: Transport Canada
