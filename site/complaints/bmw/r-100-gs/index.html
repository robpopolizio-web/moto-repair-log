Recalls (3)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

BRAKES:MECHANICAL SERVICE:PADS AND SHOES (brakes and abs): 1992 · 92V081000 · 1992-06-05 · 2,486 bikes

Problem. The bonding technique used between the pad and the backing plate can result in a porous joint layer which water and salt can penetrate, resulting in corrosion of the backing plate.

Risk. The front brake pads can separate partially or completelyfrom the corroded backing plate, resulting in decreased stopping ability and apossible vehicle accident.

Fix. Replace the front brake pads with new pads utilizing a different bonding method which ensures a non-porous joint layer to prevent water and salt penetration of the backing plate.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

WHEELS (wheels): 1977-1982 · 84V092000 · 1984-08-16 · 24,166 bikes

Problem. Wheel may develop cracks and fractures from operating on rough roads. (spokes will primarily be affected).

Risk.

Fix. Upon inspection, a redesigned wheel will be installed along with a new inner tube and the needed balance weights.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

FUEL:FUEL TANK ASSEMBLY:CAP:FILLER (fuel system): 1977 · 77V094000 · 1977-06-17 · 1,835 bikes

Problem. The filler caps which have relief pressure valves incorporated may allow pressure build-up in the gas tank. This pressure may be greater than the closing pressure of the float valve in the carburetor, which could cause overflooding.

Risk.

Fix. Dealer will modify the gas cap to prevent pressure build-up, at no cost to the owner.

1990 BMW R 100 GS · Clutch, gearbox and final drive · NJ · filed 2012-02 · NHTSA 10447105Tl* the contact owns a 1990 bmw r100gs motorcycle. The contact stated that the transmission started to rattle. The gears locked and released temporarily, but the motorcycle had to be downshifted and shut off before the gears would fully release. The contact took the vehicle to the dealer and was told there was nothing wrong with the transmission. The failure mileage was unknown and the current mileage was 33,000.
1990 BMW R 100 GS · 12,000 miles · Clutch, gearbox and final drive · NJ · filed 2012-01 · NHTSA 10445982Tl* the contact owns a 1990 bmw r100gs. The contact stated the transmission violently rattled after the vehicle stopped moving. The failure also occurred while driving at various speeds. The vehicle was taken to an authorized dealer on various occasions and the transmission was rebuilt three times. The failure continued to occur. The manufacturer was made aware of the failure and offered no assistance. The failure mileage was 12,000. Updated 03/09/12*lj the consumer stated when took the transmission apart, he discovered the gears were designed too close together. Bmw converted a four speed to a five speed by removing the floating connection disks, adding a gear and allowing the gear fingers direct gear to the gear connections. When the bushings were worn, the finger hit in the their receiver on then next gear. In neutral, with the clutch engaged the fingers all hit on one side or the other. The fingers on the clutch side of the fourth hit the second gear all the time. The consumer stated if he stepped in any gear above second, it was hard to back to first gear. Updated 07/12/12
1979 BMW R 100 GS · 92,000 miles · Brakes and ABS · WA · filed 2011-07 · NHTSA 10410927Tl* the contact owns a 1979 bmw r100rt motorcycle.
