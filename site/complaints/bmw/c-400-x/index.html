Throttle cable: Throttle cable may stick: 2019 · 20V083000 · 2020-02-13 · 416 bikes

Problem. BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2019 C400X scooters. Water may collect in the throttle cable, which can freeze in cold temperatures, resulting in a stuck throttle.

Risk. If the throttle sticks open, there would be an increased risk of a crash.

Fix. BMW will replace the throttle cable with an improved version, free of charge. The recall began August 19, 2020. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.

Brake caliper: Brake calipers may corrode: 2019 · 19V593000 · 2019-08-14 · 5,938 bikes

Problem. BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2019 C 400 X, 2018-2020 G 310 GS, and 2017-2020 G 310 R scooters and motorcycles. The front and rear brake caliper pistons may corrode, possibly causing the piston to stick or drag in the caliper bore.

Risk. A sticking or dragging brake could affect braking performance, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace the front and rear brake calipers, free of charge. The recall began March 13, 2020. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (2)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Japan: Bmw C400X · 2021 · no. 001418982.pdf · 3,732 bikes

BMW Motorrad has discovered that corrosion may occur on the brakes of the G 310 and C 400 models. This corrosion may cause functional limitations in some cases.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)

Japan: Bmw C400X · 2020 · no. 001348204.pdf · 652 bikes

The position of the throttle cable may cause an accumulation of water in the throttle cable sleeves that will not drain when driving C 400 models in the rain for an extended period of time or cleaning them thoroughly. When driving in out-side temperatures below freezing, this can lead to stiff movement up to the complete freezing of the throttle cables. Critical driving situations cannot be excluded under these conditions.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)
