Recalls (4)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Engine: 1998-2000 · 00V264000 · 2000-09-12 · 2,368 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. A spring-loaded thermostatic element in the oil thermostat housing is held in place by a cover that is retained by a snap ring. The snap ring may not have been properly installed during production. During vehicle operation, the snap ring could unseat, allowing the cover to loosen or detach, resulting in loss of the entire oil volume.

Risk. Engine damage could occur if the engine is not immediately switched off. In addition, oil could travel along the engine cover and ultimately contact the rear tire, reducing its traction and affecting the stability of the motorcycle, possibly resulting in a crash and personal injury.

Fix. Dealers will inspect the snap ring installation and if the snap ring is not fully seated, the complete oil pump intermediate flange will be replaced.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Engine: 1998 · 00V266000 · 2000-09-12 · 188 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The cylinder head contains two sealing plugs covering holes in the area near the spark plugs. One or both plugs may not have been fully seated during vehicle assembly. Either or both of the sealing plugs could loosen or completely detach from their mounting point during vehicle operation.

Risk. This condition could allow coolant to escape, travel along the engine cover, and contact the rear tire, reducing its traction and affecting the stability of the motorcycle. In addition, the loss of coolant could lead to increased engine operating temperature and possible overheating.

Fix. Dealers will replace the sealing plugs with a stainless steel plug design.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Fuel tank: 1998 · 98V258000 · 1998-10-14 · 1,000 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. During extended operation in high ambient temperature conditions, the area of the pump mounting surface of the fuel tank can deform, forming a gap between the tank mounting surface and the gasket, allowing fuel to escape from the tank at that area.

Risk. If an ignition source was present, a fire could occur resulting in personal injury.

Fix. Dealers will replace the fuel pump-to-tank gasket.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Brakes: 1998 · 97V134000 · 1997-08-08 · 973 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The adjusting screw for the pressure point of either the brake or clutch levers may not be sufficiently tight, due to grease contamination of the threads.

Risk. The screw(s) can back out over time while the vehicle is being operated causing a change in the pressure point of the master cylinder for the clutch and/or front brake increasing the risk of a vehicle crash.

Fix. Dealers will check the adjustment screws of the front brake and clutch levers for movement. If the screws are tight, no further action will be taken. If a screw is found to be loose, it will be removed, cleaned and reinstalled with new loctite.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Recalls in Canada and Japan (4)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: K1200Rs 2000-2004 · 2004-01-23 · no. 2004026 · 2,527 bikes · Fuel System

On certain motorcycles, the crimp-type hose clamps along the fuel line may not have been installed properly and could leak. Fuel leakage in the presence of an ignition source could result in a fire. Correction: Dealer will inspect, and if necessary, adjust all crimp-type hose clamps.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: K1200Rs 1998 · 2000-10-05 · no. 2000218 · 29 bikes · Engine

On certain motorcycles, the cylinder head sealing plugs, covering holes in the area of the spark plugs, may not be fully seated. If either or both of the sealing plugs were to detach from their mounting point during vehicle operation, coolant could escape from the holes, travel along the engine cover and contact the rear tire. This could result in loss of traction and possible loss of vehicle control. Correction: Sealing plugs will be replaced with stainless steel plugs.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: K1200Rs 1998 · 1999-01-14 · no. 1999005 · 88 bikes · Fuel System

On certain vehicles, deformation of the fuel tank when the vehicle is operated in high ambient temperature conditions could cause a gap to form between the fuel tank mounting surface and the fuel pump gasket.
