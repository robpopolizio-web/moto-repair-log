Labels and stickers: Tire pressure label not affixed properly/FMVSS 120: 2021 · 20V495000 · 2020-08-20 · 447 bikes

Problem. BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2021 R 1250 RT, R 1250 R, R 1250 RS, R 1250 GS and R 1250 GS Adventure motorcycles. The tire pressure labels may not remain affixed to the motorcycle frame. As a result, these motorcycles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 120, "Wheels and Rims-Other Than Passenger Cars."

Risk. If the label detaches from the motorcycle, proper tire pressure information may not be readily available. This could result in the rider improperly filling the tires which may affect motorcycle handling and control, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace the tire pressure label, free of charge. The recall began October 19, 2020. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

What the new part or software changes. Recall component: Tire Pressure Label – Part Number (8 396 854 / 838, 8 405 415 / 416), model dependent.

Part made by: Schreiner Group GmbH & Co. KG, Germany

How the problem was found.

  • On June 3, 2020, the motorcycle assembly plant became aware of several certification and noise emission labels which had partially detached from the motorcycle frame. An immediate hold was issued, and an inspection was initiated.
  • Between June and July, an engineering review was conducted to analyze the label. The review indicated that a label with a different material had been developed and released in mid-May. The review suggested that the label material was insufficient. Further activities suggested that tire pressure labels were also affected. As a result, additional reviews were conducted to determine the location of …
  • On August 13, 2020, it was determined that potentially affected motorcycles may not conform to FMVSS 120 Section 5.3(b). As a result, BMW decided to conduct a voluntary non-compliance recall. BMW has not received any reports, nor is BMW otherwise aware, of any accidents or injuries related to this issue.

Brake fluid: Brake fluid cap missing information/FMVSS 122: 2020 · 20V166000 · 2020-03-19 · 365 bikes

Problem. BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2018-2020 RnineT Scrambler and RnineT, 2020 R1250R and R1250RS, 2019-2020 Rnine T Pure, R1250GS,R1250GS Adventure and R1250RT and 2018 RnineT Racer motorcycles. The brake fluid reservoir cap may not clearly state the required type of brake fluid and the process for filling the reservoir, possibly leading to an improper maintenance procedure. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 122, "Motorcycle Brake Systems."

Risk. Improper brake system maintenance could affect braking performance and increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace the brake fluid reservoir cap, free of charge. The recall began December 23, 2020. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

What the new part or software changes. Brake Fluid Reservoir Cover – p/n – (RnineT: 8404-098/-100/-102, 1542056), (R1250: 8405-752/-753/-754, 9829547).

Part made by: GILLES TOOLINGGmbH, Luxembourg

  • In mid-February, 2020, based upon an internal review of motorcycle accessory parts, BMW became aware that certain motorcycles produced for the US market (with an order option for a color-specific brake fluid reservoir cover) could have been released with a cover that may not fully conform to FMVSS 122 Section 5.1.9(d). Brake light: Brake lights flash during braking/FMVSS 108: 2020 · 20V146000 · 2020-03-12 · 4,026 bikes

Problem. BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2020 F900R, F900XR, S1000RR, F750GS, F850GS, F850GS Adventure, R1250GS, R1250GS Adventure, R1250RS, R1250R, RnineT, RnineT Pure and RnineT Scrambler and 2019-2020 S1000R motorcycles. The brake light may flash instead of remain steady during emergency braking. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."

Risk. A flashing brake light can cause confusion to other drivers on the road, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. BMW will notify owner, and dealers will reprogram the emergency stop signal function, free of charge. The recall began May 11, 2020. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.

What the new part or software changes. Emergency stop signal function programming – no part number.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (4)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Japan: R1250Rs · 2023 · no. 001709526.pdf · 686 bikes

BMW Motorrad has determined that due to an incorrectly set encoding parameter in the software, the daytime driving lights are only activated at 32% of the required brightness. This does not comply with the legally required brightness for daytime driving lights.

Source: Japan's transport ministry (MLIT)

Canada: R1250 Rs 2020 · 2020-08-21 · no. 2020387 · 85 bikes · Label

On certain motorcycles, the material that the tire pressure, compliance and/or noise emission labels are printed on may be incorrect. As a result, the labels could detach from the frame. If this happens, and the owner’s manual isn’t consulted, the correct tire pressure information may not be known. This could lead to the tires being inflated incorrectly and cause poor handling and/or a tire failure. Safety Risk: Poor handling or a tire failure could cause a loss of control and result in a crash. Corrective Actions: BMW will notify owners by mail and instruct you to take your motorcycle to a dealer to replace the labels.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: R1250 Rs 2020 · 2020-03-20 · no. 2020120 · 58 bikes · Brakes

This recall affects certain motorcycles equipped with optional colour-specific brake fluid reservoir covers. Canadian regulations require that certain safety information is provided on the brake fluid reservoir cover. On these motorcycles, that information may be missing or difficult to read. This could lead to improper maintenance of the brake system, like using the wrong type of brake fluid. Safety Risk: Improper maintenance of the brake system could lead to brake problems and create the risk of a crash. Corrective Actions: BMW will notify owners by mail and instruct you to take your motorcycle to a dealer to replace the brake fluid reservoir cover.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: R1250 Rs 2020 · 2020-03-12 · no. 2020098 · 673 bikes · Lights And Instruments

On certain motorcycles, the stop lamp will flash during emergency braking. Canadian regulations require the stop lamp to stay on constantly while braking. Safety Risk: A flashing stop lamp could cause confusion for other drivers. This could increase the risk of a crash. Corrective Actions: BMW Motorrad will notify owners by mail and instruct you to take your motorcycle to a dealer to update the stop lamp software, so that the stop lamp does not flash during emergency braking.

Source: Transport Canada
