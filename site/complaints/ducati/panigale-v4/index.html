Brake hose or line: Loss of rear brakes: 2025-2026 · 26V050000 · 2026-01-28 · 1,440 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2025-2026 Streetfighter V4, Streetfighter V4 S, Panigale V4, and Panigale V4 S motorcycles. Excessive heat may damage the rear brake hose between the ABS control unit and brake caliper, resulting in a sudden loss of the rear brakes.

Risk. A sudden loss of rear brake function increases the risk of a crash or injury.

Fix. Dealers will replace the thermal shield, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 9, 2026. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446. Ducati's number for this recall is SRV-RCL-26-001. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on February 3, 2026. This recall expands and replaces previous NHTSA recall number 25V013. Vehicles repaired under the previous recall will need to have the new remedy completed.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. A small section of the rear brake hose between the ABS control unit and rear brake caliper is not sufficiently insulated from the heat from exhaust manifolds, Ducati determined the rigid thermal shield needed to be extended.

What the new part or software changes. The remedy consists of a larger updated thermal shield that is extended, and more protective.

Fixed in production. The same remedy has been implemented to Ducati Motor Holding production line since middle September 2025.

Fix type: Replace · Part made by: M.P.T. Plastica Srl, Italy · Maker's recall no. SRV-RCL-26-001

  • On January 14, 2025, Ducati Motor Holding's internal safety commission decided to launch the recall campaign CR285 (25V013) to improve the first version of the thermal shield position and its fixing system.

Ducati Motor Holding requested the return of the defective part, and when part was received it started to conduct tests and analyses to understand the damage root cause. Various tests were carried out but in the first phase, although these tests …
  • On January 27, 2026, Ducati Motor Holding's internal safety commission decided to launch a new recall campaign to address this condition in the affected population. Ducati NA has not received any cases of accidents, injuries or property damage related to this issue in the USA.

Rear axle: Rear wheel axle failure: 2018-2024 · 25V570000 · 2025-09-03 · 10,235 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Panigale V4 SL, 2019-2024 Panigale V4 R, 2018-2024 Panigale V4 S, 2023-2025 Streetfighter, 2018-2024 Panigale V4, and 2020-2022 Streetfighter V4 motorcycles. The rear wheel axle could lose its structural integrity and break.

Risk. A rear wheel axle that breaks while riding can result in a loss of motorcycle control, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.

Fix. Dealers will replace the rear wheel axle, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 16, 2025. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446. The manufacturer's number for this recall is SRV-RCL-25-002.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The rear wheel shaft has been machined in a way that may not be sufficiently robust in relation to some of the stressed conditions, especially due to the installation layout on these specific models. The analysis shows that this phenomenon is influenced by mileage, the type of use of the motorcycle, and compliance with recommended maintenance and/or observance of the correct tightening procedures and values for the components involved (e.g. tensioning the drive chain or replacing the rear sprocket by over tightening of the single-sided swingarm screws or rear sprocket nut).

What the new part or software changes. The remedy component is machined in such a way that the notch effect is reduced in the cross-section area where the failure occurs. Furthermore, the remedy component has an increased inertia moment (increasing the thickness) in the cross-section area where the failure occurs. Both actions render the remedy component more robust.

Fixed in production. The same remedy has been implemented to Ducati Motor Holding production line since June, 2024.

Fix type: Replace · Part made by: Russ Tiger Enterprise Co., Ltd., Taiwan, Province Of China · Maker's recall no. SRV-RCL-25-002

The more robust rear shaft was introduced into production June 2024 even if the failure mode was not understood at that moment. Fuel cap: Fuel sprays when cap is removed: 2017-2019 · 19V382000 · 2019-05-20 · 1,880 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Panigale V4, Panigale V4 S, Panigale Speciale, and 2019 Panigale R motorcycles. Excessive pressure in the fuel tank may cause fuel to spray when opening the fuel cap.

Risk. Fuel spray can increase the risk of injury and a fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will update the fuel cap venting system, provide an updated page for the owner's manual, and affix a warning label decal to the fuel tank, free of charge. The recall began July 9, 2019. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446. Ducati's number for this recall is SRV-RCL-19-001. Note: This recall includes motorcycles that may have been previously remedied under recall 18V-238 for a similar issue.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Too much pressure inside the fuel tank, more likely in hot weather conditions.

What the new part or software changes. The updated owner’s manual page and fuel tank decal will increase customer awareness and provide necessary precautions for customers to follow when opening the fuel cap. The venting system will be updated in order to improve the venting flow capacity from the tank.

Fixed in production. The same remedy has been introduced in the production line.

Maker's recall no. SRV-RCL-19-001

How the problem was found.

  • In Q4 2018 and Q1 2019, few customer fuel spray cases were reported to DMH. All of them where reported only when bike is stopped and customer is opening the fuel cap. DMH spent Q1 2019 to investigate case by case in order to replicate the same condition.
  • On May 14, 2019 the DMH Safety committee decided to launch a voluntary recall. No injuries nor fire cases were reported.

Brake pads: Rear brake pad friction material may detach: 2018 · 18V480000 · 2018-07-19 · 72 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2018 Ducati Panigale V4, Panigale 959, Panigale 1299 FE, and Hypermotard motorcycles. The friction material on the rear brake pads may detach from the backing plate.

Risk. If the rear brake pad friction material detaches, it may lengthen the distance needed to stop the motorcycle, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the rear brake pads. Brake pads from the affected batch will be replaced, free of charge. The recall began August 27, 2018. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Brembo has been advised that the issue was caused by a second thermal treatment during the manufacture of the brake pads by a Brembo supplier called Federal Mogul Italy S.r.l. The affected batch is FMI batch 672, which were not manufactured in accordance with the appropriate procedures, as a result of human error. This second treatment exposed some brake pads to higher temperature than Federal Mogul intended to be used for this piece of equipment. This error potentially created lower adherence between brake pad friction material and the backing plate that may result in detachment of the brake pad friction material from the back plate during vehicle use.

What the new part or software changes. Pads from the faulty batch (672) will be replaced by spare pads. (same p/n – different batch)

Fixed in production. The supplier moved the production to a new line equipped with temperature control as of June 2018.

Part made by: Brembo S.p.A., Italy

How the problem was found.

  • No reports arrived from the network.
  • On Week 28 2018, Brembo notified a non-conformity of the FMI batch 672 and the intent to proceed with a worldwide safety recall campaign.
  • On Week 28 2018 the Ducati Motor Holding safety internal commission decided to extend the recall campaign to the affected bikes by the batch 672.

Fuel tank: Fuel leak from tank breathing system valve plug: 2018 · 18V239000 · 2018-04-16 · 692 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2018 Ducati Panigale V4, Panigale V4 S, and Panigale V4 Speciale motorcycles. Fuel may leak from the gas tank's internal breathing system valve plug.

Risk. A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will replace the plug and the O-ring, free of charge. The recall began on May 21, 2018. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The Panigale V4 fuel tank is equipped with an internal breathing valve, that allows the fuel to fill the entire volume of the fuel tank. In certain cases, the internal breathing system valve plug could leak from the outer diameter O-ring. The O-ring could have been damaged during the valve plug installation process. The grease presence all around the O-ring did not allow the supplier to detect the leakage during the quality control leakage test. Additionally, the low level of fuel within the tank did not allow detection of leakage during the production phase in the factory. In case of O-ring failure, fuel leaks might occur.

What the new part or software changes. The replacement plug will have a chamfered edge and the grease has been replaced with a different lubricant to facilitate plug installation, reducing the risk to pinch the O-ring.

Fixed in production. The new plug is currently installed on bikes produced since March 28th 2018.

Maker's recall no. TBD

How the problem was found.

  • •
  • On 5th of March 2018 a field report came from a European dealer who notified to DMH a fuel leak occurred on a demo bike. The fuel leak was located on the back side of the tank, where the internal breather valve is located. After a brief inspection by the dealer the O-ring was found pinched. • DMH immediately started an internal investigation. • 3 identical cases were then reported to DMH by other …
  • In April a case was reported from the US as well. •
  • In all field reports, the leaking was due to the pinched or damaged O-ring. •
  • In April the investigation was finalized at Ducati Motor Holding and the root cause was identified as above. (Please refer to “Describe the cause”). •
  • On April 11th, 2018, Ducati determined that a worldwide safety Recall was justified. For subject motorcycles sold in the U.S there have been no reports of injuries due to the defect.

Fuel cap: Fuel sprays when cap is removed: 2018 · 18V238000 · 2018-04-16 · 692 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2018 Ducati Panigale V4, Panigale V4 S, and Panigale V4 Speciale motorcycles. Fuel may spray out of the gas tank when the tank cap is opened.

Risk. If fuel spills out of the tank when it is opened, it can increase the risk of a fire in the presence of an ignition source.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will rework the fuel tank cap to improve the fuel tank's breathing capacity, free of charge. The recall began on May 21, 2018. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The existing fuel cap is not able to vent properly all the conditions. In certain rare conditions resulting from a correlation between external temperature, bike usage and quantity of fuel in the tank; there might be a pressure increase within the tank. If this occurs, the difference of pressure allows the fuel to come out from the tank when opening the cap.

What the new part or software changes. The cap will be reworked in order to increase the fuel tank breathing capacity.

Fixed in production. From March 28 2018, we introduced the same modification to the fuel tank cap on all the bikes in production line.

Maker's recall no. SRV-RCL-18-002

How the problem was found.

  • •
  • On 21st of March 2018, DNA informs DMH about #2 field reports from our dealers: -
  • On a dealer demo bike and then on customer bike, after a test ride, some fuel has ejected out of tank once opened the cap. • Following these reports DMH started an internal investigation. •
  • On the 29th of March DNA informed DMH about two additional field reports from our network. •
  • In April the investigation was finalized, and the root cause was identified as above. (Please refer to “Describe the cause”). •
  • On April 11th, 2018, Ducati determined that a worldwide safety Recall was justified. For subject motorcycles sold in the U.S, there have been no reports of injuries due to the defect.
