The short answer

158 US owners have filed a safety complaint about the Ducati Monster (model years 2004-2009, 2011-2016, 2018-2020). The most reported areas are fuel system (72%), brakes and abs (19%), frame and bodywork (6%). Half of the complaints that gave a mileage happened before 5,000 miles.

The bike has had 10 safety recalls. Recall work is free at any Ducati dealer, so check your VIN (link under each recall below).

Rear Brake Hose May Have Heat Damage (brakes and abs): 2014-2021 · 21V335000 · 2021-05-11 · 1,312 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2015-2021 Monster 821, 2014-2021 Monster 1200 S/STD, and 2016-2019 Monster 1200 R motorcycles. The rear brake hoses may be susceptible to heat damage.

Risk. Damaged brake hoses may cause the rear brakes to fail, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the rear brake hoses, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on July 12, 2021. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Ducati Motor Holding (DMH) has identified the following causal or contributory factors that may lead to this condition: (1) installation/use of a rear brake hose material that has a lower resistance to temperature; (2) rear brake system layout in the Monster 821 and Monster 1200 application, whereby the rear brake hoses are, in a limited and specific area, exposed to the hot airflow of the exhaust manifold; and (3) conditions of use of the motorcycle, in particular if the motorcycle remains idling on the side stand or rear box stand for extended periods of time (e.g., during warm up or dealer service/diagnostics).

What the new part or software changes. The remedy brake hoses have a different length of the rigid (metallic) section to move the flexible (plastic) portion of the hose away from the hot airflow of the exhaust manifold.

Fixed in production. The motorcycle models involved in the Recall are no longer in production.

Part made by: FREN TUBO s.r.l., Italy

Air May Reduce Effectiveness of Rear Brakes (brakes and abs): 2017-2020 · 21V315000 · 2021-05-04 · 5,909 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2017-2020 Monster 797, Monster 1200, Supersport, 2016-2020 XDiavel, and 2018-2020 Monster 821 motorcycles. Air may permeate the rear brake system.

Risk. Air in the system may reduce effectiveness of the rear brakes, increasing the distance required to stop and the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will install new rear brake hoses, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on June 28, 2021. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The causal or contributory factors are: (1) excessive gas permeation into the rear brake hose due to brake system architecture; (2) long periods of riding time without application of pressure inside the rear brake hose; and/or (3) long periods of time without using the motorcycle.

What the new part or software changes. The remedy rear brake hoses use a different material.

Fixed in production. The remedy rear brake hoses were introduced in production at different times depending on the model.

How the problem was found.

  • February 2020: Ducati had discussions with NHTSA ODI regarding owner reports (VOQs) describing degraded rear brake performance due to air in the rear brake system.
  • April 2020: Ducati initiated a tracked TSB campaign for the subject vehicles to replace the PTFE hoses on the subject models with PA 11/12 hoses, which have a lower permeation effect than traditional PTFE.
  • February 18, 2021: NHTSA ODI opened PE21-003 to evaluate this issue, citing additional owner reports following issuance of the TSBs.
  • February - April 2021: Ducati shared data and other information with NHTSA ODI, and Ducati and NHTSA ODI held several technical discussions. Ducati conducted further reviews and analyses to supplement its investigation of this issue. Based upon its investigation, Ducati believes that various causal or contributory factors, in combination, contribute to the experience reported by riders of certain …

Gear Shift Knob may Detach (clutch, gearbox and final drive): 2017-2019 · 18V835000 · 2018-11-27 · 2,742 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2017-2019 Ducati Monster 1200, Monster 821, SuperSport and SuperSport S motorcycles. The shift lever may have been incorrectly assembled, possibly resulting in the shift knob detaching from the lever.

Risk. If the shift knob detaches, the rider may not be able to shift gears, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the gear lever shift knob tightness, replacing it as necessary, free of charge. The recall began January 14, 2019. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446. Ducati's number for this recall is SRV-RCL-18-007.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The existing shift lever may have been improperly assembled, resulting in the shift knob loosening while riding. This is due to poor attachment of the knob into the shift lever. If the interference fit between the knob and lever is not enough to fix the part in position, or the part is not peened correctly, the pin could move and then be rotated and/or loosen while riding. If this occurs the rider will not be able to change gears.

What the new part or software changes. DMH has reviewed the process and ensured all indication are properly implemented by the supplier, in order to avoid interface problems between the shift knob and lever.

Fixed in production. The same actions (inspection and replacement) have been implemented in production.

Maker's recall no. SRV-RCL-18-007

How the problem was found.

  • •
  • On 4th July 2018: Ducati Motor Holding has received a field report from a European dealer where the shift knob has detached while riding. • DMH has started an internal investigation. •
  • On October 2018: DMH investigated the whole assembly process and determined the root cause as reported above (ref. Describe the Cause). •
  • On 20th November 2018: DMH decided to assign this event as Safety related and started a worldwide safety campaign. •
  • On April 4th, 2019, Ducati Motor Holdings Spa determined a VIN range extension for this campaign and began the VIN range extension process.

Front Brake Master Cylinder Failure (brakes and abs): 2015-2018 · 17V812000 · 2017-12-15 · 6,758 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2015-2018 1299 motorcycles (including STD, S, FE and SL models), Monster 1200 motorcycles (including S and R models), Multistrada motorcycles (including S and PP models), 2015-2017 Panigale R, 2016-2018 XDaviel S motorcycles and 2017-2018 Scrambler 800 CafÃ© Racer motorcycles. These motorcycles are equipped with Brembo front brake master cylinders that may fail.

Risk. If the front brake master cylinder fails, the loss of front braking ability can increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will replace the front brake master cylinder piston, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin February 15, 2018. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-800-231-6696.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The anisotropy of the piston material, in addition to potential porosity introduced during the injection process, could lead to crack generation and thus potential component failure.

Fixed in production. The component manufacturer, Brembo, is now supplying aluminum front brake master cylinder pistons for the production line. Units produced before the first supply of PPS front master cylinder pistons from Brembo are excluded from the recall as they were made from aluminum. Likewise, units produced after the last supply of PPS front master cylinder pistons from Brembo are excluded from the recall as well.

Part made by: Brembo S.p.A., Italy

How the problem was found.

  • In May, 2017, Ducati received initial information from the field regarding front brake malfunction involving a motorcycle in the European market. During the same month Ducati received a second European front brake malfunction report. REAR BRAKE PADS (brakes and abs): 2012 · 12V188000 · 2012-05-01 · 283 bikes

Problem. Ducati is recalling certain model year 2012 848, streetfighter, hypermotard, multistrada, and monster motorcycles. The friction material may detach from the backing plate of the rear brake pads.

Risk. If the friction material detaches, there would be a sudden loss of rear brake efficiency, increasing the stopping distance and increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and replace, as necessary, the rear brake pads free of charge. The safety recall began on may 21, 2012. Owners may contact ducati at 1-800-231-6696 or go to their website at www.ducati.com.

CYLINDER HEAD MAIN WIRING HARNESS INTERFERENCE (electrical and instruments): 2009 · 09V142000 · 2009-04-29 · 1,755 bikes

Problem. Ducati is recalling 1,755 my 2009 monster 696 motorcycles. The main wiring harness may come in contact with the vertical cylinder head and exhaust pipe possibly resulting in damage to the motorcycle and a potential hazard to the rider.

Risk. Any damage to the harness may lead to loss of functionality of the motorcycle's lighting system or an unexpected loss of engine performance, and thereby increase the risk of a crash.

WATER PUMP IMPELLER (engine and cooling): 2007 · 07V477000 · 2007-10-11 · 8 bikes
