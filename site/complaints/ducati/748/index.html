Recalls (2)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Wheel cover: 1997 · 99V360000 · 1999-12-23 · 4,165 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles equipped with a rear wheel hub spindle. Internal cracks may be present on the rear wheel spindle.

Risk. This condition could cause the spindle to fail affecting the control of the motorcycle, and could result in a crash.

Fix. Dealers will inspect the rear wheel spindle and replace if necessary.

Fuel hose or line: 1998 · 99V048000 · 1999-03-11 · 793 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The fuel lines inside the fuel tank can leak.

Risk. Fuel leakage in the presence of an ignition source can result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will replace this fuel line.
