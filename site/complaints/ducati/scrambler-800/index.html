Turn signal: Turn signals are not bright enough/FMVSS 108: 2021 · 21V361000 · 2021-05-17 · 228 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2021 Scrambler 800 Night Shift motorcycles. The front and rear turn signal lights may not flash brightly enough. As such, these motorcycles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."

Risk. Turn signal lights that aren't bright enough can reduce other driver's ability to see that the turn signals have been activated, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the front and rear turn signals, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on June 28, 2021. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446. Ducati's number for this recall is SRV-RCL-21-009.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Due to an error on this bike model bill of material, these motorcycles were assembled on the Ducati Motor production plant with the incorrect front and rear turn indicators.

What the new part or software changes. The remedy front and rear turn signals are in line with FMVSS’s requirements.

Fixed in production. The same Recall remedy have been introduced on Ducati production plant.

Maker's recall no. SRV-RCL-21-009

How the problem was found.

  • On April 29, 2021 an internal check has raised that all the first part of production of this specific bike model was assembled with the incorrect front and rear turn signals. After that was defined that the turn signals were not meet certain requirements in FMVSS.
  • On May 11, 2021, Ducati Motor Holding's internal safety commission decided to launch a recall campaign to address this condition in the affected population.

Battery cable and exhaust: Exhaust valve cable may contact battery wiring: 2018-2021 · 20V781000 · 2020-12-15 · 1,072 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2018-2021 Scrambler 1100 motorcycles. The exhaust valve cable could contact the main positive battery wire and potentially short-circuit.

Risk. An electrical short-circuit increases the risk of fire.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will install a new cable guide and self-locking cable tie to the exhaust valve cable, free of charge. The recall began January 22, 2021. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446. Ducati's number for this recall is SRV-RCL-20-003.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The exhaust valve bowden cable could contact to main vehicle positive battery wiring, causing a short circuit with a potential risk of fire. Ducati has never received Customer complaints, and reports of crash or injuries.

What the new part or software changes. A new cable routing with an exhaust valve cable retainer and a fixing tie (zip tie) has been introduced.

Fixed in production. The same remedy was introduced on DMH Ducati production line on September 2020, and on DMT Ducati production line on October 2020.

Maker's recall no. SRV-RCL-20-003

How the problem was found.

  • From August 2019 until December 2020, two reports arrived at Ducati from the network about Scrambler 1100 short circuit between the exhaust valve bowden cable and the main vehicle positive battery cable. Since the beginning, Ducati Motor Holding have analyzed these cases.
  • On December 15, 2020 the Ducati Motor Holding safety internal commission decided to launch a recall campaign to the potential affected bikes park. Ducati has never received Customer complaints, and reports of crash or injuries.

Brake master cylinder: Front brake master cylinder failure: 2017-2018 · 17V812000 · 2017-12-15 · 6,758 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2015-2018 1299 motorcycles (including STD, S, FE and SL models), Monster 1200 motorcycles (including S and R models), Multistrada motorcycles (including S and PP models), 2015-2017 Panigale R, 2016-2018 XDaviel S motorcycles and 2017-2018 Scrambler 800 CafÃ© Racer motorcycles. These motorcycles are equipped with Brembo front brake master cylinders that may fail.

Risk. If the front brake master cylinder fails, the loss of front braking ability can increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will replace the front brake master cylinder piston, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin February 15, 2018. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-800-231-6696.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The anisotropy of the piston material, in addition to potential porosity introduced during the injection process, could lead to crack generation and thus potential component failure.

Fixed in production. The component manufacturer, Brembo, is now supplying aluminum front brake master cylinder pistons for the production line. Units produced before the first supply of PPS front master cylinder pistons from Brembo are excluded from the recall as they were made from aluminum. Likewise, units produced after the last supply of PPS front master cylinder pistons from Brembo are excluded from the recall as well.

Part made by: Brembo S.p.A., Italy

  • In May, 2017, Ducati received initial information from the field regarding front brake malfunction involving a motorcycle in the European market. During the same month Ducati received a second European front brake malfunction report. Side or centre stand: Side stand pivot bolt nut may detach: 2015-2016 · 16V891000 · 2016-12-12 · 5,502 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain model year 2015-2016 Ducati Scrambler motorcycles manufactured February 17, 2015, to November 11, 2016. The affected vehicles have a side stand pivot nut that can detach, potentially causing the side stand position sensor to no longer function properly.

Risk. If the side stand position sensor does not function properly, the engine may stall, or the motorcycle may be ridden while the stand is still down. Either scenario increases the risk of a crash.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will replace the side stand pivot bolt, free of charge. The recall began on begin January 24, 2017. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-800-231-6696. Ducati's number for this recall is SRV-RCL-16-004.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The side stand pivot bolt’s thread length is not sufficient to fully engage the nut’s metallic locking tabs.

What the new part or software changes. The difference between the old and new pivot bolt is the length of the threaded portion (from 15mm to 17mm), and the bolt now exhibits a new surface treatment (initially gray, now black).

Fixed in production. The same remedy has been introduced in the production line.

Maker's recall no. SRV-RCL-16-004

How the problem was found.

  • During June 2016 Ducati received the first warranty claims, related to the global market, where two bikes had stalled during riding. Ducati initiated a global analysis of the issue finding that a total of 34 cases were related to the issue. We are not aware of any related crashes or injuries. On December 5, 2016 Ducati determined that a worldwide safety Recall was justified.

Recalls in Canada and Japan (2)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Scrambler 800 2021 · 2021-05-19 · no. 2021302 · 34 bikes · Lights And Instruments

On certain motorcycles, the wrong front and rear turn signals were installed at the factory. As a result, the turn signals are too small, and do not meet Canadian regulations. Safety Risk: Turn signals that are too small may be difficult for others to see, which could increase the risk of a crash. Corrective Actions: Ducati will notify owners by mail and instruct you to take your motorcycle to a dealer to replace the front and rear turn signals.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Scrambler 800 2015-2018 · 2017-12-18 · no. 2017637 · 1,081 bikes · Brakes

On certain motorcycles, a crack within the polyphenilene sulphide (PPS) piston of the front brake master cylinder could occur. This could result in the master cylinder to not be able to generate sufficient hydraulic pressure, which could lead to a loss of braking capability of the front brake, increasing stopping distances and increasing the risk of a crash causing injury and/or damage to property. Correction: Dealers will replace the PPS piston with one made from aluminum.

Source: Transport Canada
