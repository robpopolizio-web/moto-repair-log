Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Side or centre stand: Side stand may break: 2018 · 19V825000 · 2019-11-18 · 493 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2018 Multistrada 1260, 1260S, and Pikes Peak motorcycles. The side stand may be improperly welded, potentially causing the stand to break.

Risk. If the stand breaks the motorcycle may unexpectedly fall over, increasing the risk of injury.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will replace the side stand, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin January 2, 2020. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446. Ducati's number for this recall is SRV-RCL-19-004.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The cause for this is that the length of the tube may have been cut out of tolerance causing an improper weld at the joint.

What the new part or software changes. Since February 2018 a compliant stand with the correct length tube has been in production.

Fixed in production. The same remedy has been introduced in production line.

Part made by: Donati s.r.l., Italy · Maker's recall no. SRV-RCL-19-004

How the problem was found.

  • Some field requests were notified by dealers to Ducati Motor Holding (DMH) on April 2018. Since that month DMH started the investigation. The root cause was identified within the supplier process on a limited production batch supplied to DMH. A remedy was implemented at the supplier level, in order to improve the welding process. Extended Stopping Distance: 2012-2021 · Preliminary evaluation PE21003 · 2021–2021

The Office of Defects Investigation (ODI) opened this Preliminary Evaluation (PE) based on twenty-two (22) Vehicle Owner Questionnaires (VOQs) for soft rear brakes caused by air seeping into the rear brake lines of model year (MY) 2012-2020 Ducati Monster, Multistrada, SuperSport, Panigale, Scrambler and Diavel motorcycles.The consumers were alleging that the rear brakes would not properly function unless the rear brake pedal was applied several times or that there was a total loss of rear braking during normal applications, increasing the risk of a crash. In a March 2020 meeting, Ducati indicated that they were aware of certain models in which air may enter the motorcycles' rear braking system through the brake lines, due to exposure to heat from the exhaust system. Although this condition compromises rear braking performance, Ducati stated that they did not consider this to be a … It led to recall 21V315000.

Fuel leak at pump/tank interface: 2004-2007 · Preliminary evaluation PE10045 · 2010–2011

After opening this PE, we met with subject owners, dealers, and Ducati North America personnel.We learned that the subject leakage is limited to the Multistrada line alone and is unrelated to composite fuel tank deformation.Rather it stems from the inability of the original sealing O-ring to conform to the tank's relatively rough sealing surface.Recall 11V-020 provides a new X section O-ring to address the Multistrada issue and with that recall, this PE is closed for the Multistrada.Meanwhile, unlike the Multistrada, the Monster line fails to exhibit a current safety defect trend and, on that basis, this PE is closed for those models as well. It led to recall 11V020000.
