Recalls (2)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix.

Brakes and ABS: 2012 · 12V188000 · 2012-05-01 · 283 bikes

Problem. Ducati is recalling certain model year 2012 848, streetfighter, hypermotard, multistrada, and monster motorcycles. The friction material may detach from the backing plate of the rear brake pads.

Risk. If the friction material detaches, there would be a sudden loss of rear brake efficiency, increasing the stopping distance and increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and replace, as necessary, the rear brake pads free of charge. The safety recall began on may 21, 2012. Owners may contact ducati at 1-800-231-6696 or go to their website at www.ducati.com.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Fuel system: 2012 · 12V053000 · 2012-02-14 · 913 bikes

Problem. Ducati is recalling certain model year 2011-2012 hypermotard and monster motorcycles manufactured from june 25, 2011, through november 30, 2011. The fuel lines contain micro-cracking due to insufficient drying of raw materials prior to extrusion.

Risk. This could cause a fuel leak, which in the presence of an ignition source, may lead to a fire and increased risk of property damage or personal injury.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will replace the fuel lines free of charge. The safety recall began on march 1, 2012. Owners may contact ducati at 1-800-231-6696 or go to their website at www.ducati.com.

2014 Ducati Hypermotard (HYM) · 8,250 miles · Brakes and ABS · MI · filed 2020-02 · NHTSA 11306686While pulling into a parking lot at low speed the bike seemed to be dragging with very little free momentum. Upon inspection the rear tire was flat. After dismounting the cause of the flat was discovered to be that the tire valve stem was sheared completely off by the rear brake caliper, which had detached while the bike was in motion and impacted the valve stem. It was fortunate that this occurred at very low speed and not on the highway, which could have potentially caused a major accident. The rear caliper has never been serviced so the bolts were insufficiently torqued prior to customer sale, either at the factory or the dealer. Photos are attached.

Written documentation is in my possession and other affected owners are available to nhtsa for its review. While replacing failed units, ducati remains mute and unresponsive to vehicle owners and operators on the safety subject. Evidence in the form of failed canbus units are apparently returned to ducati and even possibly re-used as new after repair.
