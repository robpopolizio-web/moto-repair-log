Side or centre stand: Side stand not welded properly: 2019 · 21V738000 · 2021-09-23 · 205 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2019 Hypermotard 950 SP motorcycles. The side stand may be improperly welded, potentially causing the stand to break.

Risk. A broken side stand could result in the motorcycle falling down, increasing the risk of injury.

Fix. Dealers will install a new side stand, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 22, 2021. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446. Ducati's number for this recall is SRV-RCL-21-011.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The cause for this is an improper weld that joins the tube to the forged part.

What the new part or software changes. The non-conforming components may have an improper weld. Replacement components will ensure a proper weld for structural rigidity.

Fixed in production. The non-conforming components were part of a specific batch. This is not widespread or continuing in production.

Part made by: La Tecnomeccanica Sud Spa, Italy · Maker's recall no. SRV-RCL-21-011

How the problem was found.

  • In September 2020 Ducati received a report from the field about a broken side stand that caused the motorcycle to fall down to the ground. An investigation was started at that time. During this investigation, Ducati became aware of a total of 10 field cases worldwide. Of these, 7 cases complained that the side stand bent excessively and 3 cases complained that the side stand broke, causing the …
  • On September 21, 2021, Ducati Motor Holding's internal safety commission decided to launch a recall campaign to address this condition in the affected population.

Battery: Negative battery terminal may fail causing stall: 2019-2020 · 19V780000 · 2019-10-30 · 793 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Hypermotard 950 and Hypermotard 950 SP motorcycles. The negative terminal on the battery may break, potentially causing a sudden loss of power and an engine stall.

Risk. An engine stall can increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the routing of the negative cable, rerouting it as necessary. In addition, the battery will be better secured with insulation pads. These repairs will be performed free of charge. The recall began December 10, 2019. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446. Ducati's number for this recall is SRV-RCL-19-003.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The negative cable terminal to the battery might break as a result of the cable terminal being too tight, combined with vibration of the battery.

What the new part or software changes. The routing will be better located, in order to reduce tension when connected to the battery terminal. In addition, two insulation pads will be installed in order to reduce the impact of the vibrations to the battery.

Fixed in production. The same remedy has been introduced in production line.

Maker's recall no. SRV-RCL-19-003

How the problem was found.

  • Field requests were notified by dealers to DMH between July and October 2019. DMH investigated the root cause, identifying some improvements to be implemented in the installation process (cable length and battery installation). A remedy was implemented in order improve the reliability of the terminal connection, ensuring the proper installation to the battery.
  • On October 29, 2019 the DMH Safety committee decided to launch a voluntary recall. To date, no accidents, no injuries, no fatalities have been reported.

Brake pads: Rear brake pad friction material may detach: 2018 · 18V480000 · 2018-07-19 · 72 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2018 Ducati Panigale V4, Panigale 959, Panigale 1299 FE, and Hypermotard motorcycles. The friction material on the rear brake pads may detach from the backing plate.

Risk. If the rear brake pad friction material detaches, it may lengthen the distance needed to stop the motorcycle, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the rear brake pads. Brake pads from the affected batch will be replaced, free of charge. The recall began August 27, 2018. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. Brembo has been advised that the issue was caused by a second thermal treatment during the manufacture of the brake pads by a Brembo supplier called Federal Mogul Italy S.r.l. The affected batch is FMI batch 672, which were not manufactured in accordance with the appropriate procedures, as a result of human error. This second treatment exposed some brake pads to higher temperature than Federal Mogul intended to be used for this piece of equipment. This error potentially created lower adherence between brake pad friction material and the backing plate that may result in detachment of the brake pad friction material from the back plate during vehicle use.

What the new part or software changes. Pads from the faulty batch (672) will be replaced by spare pads. (same p/n – different batch)

Fixed in production. The supplier moved the production to a new line equipped with temperature control as of June 2018.

Part made by: Brembo S.p.A., Italy

How the problem was found.

  • No reports arrived from the network.
  • On Week 28 2018, Brembo notified a non-conformity of the FMI batch 672 and the intent to proceed with a worldwide safety recall campaign.
  • On Week 28 2018 the Ducati Motor Holding safety internal commission decided to extend the recall campaign to the affected bikes by the batch 672.

Battery cable: 2008 · 07V474000 · 2007-10-10 · 385 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, the battery can move side to side inside the fuel tank mounting compartment. This movement could result in main wiring harness damage at the battery terminal. This condition could cause an electrical short consequently stopping the engine.

Risk. If the engine stops while the motorcycle is being driven, it could increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will install a battery mounting bracket and two double lock velcro strips on the bottom of the battery. The recall began on november 19, 2007. Owners may contact ducati motorcycles at 1-408-253-0499.

Canada: Hypermotard 950 2019 · 2021-09-24 · no. 2021594 · 25 bikes · Structure

On certain Hypermotard 950 SP models, the side stand may break. If this happens, the motorcycle could fall over while it is parked. Safety Risk: A motorcycle that falls over could create a risk of injury to nearby people. Corrective Actions: Ducati will notify owners by mail and instruct you to take your motorcycle to a dealer to replace the side stand.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: Hypermotard 950 2019-2020 · 2019-11-12 · no. 2019571 · 95 bikes · Electrical

On certain motorcycles, the connection between the negative battery cable and the battery terminal may be too tight. Over time, this could cause the battery terminal to break. If this happens, there would be sudden loss of power and the engine may stall. Safety Risk: A sudden loss of power could increase the risk of a crash. Corrective Actions: Ducati will notify owners by mail and instruct you to take your motorcycle to a dealer to inspect and re-route the negative battery cable, as needed. Dealers will also ensure that the battery is properly secured.

Source: Transport Canada

Safety Risk: Reduced braking could cause extended stopping distances and increase the risk of a crash. Corrective Actions: The company will notify owners by mail.
