Brakes: Air may reduce effectiveness of rear brakes: 2017-2020 · 21V315000 · 2021-05-04 · 5,909 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2017-2020 Monster 797, Monster 1200, Supersport, 2016-2020 XDiavel, and 2018-2020 Monster 821 motorcycles. Air may permeate the rear brake system.

Risk. Air in the system may reduce effectiveness of the rear brakes, increasing the distance required to stop and the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will install new rear brake hoses, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on June 28, 2021. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The causal or contributory factors are: (1) excessive gas permeation into the rear brake hose due to brake system architecture; (2) long periods of riding time without application of pressure inside the rear brake hose; and/or (3) long periods of time without using the motorcycle.

What the new part or software changes. The remedy rear brake hoses use a different material.

Fixed in production. The remedy rear brake hoses were introduced in production at different times depending on the model.

How the problem was found.

  • February 2020: Ducati had discussions with NHTSA ODI regarding owner reports (VOQs) describing degraded rear brake performance due to air in the rear brake system.
  • April 2020: Ducati initiated a tracked TSB campaign for the subject vehicles to replace the PTFE hoses on the subject models with PA 11/12 hoses, which have a lower permeation effect than traditional PTFE.
  • February 18, 2021: NHTSA ODI opened PE21-003 to evaluate this issue, citing additional owner reports following issuance of the TSBs.
  • February - April 2021: Ducati shared data and other information with NHTSA ODI, and Ducati and NHTSA ODI held several technical discussions. Ducati conducted further reviews and analyses to supplement its investigation of this issue. Based upon its investigation, Ducati believes that various causal or contributory factors, in combination, contribute to the experience reported by riders of certain …
  • May 3, 2021: Although the field data indicate that this condition does not “relate to motor vehicle safety” (as this phrase is defined in the Safety Act), in a good faith effort to address NHTSA’s concerns about this issue in the subject models, Ducati’s Internal Safety Commission decided to conduct a safety recall for the subject vehicles, which were covered by the previously issued TSBs. Gearbox: Gear shift knob may detach: 2017-2019 · 18V835000 · 2018-11-27 · 2,742 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2017-2019 Ducati Monster 1200, Monster 821, SuperSport and SuperSport S motorcycles. The shift lever may have been incorrectly assembled, possibly resulting in the shift knob detaching from the lever.

Risk. If the shift knob detaches, the rider may not be able to shift gears, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the gear lever shift knob tightness, replacing it as necessary, free of charge. The recall began January 14, 2019. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446. Ducati's number for this recall is SRV-RCL-18-007.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The existing shift lever may have been improperly assembled, resulting in the shift knob loosening while riding. This is due to poor attachment of the knob into the shift lever. If the interference fit between the knob and lever is not enough to fix the part in position, or the part is not peened correctly, the pin could move and then be rotated and/or loosen while riding. If this occurs the rider will not be able to change gears.

What the new part or software changes. DMH has reviewed the process and ensured all indication are properly implemented by the supplier, in order to avoid interface problems between the shift knob and lever.

Fixed in production. The same actions (inspection and replacement) have been implemented in production.

Maker's recall no. SRV-RCL-18-007

How the problem was found.

  • •
  • On 4th July 2018: Ducati Motor Holding has received a field report from a European dealer where the shift knob has detached while riding. • DMH has started an internal investigation. •
  • On October 2018: DMH investigated the whole assembly process and determined the root cause as reported above (ref. Describe the Cause). •
  • On 20th November 2018: DMH decided to assign this event as Safety related and started a worldwide safety campaign. •
  • On April 4th, 2019, Ducati Motor Holdings Spa determined a VIN range extension for this campaign and began the VIN range extension process.

Mirror: Vibrations may distort rear view mirror images: 2017-2019 · 18V793000 · 2018-11-09 · 1,676 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2017-2019 Ducati Supersport motorcycles. Vibrations may distort the images in the mirrors, reducing the driver's visibility.

Risk. Reduced rearward visibility can increase the risk of a crash.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will replace the mirrors with new mirrors, free of charge. The recall began January 28, 2019. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446. Ducati's number for this recall is SRV-RCL-18-005.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The existing rear-view mirrors could vibrate at certain frequencies and in some specific riding conditions. If this occurs the reflected images may not be detailed, affecting the rider’s visibility rearward. Under these conditions, due to vehicle speed and engine revs, the reflected image is not clearly visible on the rear-view mirrors.

What the new part or software changes. The latest version will reduce the rear-view mirror vibration level at certain vehicle speeds and engine RPMs, based on gear position, due to a new and improved design.

Fixed in production. The latest version of the rear-view mirrors is currently implemented in production since late October 2018.

Maker's recall no. SRV-RCL-18-005

How the problem was found.

  • •
  • In the month of September 2018, DNA informed DMH about some feedback regarding mirror image clarity. • During September 2018, DMH investigated the feedback. •
  • On November 8th, 2018, DMH decided to inform NHTSA and launch a dedicated recall campaign to provide improved design mirrors, in order to update North American units.

Fuel system: Hoses near exhaust manifold may melt causing fire: 2017-2018 · 18V371000 · 2018-06-05 · 1,462 bikes

Problem. Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Ducati Supersport, and Supersport S motorcycles. The Airbox blow-by and fuel tank overfill hoses may be routed too close to the exhaust manifold, which may cause the hoses to melt.

Risk. If the hoses melt, it can cause a fuel leak, increasing the risk of a fire.

Fix. Ducati will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the hose routing and correct as necessary, free of charge. The recall began on July 6, 2018. Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446.

From the maker's defect report to NHTSA

Cause. The routing and clamping design for the fuel drain hose does not adequately prevent possible contact between the hoses and the horizontal exhaust manifold.

What the new part or software changes. New routing moves airbox and fuel tank drain hoses in front of existing electrical wiring. Rubber clamps securing the hoses will be lowered so both hoses avoid contact with the exhaust manifold.

Fixed in production. From May 23, 2018, we introduced the same modification to routing on all the bikes in production line.

Maker's recall no. TBD

How the problem was found.

  • Within the past three months, Ducati received three separate field reports, from three different countries, reporting fires. One such report was from the United States. Ducati Motor Holding immediately began a root-cause investigation with the first reported case in early March.
  • On June 5th, 2018, Ducati’s Internal Safety Commission made the decision to launch a world-wide safety recall campaign.
