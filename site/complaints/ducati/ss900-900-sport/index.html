Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Fuel pump or line: 2001 · 02V011000 · 2002-01-11 · 147 bikes

Problem. On certain motorcycles, fuel can leak from the fuel pump flange.

Risk. Fuel leakage in the presence of an ignition source can result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will install spacers between the fuel pump flange and the fuel tank, and will also replace the flange gasket if it is damaged. Owner notification is expected to begin during january/february 2002. Owners who take their vehicles to an authorized dealer on an agreed upon service date and do not receive the free remedy within a reasonable time should contact ducati at 1-973-839-2600.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov
