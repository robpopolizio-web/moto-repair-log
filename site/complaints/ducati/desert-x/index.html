Recalls in Canada and Japan (1)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: Desert X 2023 · 2023-03-21 · no. 2023159 · 12 bikes · Accessories

This recall is for certain motorcycles equipped with Ducati Performance accessory soft side bags (part number 96781971AA). The instructions for how to install the right-side soft bag may be unclear. As a result, the bag could contact the exhaust. If this happens while riding, the bag could melt. Safety Risk: A soft bag that melts onto a hot surface can create the risk of a fire. Corrective Actions: Ducati will notify owners by mail and advise you not to ride your motorcycle with the side bags attached until the recall repairs are completed.
