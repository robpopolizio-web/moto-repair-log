Recalls (1)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

DUCATI/REAR BRAKE REACTION ROD NUT AND BOLT (brakes and abs): 1994-1997 · 97V222000 · 1997-12-08 · 2,380 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The rear brake reaction rod nut and bolt will need to be replaced.

Risk. This condition can cause increased braking effort.

Fix. Dealers will replace the rear brake reaction rod nut and bolt with a new modified nut and bolt.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

What owners wrote

The 3 most recent complaints, as filed (lightly reformatted).

1996 Ducati 900FE/900CR · Frame and bodywork · CA · filed 2001-06 · NHTSA 747293The main frame of the motorcycle cracked across the upper frame tubes at the welds where the stearing head is attached to the remainder of the frame. Because I noticed the damage before complete failure occured there were no injuries. Had complete failure occured the motorcycle would have been un controlable. Subsequent to my discovery of the dammage I discovered that this type of failure has occured in the past with many other 900ss model ducatis. The vehicle is now at the dealership who is contacting the manufacture. Information regarding this type of failure may be found at several web sites including club desmo's bulletin www.clubdesmo.com/newbbs/messages/23/23.htm.
1994 Ducati 900FE/900CR · Engine and cooling · MA · filed 2000-04 · NHTSA 721357Head stud broke on horizontal cylinder (silver finished ones seem to be more prone to this than black ones)causing oil to pour out directly onto exaust header and rear tire, high potential of fire and loss of vehicle control. This is a known and fairly common problem on three or four model years of this bike. Company has a very inconsistent policy regarding repairs. Bob nevola is the the contact person at ducati usa. Ask him about steering head frame cracks while you're at it.
1996 Ducati 900FE/900CR · Frame and bodywork · CA · filed 2000-01 · NHTSA 717884Frame cracking is a problem with ducati 900cc motorcycles. It is a regular topic of discussion on the web sites and on group rides. Depending on location and milage the dealer reaction has varied from warrentied frame replacement to denial of manufacturer fault. While I have not experianced this fault myself, it is dangerous enough that I believe t should be reported and investigated.
