Recalls (2)

Newest first. Open one to read the problem, the risk and the fix, plus the cause and timeline from the maker's own defect report and links to the official documents.

Driveshaft: 1998 · 99V137000 · 1999-06-01 · 800 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The front sprocket stop plate on the gearbox transmission shaft can come off damaging the chain.

Risk. This condition can the driver to lose control of the motorcycle, increasing the risk of a crash.

Fix. Dealers will replace the front sprocket stop plate.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Fuel hose or line: 1998 · 99V048000 · 1999-03-11 · 793 bikes

Problem. Vehicle description: motorcycles. The fuel lines inside the fuel tank can leak.

Risk. Fuel leakage in the presence of an ignition source can result in a fire.

Fix. Dealers will replace this fuel line.

Check if your VIN is affected on nhtsa.gov

Recalls in Canada and Japan (2)

Recalls filed with Transport Canada and Japan's transport ministry for bikes listed under this model name. Many are the same fault as a US recall above; some were only issued there.

Canada: St2 1993-2002 · 2002-04-22 · no. 2002075 · 1,415 bikes · Powertrain

On certain motorcycles, it is possible that the clutch slave cylinder may leak internally causing the clutch control to jam. This could affect the control of the motorcycle and cause a crash. Correction: Dealers will replace the clutch slave cylinder.

Source: Transport Canada

Canada: St2 1998 · 1999-06-07 · no. 1999107 · 32 bikes · Powertrain

On certain vehicles, the front sprocket stop plate can come off the output shaft locating groove and cause the chain to go out of alignment. This condition could damage the chain and cause an accident. Correction: The front sprocket stop plate on the transmission shaft will be replaced.

Source: Transport Canada

What owners wrote

The 5 most recent complaints, as filed (lightly reformatted).

1998 Ducati ST2 · 3,942 miles · Fuel system · CT · filed 2008-07 · NHTSA 10235463I purchased a 1998 ducati st2 in may 2008 with 3946 miles on the odometer. I checked your site and found there was a recall notice for the fuel system hoses(99v048000). The original owner did not have any recall work performed. I made an appointment with my local ducati dealer to have the recall work done. Their backlog was 6 weeks. The bike needed a new clutch so I took it to my local mechanic. While working on the bike he noticed it had drivable issues. He found the hoses inside the tank and those connecting to the fuel pump had literally disintegrated. Pieces of the delaminated hose found their way into the fuel pump. Had I been driving the bike, this could have led to the fire due to leaky hoses. Now the hoses & fuel pump need replacement. I contacted the dealer who called the ducati rep to see if they would replace the hoses/fuel pump. The service manager gave up due to a lack of response. He told me to call the ducati rep which I did. I am not getting any response. I wanted to know if your recall covered the fuel pump as well as the hoses since the fuel pump malfunction was a direct result of the faulty hoses. I also contacted the nhsta because I have gotten no response from ducati.
1998 Ducati ST2 · Battery & charging · CO · filed 2002-08 · NHTSA 765045Alternator (stator) failed 3x in 2000 miles of vehicle operation - causes battery to discharge and engine to stop abruptly - repaired by dealer each time, other associated parts replaced also still the defect remains. Certainly seems to be design defect!

This is a major part that keeps the bike together. The bolt itself is not broken but it is expose. If it continueds to crack, it could cause an accident. I would like it fixed by ducati.
